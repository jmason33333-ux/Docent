# Rowan Platform Vision: The Three-Sided Marketplace for Fantasy Publishing

**Last Updated:** December 2, 2025
**Status:** Strategic Vision Document
**Horizon:** 3-7 Year Roadmap

---

## Executive Summary

**Rowan is not just an AI reading companion—it's the infrastructure layer for the fantasy publishing ecosystem.**

By building a three-sided marketplace connecting readers, creators, and publishers, Rowan becomes:
- **For Readers:** The gateway to fantasy fandom (community, guidance, depth)
- **For Creators:** A monetization platform for curated content and fan engagement
- **For Publishers/Authors:** An insights engine and direct engagement channel with their most dedicated fans

This creates compounding network effects where each side makes the others more valuable, building a defensible moat that competitors cannot easily replicate.

## The Three-Sided Marketplace

### Side 1: Readers (Community Members)

**Current State (MVP):**
- AI chat companion for understanding complex fantasy books
- Spoiler-safe answers
- Chapter notes and context

**Future State (Membership Ecosystem):**

#### 1. **Community Access**
- **Discord/Forum:** Fantasy readers discussing books, sharing theories, comparing notes
- **Book Clubs:** Structured read-alongs with Rowan as the "companion in the room"
- **IRL Events:**
  - Local meetups for specific series fans
  - Virtual watch parties for fantasy adaptations
  - Author Q&As (e.g., Brandon Sanderson sessions where Rowan helps prep questions)

**Value:** Readers want to geek out WITH other humans, not just an AI. Community creates stickiness.

#### 2. **Newsletter**
- **Weekly Lore Drops:** Deep dives into Cosmere mechanics, magic systems, cross-book connections
- **New Chapter Notes:** Curated summaries and analysis as books release
- **Creator Spotlights:** Feature fan artists, theorists, podcast hosts
- **"This Week in Fantasy":** News about upcoming releases, adaptations, conventions

**Value:** Keeps Rowan top-of-mind even when users aren't actively reading. Builds authority.

#### 3. **Software (Rowan AI)**
**Base Features:**
- Chat interface, spoiler-safe answers, chapter notes
- Multi-book tracking
- Citation-backed responses

**Enhanced Features for Members:**
- Unlimited queries (free tier = 10/month)
- Voice mode (Rowan reads answers aloud in sage voice)
- Custom knowledge bases (upload your own notes, theories)
- Export conversation transcripts
- Advanced search across all notes

**Value:** AI is the hook, but it's ONE benefit, not the only one.

#### 4. **Digital Assets**
- **Exclusive Chapter Notes:** Early access to new books (e.g., Wind and Truth chapter notes before public release)
- **Cosmere Connection Maps:** Interactive visualizations of how books/magic systems connect
- **Character Relationship Diagrams:** Visual family trees, allegiance maps
- **Downloadable Study Guides:** PDFs for offline reading prep
- **Wallpapers/Art:** Dark academia Rowan-themed desktop backgrounds, phone lockscreens

**Value:** Tangible value beyond software. Collectable, shareable.

#### 5. **Databases**
- **Searchable Cosmere Wiki:** Every character, location, magic system with spoiler-level filters
- **Quote Library:** Searchable database of memorable lines by book/character/theme
- **Timeline Database:** Interactive chronology of events across series
- **Theory Vault:** Community-submitted theories tagged by book/chapter

**Value:** Reference material that compounds in value over time.

#### 6. **Job Board / Opportunities**
- **Fantasy Publishing Jobs:** Curated listings for editors, cover designers, sensitivity readers
- **Creator Opportunities:** Paid gigs for fan artists, writers, podcasters
- **Beta Reader Signups:** Connect authors with engaged fantasy readers
- **Convention Networking:** "Looking for roommate for WorldCon" type posts

**Value:** Transforms community from hobby space to career/opportunity network.

#### 4. **Analytics & Data Products**

**This is where Rowan becomes indispensable to publishing.**

##### **Product 1: Reader Confusion Heatmaps** ($10,000/book)

**What it shows:**
- Which chapters generate the most "I'm confused" queries
- Specific plot points that need clarification
- Characters readers forget or mix up
- World-building elements that don't land

**Example Report for "The Way of Kings":**
```
CONFUSION HOTSPOTS:
- Chapter 3: 68% of readers ask "Who is Wit?" within 2 chapters
- Chapter 12: 45% confused about Kaladin's timeline (flashback vs present)
- Chapter 25: 52% don't understand what a Shardblade is at first mention
- Character confusion: 34% mix up Adolin and Renarin in first 10 chapters

RECOMMENDATIONS:
- Consider adding brief Wit context on first appearance
- Clarify timeline markers in Kaladin flashbacks
- Introduce Shardblade mechanics earlier or with more explanation
- Differentiate Adolin/Renarin descriptions more strongly
```

**Value to authors:**
- Informs editing decisions for future editions
- Helps with pacing/exposition choices in next books
- Improves reader experience = better reviews = more sales

##### **Product 2: Engagement & Drop-off Analysis** ($15,000/series)

**What it tracks:**
- Where readers stop asking questions (abandoned the book?)
- Pacing: Query density by chapter (high engagement vs. slog)
- "Aha moments": Spikes in excited/theory queries
- Completion rates by demographic

**Example Metrics:**
```
BOOK 1 ENGAGEMENT:
- Average questions per reader: 12.3
- Chapters 1-10: High engagement (3.2 queries/chapter)
- Chapters 15-20: Drop-off (-40% query volume) ← potential pacing issue
- Chapter 32: Massive spike (+215% queries) ← twist landed well
- Completion rate: 78% (industry avg: 60%)

TOP DISCUSSION TOPICS:
1. Magic system mechanics (32%)
2. Character motivations (28%)
3. Worldbuilding lore (22%)
4. Plot predictions/theories (18%)
```

**Value to publishers:**
- Identify pacing issues before print runs
- Understand what readers care most about (inform marketing)
- Compare performance across series
- Benchmark against genre averages

**What it measures:**
- Positive vs. negative discussion sentiment

## Risks & Mitigations

### Risk 1: Authors/Publishers Skeptical of AI

**Mitigation:**
- Position as "AI-assisted," not "AI-generated"
- Emphasize human curation (chapter notes written by fans)
- Lead with analytics value, not AI capabilities
- Get early author advocates (Sanderson ideal)

### Risk 2: Creators Fear Platform Risk

**Mitigation:**
- Fair revenue split (70/30)
- Portable content (let them own their work)
- Clear terms: no lock-in, no non-competes
- Build trust through transparency

### Risk 3: Community Toxicity

**Mitigation:**
- Strong moderation from day one
- Clear code of conduct
- Invest in community managers
- Author-approved spaces have stricter rules

### Risk 4: Copyright/IP Issues

**Mitigation:**
- Chapter notes are transformative fair use (commentary/criticism)
- No verbatim text reproduction
- Partner with publishers where possible
- Legal review of all content policies

### Risk 5: Competition from Fandom Wikis

**Mitigation:**
- Wikis are reference, Rowan is companion (different use case)
- Spoiler-safety is killer feature
- AI interaction > static pages
- Community integration
