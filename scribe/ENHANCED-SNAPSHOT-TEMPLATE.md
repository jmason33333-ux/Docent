# Enhanced Knowledge Snapshot Template

## Current Issues with Snapshot

1. **Too Brief**: Characters, plot threads, and world-building are high-level summaries
2. **Missing Details**: No relationships, motivations, key moments, or granular plot beats
3. **Incomplete Coverage**: Only 2 world-building concepts, 2 locations, 2 factions
4. **No Character Arcs**: Missing progression of character development
5. **No Relationships Map**: Can't answer "How do X and Y relate?"
6. **Sparse Timeline**: Too vague (just "Chapter 1-5: Concurrent events")
7. **Limited Questions**: Only 2 common questions

---

## Enhanced Template Structure

### 1. **Major Characters** (Enhanced)

**Current format is too brief. Need:**

```markdown
### [Character Name]
- **First appearance:** Chapter [X]
- **Role:** [Detailed role description]
- **Physical description:** [If relevant/apparent]
- **Key relationships:**
  - **[Character 2]:** [Nature of relationship, how they interact]
  - **[Character 3]:** [Relationship context]
- **Abilities/Powers:** [What they can do, if relevant]
- **Motivations/Goals:** [What drives them, what they want]
- **Character Arc (through Ch X):**
  - **Starting point:** [Where they were at book start]
  - **Key moments:** [Major developments, chapter references]
  - **Current state:** [Where they are now, emotionally/physically]
- **Key scenes/chapters:** [Important chapters they appear in]
- **Notable quotes:** [1-2 memorable lines, if applicable]
```

**Example:**
```markdown
### Kaladin Stormblessed
- **First appearance:** Chapter 1 (mentioned), Chapter 2 (POV)
- **Role:** Windrunner of the Fourth Ideal, leader of Bridge Four, former slave
- **Physical description:** Alethi with slave brands on forehead, dark hair
- **Key relationships:**
  - **Lirin (father):** Complex relationship - Lirin disapproves of violence, Kaladin struggles with his father's pacifism
  - **Sylphrena (spren):** Deep bond, she helps him through depression
  - **Dalinar:** Respects him, but feels he's failing him
  - **Moash (Vyre):** Former friend turned enemy, killed Elhokar
- **Abilities/Powers:** 
  - Windrunner abilities (Lashings, Shardblade, healing)
  - Can create Honor's Perpendicularity (with Dalinar)
- **Motivations/Goals:** 
  - Protect those he can't save (his core struggle)
  - Struggling with whether he can continue fighting
- **Character Arc (through Ch 10):**
  - **Starting point:** Exhausted, dealing with PTSD from endless battles
  - **Key moments:**
    - Ch 2: Fights Lezian (Pursuer), discovers new Fused abilities
    - Ch 5: Shows mercy to Fused, Leshwi shows him respect
    - Ch 6: Confronts Moash, sees Renarin's vision
    - Ch 10: Dalinar relieves him of active duty, given 10 days to decide
  - **Current state:** Retired from active combat, considering becoming a surgeon with his father
- **Key scenes/chapters:** Ch 2 (Lezian fight), Ch 5 (Leshwi duel), Ch 6 (Moash confrontation), Ch 10 (retirement discussion)
- **Notable quotes:** "I can't save everyone" (recurring theme)
```

### 2. **Plot Threads** (Enhanced)

**Current format is too high-level. Need:**

```markdown
### [Plot Thread Name]
- **Started:** Chapter [X]
- **Status:** [Detailed current state]
- **Key characters involved:** [List with roles]
- **Stakes/Conflicts:** [What's at risk, what's the conflict]
- **Key events (chronological):**
  - **Ch [X]:** [Specific event with details]
  - **Ch [Y]:** [Next development]
  - **Ch [Z]:** [Current state]
- **Connections to other threads:** [How this relates to other plots]
- **Unresolved questions:** [What readers might be wondering]
```

**Example:**
```markdown
### Kaladin's Mental Health Struggle
- **Started:** Chapter 1 (implied), Chapter 2 (explicit)
- **Status:** Kaladin has been relieved of active duty by Dalinar. He's given 10 days to decide his future. Considering becoming a surgeon with his father.
- **Key characters involved:**
  - **Kaladin:** Struggling with depression, PTSD, inability to speak Fourth Ideal
  - **Dalinar:** Supportive but recognizes Kaladin needs a break
  - **Syl:** Trying to help but Kaladin is withdrawn
  - **Lirin:** Kaladin's father, pacifist surgeon
- **Stakes/Conflicts:** 
  - Kaladin's ability to continue as a Radiant
  - The war effort needs him, but he's breaking
  - Personal vs. duty conflict
- **Key events (chronological):**
  - **Ch 2:** Kaladin fights Lezian, exhausted, admits to Syl he's struggling
  - **Ch 5:** Shows mercy to Fused, Leshwi respects him
  - **Ch 6:** Confronts Moash, sees Renarin's vision of what Moash could have been
  - **Ch 10:** Dalinar relieves him, offers teaching role, Kaladin needs time to decide
- **Connections to other threads:** 
  - Tied to Hearthstone evacuation (his family)
  - Affects Windrunner leadership (Sigzil/Skar promoted)
- **Unresolved questions:** 
  - Will Kaladin speak the Fourth Ideal?
  - Can he find peace as a surgeon?
  - Will he return to combat?
```

### 3. **World-Building / Lore** (Enhanced)

**Current format is too sparse. Need:**

```markdown
### [Concept/System Name]
- **First explained:** Chapter [X]
- **What we know:** [Comprehensive explanation]
- **How it works:** [Detailed mechanics]
- **Limitations/Rules:** [What can't it do, what are the constraints]
- **Key examples:** [Specific instances where it's used]
- **Related concepts:** [How it connects to other systems]
- **Unanswered questions:** [What's still mysterious]
```

**Example:**
```markdown
### Fabrials
- **First explained:** Chapter 1 (epigraph), expanded throughout
- **What we know:** 
  - Devices that trap spren in gemstones to create magical effects
  - Require specific gemstone types matched to spren types
  - Use metal "cages" (wires) to control spren behavior
  - Different metals create different effects:
    - Zinc/brass: Control expression strength
    - Bronze: Warning fabrials
    - Pewter: Augmenters (express attribute in force)
    - Tin: Diminishers (reduce attributes)
    - Iron: Attractors
    - Steel/Iron: Polarity control
- **How it works:** 
  - Spren must be attracted, inspected, then captured
  - Stormlight must be withdrawn from gemstone to trap spren
  - Metal cage wires touch gemstone to control spren
  - Different metals cause spren to react differently
- **Limitations/Rules:**
  - Spren can escape if gemstone structure is damaged
  - Stormlight leaks slowly from gemstones
  - Some spren types are harder to capture than others
  - Logicspren work differently (don't manifest attributes, used for clocks)
- **Key examples:**
  - Fourth Bridge: Flying platform using fabrials
  - Suppression fabrial: Fused device that disables Radiant powers (Ch 7)
  - Navani's research: Developing new fabrial techniques
- **Related concepts:**
  - Connected to Urithiru's tower fabrial (Navani studying)
  - Related to Soulcasters (different type, no trapped spren)
  - Fused use similar technology for weapons
- **Unanswered questions:**
  - How does Urithiru's tower fabrial work?
  - What are the Vriztl Guild's secret techniques?
  - Can fabrials be used to transport Stormlight off Roshar?
```

## Summary of Enhancements

1. **Characters**: Add relationships, motivations, arcs, key scenes, quotes
2. **Plot Threads**: Add granular events, character involvement, stakes, connections
3. **World-Building**: Add limitations, examples, related concepts, unanswered questions
4. **NEW: Character Relationships Map**: Explicit relationship tracking
5. **NEW: Key Moments**: Memorable scenes with significance
6. **NEW: Foreshadowing**: Track setup for later payoffs
7. **Timeline**: More granular with specific events per chapter range
8. **Questions**: Expand from 2 to 10-15 comprehensive questions

**Result**: Snapshot becomes a comprehensive reference document that can answer detailed questions without loading individual chapters.
