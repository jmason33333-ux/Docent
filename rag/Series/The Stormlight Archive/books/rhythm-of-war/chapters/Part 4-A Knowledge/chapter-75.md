## Quick Summary
**Adolin:** Adolin is accompanied by guards through Lasting Integrity as he climbs to the top of the city wall. There, he can see the deadeyes outside the wall that have been gathering for weeks--a lot more than they have ever seen before. Vaiu says that they must be responding to a Connection happening, and he attributes it to the upcoming justice that will be dispensed.

**Shallan:** Veil and Radiant try to coax Shallan out, but she is still very upset about learning that Pattern has lied to her. They have been trying to find Restares and have only one more human in the city fortress to identify, someone named Sixteen. Pattern finds Veil, and it is clear that Shallan has been avoiding him. Pattern asks Shallan if he remembers the first time they met on the boat with Jasnah, but Radiant says that that couldn't have been the first time they met because Shallan had spoken oaths before that. Pattern reveals that he had once tried to get Shallan to remember her past, but the pain she experienced in doing so convinced him to play along with the deception. Pattern says he doesn't remember the events surrounding her mother's death. Radiant asks why Pattern wanted to bond a Radiant, given that he was convinced she would kill him. He responds by saying that he knew danger was coming and that those in both the Physical and Cognitive Realms needed to work together to defeat the enemy. He believed that even if he died, it might be enough to get other Cryptics to join. Radiant asks Pattern if he has lied, and he admits that he has. She asks if he's been spying, and he apologizes, saying that he couldn't think of another way. He says that he brought someone to meet Shallan and introduces a deadeye Cryptic. Shallan emerges suddenly, locking away Veil and Radiant. She calls Pattern a traitor and a liar and, just then, realizes that Sixteen has emerged from his shelter. Shallan is suddenly convinced she needs to kill Restares, but when she sneaks up on Sixteen and sees what he looks like, she knows it's not him. She starts to leave, and Pattern asks her what's wrong, alarmed that she has suddenly changed. She said she's finally made a difficult decision.

## Rowan's "If Asked" Notes

**Q: Who is Testament?**  
A: Testament is the deadeye Cryptic that Pattern introduces to Shallan. Testament appears to be Shallan's previous spren, the one she bonded before Pattern. This is a major revelation that explains why Shallan had spoken oaths before meeting Pattern on the boat with Jasnah.

**Q: Why has Pattern been lying?**  
A: Pattern reveals that he tried to get Shallan to remember her past, but the pain she experienced convinced him to play along with the deception. He also admits to spying, saying he couldn't think of another way. He's been hiding the truth about Shallan's previous bond and her past.

**Q: Why did Pattern bond Shallan if he thought she would kill him?**  
A: Pattern responds that he knew danger was coming and that those in both the Physical and Cognitive Realms needed to work together to defeat the enemy. He believed that even if he died, it might be enough to get other Cryptics to join the cause. This shows his commitment to the larger fight.

**Q: What is Shallan's difficult decision?**  
A: Shallan says she's finally made a difficult decision, but the exact nature of it isn't fully revealed. It may relate to how she'll handle Pattern's lies, what she'll do about Testament, or how she'll move forward with her investigation of Restares.

**Q: Why are deadeyes gathering?**  
A: Vaiu says the deadeyes must be responding to a Connection happening, and he attributes it to the upcoming justice that will be dispensed (Adolin's trial). The fact that so many are gathering suggests something significant is happening.

**Q: Who is Sixteen?**  
A: Sixteen is one of the humans in the fortress that Shallan has been trying to identify. She thinks he might be Restares, but when she sees what he looks like, she knows it's not him. He's the last human she needed to check.

**Q: Why does Shallan lock away Veil and Radiant?**  
A: When Shallan emerges suddenly after seeing Testament, she locks away Veil and Radiant. This suggests she wants to deal with this situation as herself, not through her personas, or that she's so upset she's suppressing them.

**Q: What happened to Shallan's mother?**  
A: Pattern says he doesn't remember the events surrounding Shallan's mother's death. This is significant because Shallan's memories of her mother's death are blocked, and Pattern's lack of memory (or unwillingness to remember) may be related to this trauma.

**Q: Why is this chapter called "The Middle Step"?**  
A: The title "The Middle Step" likely refers to Shallan being in the middle of her journey—between her past (Testament) and her present (Pattern), between truth and deception, between her personas and herself. It's a transitional moment where she must make a decision about how to move forward.
