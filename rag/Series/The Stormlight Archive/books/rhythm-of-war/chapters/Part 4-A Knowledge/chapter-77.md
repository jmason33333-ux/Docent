## Quick Summary
**Seven and a half years ago**

Venli is in the palace at Kholinar for the signing of the peace treaty. Ulim is leading her to a secret place where his agents exchange messages and, in this case, where he hopes to find other Voidspren in spheres. Ulim has left Venli's gemheart, and she is less certain about what they are doing, especially once Ulim reveals that he has lost contact with his agent, Axindweth. They recover the message she left behind--no spheres--and they learn that she had been discovered and left the planet. Ulim is upset and says that they have to come up with another plan. Her people aren't even intimidated by the humans and are instead excited. Venli tries to make her way back on her own, because Ulim left her, but is caught by a guard. He takes her to a room filled with other humans and questions her. She says that she got lost, but they aren't entirely convinced, and say they will hold her until after the treaty has been signed. Ulim returns, shocked, saying that there are Heralds in the palace--Shalash, Kelek, and Nale. Just then, Nale enters, and he takes Venli into his custody. Once alone, it's clear that Nale recognizes Ulim, and they are not friends. Nale asks Venli how many Voidspren are there, and she tells him that Ulim is the only one. Nale asks how she was able to bring him back, and Venli deflects his question in a way that implies that it was the fault of whatever the Heralds are doing. Nale, in his paranoia, concludes that Gavilar's plan is working and that he will destroy them all. Nale decides that he has to stop bonds from happening, and tells Venli that Gavilar is planning to betray the listeners, and that she has legal grounds to break their treaty. He tells her of Szeth, that he has an Honorblade and is for sale as a slave. Nale leaves, and Ulim convinces Venli that a war with the humans would make the listeners desperate enough to pursue the forms of power that they want them to.

## Rowan's "If Asked" Notes

**Q: Why does Ulim leave Venli's gemheart?**  
A: Ulim has left Venli's gemheart, and she is less certain about what they are doing. This may be because he needs to move around or because he's trying to avoid detection. When he's not in her gemheart, Venli has more clarity and is less influenced by him.

**Q: What happened to Axindweth?**  
A: Ulim reveals that he has lost contact with his agent Axindweth. They recover a message she left behind (no spheres), and learn that she had been discovered and left the planet. This means Ulim's network of agents has been compromised.

**Q: Why are the Heralds in the palace?**  
A: Ulim is shocked to discover that there are Heralds in the palace: Shalash, Kelek, and Nale. They're likely there for the treaty signing or for some other purpose related to Gavilar's plans. Nale takes Venli into his custody when he finds her.

**Q: How does Nale know Ulim?**  
A: Once alone, it's clear that Nale recognizes Ulim, and they are not friends. This suggests they have a history, possibly from previous Desolations or conflicts. Nale is a Herald and has been around for thousands of years, so he would know about voidspren.

**Q: What is Gavilar's plan?**  
A: Nale tells Venli that Gavilar is planning to betray the listeners, and that she has legal grounds to break their treaty. Nale, in his paranoia, concludes that Gavilar's plan is working and that he will destroy them all. The exact nature of the plan isn't fully revealed, but it involves betrayal.

**Q: Why does Nale tell Venli about Szeth?**  
A: Nale tells Venli about Szeth, that he has an Honorblade and is for sale as a slave. This may be part of giving her information she can use, or it may be related to Gavilar's plan. Szeth's role in the events to come may be significant.

**Q: Why does Nale want to stop bonds?**  
A: Nale decides that he has to stop bonds from happening. This is likely because he believes that more Radiants will make things worse, or because he thinks Gavilar's plan involves creating bonds. His paranoia about Gavilar's plan drives this decision.

**Q: What does Ulim convince Venli to do?**  
A: After Nale leaves, Ulim convinces Venli that a war with the humans would make the listeners desperate enough to pursue the forms of power that they want them to. This aligns with their earlier plan to intimidate the listeners, but now it's about making them desperate through war.

**Q: Why is this chapter called "The Proper Legality"?**  
A: The title "The Proper Legality" refers to Nale telling Venli that she has legal grounds to break the treaty. Nale is a Skybreaker who follows the law, so he's concerned with legal justification. He's giving Venli a legal reason to break the peace treaty.
