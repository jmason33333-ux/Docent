## Quick Summary
As Rlain makes his way through the tower, he is greeted with suspicion, hostility, and dislike from humans, singers, and Fused. He misses how Bridge Four made him feel; while not perfect, he felt that they could see him more as himself and not a stereotype. He arrives to the Radiant infirmary and eventually finds Lirin. Lirin says that he and Hesina have been seen by stormforms and that they don't know how long they'll be able to remain in hiding. Neither is certain if they can trust Leshwi and Venli. Rlain goes over to Hesina and the maps, and they talk. Her attitude towards Kaladin and the occupation is different from Lirin, and they worry over Kaladin, whom the Fused are claiming is dead. As Rlain looks at a map of the Shattered Plains, he sees a note and asks Hesina to read it. It says that a group of people were found way out on the eastern side of the Plains. They are interrupted by Lirin who says that he thinks he recognizes one of the workers. Rlain looks and finds Dabbid, who, after he greets him, tells him that Kaladin is asleep and won't wake up.

## Rowan's "If Asked" Notes

**Q: Why is everyone suspicious of Rlain?**  
A: Rlain is greeted with suspicion, hostility, and dislike from humans, singers, and Fused. This is because he's a listener who has been working with humans (Bridge Four), which makes him an outsider to both sides. He doesn't fully belong to either group, which causes suspicion from everyone.

**Q: Why does Rlain miss Bridge Four?**  
A: Rlain misses how Bridge Four made him feel. While not perfect, he felt that they could see him more as himself and not a stereotype. Bridge Four accepted him despite being a listener, which is something he's not getting from either humans or singers in the tower.

**Q: What does Dabbid tell Rlain?**  
A: Dabbid tells Rlain that Kaladin is asleep and won't wake up. This is important information because the Fused are claiming Kaladin is dead (from jumping into the highstorm), but Dabbid's information suggests he's alive, just unconscious.

**Q: Why are Lirin and Hesina in hiding?**  
A: Lirin says that he and Hesina have been seen by stormforms and that they don't know how long they'll be able to remain in hiding. This suggests that they're trying to avoid being captured or identified by the occupiers, possibly because they're Kaladin's parents.

**Q: Can they trust Leshwi and Venli?**  
A: Neither Lirin nor Rlain is certain if they can trust Leshwi and Venli. While Leshwi protected Kaladin's family (from Chapter 53) and Venli has been helping, there's still uncertainty about their true motivations and whether they can be relied upon.

**Q: What is the note about?**  
A: As Rlain looks at a map of the Shattered Plains, he sees a note and asks Hesina to read it. It says that a group of people were found way out on the eastern side of the Plains. This may be significant—it could be refugees, Bridge Four members, or something else important.

**Q: What is Hesina's attitude?**  
A: Hesina's attitude towards Kaladin and the occupation is different from Lirin. While Lirin is more pacifist and despairing, Hesina may be more hopeful or supportive of resistance. They worry over Kaladin together, but their approaches may differ.

**Q: Why is this chapter called "Open Wound"?**  
A: The title "Open Wound" likely refers to Rlain's isolation and pain at being rejected by all sides, as well as the worry about Kaladin. It's an emotional wound that's still open, not healed. The epigraph about Jezrien also relates to loss and death, which is another kind of open wound.

**Q: What does the epigraph mean?**  
A: The epigraph is from Kelek (the High Judge from Chapter 78) talking about Jezrien being gone. He felt Jezrien being "ripped away" even from Lasting Integrity, and he believes it was death. This relates to the Oathpact and the Heralds' Connection to each other.
