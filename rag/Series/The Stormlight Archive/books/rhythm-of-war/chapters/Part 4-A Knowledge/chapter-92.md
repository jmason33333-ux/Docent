## Quick Summary
Venli is fulfilling her duty as Voice to Raboniel by interpreting between her and the Regals coming to give reports. She relays instructions to the last one to continue searching for Kaladin, then follows Raboniel to her habitual workstation. The two of them discuss the listeners rejecting Odium's rule, and then Venli admits to Raboniel that she is ashamed of being the one directly responsible for her people's downfall. Venli slips up and uses an old rhythm when she speaks of how she used to be so ambitious, then she asks Raboniel why they must fight and whether they will ever be able to have peace themselves.

Raboniel commends Venli's service despite her true loyalty lying with Leshwi and gives her a reward: a writ of authority and a map. On the map, Venli finds a report of a group of Parshendi living on the Shattered Plains. Realizing that this could be the group of listeners who had fled prior to the advent of the Everstorm, Venli takes the papers and runs from the room.

## Rowan's "If Asked" Notes

**Q: Why is Venli ashamed?**  
A: Venli admits to Raboniel that she is ashamed of being the one directly responsible for her people's downfall. This refers to her role in bringing voidspren and forms of power to the listeners, which led to the war and their current situation. This is part of her growing awareness of the consequences of her actions.

**Q: What is the old rhythm?**  
A: Venli slips up and uses an old rhythm when she speaks of how she used to be so ambitious. This shows her connection to her past and listener culture, even though she's been working with voidspren and Fused. It's a moment that reveals her true identity beneath her current role.

**Q: What is Raboniel's gift?**  
A: Raboniel gives Venli a reward: a writ of authority and a map. The writ gives Venli some authority, and the map shows a report of a group of Parshendi (listeners) living on the Shattered Plains. This is significant because these may be the listeners who fled before the Everstorm.

**Q: Who are the listeners on the Shattered Plains?**  
A: Venli finds a report of a group of Parshendi living on the Shattered Plains. She realizes this could be the group of listeners who had fled prior to the advent of the Everstorm. These would be listeners who escaped before the war and may still be free from Odium's influence.

**Q: Why does Venli run from the room?**  
A: Venli takes the papers and runs from the room. This is because she's excited and hopeful about finding her people—the listeners who may have escaped before the war. This is a moment of hope and possibility for her.

**Q: What does Raboniel mean about Venli's loyalty?**  
A: Raboniel commends Venli's service despite her true loyalty lying with Leshwi. This shows that Raboniel knows Venli is working with Leshwi (who protects Kaladin's family), but she still appreciates Venli's work and gives her the gift anyway.

**Q: Why does Venli ask about peace?**  
A: Venli asks Raboniel why they must fight and whether they will ever be able to have peace themselves. This shows Venli's desire for peace and her questioning of the ongoing war. It's part of her growth and her realization that there might be another way.

**Q: What does the epigraph mean?**  
A: The epigraph explains that the bond (between spren and Radiants) is what keeps spren alive. If the bond is severed, spren will decompose into ordinary souls. If captured with knives (like the one Mraize gave Shallan), they won't be left with a spren in a jar, but a being that fades away into the Beyond. This relates to capturing Heralds or spren.

**Q: Why is this chapter called "A Gift"?**  
A: The title "A Gift" refers to Raboniel's gift to Venli—the writ of authority and the map. However, it's also a gift in a deeper sense—it gives Venli hope of finding her people and potentially a way forward. It's a gift that could change everything for her.
