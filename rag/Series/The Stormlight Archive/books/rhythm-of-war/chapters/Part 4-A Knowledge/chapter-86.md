## Quick Summary
**A year and a half ago**

Venli speaks with Denshil, one of the listener farmers. They are in the thick of the war with the Alethi, and Venli is increasingly certain that she made the right choice. Venli has Denshil cutting gemstones, presumably so that they can hold more Stormlight, but really so that Venli can have gems to capture stormspren. The farmer is hesitant, but she convinces him that this is necessary for them to survive. As she leaves, Ulim comes to her and tells her that the storm is nearly to Narak. They talk about their plans to bring more stormspren over, but she is interrupted by Eshonai, who takes her to their mother who had been found injured, lost, and confused out on the plateaus. They decide to get her a permanent servant to watch over her and then discuss their past, both as children and during the time leading up to the war. Venli tries to talk to Eshonai about possibly bringing back the gods, but Eshonai shuts her down. They sing together a little before Eshonai drifts off to sleep just as Venli starts to open up to her. That night, Venli goes into the highstorm to hunt stormspren.

## Rowan's "If Asked" Notes

**Q: Why does Venli have Denshil cut gemstones?**  
A: Venli tells Denshil that cutting gemstones will help them hold more Stormlight, but really she needs the gems to capture stormspren. This is part of her manipulation—she's using the farmer for her own purposes while making him think it's for survival.

**Q: What are stormspren?**  
A: Stormspren are spren that appear in highstorms. Venli is hunting them, presumably to capture them in gemstones so they can be used to give listeners forms of power. This is part of Ulim's plan to bring more voidspren and stormspren to help the listeners.

**Q: What happened to Jaxlim?**  
A: Jaxlim had been found injured, lost, and confused out on the plateaus. Her memory loss condition has gotten worse, and she's now unable to take care of herself. Venli and Eshonai decide to get her a permanent servant (Mikaim) to watch over her.

**Q: What does Venli want to talk about with Eshonai?**  
A: Venli tries to talk to Eshonai about possibly bringing back the gods (the Fused/Odium), but Eshonai shuts her down. Venli wants to open up to her sister about what she's been doing, but Eshonai falls asleep just as Venli starts to open up, missing this opportunity for honesty.

**Q: Why does Venli hunt stormspren?**  
A: That night, Venli goes into the highstorm to hunt stormspren. This is part of her plan with Ulim to bring more spren over from Shadesmar and give listeners forms of power. She's continuing to work toward their goal despite Eshonai's rejection of bringing back the gods.

**Q: Why is Venli certain she made the right choice?**  
A: Venli is increasingly certain that she made the right choice. This likely refers to her decision to work with Ulim and pursue forms of power. However, her certainty may be misplaced, as she's been manipulating people and hiding the truth from her sister.

**Q: What is the "Song of Mornings"?**  
A: The title "The Song of Mornings" likely refers to the songs that Venli and Eshonai sing together, or to listener songs in general. It may also refer to a new beginning or a hopeful moment, though the chapter ends with Venli continuing her secretive work.

**Q: Why does Eshonai shut down talk of bringing back the gods?**  
A: Eshonai shuts Venli down when she tries to talk about bringing back the gods. This shows Eshonai's rejection of Odium and the Fused—she doesn't want to return to that way of life, even if it might give them more power. This is a fundamental disagreement between the sisters.
