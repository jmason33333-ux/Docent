## Quick Summary
**Adolin:** Adolin arrives for the second day of his trial and takes the stand. As he begins his carefully laid out defense, he is continually interrupted by the honorspren audience, who are doing everything they can to disrupt him with their own accusations. When they bring up Dalinar almost killing the Stormfather and Kaladin nearly killing Syl, the trial devolves into a full fledged argument. Adolin finally gives up, inviting the honorspren to continue their attack, as it is clear that they have never had honorable intentions in the trial. The audience is initially cowed at his frankness, but their anger reignites when they learn that Adolin knew about the recently killed deadeye Cryptic he had seen in Nameless.

**Shallan:** Shallan is with Adolin after the trial, and Adolin is certain that he will be locked away and put on display. Shallan says that she'll break him out with the Stormlight she stole, but Adolin says that leaving him would be the honorable thing to do and may even convince some of the honorspren to bond more Radiants. Shallan reflects on whether to impersonate Kelek to save Adolin, and just as she decides to do it, Veil changes her mind and sides with Radiant against the plan. Formless surfaces and takes over, telling Adolin that he may be surprised by what the High Judge does.

## Rowan's "If Asked" Notes

**Q: Why do the honorspren interrupt Adolin?**  
A: As Adolin begins his carefully laid out defense, he is continually interrupted by the honorspren audience, who are doing everything they can to disrupt him with their own accusations. This shows that the trial is not fair and that the honorspren have already made up their minds.

**Q: What accusations do they make?**  
A: The honorspren bring up Dalinar almost killing the Stormfather and Kaladin nearly killing Syl. These are examples of humans harming spren, which they use as evidence against Adolin and humans in general. The trial devolves into a full fledged argument.

**Q: Why does Adolin give up?**  
A: Adolin finally gives up, inviting the honorspren to continue their attack, as it is clear that they have never had honorable intentions in the trial. He realizes that no matter what he says, they won't listen, so he stops trying to defend himself.

**Q: What about the deadeye Cryptic?**  
A: The honorspren's anger reignites when they learn that Adolin knew about the recently killed deadeye Cryptic (Testament) he had seen in Nameless. This is another example of humans being associated with spren death, which makes them even angrier.

**Q: What does Adolin say about being left?**  
A: Adolin says that leaving him would be the honorable thing to do and may even convince some of the honorspren to bond more Radiants. He's willing to sacrifice himself if it means that other spren might be convinced to bond humans, showing his selflessness.

**Q: Who is Formless?**  
A: Formless is a persona of Shallan's that surfaces and takes over. Just as Shallan decides to impersonate Kelek to save Adolin, Veil changes her mind and sides with Radiant against the plan. Then Formless takes over, suggesting there's conflict between Shallan's personas.

**Q: What does Formless mean?**  
A: Formless tells Adolin that he may be surprised by what the High Judge does. This suggests that Kelek (the High Judge) may not rule against Adolin as expected, or that there's something unexpected about his decision. Formless seems to know something the others don't.

**Q: Why is this chapter called "One Chance"?**  
A: The title "One Chance" likely refers to Adolin having one chance to make his defense, or to the idea that this is their one chance to convince the honorspren. It may also refer to Shallan having one chance to decide whether to impersonate Kelek, though Formless takes over before she can act.

**Q: What does the epigraph mean?**  
A: The epigraph is from Kelek, saying he knows "they" are coming for him. They got Jezrien, and they'll claim him too, even in the honorspren stronghold. This suggests that someone (possibly the Ghostbloods or another group) is hunting Heralds, and Kelek knows he's a target.
