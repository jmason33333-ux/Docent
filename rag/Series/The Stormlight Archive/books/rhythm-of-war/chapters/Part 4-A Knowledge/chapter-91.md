## Quick Summary
Teft thinks about when he first woke up after being healed by Lift; he had felt like he usually does after binging on firemoss, but when he saw Dabbid and Rlain he had determined to get up and start over again to try to live clean, for their sakes. It was only when they told him what happened that he understood he hadn't given in to his craving. After a couple of days of healing, he is sitting eating soup with Kaladin and Dabbid when Kaladin mentions losing Syl in the fighting. Phendorana appears to the others and reassures Kaladin that their bond is not broken so long as he can still Surgebind.

As Teft thinks about the grim tidings he had awoken to, Phendorana reminds him by a glance not to be down on himself. Rlain and Lift enter next, the latter of whom heals Teft; he has to be healed every ten hours or he falls unconscious again. Rlain reports on Navani's forced seclusion, and they discuss what needs to be done. Kaladin asks whether or not it would be better to quit fighting, and in answer Teft gives them a detailed account of what happened when he was eight years old and he reported his parents, who were part of the Envisagers, to the citylord. He encourages them just to do what they think is right with the information they have and be content with the fact that they can't predict the future.

After Teft spurs them to attempt to save the Radiants, they all pitch in and discuss how they might accomplish it. Kaladin suggests that they take a couple of days to consider their options, and they hope to be able to contact Navani and ask for her help. Then they start discussing less consequential things, and Teft finds himself smiling because of all of the good things there still are to enjoy. He admits to Phendorana that she had been right to tell Teft he was worth saving.

## Rowan's "If Asked" Notes

**Q: What happened when Teft woke up?**  
A: Teft thinks about when he first woke up after being healed by Lift. He had felt like he usually does after binging on firemoss (his addiction), but when he saw Dabbid and Rlain, he determined to get up and start over again to try to live clean, for their sakes. It was only when they told him what happened that he understood he hadn't given in to his craving—he had been unconscious from the suppression field.

**Q: What is Teft's story about his parents?**  
A: Teft gives a detailed account of what happened when he was eight years old and he reported his parents, who were part of the Envisagers (a cult), to the citylord. This is a significant moment in Teft's past that shaped him, and he uses it to encourage the others to do what they think is right with the information they have.

**Q: Why does Teft need to be healed every ten hours?**  
A: Lift heals Teft, but he has to be healed every ten hours or he falls unconscious again. This is because the suppression field is still affecting him, and Lift's healing (using Lifelight) can only temporarily counteract it. This shows the ongoing danger they're in.

**Q: What does Phendorana tell Kaladin?**  
A: Phendorana appears and reassures Kaladin that his bond with Syl is not broken so long as he can still Surgebind. This is important because Kaladin mentioned losing Syl in the fighting, and he's worried about their connection. Phendorana's reassurance gives him hope.

**Q: What is their plan?**  
A: After Teft spurs them to attempt to save the Radiants, they all pitch in and discuss how they might accomplish it. Kaladin suggests they take a couple of days to consider their options, and they hope to be able to contact Navani and ask for her help. This sets up their next actions.

**Q: Why does Teft find himself smiling?**  
A: They start discussing less consequential things, and Teft finds himself smiling because of all of the good things there still are to enjoy. This is a moment of hope and joy despite their difficult situation, showing that there's still good in the world.

**Q: What does Teft admit to Phendorana?**  
A: Teft admits to Phendorana that she had been right to tell Teft he was worth saving. This is a significant moment of self-acceptance and recognition of his own value, which is important for his recovery and his ability to help others.

**Q: What are the Envisagers?**  
A: The Envisagers were a cult that Teft's parents belonged to. They believed in various things related to the Knights Radiant and the Desolations. When Teft was eight, he reported them to the citylord, which had significant consequences for his family and shaped his life.

**Q: Why is this chapter called "Worth Saving"?**  
A: The title "Worth Saving" refers to Teft's realization that he is worth saving, which he admits to Phendorana. It's about recognizing one's own value and the value of others, and about the importance of not giving up on people, even when they struggle with addiction or other problems.
