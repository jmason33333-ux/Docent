## Quick Summary
**Fourteen months ago**

During the Battle of Narak Venli realizes the danger the listeners are in because of her actions in giving them access to the stormform. She tries to get Faridai to retreat with her as the humans approach in their direction but then realizes that the listeners, who are chanting a song of summoning, are oblivious, consumed by the new form. As she runs away through the storm, Venli is intercepted by a human soldier, who lets her go after she asks him for mercy. Later she notices Eshonai fighting with a human Shardbearer and falling into a chasm. Venli realizes she might be more affected by the form than she initially thought, as she does not feel any concern for her sister.

Panicked, Venli finds a hiding place between the buildings of Narak and later finds her way into one of them. As she hides from the storm and the battle, she admits to herself that she is not deserving of any of the recognition she got for "discovering" new forms, as all the knowledge was given to her by Ulim, and that she is - and has always been - a coward.

## Rowan's "If Asked" Notes

**Q: What is the Battle of Narak?**  
A: The Battle of Narak is a major battle that took place fourteen months ago (in the timeline of the flashback). It's when the humans attacked the listener city of Narak, and it's where Eshonai dies. Venli realizes the danger the listeners are in because of her actions in giving them access to stormform.

**Q: Why are the listeners oblivious?**  
A: Venli realizes that the listeners, who are chanting a song of summoning, are oblivious and consumed by the new form (stormform). The form has taken over their minds, making them unable to see the danger or respond to Venli's attempts to get them to retreat.

**Q: What happens to Eshonai?**  
A: Venli notices Eshonai fighting with a human Shardbearer (likely Adolin) and falling into a chasm. This is the moment of Eshonai's death, though Venli doesn't fully process it at the time because the form is affecting her emotions.

**Q: Why doesn't Venli feel concern for Eshonai?**  
A: Venli realizes she might be more affected by the form than she initially thought, as she does not feel any concern for her sister when Eshonai falls. This shows how the forms of power (especially stormform) can affect emotions and connections, making Venli less human and more disconnected.

**Q: Why does the human soldier let Venli go?**  
A: As Venli runs away through the storm, she is intercepted by a human soldier, who lets her go after she asks him for mercy. This shows that not all humans are monsters, and that mercy and compassion can exist even in war. This moment may be significant for Venli's later understanding.

**Q: What does Venli admit to herself?**  
A: As she hides from the storm and the battle, Venli admits to herself that she is not deserving of any of the recognition she got for "discovering" new forms, as all the knowledge was given to her by Ulim. She also admits that she is - and has always been - a coward.

**Q: Why is this chapter called "What She Truly Was"?**  
A: The title "What She Truly Was" refers to Venli's self-realization that she is a coward and that she doesn't deserve the recognition she received. It's about her facing the truth about herself—what she truly is, rather than what she pretended to be or what others thought she was.

**Q: What is the significance of this flashback?**  
A: This flashback shows Venli at her lowest point—during the battle that killed her sister, hiding in fear, admitting she's a coward. It contrasts with her growth in the present timeline, where she's trying to help and make amends. It shows how far she's come and how much she's changed.

**Q: What is stormform?**  
A: Stormform is one of the forms of power that Venli gave the listeners access to. It's a dangerous form that consumes the listener, making them oblivious and disconnected from their emotions. It's what leads to the listeners' downfall in the battle.
