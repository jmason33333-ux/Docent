## Quick Summary
Rlain finds Venli crying, and she tells him that some of the other listeners might be alive, explaining that there was a group led by Thude who refused to take stormform and instead fled Narak the night the Everstorm was summoned. When she confesses that she would have had them killed had Eshonai not let them escape, Timbre prompts her to tell Rlain the whole story. She does so, and Rlain draws away from her in anger.

Venli states her intention to escape with her writ from Raboniel and find her people, but Rlain tries to tell her that they need to save the Radiants first. Venli refuses to help, so Rlain storms off after calling her a traitor. Instead, Venli finds Dul and Mazish and informs them that it is time for the group to gather their stores and leave. She plans to use the stir caused by the attempted rescue of the Radiants to cover their escape.

## Rowan's "If Asked" Notes

**Q: Who is Thude's group?**  
A: Venli tells Rlain that there was a group led by Thude who refused to take stormform and instead fled Narak the night the Everstorm was summoned. These are listeners who escaped before the war and may still be free from Odium's influence. This is the group Venli learned about in Chapter 92.

**Q: What does Venli confess?**  
A: When Venli confesses that she would have had them killed had Eshonai not let them escape, Timbre prompts her to tell Rlain the whole story. She does so, and Rlain draws away from her in anger. This shows Venli's past actions and her role in the listeners' downfall.

**Q: Why is Rlain angry?**  
A: Rlain is angry because Venli confesses that she would have had Thude's group killed, and because she tells him the whole story of her role in the listeners' downfall. He draws away from her in anger and later calls her a traitor when she refuses to help save the Radiants.

**Q: Why does Venli refuse to help save the Radiants?**  
A: Venli states her intention to escape with her writ from Raboniel and find her people (Thude's group). Rlain tries to tell her that they need to save the Radiants first, but Venli refuses to help. She prioritizes finding her people over helping save the Radiants.

**Q: What is Venli's escape plan?**  
A: Venli finds Dul and Mazish and informs them that it is time for the group to gather their stores and leave. She plans to use the stir caused by the attempted rescue of the Radiants to cover their escape. This means she'll escape while everyone is distracted by the rescue attempt.

**Q: What does the epigraph mean?**  
A: The epigraph is about releasing the captive Unmade (Ba-Ado-Mishram). It says that if left as she is, she will remain imprisoned for eternity, unlike the speaker who will fade. This relates to the importance of freeing Ba-Ado-Mishram, which was mentioned in earlier chapters.

**Q: Why is this chapter called "A Thousand Lies"?**  
A: The title "A Thousand Lies" refers to Venli's confession and the many lies she's told—to herself, to others, about her role in the listeners' downfall, about her recognition for discovering forms, etc. It's about her finally telling the truth, even though it costs her Rlain's friendship.

**Q: What is the significance of Eshonai letting them escape?**  
A: Eshonai let Thude's group escape, which saved them from Venli's plan to have them killed. This shows Eshonai's wisdom and compassion, and it's part of why Venli feels guilty—she would have killed innocent listeners if not for Eshonai.
