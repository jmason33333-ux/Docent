## Quick Summary
**Seven years ago**

**Eshonai:** Eshonai and the listeners have returned to the Shattered Plains after accomplishing the assassination of Gavilar. She, Thude, and Rlain have assumed warform and jump across the chasms to the west where they can see the Alethi war camps. She and Thude talk about why they had to order Gavilar's death and what their strategy is now that they are trapped on the Plains.

**Venli:** Venli takes out five gemstones with Voidspren trapped inside and shows them to a few of her scholars. She explains to them where the Voidspren came from and how they are going to bring more across from Shadesmar and persuade the other listeners to adopt these forms. She convinces them that this time they won't be ruled by the spren; the listeners will rule them instead.

## Rowan's "If Asked" Notes

**Q: Why did they assassinate Gavilar?**  
A: Eshonai and Thude talk about why they had to order Gavilar's death. From earlier chapters, they learned that Gavilar was planning to betray the listeners, so they assassinated him to prevent this. Now they're dealing with the consequences of that action.

**Q: Why are they trapped on the Plains?**  
A: Eshonai and the listeners have returned to the Shattered Plains after the assassination and are now trapped there. The Alethi war camps are visible to the west, and they need to figure out their strategy for dealing with the war that's coming.

**Q: Where did the Voidspren come from?**  
A: Venli shows five gemstones with Voidspren trapped inside to her scholars. She explains where they came from and how they're going to bring more across from Shadesmar. This is part of Ulim's plan to get more listeners to take forms of power.

**Q: What does Venli mean about ruling the spren?**  
A: Venli convinces her scholars that this time they won't be ruled by the spren; the listeners will rule them instead. This is likely a promise to make the forms of power more appealing, suggesting that listeners will have control rather than being controlled by the voidspren.

**Q: Who are Eshonai, Thude, and Rlain?**  
A: Eshonai, Thude, and Rlain are listeners who have assumed warform. They jump across the chasms to see the Alethi war camps and discuss strategy. Rlain is the same Rlain who later joins Bridge Four, showing his connection to both listener and human communities.

**Q: What is their strategy?**  
A: Eshonai and Thude discuss their strategy now that they are trapped on the Plains. They need to figure out how to deal with the Alethi forces and survive the war that's coming. The details aren't fully revealed, but they're planning for a difficult situation.

**Q: How will they bring more Voidspren from Shadesmar?**  
A: Venli explains to her scholars how they are going to bring more Voidspren across from Shadesmar and persuade other listeners to adopt these forms. The exact method isn't fully explained, but it's part of Ulim's larger plan to get listeners to take forms of power.

**Q: Why is this chapter called "Trapped"?**  
A: The title "Trapped" refers to the listeners being trapped on the Shattered Plains after assassinating Gavilar. They're surrounded by Alethi forces and need to figure out how to survive. It also refers to the Voidspren being trapped in gemstones, and potentially to listeners being trapped by their situation.
