## Quick Summary
Navani continues to try to get the Sibling to speak to her, repeatedly apologizing for her failure to realize that they were being spied on. Raboniel enters, and Navani is amazed to hear her reminisce about the shadowdays. She is so tired of the war and is dismissive of Navani's suggestion of peace because their previous attempts had failed. Navani realizes that Raboniel's desire to combine the Lights is related to her desire to end the war. They discuss her research progress, including Navani's realization that Stormlight responds to sound. She learns that Raboniel can hear the tone of Light, the same tone that she uses to get the Light to transfer spheres. Raboniel explains that there is a rhythm as well as a tone, and that Towerlight has two rhythms that work in harmony. Navani wonders if the rhythms of Stormlight and Voidlight could harmonize, but Raboniel says that Stormlight is too orderly and Voidlight too chaotic. Navani convinces her to try, Raboniel singing the rhythm and tone of Voidlight, and with some effort, Navani doing the same for Stormlight. Their two rhythms eventually come into harmony, and the two kinds of Light successfully mix and infuse the sphere. Raboniel recognizes the rhythm of the Light as the Rhythm of War and is shocked that the Lights did not destroy each other. Navani asks about her motivations, and Raboniel suggests that the discovery might allow them to kill a god--like how Honor was killed. Navani realizes that Gavilar had been looking for a way to kill a god. Even more, she realizes that he had discovered the opposite to Voidlight, and that that is what had been in the sphere that killed her scholars. Raboniel gets ready to leave, thanking Navani for her assistance and telling her that though Navani has earned her gratitude, she is not going to leave Urithiru.

## Rowan's "If Asked" Notes

**Q: How do they mix Stormlight and Voidlight?**  
A: Navani and Raboniel discover that they can mix Stormlight and Voidlight by harmonizing their rhythms and tones. Raboniel sings the rhythm and tone of Voidlight, and Navani does the same for Stormlight. When their rhythms come into harmony, the two kinds of Light successfully mix and infuse the sphere. This is a major breakthrough.

**Q: What is the Rhythm of War?**  
A: Raboniel recognizes the rhythm created when Stormlight and Voidlight harmonize as the Rhythm of War. This is significant because it's a new rhythm that emerges from combining the two Lights, and it may have important implications for the war and for understanding Light itself.

**Q: Why is Raboniel shocked?**  
A: Raboniel is shocked that the Lights did not destroy each other. She believed that Stormlight and Voidlight were opposites that couldn't be mixed, but by harmonizing their rhythms, they successfully combine. This contradicts her previous understanding.

**Q: What does Raboniel mean about killing a god?**  
A: Raboniel suggests that the discovery might allow them to kill a god, like how Honor was killed. This suggests that the ability to mix Lights (or create anti-Light) could be used to destroy a Shard. This may be related to Gavilar's goal and to what was in the sphere that killed Navani's scholars.

**Q: What was in Gavilar's sphere?**  
A: Navani realizes that Gavilar had discovered the opposite to Voidlight (anti-Voidlight), and that is what had been in the sphere that killed her scholars. When anti-Voidlight was combined with regular Voidlight (or possibly Stormlight), it caused the explosion that killed them.

**Q: What were the shadowdays?**  
A: Raboniel reminisces about the shadowdays, which seems to be a time in the past. Navani is amazed to hear her talk about it, suggesting it's a significant period. It may be related to a time before the current war or to a different era in Rosharan history.

**Q: Why won't Raboniel leave Urithiru?**  
A: Raboniel tells Navani that though she has earned her gratitude, she is not going to leave Urithiru. This suggests that despite their cooperation and Raboniel's appreciation, she still has her own goals and won't abandon the occupation, even if she's grateful to Navani.

**Q: How does Light respond to sound?**  
A: Navani realizes that Stormlight responds to sound. Raboniel can hear the tone of Light and uses it to transfer Light between spheres. This shows that Light has both a tone and a rhythm, and that understanding these musical properties is key to working with Light.

**Q: Why is this chapter called "Harmony"?**  
A: The title "Harmony" refers to the harmony created when Stormlight and Voidlight's rhythms align. It also refers to the harmony between Navani and Raboniel working together, despite being enemies. The chapter is about finding harmony in opposition.
