## Quick Summary
**Shallan:** Shallan enters her room, pushing away thoughts of the deadeye Cryptic and her feelings about Pattern. She says that she is going to contact Mraize, and Pattern trails her, saying that she feels wrong. While Shallan gets out the communication cube, Pattern tells her that he used the cube to talk to Wit but that Wit had been overheard by Ghostblood spies. Shallan starts to relax as she realizes that none of her friends are spies, which allows Veil and Radiant out, and Veil takes control. She realizes that they still don't know who killed Ialai, and she calls Mraize on the cube. Veil tells him that Restares is not there and Mraize finds that troubling. He warns Shallan that Restares is one of the most dangerous beings on the planet and that she should be careful. He asks after Shallan's mental health, and Shallan says that it could be better, but they are interrupted by shouting.

**Adolin:** Adolin is being tutored by an inkspren ambassador to the honorspren. She tells him that his trial is set for three days from then, and that they should motion for a trial by witnesses, even though this opens him up to self-incrimination. Adolin is certain that this will be necessary to appeal to their honor. Blended tells him that because the honorspren define what honor means to them, they believe that what they do must be honorable. Adolin realizes that this is how they are able to avoid being convinced using his arguments against their honor. They leave to find the High Judge, whom Blended says is arriving. Adolin sees him and learns that he is human.

**Shallan:** Veil is stunned by the appearance of the High Judge. She asks one of the honorspren why she had been told that the High Judge was a spren, and he tells her that it is Kelek, and because he is a Herald and immortal, he's close enough to being a spren that his command to them to keep his identity a secret was good enough for them. Veil realizes that Mraize may have sent them to kill a Herald.

## Rowan's "If Asked" Notes

**Q: Who is the High Judge?**  
A: The High Judge is Kelek (Kalak), one of the ten Heralds. Adolin learns that he is human, and Veil is stunned by this revelation. The honorspren had kept his identity secret because he commanded them to, and because he's a Herald and immortal, they considered him "close enough to being a spren" that his command was valid.

**Q: Why does Mraize want to kill a Herald?**  
A: Veil realizes that Mraize may have sent them to kill a Herald. The Ghostbloods' mission to find Restares may actually be a cover for assassinating Kelek. This is a major revelation that suggests the Ghostbloods have their own agenda that's separate from (or in addition to) finding Restares.

**Q: Why is Restares not being there troubling?**  
A: Veil tells Mraize that Restares is not there, and Mraize finds this troubling. This suggests that Restares was supposed to be in Lasting Integrity, or that his absence complicates the Ghostbloods' plans. Mraize warns that Restares is one of the most dangerous beings on the planet.

**Q: What is trial by witnesses?**  
A: Adolin is being tutored about his trial, and the inkspren ambassador tells him they should motion for a trial by witnesses, even though this opens him up to self-incrimination. Adolin is certain this will be necessary to appeal to the honorspren's honor, showing his understanding of how to work with them.

**Q: Why can't Adolin convince honorspren using arguments?**  
A: Blended tells Adolin that because the honorspren define what honor means to them, they believe that what they do must be honorable. Adolin realizes that this is how they are able to avoid being convinced using arguments against their honor—they simply redefine honor to match their actions.

**Q: What does Pattern tell Shallan about Wit?**  
A: Pattern tells Shallan that he used the communication cube to talk to Wit, but that Wit had been overheard by Ghostblood spies. This is concerning because it means the Ghostbloods know about their communications and may be spying on them.

**Q: Why does Shallan relax?**  
A: Shallan starts to relax as she realizes that none of her friends are spies. This allows Veil and Radiant to come out, and Veil takes control. This shows that Shallan's paranoia about her friends being spies was unfounded, which is a relief.

**Q: What is Shallan's mental health?**  
A: Mraize asks after Shallan's mental health, and Shallan says that it could be better. This acknowledges her ongoing struggles with her personas, her past, and the revelations about Pattern. Her mental health is a concern throughout her storyline.

**Q: Why is this chapter called "The High Judge"?**  
A: The title "The High Judge" refers to Kelek, who is the High Judge presiding over Adolin's trial. The revelation that he is a Herald (and human) is a major plot point, and his role as judge will be crucial for the trial's outcome.
