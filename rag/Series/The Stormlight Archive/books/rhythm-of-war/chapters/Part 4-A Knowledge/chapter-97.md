## Quick Summary
Navani's work has consumed her to the point where sleeping and eating is a distraction. She obsesses over the tones, the rhythms, the numbers, the patterns. She is seeking an exact opposite of the wave that represents Odium's rhythm which would nullify it through destructive interference. Finally, the tone is exactly right, and she watches as the sound pushes Voidlight out of its sphere.

She attempts to hide her discovery from those watching her as she seeks a Light that would express the sound. The day after her discovery, Raboniel comes to check on her, asking about the sound that her guards say is so terrible. Initially, Navani manages to deflect Raboniel's questioning, but as Raboniel goes to leave, she sees the new plate that creates the new tone and she tests it. She watches as the sound forces Voidlight out of a sphere, and she says she felt the Voidlight in her gemheart respond. Raboniel reviews Navani's notes while Navani explains what she is doing: Navani believes that Voidlight echoes Odium's tone, and that by transferring Voidlight from one sphere to another in a vacuum, it will not be able to hear a tone to echo--until it hears Navani playing the anti-Voidlight tone against the new sphere. The experiment works, and the new sphere has a warping to it; Raboniel screams when she touches it, though Navani cannot tell a difference. Navani leaves Raboniel in the room, and soon, as Navani hoped, she hears an explosion as Raboniel tries to mix Voidlight with anti-Voidlight. Raboniel survives and has Navani make another gemstone immediately. Raboniel affixes it to one of the daggers in such a way that it would force the anti-Voidlight outward, not inward, and then uses it to kill her daughter. Navani realizes that this will end the rebirth cycle and that Raboniel never wanted to kill Odium--she wanted to free her daughter. Raboniel takes Navani's notebook, saying that she will make a copy for Navani to keep, but that she needs to do the same process with Stormlight. Navani is horrified, but Raboniel says that she has told her all along that her goal was to end the war, and that means killing spren so they can no longer bond more Radiants.

## Rowan's "If Asked" Notes

**Q: What is anti-Voidlight?**  
A: Navani creates anti-Voidlight by finding the exact opposite of the wave that represents Odium's rhythm, which nullifies it through destructive interference. When the tone is exactly right, the sound pushes Voidlight out of its sphere. This is the opposite vibration that cancels out Voidlight.

**Q: How does it work?**  
A: Navani believes that Voidlight echoes Odium's tone. By transferring Voidlight from one sphere to another in a vacuum, it cannot hear a tone to echo—until it hears Navani playing the anti-Voidlight tone against the new sphere. The experiment works, creating anti-Voidlight in the new sphere.

**Q: What happens when Raboniel touches it?**  
A: The new sphere has a warping to it, and Raboniel screams when she touches it, though Navani cannot tell a difference. This suggests that anti-Voidlight is extremely painful or harmful to those who use Voidlight (like Raboniel, who is a Fused).

**Q: Why does Raboniel kill her daughter?**  
A: Raboniel affixes the anti-Voidlight gemstone to a dagger in such a way that it forces the anti-Voidlight outward, not inward, and then uses it to kill her daughter Essu. Navani realizes that this will end the rebirth cycle—Essu will not be reborn as a Fused. Raboniel never wanted to kill Odium; she wanted to free her daughter from the endless cycle of death and rebirth.

**Q: What is the rebirth cycle?**  
A: The rebirth cycle is how Fused work—when they die, they are reborn in new bodies, continuing the cycle of death and rebirth. Anti-Voidlight ends this cycle, allowing Fused to truly die and be free.

**Q: Why is Navani horrified?**  
A: Raboniel takes Navani's notebook and says she needs to do the same process with Stormlight. Navani is horrified because Raboniel says that her goal was to end the war, and that means killing spren so they can no longer bond more Radiants. This would end the Knights Radiant by killing all spren.

**Q: What does the epigraph mean?**  
A: The epigraph is about releasing Ba-Ado-Mishram (the captive Unmade). It says that in confining her, they caused a greater wound to Roshar than any ever realized. This relates to the Recreance and the damage done to spren and the world.

**Q: Why is this chapter called "Freedom"?**  
A: The title "Freedom" refers to Raboniel freeing her daughter from the rebirth cycle, allowing her to truly die and be free. It's also about the freedom that comes from ending the endless cycle of death and rebirth, even if it means death itself.

**Q: What is destructive interference?**  
A: Destructive interference is a physics principle where two waves of opposite phase cancel each other out. Navani uses this principle to create anti-Voidlight—the opposite vibration that nullifies Voidlight through destructive interference.
