## Rowan's "If Asked" Notes

**Q: What is the stormspren?**  
A: Venli enters with a gemstone containing a captured stormspren. This is what she hunted in the highstorm the previous night (from Chapter 86). Stormspren are spren that appear in highstorms and can be used to give listeners forms of power.

**Q: What does Eshonai mean about preserving freedom?**  
A: Eshonai yells into the storm to the Rider (the Stormfather), saying that everything she has done has been to preserve their freedom. This refers to her actions in the war—she's been fighting to keep the listeners free from human control, even though it's led to conflict and death.

**Q: What is the chasmfiend?**  
A: Eshonai turns to leave and is greeted by the sight of a giant chasmfiend. Chasmfiends are large, dangerous creatures that live in the chasms of the Shattered Plains. Their appearance here may be significant or symbolic.

**Q: What is the burst of light?**  
A: A burst of light appears and follows Eshonai home. This is mysterious and may be related to the Stormfather, to a spren wanting to bond with her, or to some other significant event. The light following her suggests it's intentional and connected to her.

**Q: Who is the Rider?**  
A: The Rider is the Stormfather. Eshonai yells into the storm to him, expressing her frustration and explaining her motivations. This shows her connection to the storms and possibly to the Stormfather himself.

**Q: What are the ramifications of the stormspren?**  
A: Eshonai and Venli discuss the ramifications of Venli capturing a stormspren. This is significant because it means Venli has succeeded in her plan to bring spren that can give forms of power, which will allow more listeners to take forms like stormform.

**Q: Why is this chapter called "Falling Star"?**  
A: The title "Falling Star" likely refers to the burst of light that appears and follows Eshonai. It may be a spren falling from the sky (like a star) to bond with her, or it may be symbolic of hope or change. The light "falling" and following her suggests it's coming to her.

**Q: What does Eshonai consider?**  
A: Eshonai walks outside and considers all that has happened. She's reflecting on the war, the choices she's made, and the situation the listeners are in. This is a moment of contemplation before the mysterious light appears.
