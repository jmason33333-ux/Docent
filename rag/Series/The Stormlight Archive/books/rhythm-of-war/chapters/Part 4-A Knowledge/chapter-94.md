## Quick Summary
Adolin arrives at the forum for the last day of his trial to find out that Sekeir will be sitting as High Judge in Kelek's place. Sekeir begins by giving a speech condemning Adolin and all humans and then calls for the final witness: Mayalaran. Sekeir claims to be speaking for Maya as he accuses Adolin of enslaving her and humans of being murderous monsters belonging to Odium. As he continues with his accusations Maya grows more and more distressed and Adolin keeps asking him to let her go.

Finally, as the High Judge refuses judgement, claiming that Maya's condemnation is all that's needed to judge Adolin, Maya and Adolin share a moment of connection and Maya speaks. She reveals that the ancient spren were not betrayed by their Knights Radiant, but decided together with them to break their bonds.

Most of the gathered spren disperse, shocked by the news, but Blended approaches to speak with Adolin. She tells him that she suggested to the honorspren elders to use Maya as a witness because she wanted to verify his claim that he and Maya were friends and that the deadeye could speak. She is not convinced that the revelation means that the spren should now bond humans, worrying instead what could have convinced spren to choose death instead of letting the Radiants continue. Adolin muses that in the light of this revelation, the Radiant-spren bond will never be the same again.

## Rowan's "If Asked" Notes

**Q: Why is Sekeir the judge?**  
A: Adolin arrives to find that Sekeir will be sitting as High Judge in Kelek's place. This is because Kelek was sequestered in Chapter 93 after being accused of collusion with Shallan. Sekeir is an honorspren who has been hostile to Adolin throughout the trial.

**Q: What does Maya reveal?**  
A: Maya speaks and reveals that the ancient spren were not betrayed by their Knights Radiant, but decided together with them to break their bonds. This is a momentous revelation that changes everything spren believe about the Recreance—it wasn't a betrayal, but a mutual decision.

**Q: Why is this revelation so shocking?**  
A: Most of the gathered spren disperse, shocked by the news. This is because spren have believed for thousands of years that the Radiants betrayed them and abandoned their oaths, causing the spren to become deadeyes. Learning that it was a mutual decision changes their entire understanding of history.

**Q: What does Blended say?**  
A: Blended tells Adolin that she suggested using Maya as a witness because she wanted to verify his claim that he and Maya were friends and that the deadeye could speak. However, she's not convinced this means spren should now bond humans, worrying instead what could have convinced spren to choose death.

**Q: What does Adolin mean about the bond never being the same?**  
A: Adolin muses that in the light of this revelation, the Radiant-spren bond will never be the same again. This is because understanding that spren chose to break bonds changes how people understand the nature of the bond and the relationship between spren and humans.

**Q: Why is Maya distressed?**  
A: As Sekeir continues with his accusations (claiming Adolin enslaved her, that humans are monsters), Maya grows more and more distressed. Adolin keeps asking Sekeir to let her go, showing his concern for her. This distress may be what finally allows her to speak.

**Q: What is the Recreance?**  
A: The Recreance was when the Knights Radiant abandoned their oaths, causing their spren to become deadeyes. Spren have always believed this was a betrayal, but Maya reveals that it was actually a mutual decision—both spren and Radiants chose to break their bonds together.

**Q: What does the epigraph mean?**  
A: The epigraph is from Maya (or another deadeye), saying they have a fragment of memory of what they once were. They were there when Ba-Ado-Mishram was captured and know the truth about the Radiants, the Recreance, and the Nahel spren. This suggests that capturing Ba-Ado-Mishram was related to the Recreance.

**Q: Why is this chapter called "Sacrifice"?**  
A: The title "Sacrifice" refers to the sacrifice that spren made when they chose to break their bonds with Radiants, knowing it would turn them into deadeyes. It was a sacrifice they made together with the Radiants, not a betrayal. It's about the mutual sacrifice that led to the Recreance.
