## Quick Summary
**Adolin:** Adolin arrives at his trial, where the judge and the spren audience await. They discuss the terms of the trial, and Sekeir calls his witnesses. The first, Amuna, brings two of her deadeye charges and testifies to the travesty that killed them. The second witness is Blended. She not only says that humans should not be trusted, but that they might even consider bonding with the Fused as the rightful heirs of the land. However, she would rather no bonds happen in order to ensure that no spren will be killed. The third witness is Notum. He appears in uniform, and Adolin realizes that Notum has been told that if he will testify against Adolin, they will end his exile. Notum begins to speak, looking at the paper he brought, but then stops and says, "Honor is not dead so long as he lives in the hearts of men." The crowd erupts, and Notum continues to bellow this phrase as he is dragged from the room. Adolin goes to speak to Kelek and asks what happened. Kelek tells him that the stakes of this trial are much more than he thought because some spren are considering joining the highspren and the Fused, and Adolin could tip the scales in the opposite direction.

**Shallan:** After the trial, Shallan climbs a tree and falls to the ground, faking a severe injury. Pattern plays along, and they convince the honorspren to take her to where their Stormlight is kept. They do so, and she steals a sphere, replacing it with a dun one and pretending that she had drained it to heal herself. The honorspren are appalled at how she could consume so much Stormlight, and they shuffle her from the room.

## Rowan's "If Asked" Notes

**Q: What does Amuna testify about?**  
A: Amuna brings two of her deadeye charges and testifies to the travesty that killed them. This is evidence against humans, showing that Radiants have killed spren and turned them into deadeyes. This is part of the case against Adolin and humans in general.

**Q: What does Blended say?**  
A: Blended testifies that humans should not be trusted, and that spren might even consider bonding with the Fused as the rightful heirs of the land. However, she would rather no bonds happen at all to ensure that no spren will be killed. This shows the depth of distrust some spren have for humans.

**Q: Why does Notum appear in uniform?**  
A: Adolin realizes that Notum has been told that if he will testify against Adolin, they will end his exile. Notum is wearing his uniform, suggesting he's ready to return to honorspren society if he testifies as they want. However, he defies this.

**Q: What does Notum say?**  
A: Notum begins to speak, looking at the paper he brought, but then stops and says, "Honor is not dead so long as he lives in the hearts of men." This is a quote from the First Ideal of the Knights Radiant, and it's a defiant statement that shows Notum's true beliefs. The crowd erupts, and he's dragged away.

**Q: What are the stakes of the trial?**  
A: Kelek tells Adolin that the stakes are much more than he thought because some spren are considering joining the highspren and the Fused, and Adolin could tip the scales in the opposite direction. This means the trial isn't just about Adolin—it's about whether spren will bond with humans or with the Fused.

**Q: Why does Shallan steal Stormlight?**  
A: After the trial, Shallan fakes an injury and convinces the honorspren to take her to where their Stormlight is kept. She steals a sphere, replacing it with a dun one and pretending she drained it to heal herself. She likely needs this Stormlight for her plan to impersonate Kelek or for some other purpose.

**Q: Why are the honorspren appalled?**  
A: The honorspren are appalled at how much Stormlight Shallan could consume. They're shocked by the amount, which suggests that Shallan may have actually used a lot of Stormlight, or that they're surprised by her ability to use it. This may relate to her being a Lightweaver.

**Q: What does the epigraph mean?**  
A: The epigraph suggests that if the speaker (likely Kelek) remembered his life, he'd be more confident and decisive. However, he may have chosen to forget or may have lost memories over the millennia. This relates to the Heralds' long lives and the toll it takes on them.

**Q: Why is this chapter called "Trial by Witness"?**  
A: The title "Trial by Witness" refers to the trial format that Adolin requested, where witnesses testify. It's also about the witnesses themselves—Amuna, Blended, and Notum—and what they reveal about the situation and the stakes of the trial.
