## Quick Summary
Navani is fully engrossed in her research, something she has never been able to be before. She believes that what they find might allow them to kill Odium, and as she works, she makes fabrial weapons that can be left in the hall appearing to be boxed up half-finished projects. She and Raboniel make more Warlight for her experiments, and she also tries to find a way to rejoin split spren. Upon request, Raboniel delivers some raysium, which is used to draw Stormlight out of Radiants. After transferring one of the spren halves to the gemstone at the base of the raysium blade, they learn that putting one half of a spren in a larger gem and moving that gem will cause the other half to move a greater amount. Navani asks Raboniel for a way to measure the strength of Stormlight, and the Fused says that she will get her some sand.

## Rowan's "If Asked" Notes

**Q: Why is Navani so engrossed?**  
A: Navani is fully engrossed in her research, something she has never been able to be before. This may be because she's finally free from other responsibilities (being captured), or because the research is so important and fascinating that it completely absorbs her attention.

**Q: What are the fabrial weapons?**  
A: As Navani works, she makes fabrial weapons that can be left in the hall appearing to be boxed up half-finished projects. This is a clever way to hide weapons in plain sight, preparing for potential resistance or escape. This relates to her earlier idea from Chapter 69.

**Q: What is raysium?**  
A: Raysium is a metal that is used to draw Stormlight out of Radiants. Raboniel delivers some to Navani for her experiments. It's a dangerous substance that can be used to harm Radiants by draining their Stormlight.

**Q: What do they learn about split spren?**  
A: After transferring one of the spren halves to the gemstone at the base of the raysium blade, they learn that putting one half of a spren in a larger gem and moving that gem will cause the other half to move a greater amount. This is an important discovery about how spren halves interact.

**Q: Why does Navani want to rejoin split spren?**  
A: Navani tries to find a way to rejoin split spren. This may be related to helping deadeyes (who are split spren) or to understanding how spren work. The discovery about larger gems moving spren halves may be part of this research.

**Q: What is the sand for?**  
A: Navani asks Raboniel for a way to measure the strength of Stormlight, and Raboniel says she will get her some sand. This sand is likely from Taldain (based on the epigraph mentioning Midius, who is from that world) and may have special properties for measuring Investiture.

**Q: Can they kill Odium?**  
A: Navani believes that what they find might allow them to kill Odium. This relates to their earlier discovery about mixing Lights and creating anti-Light, which Raboniel suggested could be used to kill a god (from Chapter 76).

**Q: What is Warlight?**  
A: Warlight is the name for the mixed Light created when Stormlight and Voidlight are harmonized (from Chapter 76). Navani and Raboniel make more of it for Navani's experiments, continuing their research into Light and Investiture.

**Q: Why is this chapter called "Scholar"?**  
A: The title "Scholar" refers to Navani's role and her complete absorption in her research. She's finally able to be the scholar she's always wanted to be, fully focused on discovery and learning without other distractions. It's about her identity and her passion for knowledge.
