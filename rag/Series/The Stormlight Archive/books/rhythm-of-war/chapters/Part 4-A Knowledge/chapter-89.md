## Quick Summary
Navani is fully engrossed in her work, completely oblivious to everything except her lab as she tries to find the opposite of Light. She uses the sand that Raboniel gave her, which starts black and turns white when exposed to Investiture.

Navani and Raboniel discuss where the sand came from and how you have to travel through Shadesmar to get to other worlds. As they work, Raboniel asks why Navani does not believe herself a true scholar, declaring that she believes Navani to be one. Raboniel gives her the title "Voice of Lights," telling her that she is an equal to herself; Navani, on the other hand, is convinced that she has Raboniel fooled. Navani is getting better at interpreting the rhythms, and she can tell that Raboniel has a different purpose in mind behind this project than to kill Odium. Navani tries to convince Raboniel to negotiate a treaty with her, but she refuses. Raboniel leaves Navani to her work but later returns with metal plates and two singers to help her create the Rhythm of War using an ancient method of music. After Raboniel leaves, Navani tries to speak with the Sibling and finally hears a response. The Sibling says that they are weak and cold and don't want to die, and soon their voice fades away.

Navani looks around her lab and realizes that the music left patterns in the sand. She then realizes that Stormlight and Voidlight are not just illumination or a substance that bears properties of fluid. They are sounds and vibrations, and therefore, she is convinced she can find an opposite vibration.

## Rowan's "If Asked" Notes

**Q: What is the sand?**  
A: The sand that Raboniel gave Navani starts black and turns white when exposed to Investiture. This is likely sand from Taldain (based on the epigraph mentioning Midius), and it can detect and measure Investiture. Navani uses it to help find the opposite of Light.

**Q: What does "Voice of Lights" mean?**  
A: Raboniel gives Navani the title "Voice of Lights," telling her that she is an equal to herself. This is a significant honor, recognizing Navani's expertise and contribution to their research. However, Navani is convinced that she has Raboniel fooled, suggesting she may be hiding her true intentions.

**Q: What is Raboniel's real purpose?**  
A: Navani is getting better at interpreting the rhythms, and she can tell that Raboniel has a different purpose in mind behind this project than to kill Odium. Raboniel's true motivation isn't fully revealed, but it's not simply ending the war by killing Odium.

**Q: What does Navani realize about Light?**  
A: Navani realizes that Stormlight and Voidlight are not just illumination or a substance that bears properties of fluid. They are sounds and vibrations. This is a major breakthrough that changes how she understands Light and Investiture, and it convinces her that she can find an opposite vibration (anti-Light).

**Q: What are the patterns in the sand?**  
A: Navani looks around her lab and realizes that the music (used to create the Rhythm of War) left patterns in the sand. This visual representation of the music helps her understand that Light is vibrational, leading to her breakthrough realization.

**Q: Why does the Sibling finally respond?**  
A: After Raboniel leaves, Navani tries to speak with the Sibling and finally hears a response. The Sibling says that they are weak and cold and don't want to die, and soon their voice fades away. This suggests the Sibling is dying or very weak, which is why they haven't been responding.

**Q: What is the Rhythm of War?**  
A: Raboniel returns with metal plates and two singers to help create the Rhythm of War using an ancient method of music. This is the rhythm that was created when Stormlight and Voidlight were harmonized (from Chapter 76), and creating it through music may help with their research.

**Q: Why does Navani try to negotiate a treaty?**  
A: Navani tries to convince Raboniel to negotiate a treaty with her, but Raboniel refuses. This shows Navani's desire for peace and her attempt to find a diplomatic solution, but Raboniel is not interested in negotiating.

**Q: Why is this chapter called "Voice of Lights"?**  
A: The title "Voice of Lights" refers to the title that Raboniel gives Navani. It recognizes her as someone who understands and can work with Light, essentially giving her a voice in how Light is understood and used. It's both an honor and a recognition of her expertise.
