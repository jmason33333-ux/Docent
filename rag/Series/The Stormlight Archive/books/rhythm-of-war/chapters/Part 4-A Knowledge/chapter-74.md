## Quick Summary
Venli and Rlain are headed to Lirin's infirmary. News of Kaladin's fight has spread, and other fights between humans and singers have been breaking out in the tower. They reach the infirmary where the other Radiants are being cared for. Venli takes notice of Rlain's comfort and casualness with the humans. Rlain reveals that he had been able to sneak maps of the tower out of Navani's information vault before the Fused learned where they were; though Hesina is grateful, Lirin says that he isn't certain that the risk was worth it. Venli, on the other hand, believes that the tower's maps will show them a way out of the tower. Venli and Lirin talk, and Venli realizes that Lirin isn't interested in fighting against the occupation. Some human workers enter, and it upsets Lirin to see that one has a shash glyph on his forehead in honor of Kaladin. Lirin is disdainful of it, and though Venli defends it by saying it gives people hope, Lirin says that it's just going to cause more deaths.

## Rowan's "If Asked" Notes

**Q: What is the shash glyph?**  
A: The shash glyph is a symbol that some human workers are painting on their foreheads in honor of Kaladin. "Shash" means "dangerous" in Alethi, and it was the brand Kaladin had as a slave. Now it's being used as a symbol of resistance and hope, though Lirin sees it as dangerous.

**Q: Why is Lirin upset about the shash glyph?**  
A: Lirin is upset and disdainful of the shash glyph because he believes it will cause more deaths. He sees it as a symbol that will provoke the occupiers and lead to violence, rather than as a source of hope. This reflects his pacifist philosophy and his fear of escalation.

**Q: Why does Venli defend it?**  
A: Venli defends the shash glyph by saying it gives people hope. She sees it as a positive symbol that helps people resist and maintain their spirit, even if Lirin sees it as dangerous. This shows the different perspectives on resistance and hope.

**Q: What are the maps Rlain brought?**  
A: Rlain reveals that he sneaked maps of the tower out of Navani's information vault before the Fused learned where they were. These maps may show ways out of the tower or important locations. Venli believes they will show a way out, while Lirin isn't certain the risk was worth it.

**Q: Why are fights breaking out?**  
A: News of Kaladin's fight has spread, and other fights between humans and singers have been breaking out in the tower. This suggests that Kaladin's actions have inspired resistance, but it's also causing tension and violence between the two groups.

**Q: Why isn't Lirin interested in fighting?**  
A: Venli realizes that Lirin isn't interested in fighting against the occupation. This is consistent with his pacifist philosophy—he believes that fighting will only lead to more death and suffering, and he wants to avoid violence even under occupation.

**Q: What does Venli notice about Rlain?**  
A: Venli takes notice of Rlain's comfort and casualness with the humans. This shows that Rlain has been working with humans for a long time and is comfortable with them, which is why he was able to get the maps and why he's trusted by people like Hesina and Lirin.

**Q: Why is Hesina grateful for the maps?**  
A: Hesina is grateful that Rlain brought the maps, seeing them as potentially useful for escape or resistance. However, Lirin isn't certain the risk was worth it, showing their different perspectives on the value of taking risks.
