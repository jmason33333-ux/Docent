## Quick Summary
Dabbid, Rlain, and Lift are making their way to where Kaladin is kept. Dabbid thinks about how he has always been different and that he chose not to speak after he was rescued by Kaladin because he didn't want everyone to know that he was different. They reach Kaladin, who is not well at all. Lift heals him with a lot of effort, but he doesn't wake up immediately. The three talk about why Dabbid hasn't spoken until now, and Rlain tells Dabbid that he wishes that he hadn't felt like he needed to be quiet. Dabbid says that the tower told him that he will be fixed after getting a spren. They are interrupted by Teft, whom Lift healed while they were talking. As they start to explain to him what has been going on, Kaladin wakes as well.

## Rowan's "If Asked" Notes

**Q: Why doesn't Dabbid speak?**  
A: Dabbid thinks about how he has always been different and that he chose not to speak after he was rescued by Kaladin because he didn't want everyone to know that he was different. This is a conscious choice to hide his difference, showing his fear of being judged or rejected.

**Q: What does the tower tell Dabbid?**  
A: Dabbid says that the tower (the Sibling) told him that he will be fixed after getting a spren. This suggests that the Sibling has been communicating with Dabbid (similar to how they communicated with Navani and Kaladin) and that bonding a spren might help with whatever makes Dabbid "different."

**Q: Can Lift heal Kaladin?**  
A: Lift heals Kaladin with a lot of effort, but he doesn't wake up immediately. This shows that Lift's healing ability (using Lifelight) can work even when other Radiants' powers are suppressed, but it takes effort. Eventually, Kaladin does wake up.

**Q: Why does Rlain wish Dabbid hadn't been quiet?**  
A: Rlain tells Dabbid that he wishes that he hadn't felt like he needed to be quiet. This shows Rlain's understanding and empathy—he recognizes that Dabbid's silence was driven by fear and that he shouldn't have had to hide who he is.

**Q: What happens to Teft?**  
A: They are interrupted by Teft, whom Lift healed while they were talking. Teft was also unconscious (from the suppression field), and Lift's healing has brought him back. This means there are now more Radiants awake and able to help.

**Q: Why is this chapter from Dabbid's POV?**  
A: This chapter is from Dabbid's perspective, which is rare. It gives insight into his thoughts and why he chooses not to speak, showing his internal experience and his relationship with Bridge Four and the tower. It's an important moment for his character.

**Q: What does "fixed" mean?**  
A: When Dabbid says the tower told him he will be "fixed" after getting a spren, it may mean that bonding a spren will help with whatever condition or difference he has. However, the word "fixed" is problematic—it suggests there's something wrong with Dabbid that needs to be corrected, when in reality he may just be different.

**Q: Why is this chapter called "Dabbid"?**  
A: The title "Dabbid" reflects that this is his chapter, told from his perspective. It's a rare POV for him, and it focuses on his story, his choice not to speak, and his relationship with the others. It's about understanding who Dabbid is.

**Q: What does the epigraph mean?**  
A: The epigraph "Why would I want to remember?" may relate to Dabbid's choice not to speak or to remember certain things. It could also relate to the broader theme of memory and forgetting, possibly connected to the epigraph from Chapter 84 about using Investiture to enhance memories.
