## Quick Summary
**Shallan:** Radiant is yelling at Mraize through the cube because he hadn't told them who Restares really is. Veil takes over and tells Mraize that they no longer have an incentive to work with him, as Mraize's hiding of this information suggests that they may not be able to trust what Mraize has told them. Mraize has Veil open the cube and pull out a metal dagger with a gemstone that he tells her to use on Kelek to capture his soul. He explains that the leader of the Ghostbloods has a similar condition to Kelek and needs to learn more about the Herald in order to avoid his fate. He convinces Veil that Kelek is dangerous must be locked away, and that Shallan can impersonate him in order to rule in Adolin's favor. He tells her that if she does this, she will become a full Ghostblood.

**Adolin:** Adolin goes to speak to Kelek and tells him why he is there. Kelek is astounded that they would let him in and thinks that his cause is doomed. Adolin stands by his decision and asks for a trial by witness, which Kelek grants, despite saying that it won't matter anyway.

## Rowan's "If Asked" Notes

**Q: What is the dagger?**  
A: Mraize gives Veil a metal dagger with a gemstone that she can use on Kelek to capture his soul. This is a weapon designed to trap a Herald, which would allow the Ghostbloods to study him and learn how to avoid his fate (madness/immortality issues).

**Q: Why do the Ghostbloods want to capture Kelek?**  
A: Mraize explains that the leader of the Ghostbloods (Thaidakar) has a similar condition to Kelek and needs to learn more about the Herald in order to avoid his fate. This suggests that Thaidakar is also immortal or has some condition related to immortality that's causing problems.

**Q: What does Mraize want Shallan to do?**  
A: Mraize convinces Veil that Kelek is dangerous and must be locked away, and that Shallan can impersonate him in order to rule in Adolin's favor at the trial. If she does this, she will become a full Ghostblood, which is a significant promotion.

**Q: Who is Thaidakar?**  
A: Thaidakar is the leader of the Ghostbloods. Mraize reveals that he has a similar condition to Kelek (likely related to immortality or being trapped in some way) and needs to learn from Kelek to avoid his fate. This is why they want to capture Kelek.

**Q: Why is Kelek astounded?**  
A: Kelek is astounded that they would let Adolin in to speak with him. He thinks that his cause is doomed, suggesting he's given up hope. However, Adolin stands by his decision and asks for a trial by witness, which Kelek grants.

**Q: What is trial by witness?**  
A: Adolin asks for a trial by witness, which Kelek grants. This opens Adolin up to self-incrimination, but Adolin is certain it will be necessary to appeal to the honorspren's honor. This is part of his strategy for the trial.

**Q: Why does Veil say they have no incentive?**  
A: Veil tells Mraize that they no longer have an incentive to work with him because he hadn't told them who Restares really is. His hiding of this information suggests they may not be able to trust what he's told them, which undermines their working relationship.

**Q: What does the epigraph mean?**  
A: The epigraph "Oh … Father … Seven thousand years" is likely from Kelek, expressing grief or regret about something that happened seven thousand years ago, possibly related to the Oathpact or the Heralds' situation. It may relate to his feeling that his cause is doomed.

**Q: Why is this chapter called "Knife"?**  
A: The title "Knife" refers to the metal dagger that Mraize gives to Veil to use on Kelek. It's a literal knife, but it's also a metaphor for the dangerous plan to capture a Herald and the moral knife-edge that Shallan is on in deciding whether to go through with it.
