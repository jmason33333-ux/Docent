---

## Quick Summary
Shallan, as Formless, rises early the day of Adolin's final judgement. Adolin speaks to her about the mistake often made when judging strength and weakness before she convinces him to stay in bed. Formless plans her entry into Kelek's house but is interrupted by both Veil speaking up from deep within her and by Pattern. She Lightweaves an illusion of Lusintia around herself, but then Pattern makes a final attempt to speak with her and she firmly tells him to stay in the house.

Pattern catches up with Formless on the way to Kelek's house, and he delivers a message from Wit: Shallan deserves to be trusted and loved. Formless turns away from him again and goes on alone. Radiant interrupts her by confessing that she killed Ialai Sadeas just as Formless gets to the house, but she musters her determination to go through with her plan and enters anyway. Just after drawing Mraize's knife, Formless feels Stormlight leaving her and looks around to see Veil. Veil accuses her of really just being Shallan, and she admits the truth but still feels like she wants to take this step and join the Ghostbloods. Veil keeps telling Shallan that she really is strong while Shallan admits to killing her parents. Then Shallan accepts Veil's memories and sees that the Cryptic she had bonded as a child was not Pattern at all, but Testament.

As Radiant whispers encouragement to her, Shallan makes one last struggle with herself, reaching for the door to Kelek's room before finally turning away and creating a Stormlight reproduction of the scene when she killed Testament. At the conclusion of the scene, Veil integrates herself into Shallan and Kelek walks into the room. Shallan admits that she had been sent by the Ghostbloods to kill him and asks him to go easy on Adolin in the trial. As they converse, a group of honorspren burst into the house and accuse them of collusion. Despite their denials, Sekeir orders Kelek sequestered.

## Rowan's "If Asked" Notes

**Q: Who is Formless?**  
A: Formless is a persona of Shallan's that takes over in this chapter. She plans to kill Kelek and join the Ghostbloods, but Veil and Radiant fight against this. Eventually, Shallan overcomes Formless and chooses not to kill Kelek.

**Q: What does Veil integrating mean?**  
A: At the conclusion of the scene where Shallan recreates killing Testament, Veil integrates herself into Shallan. This means Veil is no longer a separate persona but becomes part of Shallan herself. This is a significant moment of healing and integration for Shallan.

**Q: What does Shallan realize about Testament?**  
A: When Shallan accepts Veil's memories, she sees that the Cryptic she had bonded as a child was not Pattern at all, but Testament. This confirms what was revealed in Chapter 75—Testament was Shallan's first spren, and she killed her, turning her into a deadeye.

**Q: Why does Shallan create the scene of killing Testament?**  
A: Shallan creates a Stormlight reproduction of the scene when she killed Testament. This is a way for her to face her past and accept what happened. It's part of her healing process and her decision not to repeat her mistakes by killing Kelek.

**Q: What does Radiant confess?**  
A: Radiant interrupts Formless by confessing that she killed Ialai Sadeas. This is a revelation about Ialai's death—it was Radiant (one of Shallan's personas) who killed her, not someone else. This is part of Shallan accepting her actions and taking responsibility.

**Q: Why does Shallan not kill Kelek?**  
A: Despite Formless's plan and Mraize's instructions, Shallan makes one last struggle with herself and finally turns away from killing Kelek. She creates the scene of killing Testament instead, faces her past, and chooses not to repeat it. This is a moment of growth and healing.

**Q: What does Wit's message mean?**  
A: Pattern delivers a message from Wit: "Shallan deserves to be trusted and loved." This is a message of support and validation, telling Shallan that she is worthy of trust and love despite her past actions and struggles. It's meant to help her see her own value.

**Q: Why is Kelek sequestered?**  
A: A group of honorspren burst into the house and accuse Shallan and Kelek of collusion. Despite their denials, Sekeir orders Kelek sequestered. This means Kelek is removed from his position as High Judge, which will affect Adolin's trial.

**Q: Why is this chapter called "Strong Enough"?**  
A: The title "Strong Enough" refers to Veil telling Shallan that she really is strong, and to Shallan's struggle to be strong enough to overcome Formless and choose not to kill. It's about Shallan finding the strength to face her past and make the right choice.

**Q: What does the epigraph mean?**  
A: The epigraph is from Kelek, talking about Jezrien. He says that Jezrien faded away after being captured, and that while Kelek should welcome the same (death/escape), he doesn't—he fears "you" (possibly the Ghostbloods or whoever is hunting Heralds). This relates to the danger Kelek is in.
