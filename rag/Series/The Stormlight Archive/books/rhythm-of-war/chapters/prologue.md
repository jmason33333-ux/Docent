## Quick Summary
Navani Kholin is busy with preparations for a feast in honor of her husband, King Gavilar. She encounters various characters, including a tense moment with her husband about mysterious spheres and personal accusations. The prologue concludes with Gavilar's assassination, and Navani's grief and concern over her earlier actions.

---

## Key Beats
- Navani manages logistics for Gavilar's feast, juggling interactions and responsibilities.
- She observes Aesudan speaking with Rushur Kris about fabrial technology.
- Navani overhears Gavilar speaking with two men about strange spheres.
- Confrontation with Gavilar over his absences and accusations of her infidelity.
- Argument about their daughter Jasnah's refusal to court Meridas Amaram.
- Navani burns a glyphward with the words "Death. Gift. Death."
- Gavilar is assassinated, leaving Navani to confront his death and her fears about the glyphward.
