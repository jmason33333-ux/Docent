## Chapter Epigraph
> "The world becomes an increasingly dangerous place, and so I come to the crux of my argument. We cannot afford to keep secrets from one another any longer. The Thaylen artifabrians have private techniques relating to how they remove Stormlight from gems and create fabrials around extremely large stones. I beg the coalition and the good people of Thaylenah to acknowledge our collective need. I have taken the first step by opening my research to all scholars. ## Quick Summary
Navani visits Rushu in the Crystal Pillar Room to discuss progress on mapping gems to fabrial constructions and learns about a corrupted spren in the captured suppression fabrial. She then meets with other scholars to explore a lifting fabrial prototype. Later, during a coalition meeting, strategic plans are discussed, including an attack on Emul and an envoy mission to the honorspren capital.

---

## Key Beats
- Navani discusses the lack of progress in mapping the gems in the pillar room with Rushu.
- The captured suppression fabrial is found to contain a corrupted spren.
- Navani explores a lifting fabrial prototype with Tomor and Falilar, considering its implications for labor efficiency.
- At a coalition meeting, Dalinar outlines a proposed attack on Emul, with support from various monarchs.
- Discussions lead to the decision to send a mission to the honorspren capital, with Shallan and Adolin volunteering.
- Navani and Dalinar discuss Taravangian's motivations and future plans.
