## Quick Summary
Kaladin engages in a fierce battle with a Fused who displays unusual combat abilities, leaving him exhausted. Meanwhile, Shallan, in the guise of Veil, enacts a plan to infiltrate the Sons of Honor, successfully getting herself kidnapped. Kaladin returns to Hearthstone, reunites with his family, and learns of an evacuation plan for the town.

---

## Key Beats
- Kaladin challenges a Fused to single combat, discovering the Fused's ability to teleport and regenerate using Voidlight.
- The Fused employs an unorthodox fighting style, repeatedly disabling Kaladin.
- Shallan, as Veil, attempts to draw out the Sons of Honor by posing as a discontented merchant and gets drugged and kidnapped.
- Kaladin and Syl discuss his insomnia and sense of disconnection from others.
- Kaladin reunites with his family in Hearthstone and argues with Lirin about the necessity of his violent actions.
- The chapter concludes with the arrival of Navani's floating platform for evacuating Hearthstone.
