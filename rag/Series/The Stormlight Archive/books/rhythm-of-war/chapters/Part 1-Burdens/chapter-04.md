## Chapter Epigraph
The epigraph for this chapter provides insight into the method of extracting Stormlight from gemstones, known as the Arnist Method. It involves using larger empty gemstones to draw Stormlight from smaller infused ones, highlighting the resource-intensive nature of this technique.

> "To draw Stormlight out of a gemstone, I use the Arnist Method. Several large empty gemstones are brought close to the infused one while the spren is inspecting it. Stormlight is slowly absorbed from a small gemstone by a very large gemstone of the same type—and several together can draw the Light out quickly. The method's limitation is, of course, the fact that you need not merely acquire one gemstone for your fabrial, but several larger ones to withdraw the Stormlight."

## Quick Summary
Radiant, a persona of Shallan Davar, finds herself kidnapped and taken into the chasms. Veil, another of Shallan's personas, takes over to navigate an initiation into the Sons of Honor. After questioning the cultists and using a fake fabrial, Veil learns of a spy near Dalinar. Shallan ultimately reveals her true self to the cultists.

---

## Key Beats
- Radiant awakens to find herself being carried with a sack on her head.
- She is taken into the chasms by cultists of the Sons of Honor.
- Veil takes control, pretending to be interested in joining the group.
- The cultists attempt to use a fake fabrial sold to them by Veil to detect Lightweavings.
- Veil swears a false oath of loyalty to the Sons of Honor.
- Veil discovers the existence of a spy within Dalinar's inner circle.
- Shallan reasserts control, revealing she is not who the cultists believe her to be.

## Rowan's "If Asked" Notes

**Q: How does the fake fabrial work?**  
A: The fake fabrial doesn't actually detect Lightweavings; it's a trick by Veil to manipulate the cultists.
