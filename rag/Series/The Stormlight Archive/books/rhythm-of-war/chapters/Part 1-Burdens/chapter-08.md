## Quick Summary
Kaladin discovers a murder scene in the manor's cellar, where he confronts Moash, who has just killed Roshone. Moash tries to convince Kaladin that the only escape from pain is death before fleeing. Meanwhile, Shallan investigates maps and finds a mysterious notebook hidden behind a carving of Shin Kak Nish.

---

## Key Beats
- Kaladin enters the manor's cellar, passing a wall of flames.
- He finds Jeber and another man dead, killed by a Shardblade.
- Moash holds Roshone at knifepoint, then kills him.
- Kaladin confronts Moash, who surrenders after a brief exchange.
- Shallan examines wines, identifying one as Shin wine with Adolin's help.
- With Pattern's assistance, Shallan discovers carvings of Epoch Kingdom maps.
- Shallan uncovers a notebook hidden behind a stone carved with Shin Kak Nish.
- Moash tells Kaladin that people he loves will continue to die.
- An illusion of a radiant version of Moash appears, causing Moash to flee.
- Renarin arrives to inform Kaladin that the ship is ready to leave.
