## Quick Summary
Kaladin is on his way to the Shattered Plains when Syl informs him that Yunfah wishes to speak with him. Yunfah, a spren, is still choosing a Radiant to bond with. Kaladin suggests Rlain, but Yunfah refuses. At Narak, Kaladin discusses battle tactics with Dalinar, who suggests relieving him of active duty due to his mental exhaustion. Kaladin is given ten days to decide his future role.

---

## Key Beats
- Kaladin travels to the Shattered Plains and is approached by Syl about Yunfah.
- Yunfah expresses frustration over selecting a Radiant, rejecting Kaladin's suggestion of Rlain.

- Dalinar observes Kaladin's worn state and proposes removing him from active duty.
- Kaladin struggles with his inability to speak the Fourth Ideal.
- Dalinar offers Kaladin a supportive role in teaching and training soldiers.
- Kaladin is given a timeframe of ten days to decide his course of action.
