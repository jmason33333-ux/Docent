## Quick Summary
In the occupied town of Hearthstone, Lirin, a surgeon, treats Herdazian and Alethi refugees who have suffered from the ongoing war with Odium's forces. He encounters a traumatized Alethi man, Noril, and learns about a Herdazian general, known as "the Mink," who is nearby. Lirin plans to connect this general with his son Kaladin for a rescue mission. Events escalate when a Fused arrives looking for a spy, leading to Kaladin's exposure as a refugee.

---

## Key Beats
- Lirin treats refugees in Hearthstone, dealing with the fallout of the Everstorm and Odium's conquest.
- He attends to Noril, a one-armed Alethi man, and sends him to a tent for suicide watch.
- Abiajan, a singer overseeing the town, checks the refugees for signs of plague.
- Laral informs Lirin about the presence of the Herdazian general, "the Mink."
- Lirin inspects the Mink and plans a covert extraction with Kaladin's help.
- Roshone causes a distraction to assist with the escape plan.
- Abiajan interrupts, announcing the arrival of a Fused searching for a spy.
- Kaladin is revealed under the hood of a refugee.
