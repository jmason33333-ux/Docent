## Quick Summary
Kaladin returns to the Fourth Bridge, where he grapples with his depression despite the celebrations for Laran swearing the Third Ideal. Navani, traveling in a wooden sphere, contemplates contradictions in fabrial technology and receives a mysterious warning about her experiments. Shallan, with Adolin, debates the ethics of assassination and considers her ties to the Ghostbloods.

---

## Key Beats
- Kaladin is given broth by an ardent and avoids joining the Windrunners' celebrations.
- Syl discusses Kaladin’s mental state and efforts to help him.
- Teft encourages Kaladin to congratulate Laran and they briefly discuss Moash.
- Navani studies a Radiant suppression fabrial and compares it to other fabrials' designs.
- Navani finds a hidden spanreed with a message warning against her fabrial experiments.
- Shallan and Adolin discuss the implications of Ialai’s assassination and Shallan’s connection to the Ghostbloods.
