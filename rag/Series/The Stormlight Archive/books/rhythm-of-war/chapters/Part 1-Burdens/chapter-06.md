## Quick Summary
Shallan chooses to confront Ialai Sadeas directly, risking a dangerous encounter. Kaladin witnesses Leshwi sparing Sigzil and engages her in a tense midair battle. Navani manages the evacuation of Hearthstone, coordinating efforts on the Fourth Bridge and observing Dalinar open Honor's Perpendicularity.

---

## Key Beats
- Shallan decides to meet Ialai Sadeas face-to-face and is led through Kholinar by her captors.
- Veil, one of Shallan's personas, considers assassinating Ialai but recognizes the political ramifications.
- Kaladin sees Leshwi sparing Sigzil and honors the gesture by directing Sigzil to bow to the Fused.
- Navani organizes the evacuation of refugees and reflects on procedural improvements for future emergencies.
- Navani witnesses Dalinar opening Honor's Perpendicularity, seeing into Shadesmar.
- Kaladin and Leshwi engage in a midair duel, both demonstrating mutual respect by withdrawing weapons before fatal blows.
- They are interrupted by a nearby crisis; Leshwi allows Kaladin to address the threat to civilians.
