## Chapter Epigraph
> "One of my pleas is for artifabrians to stop shrouding fabrial techniques with so much mystery. Many decoy metals are used in cages, and wires are often plated to look like a different metal, with the express intent of confusing those who might try to learn the process through personal study. ## Quick Summary
Kaladin returns to Urithiru feeling despondent, despite efforts from friends to lift his spirits. He maintains a strong front but struggles with thoughts of Moash. Adolin arrives, prompted by Syl, to take Kaladin for a drink. At Jez's Duty winehouse, they discuss various topics, including the recent assassination of Ialai Sadeas, Shallan's personas, and Kaladin's personal struggles. Rock informs Kaladin of his upcoming departure to face trials back home.

---

## Rowan's "If Asked" Notes

**Q: Why is Kaladin so depressed despite being with friends?**
A: Kaladin's depression is a chronic issue, compounded by recent traumatic events, making it difficult for him to find solace even among friends.

**Q: What are Shallan's personas?**
A: Shallan's personas, Veil and Radiant, are aspects of her personality she uses to cope with trauma and to manage different social situations.

**Q: Why is Rock leaving Urithiru?**
A: Rock is returning to his homeland to face a trial related to cultural obligations and events that occurred during the war.
