## Quick Summary
Venli accompanies Leshwi through Kholinar as they gather information about the Fused. The next day, Venli attends a conclave of significant singers where Raboniel proposes capturing Urithiru. Leshwi explains her distrust of Raboniel, while Venli contemplates the possibility of freeing her people from the ongoing conflict.

---

## Key Beats
- Venli and Leshwi move through Kholinar gathering intelligence on the Fused.
- The Nine hold a meeting discussing the recent battle in Alethkar and a suppressor device.
- Lezian is interrogated about the device malfunction, expressing his intent to pursue Kaladin.
- Raboniel proposes capturing Urithiru, suggesting the use of Taravangian's information.
- Despite objections, the Nine approve Raboniel's plan, focusing on Dalinar and Jasnah.
- Leshwi expresses her distrust of Raboniel to Venli.
- Venli questions the possibility of liberating her people from the conflict, as Timbre shows interest in their Radiant powers.
