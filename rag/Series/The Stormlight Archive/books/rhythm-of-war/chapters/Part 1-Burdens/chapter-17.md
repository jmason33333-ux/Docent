## Quick Summary
Navani observes as Dalinar and Shallan create a map of Roshar using Stormlight. The Mink evaluates the map, discussing strategy with Dalinar, Jasnah, and others. They plan to free Herdaz and debate the advisability of attacking Alethkar versus Emul. Jasnah proposes freeing Alethi slaves and consults with the Heralds about the Oathpact.

- Navani watches Dalinar and Shallan form a Stormlight map of Roshar.

- The Mink suggests avoiding an attack on Alethkar, advising a focus on Emul.
