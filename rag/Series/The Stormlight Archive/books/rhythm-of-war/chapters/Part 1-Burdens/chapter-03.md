## Quick Summary
Navani Kholin oversees the landing of the flying platform, the Fourth Bridge, in Hearthstone. The crew, including scientists and military personnel, prepare for a mission to evacuate the town's residents, threatened by the presence of Fused. As Kaladin and Lirin discuss the evacuation with Dalinar, Navani reflects on her work with fabrials and the potential of the new technology. The chapter ends with a warning of incoming Fused, prompting Navani and the crew to prepare for a potential battle.

---

## Key Beats
- Navani observes Alethkar's terrain from the Fourth Bridge as it lands in Hearthstone.
- Velat, the cartographer, cautions Navani, but Dalinar supports her actions.
- Navani reflects on the creation and significance of the Fourth Bridge, named after Kaladin's old team.
- Edgedancers, including Lift, disembark to assist and heal Hearthstone's townspeople.
- Navani discusses the Fourth Bridge's military potential with Admiral Kmakl.
- Kaladin and Lirin arrive; Kaladin requests Stormlight to confront the Fused.
- Dalinar creates Honor's Perpendicularity to recharge Kaladin's Stormlight.
- Navani considers how Dalinar's bond with the Stormfather might reveal insights about Urithiru's tower fabrial.
- The Mink appears, seeking assistance for Herdazian troops; Dalinar agrees to help without consulting Navani.
- Lyn warns of three flights of incoming Fused, prompting preparations for defense.

## Rowan's "If Asked" Notes

**Q: What is the purpose of the Fourth Bridge?**
A: The Fourth Bridge is a flying platform designed for transporting people and resources, used here to evacuate Hearthstone.

**Q: How does Navani feel about her contributions to the fabrial technology?**
A: Navani feels her contributions are significant but struggles with self-doubt, thinking her role is mainly guiding smarter people.

**Q: Why is the Mink wary of Dalinar?**
A: The Mink is cautious due to Dalinar's reputation as the Blackthorn but is willing to collaborate for mutual benefit.
