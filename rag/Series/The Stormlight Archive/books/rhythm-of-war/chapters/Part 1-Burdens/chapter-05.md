## Quick Summary
Kaladin and the Windrunners engage the Heavenly Ones, a group of Fused, in aerial combat over a populated area. Meanwhile, Shallan navigates a tense situation with cultists by bluffing about her role as a secretive information broker. The chapter explores the tactics and challenges faced by both characters as they manage the threats in their respective environments.

## Key Beats
- Kaladin and the Windrunners confront the Heavenly Ones in the air, with Kaladin specifically targeting Leshwi.
- Dalinar opens a perpendicularity to assist, heightening the intensity of the battle.
- Shallan, under pressure from cultists, pretends to deal in military secrets, using her Lightweaving abilities to demonstrate her claims.
- Kaladin clashes with a Fused, sparing him after disabling his weapon arm, showcasing his strategic control and restraint.
- A new type of Fused, Lezian, attacks Kaladin with a unique ribbon-like movement, highlighting a fresh threat.
- Syl confronts Kaladin about his attitude towards Roshone, adding a layer of personal conflict to the larger battle.
- Kaladin directs Lyn to alert others about Lezian's abilities, emphasizing the importance of communication and adaptation in combat.
