## Quick Summary
Navani visits Szeth in his cell to investigate a mysterious sphere given to him by Gavilar. She grapples with the unsettling nature of the sphere and Szeth’s strange Blade. Later, she observes the community at Urithiru and discusses scientific observations with Brother Benneh. The chapter concludes with a meeting involving Dalinar, Shallan, and Adolin, where tensions about past events resurface.
