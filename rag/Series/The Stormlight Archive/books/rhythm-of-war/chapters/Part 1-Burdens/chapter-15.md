## Quick Summary
Kaladin navigates his forced retirement by promoting Sigzil and Skar to his previous duties. He seeks out Zahel for guidance on his future path and ends up in a sparring match that leads to a deeper understanding of his internal conflicts. Meanwhile, Rlain expresses his desire for a spren who chooses him genuinely, rejecting the bond with Yunfah.

---

## Key Beats
- Dalinar announces Kaladin's change in status.
- Kaladin promotes Sigzil and Skar to manage his former responsibilities.
- Kaladin encounters Rlain in a field, who is using a new listener farming method.
- Rlain declines the bond with Yunfah, seeking a spren who values him for who he is.
- Kaladin finds Zahel doing laundry and engages in a conversation that turns into a sparring session.
- Zahel challenges Kaladin to understand his true reasons for fighting.
- Kaladin and Zahel discuss faith and the nature of fighting, leading Kaladin to introspection.
- Kaladin leaves with a deeper understanding of his internal struggles.

## Rowan's "If Asked" Notes

**Q: Why does Rlain refuse the bond with Yunfah?**
A: Rlain seeks a spren who chooses him for who he is, not out of obligation or convenience.

**Q: What is the significance of the sparring match with Zahel?**
A: It's a metaphor for Kaladin's internal struggle with his role and beliefs, pushing him to introspection.

**Q: How do logicspren function in fabrials?**
A: They vary the light they emit based on stimuli, enabling the creation of complex mechanisms like fabrial clocks.
