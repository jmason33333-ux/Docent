## Quick Summary
Shallan, under her persona of Veil, engages Ialai Sadeas in conversation to extract information about her activities and connections. Ialai reveals suspicions about the Ghostbloods and hints at a secret she keeps hidden. Meanwhile, Kaladin is forced into a fight by the Pursuer, who uses a fabrial to negate Kaladin's powers. Kaladin manages to outmaneuver and defeat the Pursuer. After Ialai is found dead by poisoning, Shallan reflects on the possibility of betrayal within her ranks and searches for Ialai's hidden secret.
