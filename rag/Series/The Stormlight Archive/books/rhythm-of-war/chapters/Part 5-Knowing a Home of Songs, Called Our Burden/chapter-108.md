## Quick Summary
Kaladin finds himself in a tent on a battlefield from his time in Amaram's army. Varth walks up to speak with Gor, who tells him that their Brightlord's orders are to hold where they are. Varth directs Kaladin and the other messenger boy to grab spears and get out front, and Kaladin recognizes the scene. He sees Tien volunteer to go with them, and Tien explains to Kaladin that although he had been terrified, he needed to be there to help the others feel brave.

The rest of the vision vanishes, leaving Kaladin and Tien, who now looks fully grown, alone. Kaladin weeps over Teft's death, expressing that nothing matters, since everyone eventually dies. Tien shifts his perspective, telling him that that means that the moments they help each other are what truly matter; instead of focusing on failing to keep Teft alive, Kaladin should think about how lucky they were to have what time they did together. Tien affirms that Kaladin is good and strong enough for him and envisions Odium realizing he has failed to beat Kaladin yet again. After Kaladin asks if the vision is real, Tien gives him a carved wooden horse.

The vision completely disappears, and Kaladin locates Syl among pinpricks of light. Grabbing her hand, he reassures her that he has always known the Words of his next Ideal. He states the Fourth Ideal of the Order of Windrunners, addressing the Stormfather, and hears a different, familiar voice accepting them as they reenter the storm.

## Rowan's "If Asked" Notes

**Q: What is the vision?**  
A: Kaladin finds himself in a tent on a battlefield from his time in Amaram's army. This is the battle where Tien died. The vision is created by Dalinar using his Bondsmith powers, showing Kaladin a moment from his past to help him understand and recover.

**Q: What does Tien teach Kaladin?**  
A: Kaladin weeps over Teft's death, expressing that nothing matters since everyone eventually dies. Tien shifts his perspective, telling him that that means the moments they help each other are what truly matter. Instead of focusing on failing to keep Teft alive, Kaladin should think about how lucky they were to have what time they did together. This is a profound shift—from focusing on loss to focusing on the value of the moments they had.

**Q: What is the carved wooden horse?**  
A: After Kaladin asks if the vision is real, Tien gives him a carved wooden horse. This is a physical object from the vision, which suggests the vision has some reality to it. The horse may be a symbol of hope, connection, or memory.

**Q: What is the Fourth Ideal?**  
A: Kaladin states the Fourth Ideal of the Order of Windrunners, addressing the Stormfather. The exact words are not fully revealed in the summary, but it's clear that Kaladin has always known them and now truly believes them. This is a major moment—Kaladin speaking his next Ideal and potentially saving himself.

**Q: Who is the familiar voice?**  
A: Kaladin hears a different, familiar voice accepting the Ideal as they reenter the storm. This is likely Wit/Hoid, who has been helping throughout. The fact that it's a "different, familiar voice" suggests it's not the Stormfather accepting the Ideal, but someone else.

**Q: Why is this chapter called "Moments"?**  
A: The title "Moments" refers to Tien's teaching—that the moments they help each other are what truly matter. It's about focusing on the value of the moments they had together rather than the loss. It's about the importance of connection and helping others in the present moment.

**Q: What does the epigraph mean?**  
A: The epigraph says "For ones so confused, they are somehow brilliant." This refers to Kaladin—he's confused and lost, but there's still brilliance in him, or to humans in general who may be confused but still have moments of brilliance and insight.

**Q: Does Kaladin survive?**  
A: The chapter ends with Kaladin speaking the Fourth Ideal and reentering the storm, which suggests he may survive. However, the full outcome isn't revealed in this chapter. The fact that he speaks the Ideal and is accepted suggests he's on the path to recovery.
