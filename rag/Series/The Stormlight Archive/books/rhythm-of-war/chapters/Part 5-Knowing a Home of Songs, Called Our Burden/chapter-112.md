## Quick Summary
Dalinar, Szeth, and the Windrunners return from Tukar just in time to hear news of Urithiru's liberation. After sending a message to Navani and asking to meet with Jasnah and the Prime after the impending highstorm, Dalinar goes to his quarters. As he enters, he walks onto a golden field and realizes that Odium has come to speak with him. As Odium tries to provoke Dalinar into revealing the nature of his relationship with Ishar, Dalinar realizes that Odium is frightened of him and attempts to provoke Odium in turn. Odium looks at the terms Wit has written on Dalinar's behalf and angrily detects the worldhopper's hand in them. He tells Dalinar that he is incapable of accepting the terms now that the Everstorm has changed the situation, then explains that his plan has been to use the Rosharans as hardened warriors to fight for him in the cosmere.

Odium expresses interest in making an agreement that day, so he describes his terms: as Dalinar has to give up the idea of Odium restraining the Fused in the event of Dalinar's victory, Odium would give up his designs for Roshar and leave the Rosharan system in the event of his own victory. Dalinar, forewarned by Wit, refuses to make any agreement that will allow Odium to leave the Rosharan system, so they continue to negotiate until they settle on something acceptable--despite Dalinar's horror at the prospect of his failure--to them both: a battle of champions in eleven days. Should Dalinar's champion win, Odium will cease all hostilities, return Alethkar and Herdaz to the coalition, and remain bound to the system. Should Odium's champion win, he will still cease hostilities and remain bound to the system, but he will keep what land he has conquered and will gain Dalinar's soul as a Fused.

## Rowan's "If Asked" Notes

**Q: What are the terms of the agreement?**  
A: They agree to a battle of champions in eleven days. Should Dalinar's champion win, Odium will cease all hostilities, return Alethkar and Herdaz to the coalition, and remain bound to the system. Should Odium's champion win, he will still cease hostilities and remain bound to the system, but he will keep what land he has conquered and will gain Dalinar's soul as a Fused.

**Q: Why does Odium want to leave the system?**  
A: Odium explains that his plan has been to use the Rosharans as hardened warriors to fight for him in the cosmere. He wants to leave the Rosharan system to expand his influence to other worlds. However, Dalinar, forewarned by Wit, refuses to make any agreement that allows Odium to leave the system.

**Q: Why is Odium frightened of Dalinar?**  
A: As Odium tries to provoke Dalinar into revealing the nature of his relationship with Ishar, Dalinar realizes that Odium is frightened of him and attempts to provoke Odium in turn. This is significant—Odium, a Shard, is afraid of Dalinar, a mortal. This shows Dalinar's power and potential threat.

**Q: What does Wit's involvement mean?**  
A: Odium looks at the terms Wit has written on Dalinar's behalf and angrily detects the worldhopper's hand in them. Wit has been helping Dalinar craft the terms to ensure Odium can't leave the system and to structure the agreement in a way that's favorable to Dalinar. This shows Wit's strategic involvement.

**Q: What happens if Dalinar's champion wins?**  
A: Should Dalinar's champion win, Odium will cease all hostilities, return Alethkar and Herdaz to the coalition, and remain bound to the system. This is a victory condition that ends the war and restores the coalition's lands, while keeping Odium trapped in the Rosharan system.

**Q: What happens if Odium's champion wins?**  
A: Should Odium's champion win, he will still cease hostilities and remain bound to the system, but he will keep what land he has conquered and will gain Dalinar's soul as a Fused. This is the horror that Dalinar faces—not just losing, but having his soul taken and turned into a Fused, an immortal enemy.

**Q: Why is this chapter called "Terms"?**  
A: The title "Terms" refers to the terms of the agreement being negotiated between Dalinar and Odium. It's about the conditions of the battle of champions and what each side will gain or lose. It's a critical moment where the rules of the final conflict are established.

**Q: What does the epigraph mean?**  
A: The epigraph says "Roshar will be united in its service of the greater war." This refers to Odium's plan to use Rosharans as warriors in the cosmere—he wants to unite Roshar under his control to fight in a larger war across the universe. It's about his expansionist goals.
