## Quick Summary
**Shallan:** Adolin and Shallan, in a room with their three spren, think that Testament looks better since Adolin's talk with her. They use Ala to contact Mraize, and Shallan tells him that, instead of killing Kelek, Veil has reintegrated herself with Shallan. Mraize tries to entice her back to the Ghostbloods with both promises of secrets and threats against her family, but Shallan is adamant and tells him that she will get to Ba-Ado-Mishram before the Ghostbloods can. Before ending the call, she tells Mraize the Ghostbloods had better vacate Urithiru before she returns and gives him messages from herself and from Wit to deliver to Thaidakar when his avatar next visits. Shallan thinks about the other worlds in the cosmere that Kelek had told her about with longing.

**Venli:** Venli and her group from Urithiru find the listeners on the Shattered Plains. Venli approaches alone; when Thude recognizes her, he at first runs away before he sees Venli draw in Stormlight. The other listeners all look at her with suspicion and hostility, but they allow her into the camp to see her mother. She brings a Reacher with her in the hope that the spren and her mother can bond so Jaxlim's mind can begin to heal, promising Thude that there are hundreds more willing to bond singers. Kneeling before her mother, Venli sings the first song Jaxlim had ever taught her; afterwards, she reasons with Thude about their mutual need for allies. A chasmfiend shows itself, and Venli realizes that that was how the group escaped the chasms the night of the everstorm. Jaxlim suddenly recognizes Venli, and Venli hears a female voice in her mind finally accepting her second Ideal.

## Rowan's "If Asked" Notes

**Q: Why does Testament look better?**  
A: Adolin and Shallan think that Testament looks better since Adolin's talk with her. This suggests that Adolin's interaction with Testament (likely from earlier chapters) has helped her recover or improve. Testament is Shallan's first spren who became a deadeye, and she's showing signs of recovery.

**Q: What does Veil reintegrating mean?**  
A: Shallan tells Mraize that, instead of killing Kelek, Veil has reintegrated herself with Shallan. This means Veil is no longer a separate persona but has become part of Shallan, similar to how Veil integrated in Chapter 93. This is part of Shallan's healing and growth.

**Q: Who is Ba-Ado-Mishram?**  
A: Shallan tells Mraize that she will get to Ba-Ado-Mishram before the Ghostbloods can. Ba-Ado-Mishram is one of the Unmade who was captured and bound during the False Desolation. She's important because her binding affected all spren and singers, and freeing her might help restore connections. This is a major goal for Shallan.

**Q: Why do the listeners allow Venli in?**  
A: When Thude recognizes Venli, he at first runs away before he sees Venli draw in Stormlight. The other listeners look at her with suspicion and hostility, but they allow her into the camp to see her mother. This is because she's a Radiant now, which changes how they see her, and because she wants to see her mother.

**Q: What happens when Jaxlim recognizes Venli?**  
A: Venli sings the first song Jaxlim ever taught her, and Jaxlim suddenly recognizes Venli. This is a moment of clarity for Jaxlim, who has been suffering from memory loss. Venli hears a female voice in her mind finally accepting her second Ideal. This is Timbre accepting Venli's Second Ideal, showing Venli's growth and Jaxlim's recognition helping her progress.

**Q: How did the listeners escape?**  
A: A chasmfiend shows itself, and Venli realizes that was how the group escaped the chasms the night of the everstorm. The chasmfiend helped them escape, which is why they were able to flee before the war began.

**Q: Why is this chapter called "Testament"?**  
A: The title "Testament" refers to Shallan's first spren, who is showing signs of recovery. It also relates to the idea of testimony, witness, or proof—Testament is proof of Shallan's past, and her recovery is a testament to growth and healing. It's about facing the past and moving forward.

**Q: What does Shallan mean about other worlds?**  
A: Shallan thinks about the other worlds in the cosmere that Kelek told her about with longing. This refers to the wider universe beyond Roshar—other planets and worlds in the cosmere. Shallan is curious about these other worlds, which relates to her desire for knowledge and exploration.
