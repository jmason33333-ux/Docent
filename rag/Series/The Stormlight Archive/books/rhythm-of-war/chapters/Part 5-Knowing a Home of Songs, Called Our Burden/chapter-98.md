## Quick Summary
Dalinar walks through the battlefield on the Emuli front with Szeth and several allied generals. He analyzes the battle, remembering the Rift and thinking about Taravangian's advice to stop fighting Odium. After congratulating other groups of victors, the Mink comes to speak with him. They discuss the enemy's tactics before Dalinar again promises to free Herdaz first after they free Alethkar.

Back in the command tent, Sigzil brings Stargyle, Lyn, and Leyten to see Dalinar. Stargyle makes a Lightweaving that plays back a view of what he saw on their scouting trip to Tukar, showing Ishar and his soldiers. Dalinar states his intention to go visit the Herald, telling the Radiants to make one more scouting trip while he consults with Jasnah.

## Rowan's "If Asked" Notes

**Q: What is the Emuli front?**  
A: The Emuli front is where Dalinar's coalition forces are fighting. Dalinar walks through the battlefield with Szeth and several allied generals, analyzing the battle and congratulating victors. This is part of the ongoing war against Odium's forces.

**Q: What does Dalinar remember about the Rift?**  
A: Dalinar analyzes the battle, remembering the Rift. The Rift is where Dalinar killed Evi (his wife) by accident when he destroyed the city. This memory is significant because it represents one of Dalinar's greatest failures and sources of guilt.

**Q: What is Taravangian's advice?**  
A: Dalinar thinks about Taravangian's advice to stop fighting Odium. This is from their conversation in Chapter 66, where Taravangian told Dalinar that they're going to lose and that joining Odium is the only way to save people. Dalinar is considering this advice.

**Q: What does Stargyle show?**  
A: Stargyle makes a Lightweaving that plays back a view of what he saw on their scouting trip to Tukar, showing Ishar and his soldiers. This is a visual recording of what they found, allowing Dalinar to see what's happening in Tukar without going there himself.

**Q: Why does Dalinar want to visit Ishar?**  
A: Dalinar states his intention to go visit the Herald. Ishar is one of the Heralds who has gone mad, and he's in Tukar. Dalinar wants to confront him, possibly to learn from him or to try to get his help, as mentioned in Chapter 101.

**Q: What is the promise about Herdaz?**  
A: Dalinar again promises to free Herdaz first after they free Alethkar. This is a promise he's made to the Mink (Dieno), who is from Herdaz. It shows Dalinar's commitment to his allies and his word.

**Q: Why is this chapter called "An Unwholesome Shade"?**  
A: The title "An Unwholesome Shade" likely refers to the shadow or influence of Odium, or to the darkness of war and battle. It may also refer to the memory of the Rift or to the threat that Ishar represents. It's about the darkness and danger that surrounds them.
