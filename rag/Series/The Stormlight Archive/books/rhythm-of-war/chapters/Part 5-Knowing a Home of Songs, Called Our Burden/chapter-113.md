## Quick Summary
**Navani:** Navani leaves the pillar and walks back to Raboniel's body in the hallway. She discovers her still alive. Raboniel asks Navani to kill her with another dagger of anti-Voidlight, because her soul has been almost entirely burned away, and any more rebirths would make her insane. Navani does so, with a promise to end the war. She leaves a note on Raboniel's body to keep it from being moved. With the dagger and their notebook, Rhythm of War, Navani leaves to retake Urithiru.

**Taravangian:** Taravangian wakes up on a dumb day, the dumbest he's ever been. He begins to cry contemplating it. He leaves his bed only when his hunger gets to be too much, and finds a note and two gemstones containing corrupted spren from Renarin in his food basket. The note reads, "I'm sorry." Taravangian begins to eat, but hides in the corner as a highstorm approaches. Szeth breaks into his prison, scaring the guards away. He's there to ask about Taravangian's knowledge of his father and Ishar. As Taravangian pleads, Szeth switches subjects to the murders he committed under Taravangian's orders. After Szeth destroys the gemstones containing the corrupted spren, Taravangian attempts to run. He falls, and Szeth pins him to the wall. Odium approaches. Szeth resolves out loud to kill Taravangian according to his own choice, and pulls out a knife. Odium pulls Taravangian's mind into his realm to interrogate him about his recent failures, and Nightblood is pulled along. Szeth kills Taravangian's body as Taravangian kills Rayse with Nightblood and picks up the power, becoming Odium.

## Rowan's "If Asked" Notes

**Q: Why does Navani kill Raboniel?**  
A: Navani discovers Raboniel is still alive. Raboniel asks Navani to kill her with another dagger of anti-Voidlight, because her soul has been almost entirely burned away, and any more rebirths would make her insane. Navani does so, with a promise to end the war. This is an act of mercy—Raboniel wants to die permanently rather than go insane through endless rebirths.

**Q: What is the Rhythm of War notebook?**  
A: Navani leaves with the dagger and their notebook, Rhythm of War. This is the notebook that Navani and Raboniel worked on together, containing their research on anti-Lights and the science of Light. It's a record of their collaboration and discoveries.

**Q: How does Taravangian become Odium?**  
A: Odium pulls Taravangian's mind into his realm to interrogate him about his recent failures, and Nightblood is pulled along. Szeth kills Taravangian's body as Taravangian kills Rayse with Nightblood and picks up the power, becoming Odium. This is Taravangian's plan coming to fruition—he kills Rayse and takes up the Shard of Odium himself.

**Q: What does Renarin's note mean?**  
A: Taravangian finds a note and two gemstones containing corrupted spren from Renarin in his food basket. The note reads "I'm sorry." This suggests Renarin knew what was going to happen and was apologizing for his role in it, or for what Taravangian was about to become.

**Q: Why does Szeth kill Taravangian?**  
A: Szeth breaks into Taravangian's prison to ask about his knowledge of his father and Ishar. As Taravangian pleads, Szeth switches subjects to the murders he committed under Taravangian's orders. Szeth resolves out loud to kill Taravangian according to his own choice, and pulls out a knife. This is Szeth taking responsibility for his actions and choosing to kill Taravangian himself.

**Q: What are the corrupted spren for?**  
A: Taravangian finds two gemstones containing corrupted spren from Renarin. After Szeth destroys them, Taravangian attempts to run. These spren were likely meant to draw Odium's attention (as Sja-anat mentioned in Chapter 92), helping Taravangian's plan to get close to Odium.

**Q: Why is this chapter called "Emotion"?**  
A: The title "Emotion" refers to Taravangian being on an emotion day (the dumbest he's ever been), and to the emotional weight of the events—Raboniel's death, Taravangian becoming Odium, Szeth's choice. It's about the role of emotion in these critical moments.

**Q: What does this mean for the future?**  
A: Taravangian becoming Odium is a major shift. Taravangian's goal was to save the world, and he believed that taking up Odium would allow him to do that. However, the power of Odium may corrupt him, or he may be able to use it differently than Rayse did. This is a critical moment that changes everything.
