## Quick Summary
**Fourteen months ago**

Eshonai hits the ground of the chasm after her battle with Adolin during the Battle of Narak. After a moment of disorientation, she attempts to climb the chasm wall and manages to get halfway up, but then fails to find proper handholds to continue. Shortly afterwards, the highstorm arrives and the flood rips her off the wall. After being carried in the current and nearly drowning, she manages to pull herself onto a boulder and out of the water.

Realizing that she would die here, Eshonai begins to fight against the influence of Odium and the rhythms of power. The boulder shifts and she falls back into the water, but she summons her Shardblade and rams it into the chasm wall to use as a handhold. While drowning, she hears the pure tones of Honor and Odium and discovers the Rhythm of War. At that moment, Timbre appears to her. She loses her grip and, attuning the Rhythm of War, is carried away. Eshonai continues fighting death until the water slows and she comes to rest at the bottom of the chasm, heavily wounded. She attunes the rhythms of her childhood and closes her eyes to the Rhythm of Victory, only to find herself drifting in the Cognitive Realm.

The Stormfather reveals to her that she had died, but that he accepted her struggle underwater as swearing the First Ideal of the Knights Radiant, and so she would continue to live on as a Cognitive Shadow for a few more minutes. He assures Eshonai that she had not failed in life and then, as a final gift, takes her with him as the highstorm passes over Roshar, so that she could see the world as she had always dreamed. Then the Stormfather wishes Eshonai farewell and she passes into the Beyond.

## Rowan's "If Asked" Notes

**Q: How does Eshonai die?**  
A: Eshonai hits the ground of the chasm after her battle with Adolin. She tries to climb out but fails. The highstorm arrives and the flood rips her off the wall. After being carried in the current and nearly drowning, she pulls herself onto a boulder. The boulder shifts and she falls back into the water. She continues fighting death until the water slows and she comes to rest at the bottom of the chasm, heavily wounded. She dies there.

**Q: What is the Rhythm of War?**  
A: While drowning, Eshonai hears the pure tones of Honor and Odium and discovers the Rhythm of War. This is the harmony between Honor and Odium, representing unity and peace. Eshonai is the first to discover it, which is significant. At that moment, Timbre appears to her.

**Q: What does Timbre appearing mean?**  
A: At the moment Eshonai discovers the Rhythm of War, Timbre appears to her. Timbre is the spren that will later bond Venli. This suggests that Timbre was meant for Eshonai, but because Eshonai dies, Timbre bonds Venli instead. It's a connection between the sisters.

**Q: What is a Cognitive Shadow?**  
A: The Stormfather reveals that Eshonai had died, but he accepted her struggle underwater as swearing the First Ideal of the Knights Radiant, so she would continue to live on as a Cognitive Shadow for a few more minutes. A Cognitive Shadow is a person's soul/spirit that continues to exist in the Cognitive Realm after death, before passing into the Beyond.

**Q: What is the final gift?**  
A: The Stormfather, as a final gift, takes Eshonai with him as the highstorm passes over Roshar, so that she could see the world as she had always dreamed. Eshonai had always wanted to explore and see the world, and this is the Stormfather giving her that opportunity before she passes into the Beyond.

**Q: Why does the Stormfather do this?**  
A: The Stormfather assures Eshonai that she had not failed in life. He shows her kindness and compassion, giving her the gift of seeing the world she had always wanted to explore. This shows the Stormfather's capacity for mercy and his recognition of Eshonai's worth.

**Q: Why is this chapter called "One Final Gift"?**  
A: The title "One Final Gift" refers to the Stormfather's gift to Eshonai—taking her to see the world as the highstorm passes over Roshar. It's his final act of kindness before she passes into the Beyond. It's about giving someone one last beautiful experience before they go.

**Q: What does this reveal about Eshonai?**  
A: This chapter reveals that Eshonai fought against Odium's influence even in death, discovered the Rhythm of War, and was worthy of becoming a Radiant (swearing the First Ideal). It shows her strength, her desire to explore, and her worth. It's a beautiful and tragic end to her story.
