## Quick Summary
**Venli:** Leshwi kneels before Venli, overjoyed that the spren have returned to the singers. She asks after Riah, an old honorspren, but Timbre replies that she doesn't know her. Leshwi decides to join the battle going on around them, and her soldiers join her in fighting against the Pursuer's troops.

**Navani:** The Sibling shows Navani a vision of the fighting going on in the atrium, explaining that they hear the Rhythm of War coming from there. Navani sees Rlain there but, realizing he is too far away to come bond the Sibling, asks the spren to bond her instead. As Moash defeats Raboniel, the Sibling tells her that she is unworthy.

**Rlain:** Their guards escort Rlain and Dabbid to the atrium to find a battle raging. It ends quickly in favor of the side with the humans, and the two start a triage. Rlain relinquishes command of the triage to Hesina, then follows a group of men over to Teft's body. Rlain mourns Teft with Dabbid and asks after Lift. Then he notices the singers and the humans arguing, so he channels his best Teft and convinces both sides to trust him. He directs the group to gather the Radiants and make for the Oathgates with the hope of escaping to the Shattered Plains. As they start out, Venli confesses to him that she doesn't have a living Shardblade yet and can't operate the Oathgates.

## Rowan's "If Asked" Notes

**Q: Why does Leshwi kneel before Venli?**  
A: Leshwi kneels before Venli, overjoyed that the spren have returned to the singers. This is because Venli revealed she's a Radiant in Chapter 107, and Leshwi sees this as a sign that spren are willing to bond singers again. It's a moment of hope and joy for Leshwi.

**Q: Who is Riah?**  
A: Leshwi asks after Riah, an old honorspren, but Timbre replies that she doesn't know her. Riah was likely an honorspren that Leshwi knew in the past, possibly from before the Recreance. Leshwi is hoping to reconnect with old friends.

**Q: Why does the Sibling tell Navani she's unworthy?**  
A: Navani asks the Sibling to bond her instead of Rlain (since Rlain is too far away), but as Moash defeats Raboniel, the Sibling tells her that she is unworthy. This is likely because Navani has been working with Raboniel and may have been complicit in creating anti-Lights, or because the Sibling has specific requirements for who can bond them.

**Q: What does Rlain do?**  
A: Rlain notices the singers and humans arguing, so he channels his best Teft and convinces both sides to trust him. He directs the group to gather the Radiants and make for the Oathgates with the hope of escaping to the Shattered Plains. Rlain is stepping into a leadership role, using Teft's memory and methods to unite people.

**Q: Why can't Venli operate the Oathgates?**  
A: As they start out, Venli confesses to Rlain that she doesn't have a living Shardblade yet and can't operate the Oathgates. This is a problem because they need someone with a living Shardblade to operate the Oathgates to escape. Venli is a Radiant but hasn't progressed far enough to have a Shardblade yet.

**Q: What is the Rhythm of War?**  
A: The Sibling shows Navani a vision of the fighting in the atrium, explaining that they hear the Rhythm of War coming from there. The Rhythm of War is the harmony between Stormlight and Voidlight, representing unity and peace. It's coming from the atrium where humans and singers are fighting together.

**Q: Why is this chapter called "Emulsifier"?**  
A: The title "Emulsifier" refers to something that brings two things together that don't normally mix (like oil and water). In this chapter, Rlain acts as an emulsifier—he brings humans and singers together, uniting them despite their differences and conflicts. He's the one who makes them work together.

**Q: What does the epigraph mean?**  
A: The epigraph says "For ones so tarnished, they are somehow bright." This refers to the characters—they may be tarnished by their past actions, mistakes, or circumstances, but there's still brightness and hope in them. It relates to Rlain, Venli, and others who have been through difficult times but still have hope.
