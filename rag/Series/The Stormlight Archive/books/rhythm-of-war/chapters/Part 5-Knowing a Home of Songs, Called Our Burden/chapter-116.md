## Quick Summary
**Kaladin:** Kaladin leaves the gathering after Teft's funeral early to speak with Dalinar. Dalinar tries to say that he had been wrong to relieve Kaladin from command, but Kaladin cuts him off and explains that it does feel like the right thing for him. He asks for permission to continue pursuing the burgeoning field of mental health treatment and receives it. Dalinar tells Kaladin about the contest that is to happen in ten days and asks for him, as a surgeon, to travel to Shinovar and help Ishar so that Ishar can in turn help Dalinar to master his Bondsmith powers. Dalinar returns Wit's flute to Kaladin, explaining that Lift found it in a merchant's bin among other things salvaged from the Shattered Plains.

**Lezian:** Lezian wakes, angry, back in a body on Roshar after only one day on Braize. He realizes he is in a room with El and asks for the Voidlight in the sphere that El is holding so he can renew his pursuit of Kaladin. El agrees that the Light is for him, tricking him into walking over. In reality, the sphere is anti-Voidlight that has been set into the hilt of a knife; El stabs Lezian with it and permanently kills him.

**Kaladin:** Kaladin and Syl go to the Breakaway market to search through the merchant's bin where Lift had discovered his flute. After finding some items belonging to Rock and Sigzil, he finds a carved wooden horse that Tien had given him the day they were recruited into the army. Kaladin recounts to Syl how he had lost the horse in Alethkar when he first became a slave. He pays for the horse, then goes to the tavern to keep his promise to Teft and join in the group therapy session.

**Dalinar:** Dalinar and Navani are spending time alone at the top of the tower at the end of the day. After expressing that he should have known Navani could save the tower by herself, Dalinar brings up the contest and confesses to Navani that he is second-guessing the terms of his agreement. Navani reassures him before people arrive seeking her out to answer some matter about the tower. Dalinar lingers on the tower top, and the Stormfather admits that Dalinar had been right to show Kaladin mercy. Dalinar encourages him to change, and the Stormfather tells him that he has, in fact, shown mercy in the past.

## Rowan's "If Asked" Notes

**Q: What is Kaladin's new path?**  
A: Kaladin asks Dalinar for permission to continue pursuing the burgeoning field of mental health treatment and receives it. This is Kaladin's new focus—instead of being a soldier or commander, he wants to help people with mental health issues, which he's been doing throughout the occupation. This is a significant shift in his role.

**Q: Why does Kaladin go to Shinovar?**  
A: Dalinar tells Kaladin about the contest happening in ten days and asks for him, as a surgeon, to travel to Shinovar and help Ishar so that Ishar can in turn help Dalinar to master his Bondsmith powers. This is a mission to help Ishar recover his sanity, which will allow him to teach Dalinar.

**Q: How does Lezian die permanently?**  
A: Lezian wakes back in a body on Roshar after only one day on Braize. He asks El for Voidlight, but El tricks him—the sphere is actually anti-Voidlight set into a knife. El stabs Lezian with it and permanently kills him. This is the final death of the Pursuer, who had been a recurring enemy.

**Q: What is the significance of Tien's horse?**  
A: Kaladin finds a carved wooden horse that Tien gave him the day they were recruited into the army. Kaladin recounts to Syl how he lost the horse in Alethkar when he first became a slave. This is a powerful emotional moment—finding a piece of his past and his connection to Tien. The horse represents hope, memory, and connection.

**Q: Why does Dalinar second-guess the terms?**  
A: Dalinar brings up the contest and confesses to Navani that he is second-guessing the terms of his agreement. This shows Dalinar's doubt and concern about the battle of champions and what might happen if he loses. Navani reassures him, showing their support for each other.

**Q: What does the Stormfather admit?**  
A: The Stormfather admits that Dalinar had been right to show Kaladin mercy. Dalinar encourages him to change, and the Stormfather tells him that he has, in fact, shown mercy in the past. This shows the Stormfather's growth and his ability to change, which is significant for a spren who claims to be unchanging.

**Q: Why is this chapter called "Mercy"?**  
A: The title "Mercy" refers to multiple things: Dalinar showing Kaladin mercy by relieving him from command, the Stormfather showing mercy, and potentially El showing "mercy" by permanently killing Lezian (ending his suffering). It's about compassion and the choice to show mercy.

**Q: What does the epigraph mean?**  
A: The epigraph is from someone (likely a Fused or servant of Odium) saying they look forward to serving the newest Odium (Taravangian), who was recently one of "them" (humans). This shows that some are excited about Taravangian taking up the Shard, seeing him as more understanding than Rayse.
