## Quick Summary
**Kaladin:** Kaladin walks towards Lezian and invites him to fight. When Lezian reaches him, Kaladin activates Tomor's fabrial and sends him backwards into the window. After waiting for the wounded Fused to extricate himself, Kaladin yells at him to run, and he does. As the crowd chants his name, Kaladin turns towards the infirmary only to see Teft's body drop onto the floor in front of him.

**Moash:** Moash, after having dropped Teft in front of Kaladin, watches the now insensate Kaladin cradle the body and tells everyone to leave him be. He destroys the final node, then gives a bound Lirin into Lezian's hands with instructions to kill the surgeon should Kaladin somehow recover from Teft's loss. Before leaving again to go kill Navani, Moash explains that Kaladin can now only do one of two things: turn to Odium and give up his emotions or kill himself.

**Navani:** Navani hears a commotion when the final node is destroyed, leaving the crystal pillar clear to approach. She takes the dagger charged with anti-Voidlight and sneaks towards Raboniel, activating a painrial hidden among the boxes in the corridor on her way. Raboniel tells Navani she should flee, but Navani claims that she cannot abandon the Sibling. She gives Raboniel the dagger and convinces her to wait with her in Navani's room to see if Moash does indeed defeat Kaladin. Raboniel accepts but promptly walks into the painrial trap.

## Rowan's "If Asked" Notes

**Q: How does Kaladin defeat the Pursuer?**  
A: Kaladin walks towards Lezian and invites him to fight. When Lezian reaches him, Kaladin activates Tomor's fabrial and sends him backwards into the window. After waiting for the wounded Fused to extricate himself, Kaladin yells at him to run, and he does. This is a decisive victory—Kaladin defeats the Pursuer and forces him to flee.

**Q: What happens when Kaladin sees Teft's body?**  
A: As the crowd chants his name, Kaladin turns towards the infirmary only to see Teft's body drop onto the floor in front of him. Moash had dropped Teft's body there to break Kaladin. Kaladin becomes insensate, cradling the body, completely broken by the loss.

**Q: What does Moash do after dropping Teft?**  
A: Moash destroys the final node, then gives a bound Lirin into Lezian's hands with instructions to kill the surgeon should Kaladin somehow recover from Teft's loss. Before leaving to go kill Navani, Moash explains that Kaladin can now only do one of two things: turn to Odium and give up his emotions or kill himself.

**Q: What is the painrial?**  
A: Navani activates a painrial hidden among the boxes in the corridor on her way to Raboniel. A painrial is a fabrial that causes pain to those who enter its area. Navani uses it as a trap to catch Raboniel.

**Q: Why does Navani give Raboniel the dagger?**  
A: Navani gives Raboniel the dagger and convinces her to wait with her in Navani's room to see if Moash does indeed defeat Kaladin. This is a ruse—Navani is trying to get Raboniel into position for the trap. Raboniel accepts but promptly walks into the painrial trap.

**Q: What does destroying the final node mean?**  
A: Moash destroys the final node, leaving the crystal pillar clear to approach. The nodes protect the Sibling and the tower's defenses. With the final node destroyed, the Sibling is vulnerable and the tower's defenses are down.

**Q: Why is this chapter called "Children of Passions"?**  
A: The title "Children of Passions" likely refers to how humans, despite being "soft," are somehow strong. It's about the passion and emotion that drives humans, and how that makes them powerful even when they seem weak. It relates to Kaladin's emotional response to Teft's death.

**Q: What does the epigraph mean?**  
A: The epigraph says "For ones so soft, they are somehow strong." This refers to humans—they may seem soft or weak, but they have a strength that comes from their emotions, passions, and connections to others. It's about the paradox of human strength.
