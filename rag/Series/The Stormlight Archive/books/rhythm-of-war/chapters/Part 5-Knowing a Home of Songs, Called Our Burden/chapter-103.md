## Quick Summary
**Teft:** Teft and Lift covertly make their way to the Radiant infirmary while Kaladin faces off against the Pursuer. Teft distracts the remaining Regal guard so Lift can sneak into the infirmary, then tackles him into the room and subdues him. As Teft seals the door with a Full Lashing, and Lift moves to wake the Radiants, Moash uncovers himself and cuts through Lift's legs with his Honorblade.

**Kaladin:** The Pursuer streaks forward to confront Kaladin, and he grabs the Fused in a hold. As they wrestle on the ground, Kaladin begins undermining the Pursuer's confidence by taunting him. He uses Tomor's fabrial to slide himself out of Lezian's grasp and draws his scalpel.

**Teft:** Moash kicks Lift out of the way, then approaches Teft, who draws his knife. Moash feints to give himself room to spread out his sack of black sand before confronting Teft with his Honorblade.

## Rowan's "If Asked" Notes

**Q: What is Tomor's fabrial?**  
A: Kaladin uses Tomor's fabrial (the lifting glove from Chapter 63) to slide himself out of Lezian's grasp. This is the fabrial that Navani had Tomor create, which uses adhesion to pull the wearer in different directions. Kaladin uses it to escape the Pursuer's hold.

**Q: Why does Kaladin taunt the Pursuer?**  
A: As they wrestle on the ground, Kaladin begins undermining the Pursuer's confidence by taunting him. This is a psychological tactic—the Pursuer's power comes from his confidence and reputation, so undermining that confidence weakens him. Kaladin knows how to fight him mentally as well as physically.

**Q: What happens to Lift?**  
A: As Lift moves to wake the Radiants, Moash uncovers himself and cuts through Lift's legs with his Honorblade. This is a devastating injury that prevents Lift from helping. Moash then kicks her out of the way before confronting Teft.

**Q: What is the black sand?**  
A: Moash feints to give himself room to spread out his sack of black sand before confronting Teft. The purpose of this sand is not fully revealed, but it may be related to the sand that Raboniel gave Navani (from Taldain), or it may have some other function in Moash's plan.

**Q: Why does Teft draw his knife?**  
A: Moash approaches Teft, who draws his knife. This is a moment of confrontation between former friends—Teft and Moash were both in Bridge Four, but Moash betrayed them and became Vyre. This is a personal and emotional confrontation.

**Q: What is a Full Lashing?**  
A: Teft seals the door with a Full Lashing. A Full Lashing is a Windrunner ability that uses adhesion to stick objects together. Teft uses it to seal the door, preventing enemies from getting in while they try to wake the Radiants.

**Q: Why is this chapter called "The Legend You Live"?**  
A: The title "The Legend You Live" refers to Kaladin living up to his reputation and legend as he fights the Pursuer. It's about being the person others believe you to be, and about the weight of reputation and expectation. Kaladin is determined to live up to his legend one last time.

**Q: What does the epigraph mean?**  
A: The epigraph is about watching humans struggle and refuse to surrender, clinging to the rocks with the vigor of a Rosharan vine. This relates to the determination and resilience of humans, and how they fight even when things seem hopeless. It's about the refusal to give up.
