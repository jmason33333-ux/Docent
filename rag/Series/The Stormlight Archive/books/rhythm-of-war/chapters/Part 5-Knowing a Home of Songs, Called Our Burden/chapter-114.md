## Quick Summary
**Rlain:** Rlain, newly bonded to a corrupted mistspren, Tumi, walks with Venli and the listeners she recruited to the Oathgate to the Shattered Plains. Kaladin activates the fabrial and they greet Leshwi and four other Fused who had been transferred earlier. Kaladin offers them asylum if they need it, or if they change their minds. The group of Fused and listeners, aside from Rlain, leave to find the new listeners. Watching them leave, Rlain contemplates the possibility of humans doing again what they did to the Parshendi in the past. He resolves to stay close in order to watch. Rlain and Kaladin leave for Teft's funeral.

**Taravangian:** Taravangian starts adjusting to the power of Odium. He discovers that Rayse had been weakening for a long time, starting with his battles in the past, then being wounded by Honor, and his failures with Dalinar, Kaladin, and Urithiru. In the Physical Realm, Szeth finds Rayse's body in the prison, eaten away by Nightblood, and assumes it to be Taravangian. As Taravangian begins to struggle with his conflicting desires to both save the world and destroy it, he becomes aware of Cultivation approaching.

**Kaladin:** Teft's body is Soulcast to stone and placed in a room of ancient Radiants given the same treatment after death. All of Bridge Four attends the funeral, drinking and burning prayers to Teft. They go to a tavern afterwards. Kaladin and Syl remain at the statue as the members of Bridge Four drift away and discuss missing Teft and Syl's new feelings. Kaladin hugs the statue and thinks about moving forward and what he would do now.

**Taravangian:** Taravangian studies future possibilities as he grows more accustomed and capable with the power of Odium. He talks with Cultivation about her gamble in preparing him for this. Having knowledge of the cosmere at large from Rayse's experiences, Taravangian begins planning to himself to intervene on other worlds, and how to beat Dalinar. He realizes that there are other possibilities Rayse missed. Cultivation offers to teach him to use their powers. Taravangian accepts and resolves to save them all.

## Rowan's "If Asked" Notes

**Q: Why does Rlain stay to watch?**  
A: Watching the Fused and listeners leave, Rlain contemplates the possibility of humans doing again what they did to the Parshendi in the past. He resolves to stay close in order to watch. This shows Rlain's awareness of history and his determination to prevent past mistakes from being repeated. He's being watchful and protective.

**Q: What does Taravangian discover about Rayse?**  
A: Taravangian discovers that Rayse had been weakening for a long time, starting with his battles in the past, then being wounded by Honor, and his failures with Dalinar, Kaladin, and Urithiru. This explains why Taravangian was able to kill him—Rayse was already weakened and vulnerable.

**Q: What does Szeth find?**  
A: In the Physical Realm, Szeth finds Rayse's body in the prison, eaten away by Nightblood, and assumes it to be Taravangian. This is because Taravangian's body was killed at the same time, and Szeth doesn't realize that Rayse (Odium) was there. There's confusion about whose body it is.

**Q: What are Taravangian's plans?**  
A: Having knowledge of the cosmere at large from Rayse's experiences, Taravangian begins planning to himself to intervene on other worlds, and how to beat Dalinar. He realizes that there are other possibilities Rayse missed. Taravangian is already planning to expand his influence and to win the battle of champions.

**Q: What does Cultivation offer?**  
A: Cultivation offers to teach Taravangian to use their powers. Taravangian accepts and resolves to save them all. This is significant—Cultivation is helping the new Odium, which suggests she believes Taravangian can use the power better than Rayse did, or that this was part of her plan all along.

**Q: What happens at Teft's funeral?**  
A: Teft's body is Soulcast to stone and placed in a room of ancient Radiants given the same treatment after death. All of Bridge Four attends the funeral, drinking and burning prayers to Teft. They go to a tavern afterwards. Kaladin and Syl remain at the statue, discussing missing Teft and Syl's new feelings. Kaladin hugs the statue and thinks about moving forward.

**Q: Why is this chapter called "Broken Gods"?**  
A: The title "Broken Gods" refers to multiple things: Rayse being broken/weakened before his death, the Heralds being broken, and potentially Taravangian being broken by the power of Odium. It's about gods (Shards, Heralds) who are broken, damaged, or flawed. It may also refer to the idea that even gods can be broken.

**Q: What does the epigraph mean?**  
A: The epigraph is from Taravangian's intelligence chapters, suggesting it's from his perspective as he adjusts to being Odium. It likely relates to his plans and his understanding of the situation now that he has the power.

**Q: What does this mean for the future?**  
A: Taravangian becoming Odium and resolving to "save them all" is a major development. His goal was always to save the world, and now he has the power of a Shard. However, the power of Odium may corrupt him, or he may be able to use it in ways Rayse couldn't. This sets up major questions for the future of the series.
