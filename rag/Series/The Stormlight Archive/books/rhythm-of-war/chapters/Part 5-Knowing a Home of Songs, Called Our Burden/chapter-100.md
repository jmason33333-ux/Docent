## Quick Summary
Kaladin awakes from his nightmares, disoriented for a bit before remembering that he had tied his own hands to quell his violent reaction from being awoken. The Sibling speaks to him, telling him the enemy has found the final node and are soon going to kill the Radiants before requesting that Kaladin send Rlain down to them. Kaladin wakes Teft, informing him of the situation, and they get dressed in Bridge Four uniforms. Dabbid arrives, and they decide to send him with Rlain down to Navani. After Dabbid leaves, Kaladin tells Teft to run if things go wrong. After pointing out that the Pursuer will be waiting for him, Teft gets Kaladin to promise that he will run too and abandon the tower to the enemy so long as they can escape with as many Radiants as they can.

## Rowan's "If Asked" Notes

**Q: Why does Kaladin tie his hands?**  
A: Kaladin awakes from his nightmares, disoriented for a bit before remembering that he had tied his own hands to quell his violent reaction from being awoken. This shows how severe his trauma and PTSD are—he has violent reactions even when being woken, so he ties his hands to prevent himself from hurting anyone.

**Q: What is the final node?**  
A: The Sibling tells Kaladin that the enemy has found the final node and are soon going to kill the Radiants. The nodes are what protect the Sibling and keep the tower's defenses active. If the final node is destroyed, the Sibling will die, and the Radiants will be killed.

**Q: Why does the Sibling want Rlain?**  
A: The Sibling requests that Kaladin send Rlain down to them. This is likely because Rlain can communicate with the Sibling through the garnet veins in the walls, and they need his help to protect the final node or to do something important.

**Q: What is the promise Kaladin makes?**  
A: After pointing out that the Pursuer will be waiting for him, Teft gets Kaladin to promise that he will run too and abandon the tower to the enemy so long as they can escape with as many Radiants as they can. This is important because Kaladin has a tendency to fight to the death, but Teft wants him to prioritize survival and saving others.

**Q: Why do they get dressed in Bridge Four uniforms?**  
A: Kaladin and Teft get dressed in Bridge Four uniforms. This is a symbol of their identity, unity, and purpose. It shows who they are and what they stand for, and it may help them feel more prepared and confident for what's coming.

**Q: Why send Dabbid with Rlain?**  
A: They decide to send Dabbid with Rlain down to Navani. Dabbid can help Rlain, and he's been helping throughout the occupation. This ensures Rlain has support and that they can communicate with Navani and the Sibling.

**Q: What does the epigraph mean?**  
A: The epigraph is from a singer's perspective, saying that humans are weapons and that singers revere Passion, so why would they throw away such an excellent channeling of it. This relates to how singers view humans—as tools or weapons to be used, which is part of the conflict between them.

**Q: Why is this chapter called "Watchers at the Rim"?**  
A: The title "Watchers at the Rim" likely refers to Kaladin and Teft watching and waiting for the final confrontation, or to the people watching from the edges as the situation unfolds. It's about being on the edge, watching and waiting for what's coming.
