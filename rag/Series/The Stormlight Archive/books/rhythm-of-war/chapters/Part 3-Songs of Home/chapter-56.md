## Quick Summary
Venli has put her group in charge of taking care of the unconscious Radiants. In the infirmary with them, she notices that the Windrunners are closer to waking than the other Radiants. They're concerned about their condition, so Venli decides to bring Lirin there to take care of their health. Then Venli goes to translate for a team of Fused trying to come up with a way to find the nodes. Venli learns that their normal methods of detecting powers do not work because of the tower itself, prompting a sense of excitement that she and Timbre might finally be able to practice their powers. The Fused ask Venli where she thinks the nodes would be, and she inadvertently gives them a clue as to where one might be.

## Rowan's "If Asked" Notes

**Q: Why are Windrunners closer to waking?**  
A: Venli notices that the Windrunners are closer to waking than the other Radiants. This may be because Windrunners have a stronger connection to Honor and Adhesion, or because of some other factor related to their order. This could be significant if they recover faster.

**Q: Why does Venli bring Lirin?**  
A: Venli decides to bring Lirin to take care of the Radiants' health. Lirin is a surgeon, and Venli's group is concerned about the Radiants' condition. This shows Venli's compassion and her willingness to help, even for enemies.

**Q: Why don't the Fused's detection methods work?**  
A: Venli learns that the Fused's normal methods of detecting powers don't work because of the tower itself. This suggests that Urithiru has some property that blocks or interferes with their detection abilities, which is why they're having trouble finding the nodes.

**Q: What clue does Venli give?**  
A: The Fused ask Venli where she thinks the nodes would be, and she inadvertently gives them a clue to where one might be. This is a mistake on Venli's part—she's trying to help find the nodes (which would help the enemy), but she doesn't realize the full implications.

**Q: Why is Venli excited about practicing powers?**  
A: Venli feels excitement that she and Timbre might finally be able to practice their powers. This is because the tower blocks the Fused's detection methods, which means Venli might be able to use her Radiant powers without being detected. This is significant because she's been hiding her bond with Timbre.

**Q: What are the nodes?**  
A: The nodes are three points connected to the Sibling's heart that need to be protected from corruption. If the Fused find and corrupt them, they could gain control over the tower. This is why finding them is so important to both sides.

**Q: Why is Venli translating for the Fused?**  
A: Venli is translating for a team of Fused trying to find the nodes. This is part of her role in the occupation, but it puts her in a difficult position—she's helping the enemy while also trying to help the Radiants and potentially work against the occupation.
