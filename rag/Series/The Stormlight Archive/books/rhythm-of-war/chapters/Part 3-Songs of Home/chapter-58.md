## Quick Summary
Kaladin breaks into one of the laundry rooms in the tower by accessing a balcony, and Syl goes to see where they can find spanreeds. She finds a spanreed hub and leads Kaladin there, pretending to be a voidspren to draw guards away. Kaladin finds a ventilation shaft that he can use to get above the room while avoiding detection. He does, and then uses adhesion to pull up a group of spanreeds. Kaladin returns to the hall and finds the Sibling blinking a light at him. They tell him that the occupying force has found another node and that they need him to save them.

**Q: What is the ventilation shaft?**  
A: Kaladin finds a ventilation shaft that he can use to get above the spanreed room while avoiding detection. This allows him to observe and access the spanreeds without being seen by guards.

**Q: How does Kaladin get the spanreeds?**  
A: Kaladin uses adhesion to pull up a group of spanreeds from the room below. This shows his creative use of his powers and his ability to manipulate objects from a distance.

**Q: What does the Sibling tell Kaladin?**  
A: The Sibling blinks a light at Kaladin and tells him that the occupying force has found another node and that they need him to save them. This is urgent—if the Fused corrupt the node, they could gain control over the tower.

**Q: Why is finding another node urgent?**  
A: The nodes are connected to the Sibling's heart, and if they're corrupted, the Fused could gain control over the tower. The Sibling is desperate for Kaladin to protect the node before it's too late.

**Q: How did the Fused find the node?**  
A: From Chapter 56, Venli inadvertently gave the Fused a clue to where a node might be. This is likely how they found it, showing the consequences of Venli's mistake.

**Q: What will Kaladin do?**  
A: The Sibling needs Kaladin to save the node, which means he'll need to get to it before the Fused can corrupt it. This sets up his next action, which will likely involve a dangerous mission to protect the node.
