## Quick Summary
Dalinar is in the command tent with other leaders planning their next offensive. They have defeated Taravangian's troops and have him in prison, but Dalinar still suspects this is only one part of his plan. Reports from the tower indicate Radiant powers were knocked out by a device Taravangian had, similar to one Kaladin found. The meeting ends, and Dalinar is joined by Jasnah. She questions his decision not to be more active in battle, and he explains he wants to focus on learning about his Bondsmith powers and leave battle to the Mink. They discuss pushing Odium to an accord. Wit joins and says Odium won't proceed with a contest of champions unless he's afraid, and Dalinar expresses concern they're being manipulated. Dalinar expresses frustration at working with leaders whose government includes so much bureaucracy, and Jasnah says she would like to be the end of the Alethi monarchy, which Dalinar doesn't like.

Jasnah and Wit join the Mink at the war table, where Jasnah begins making suggestions regarding battle plans. Lyn brings news that scouts saw what they thought were Alethi soldiers on the tower. Dalinar asks them to send scouts at night to watch from a distance. Dalinar turns attention to the leaders' conversation and can tell Highprince Ruthar is getting angry at Jasnah's intrusion. He realizes Jasnah is doing this intentionally to get a reaction, and it works. Ruthar loudly criticizes what Jasnah is doing, and she calls to Wit. He begins to insult Ruthar mercilessly, then reveals Ruthar beats his children. Ruthar challenges Wit to combat; when Wit chooses Jasnah as his champion, Ruthar refuses to fight, so Jasnah stabs him in the throat. Jasnah calls for Renarin, who heals him, then she names Ruthar forfeit of his title. Dalinar expresses disappointment at how Jasnah handled the situation, and Jasnah announces she did it in part to justify eliminating the barbaric practice of trial by sword. Jasnah leaves, and Renarin tells Dalinar he has had an episode. Dalinar says he will speak to him about it soon.

## Rowan's "If Asked" Notes

**Q: Why does Dalinar want to focus on Bondsmith powers instead of battle?**  
A: Dalinar explains that he wants to learn more about his Bondsmith powers and leave the battle to the Mink, who is a skilled general. This suggests Dalinar believes his Bondsmith abilities may be more important for the larger conflict with Odium than direct battlefield leadership.

**Q: What is the device that knocked out Radiant powers?**  
A: Reports indicate that Taravangian had a device similar to one Kaladin found earlier that knocked out Radiant powers at the tower. This is part of Taravangian's plan to isolate Dalinar and his forces during the betrayal.

**Q: Why does Jasnah stab Ruthar?**  
A: Jasnah intentionally provokes Ruthar, then when he challenges Wit to combat and refuses to fight Jasnah (Wit's chosen champion), she stabs him. She announces she did this in part to justify eliminating the "barbaric practice" of trial by sword, showing her commitment to reform.

**Q: What does Jasnah mean about ending the Alethi monarchy?**  
A: Jasnah tells Dalinar that she would like to be "the end of the Alethi monarchy." This suggests she wants to transition Alethkar from a monarchy to a different form of government, which Dalinar doesn't like. This is part of her broader reform agenda.

**Q: Why are there Alethi soldiers on the tower?**  
A: Scouts report seeing what they thought were Alethi soldiers on the tower. This is suspicious because the tower is under occupation by Odium's forces. Dalinar asks them to send scouts at night to watch from a distance, suggesting he wants to investigate this further.

**Q: What does Wit mean about Odium needing to be afraid?**  
A: Wit says that Odium won't proceed with a contest of champions unless he's afraid. This suggests that to force Odium into the contest, they need to make him feel threatened or uncertain. Dalinar expresses concern that they're being manipulated, suggesting there may be more complexity to this situation.

**Q: What is Renarin's episode?**  
A: Renarin tells Dalinar that he has had an episode. This may be related to his visions, his unique bond with Glys (a corrupted spren), or his ability to see the future in ways that interfere with Odium's sight. Dalinar says he will speak to him about it soon, suggesting it's significant.

**Q: Why does Jasnah eliminate trial by sword?**  
A: Jasnah sees trial by sword as a barbaric practice. By stabbing Ruthar (who refused to fight her as Wit's champion), she creates a situation that allows her to eliminate this practice. This is part of her broader agenda to reform Alethi government and society.
