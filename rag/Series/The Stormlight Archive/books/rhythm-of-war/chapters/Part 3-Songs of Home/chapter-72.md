## Rowan's "If Asked" Notes

**Q: How long has Raboniel been listening?**  
A: Raboniel admits that she has been listening in on Navani's conversations with the Sibling from the beginning. This means that everything Navani thought was secret communication has actually been known to the enemy, which is why Navani is angry and humiliated.

**Q: Why didn't Raboniel believe the Sibling was dead?**  
A: Raboniel says she didn't actually believe the Sibling to be dead, despite what the Midnight Mother (Re-Shephir) told the Fused. This shows Raboniel's intelligence and suspicion, and explains why she's been spying on Navani's communications.

**Q: What does this mean for Navani?**  
A: Navani has been compromised from the start. Everything she's discussed with the Sibling, all her plans, and all the information she's shared have been known to Raboniel. This is a major setback and explains why the Fused have been able to counter their plans.

**Q: What happened to Kaladin?**  
A: Navani asks after Kaladin and learns that he ran out into the storm and likely didn't survive. This is devastating news, as Kaladin was one of the few active Radiants and was crucial to their resistance efforts. However, "likely didn't survive" leaves some room for hope.

**Q: Why is Navani angry and humiliated?**  
A: Navani is angry and humiliated because she's just realized that Raboniel has been listening to all her conversations with the Sibling from the beginning. She's been operating under the assumption that her communications were secret, but they've been compromised the entire time.

**Q: What does the epigraph mean about Intent?**  
A: The epigraph states that "Intent matters. Intent is king. You cannot do what I attempt by accident. You must mean it." This suggests that Intent is a fundamental law of the universe, more important than previously understood. This may relate to Navani's research about Light and how it works.

**Q: What can Navani do now?**  
A: Navani's position is severely compromised. She knows that Raboniel has been listening to everything, so she can't communicate secretly with the Sibling anymore. She'll need to find new ways to operate or accept that she's working under constant surveillance.

**Q: Is Kaladin really dead?**  
A: Raboniel says Kaladin "likely didn't survive" the highstorm, which leaves some room for doubt. However, jumping into a highstorm is extremely dangerous, and the fact that he hasn't been seen suggests he may have died. This would be a major loss for the resistance.

**Q: Why is this chapter called "Outmatched"?**  
A: The title "Outmatched" reflects Navani's position—she's been outmaneuvered by Raboniel from the beginning. Everything she thought was secret has been known to the enemy, and she's been operating at a severe disadvantage. The chapter shows how thoroughly she's been outmatched.
