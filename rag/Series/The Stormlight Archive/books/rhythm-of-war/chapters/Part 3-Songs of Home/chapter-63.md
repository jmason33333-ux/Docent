## Quick Summary
Kaladin wakes from nightmares. Syl tells Kaladin that she asked Dalinar to help her understand Kaladin better, and he seems to have Connected her to her past somehow. She now remembers her old knight who died, and she feels grief for him. She says that she thinks that because spren can choose, they can change, even though they say they can't. Kaladin and Syl agree that they will talk with each other when they feel like they can't change their sadness. Dabbid enters, bringing Kaladin the glove that Tomor had left for him. Kaladin goes to a crystal vein to talk with Navani at their appointed time. She tells him what the glove does, how to get to the weights in the shaft, and how to replace the rubies with those filled with Voidlight. Kaladin and Syl go to the shaft and replace the gemstones there. After Navani talks Kaladin through using the fabrial, he and Syl go to an empty hall and practice using it.

## Rowan's "If Asked" Notes

**Q: How did Dalinar Connect Syl to her past?**  
A: Syl tells Kaladin that she asked Dalinar to help her understand Kaladin better, and he seems to have Connected her to her past somehow. This is likely using his Bondsmith powers to forge a Connection, allowing Syl to remember Relador and process her grief. This shows Dalinar's growing understanding of his abilities.

**Q: Why is Syl's realization about change important?**  
A: Syl says that because spren can choose, they can change, even though they say they can't. This is a significant realization because it suggests that spren are not as fixed as they believe, and that they can grow and change, just like humans. This may be important for Syl's character development.

**Q: What is the glove?**  
A: The glove is a fabrial that Tomor created. Navani tells Kaladin what it does, how to get to the weights in the shaft, and how to replace the rubies with those filled with Voidlight. It seems to help with movement, possibly compensating for Kaladin's inability to fly.

**Q: Why use Voidlight instead of Stormlight?**  
A: Kaladin replaces the rubies (which likely contain Stormlight) with ones filled with Voidlight. This may be because Voidlight is more available in the occupied tower, or because the glove works better with Voidlight, or because it's part of Navani's research into mixing different types of Light.

**Q: What does Kaladin practice?**  
A: After Navani talks Kaladin through using the fabrial, he and Syl go to an empty hall and practice using it. This suggests the glove requires skill to use, and Kaladin needs to learn how to operate it effectively.

**Q: Why do Kaladin and Syl agree to talk about sadness?**  
A: Kaladin and Syl agree that they will talk with each other when they feel like they can't change their sadness. This is a healthy approach to dealing with grief and depression, showing that communication and support are important for both of them.

**Q: Who is Relador?**  
A: Relador was Syl's old knight—a previous Windrunner she was bonded to before Kaladin. Syl now remembers him and feels grief for his death. This is the first time she's really processing this loss, thanks to Dalinar's Connection.

**Q: What are the nightmares about?**  
A: Kaladin wakes from nightmares. These are likely related to his trauma, his guilt about those he couldn't save, or his experiences in battle. The nightmares are part of his ongoing struggle with depression and PTSD.

**Q: How does the glove help Kaladin?**  
A: Since Kaladin can no longer fly (from Chapter 61), the glove may help him move around the tower, climb, or navigate in other ways. It's an adaptation that allows him to continue operating despite his limitations.
