## Quick Summary
**Eight years ago**

Eshonai and her mother go out into the storm so Jaxlim can try to take on warform. Instead of entering Jaxlim's gemheart, the captured painspren enters Eshonai, who had only accompanied her mother to hold a shield for her against the storm. Eshonai transforms and discovers that there were about a dozen other listeners who did so as well. As they revel in their new powerful bodies, they hear battle drums, return to their city, and find another family about to attack. Eshonai, after retrieving the family's Shardbearer, confronts the attacking family. She tells them that they have found warform and that because they cannot win against them, they should join with them to present a unified nation to the humans. They return to the city to show their people the new forms and to train.

## Rowan's "If Asked" Notes

**Q: Why does the painspren go to Eshonai instead of Jaxlim?**  
A: Eshonai and her mother go out into the storm so Jaxlim can try to take warform, but instead of entering Jaxlim's gemheart, the captured painspren enters Eshonai. Eshonai had only accompanied her mother to hold a shield, but the painspren chose her instead. This is unexpected but leads to Eshonai taking warform.

**Q: How many listeners take warform?**  
A: Eshonai discovers that about a dozen other listeners took warform as well. This suggests that multiple listeners were trying to take the form at the same time, possibly coordinated by Venli or the Five.

**Q: Why does another family attack?**  
A: As the newly transformed listeners revel in their new powerful bodies, they hear battle drums and return to their city to find another family about to attack. This shows the division among listener families and the conflict that exists between them.

**Q: How does Eshonai unify the families?**  
A: Eshonai retrieves the attacking family's Shardbearer and confronts them. She tells them that they have found warform and that because they cannot win against them, they should join with them to present a unified nation to the humans. This shows Eshonai's leadership and her goal of unity.

**Q: What is Eshonai's goal?**  
A: Eshonai wants to present a unified nation to the humans. She believes that if the listeners are unified and strong (with warform), they can negotiate with the humans from a position of strength rather than weakness.

**Q: Why do they train?**  
A: After unifying the families, they return to the city to show their people the new forms and to train. This is preparation for future interactions with humans and potential conflicts, ensuring they're ready to use their new power effectively.

**Q: What is a Shardbearer?**  
A: A Shardbearer is someone who has a Shardblade. Eshonai retrieves the attacking family's Shardbearer, which gives her leverage in the confrontation and shows her strength.

**Q: Why is this chapter called "One Family"?**  
A: The title "One Family" refers to Eshonai's goal of unifying all the listener families into one unified nation. She wants them to work together rather than fight each other, especially when facing the humans.
