## Quick Summary
**Kaladin:** Kaladin wakes from another nightmare to Syl telling him that they've received news that the Fused are corrupting another node. They couldn't wake him, so Dabbid has already left. Kaladin catches up to him, and Dabbid hands over his spear and says "Life. Before. Death." Kaladin takes the spear and goes.

**Navani:** Navani tries to stall Raboniel, but the Fused she goes to seems to be expecting this. Navani believes it's a deliberate distraction, and she has a terrible realization that they have been able to hear her communications with the Sibling.

**Kaladin:** Kaladin uses the glove and makes his way to the Breakaway, trying to act as though he is using his normal powers. When he arrives, he finds the Pursuer, and they begin to fight. The Heavenly Ones arrive, and Kaladin deals enough damage to the Pursuer to force him to leave and recover. Kaladin goes to Leshwi and motions for a duel, and she accepts. They fight, though Leshwi questions Kaladin as to why he cannot use his powers like normal. She realizes that the fight is a sham, and they argue about whose people should rule. Kaladin leaves abruptly, follows Syl to the well, and dives in. He finds Raboniel and destroys the sapphire before she can finish corrupting it. He starts to make his way out but gets trapped inside with Syl on the outside of the well. He follows the pipes and eventually comes out at a reservoir in the tower. Exiting the reservoir, Kaladin climbs up to the ground floor of the tower, where he encounters guards. To evade them, he jumps out of the tower and into the highstorm.

## Rowan's "If Asked" Notes

**Q: What does Dabbid mean by "Life. Before. Death."?**  
A: "Life. Before. Death." is the First Ideal of the Knights Radiant. Dabbid says this to Kaladin when he hands over his spear, which is significant because Dabbid has been helping Kaladin and may be expressing his support or understanding of what Kaladin is doing.

**Q: Why does Kaladin fight Leshwi?**  
A: Kaladin goes to Leshwi and motions for a duel, and she accepts. However, Leshwi questions why Kaladin cannot use his powers like normal and realizes the fight is a sham. They argue about whose people should rule, showing their philosophical differences despite mutual respect.

**Q: Why does Kaladin destroy the sapphire?**  
A: Kaladin finds Raboniel corrupting the node (a sapphire) and destroys it before she can finish. This is the same desperate measure he used before—destroying the node to prevent corruption, even though it means the Sibling loses that node.

**Q: How does Kaladin escape the well?**  
A: Kaladin gets trapped inside the well with Syl on the outside. He follows the pipes and eventually comes out at a reservoir in the tower. This shows his resourcefulness and the tower's complex infrastructure.

**Q: Why does Kaladin jump into the highstorm?**  
A: After exiting the reservoir and climbing to the ground floor, Kaladin encounters guards. To evade them, he jumps out of the tower and into the highstorm. This is a desperate move, as highstorms are extremely dangerous, but it's his only way to escape.

**Q: What is Navani's terrible realization?**  
A: Navani tries to stall Raboniel, but the Fused seems to be expecting this. Navani realizes that they have been able to hear her communications with the Sibling. This is a major problem because it means the enemy knows their plans and can counter them.

**Q: Why is this a problem?**  
A: If the Fused can hear Navani's communications with the Sibling, they know about the nodes, Kaladin's activities, and other sensitive information. This compromises their entire operation and makes it much harder to protect the tower.

**Q: What happens to Kaladin in the highstorm?**  
A: Kaladin jumps into the highstorm to escape the guards. Highstorms are extremely dangerous, with powerful winds, lightning, and Stormlight. What happens to him there is not revealed in this chapter, but it's a cliffhanger that sets up the next chapter.

**Q: Why does Leshwi respect Kaladin?**  
A: Leshwi has shown respect for Kaladin before (protecting his family in Chapter 53). In this chapter, she accepts his duel and fights him, but she realizes it's a sham and they argue about philosophy. Despite being enemies, they have mutual respect.

**Q: What are the Heavenly Ones?**  
A: The Heavenly Ones are a type of Fused who can fly. They arrive during Kaladin's fight with the Pursuer, adding to the danger. Kaladin deals enough damage to the Pursuer to force him to leave and recover, showing his skill even without full powers.
