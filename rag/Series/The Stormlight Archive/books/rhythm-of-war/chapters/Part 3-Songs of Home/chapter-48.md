## Quick Summary
**Nine years ago**

Eshonai and the humans try to find ways to communicate with each other, and as they do so, they realize all the ways that they differ. Eshonai marvels that humans are in all forms at all times, as well as that they have singers in dullform that do not speak. She finds the humans so much more advanced than the singers and spends as much time as possible learning from them. The humans ask to see the Plains, and Eshonai escorts them. During the trip, Axindweth asks Eshonai questions about the spren that she can see and whether any have talked to her, which Eshonai thinks is a myth. Gavilar and Eshonai talk about the greatshells, and they misinterpret her and assume that the singers worship them. The party decides to take a break, and as she helps them set up a tent, Axindweth asks if she can see Eshonai's knife. She and Gavilar talk about it and think it must have come from a time around the false desolation. They are interrupted by the sounds of a chasmfiend emerging, and soon after, the group leaves. Eshonai only reveals to Gavilar that the knife was found in the ruins of a city her ancestors built, but Gavilar gets very excited, especially when he realizes that the singers have stories of ancient days that include the Radiants. The humans leave them with a gift of weapons and ask to be able to hear the singer songs the next time they meet.

## Rowan's "If Asked" Notes

**Q: What does Eshonai mean about humans being in all forms?**  
A: Listeners can take different forms (workform, warform, etc.) by bonding different spren. Eshonai observes that humans seem to be in all forms at all times—they don't change forms like listeners do. This is a fundamental difference between the two species.

**Q: What are dullform singers?**  
A: Dullform is a listener form where they don't speak. Eshonai notices that humans have singers in dullform that do not speak. This is likely referring to parshmen—listeners who were in a form that made them docile and unable to speak, used as slaves by humans.

**Q: Why is Axindweth asking about spren?**  
A: Axindweth asks Eshonai about the spren she can see and whether any have talked to her. Eshonai thinks this is a myth. Axindweth may be testing whether Eshonai could become a Radiant, or she may have her own agenda related to spren and bonds.

**Q: What is the false desolation?**  
A: The false desolation was a period in Rosharan history. Axindweth and Gavilar think Eshonai's knife must have come from around that time. The exact details may become clearer later, but it's a significant historical period.

**Q: Why is Gavilar excited about listener stories?**  
A: Gavilar gets very excited when he realizes that the listeners have stories of ancient days that include the Radiants. This suggests that listener oral history contains information about the Radiants that humans may have lost, making it valuable for understanding the past.

**Q: What is the significance of the knife?**  
A: Eshonai's knife was found in the ruins of a city her ancestors built. Gavilar and Axindweth think it's from around the false desolation. The knife may be an artifact from ancient times, and its origin suggests listeners had advanced civilizations in the past.

**Q: Why do the humans want to hear the singer songs?**  
A: The humans ask to be able to hear the singer songs the next time they meet. The songs contain listener history and knowledge, so the humans may be interested in learning from them. However, this request may have consequences, as the songs are sacred to the listeners.

**Q: Who is Axindweth?**  
A: Axindweth is a member of Gavilar's party. She asks specific questions about spren and examines Eshonai's knife. Her interest in these things may suggest she has knowledge or motivations beyond simple exploration.
