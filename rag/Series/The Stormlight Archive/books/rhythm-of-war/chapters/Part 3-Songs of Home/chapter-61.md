## Quick Summary
Navani speaks with Kaladin, thanking him for helping to protect the tower. She reassures him that he did not fail, even though he had to break the node. He tells her that he can no longer fly, but was able to get some of the Fused spanreeds, which Syl has determined work via corrupted spren. Navani goes to check on her scholars, and thinking through her problems, realizes that the lifting glove that Tomor created might be helpful for Kaladin. She arranges for Tomor to leave it in a place where Kaladin can retrieve it. Raboniel then has Navani accompany her to watch some singers testing the shield around the crystal pillar by hacking at it with some Shardblades. Raboniel asks Navani to help her understand the shield, and Navani refuses, informing her that one of the blades had been Elhokar's, something which Raboniel had not known.

Raboniel takes Navani to her former office, which Raboniel has claimed. Inside, Raboniel shows Navani a fabrial that demonstrates how the crystal pillar room is being protected. Navani is distracted by a diamond sphere filled with Towerlight, which is a combination of Stormlight and Lifelight. They discuss ways of mixing different types of Light. Raboniel believes that Voidlight and Stormlight are opposites and can't be mixed, but Navani thinks they can with the right kind of emulsifier. Raboniel leaves Navani, and Navani finds a vein of crystal and speaks with the Sibling, who is convinced they will die soon. Navani tries to convince the Sibling to tell her where the nodes are so that they can protect them, but they refuse. Navani begins her research into the science of light.

## Rowan's "If Asked" Notes

**Q: What is Towerlight?**  
A: Towerlight is a combination of Stormlight and Lifelight. Navani sees it in a diamond sphere in Raboniel's office. This is significant because it shows that different types of Light can be combined, which may be important for Navani's research.

**Q: Can Voidlight and Stormlight be mixed?**  
A: Raboniel believes that Voidlight and Stormlight are opposites and can't be mixed, but Navani thinks they can with the right kind of emulsifier. This is a key scientific question that Navani begins researching, and it may have important implications.

**Q: Why can't Kaladin fly?**  
A: Kaladin tells Navani that he can no longer fly. This may be because he's low on Stormlight, exhausted, or because the suppression field is still affecting his powers. The lifting glove that Navani arranges for him may help compensate for this loss.

**Q: What are corrupted spren spanreeds?**  
A: Kaladin got some Fused spanreeds, and Syl determined they work via corrupted spren. This suggests that the Fused have created their own version of spanreeds using corrupted spren, similar to how normal spanreeds work with normal spren.

**Q: Why does the Sibling refuse to tell Navani where the nodes are?**  
A: The Sibling is convinced they will die soon and refuses to tell Navani where the nodes are so they can protect them. This may be because the Sibling has given up hope, or because they believe telling Navani would put her in danger, or because they have other reasons for keeping the location secret.

**Q: What is the lifting glove?**  
A: The lifting glove is a fabrial that Tomor created. Navani realizes it might be helpful for Kaladin, especially since he can no longer fly. She arranges for Tomor to leave it in a place where Kaladin can retrieve it, which may help him move around the tower.

**Q: Why is Navani upset about Elhokar's blade?**  
A: Navani refuses to help Raboniel understand the shield and informs her that one of the Shardblades being used to test it had been Elhokar's. This is emotional for Navani because Elhokar was her son, and seeing his blade used by the enemy is painful.

**Q: What is Navani researching?**  
A: Navani begins her research into the science of light. She's particularly interested in whether Voidlight and Stormlight can be mixed with an emulsifier. This research may lead to important discoveries that could help in the fight against Odium.

**Q: What is an emulsifier?**  
A: An emulsifier is something that allows two substances that normally don't mix (like oil and water) to combine. Navani thinks that with the right emulsifier, Voidlight and Stormlight might be able to mix, despite Raboniel's belief that they're opposites.
