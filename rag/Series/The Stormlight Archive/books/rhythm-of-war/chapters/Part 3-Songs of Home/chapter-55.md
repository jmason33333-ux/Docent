## Quick Summary
Kaladin is feeding Teft, and Syl talks about how she feels when she thinks about her old knight, Relador. Kaladin realizes that she is processing Relador's death for the first time, and they talk about how they learn to deal with depressing thoughts. Suddenly, Dabbid appears with broth, having been led there by the Sibling.

Kaladin leaves Dabbid to watch Teft while he goes to learn what he can about the Oathgates and how the fabrials are working. He uses adhesion to climb up and down the side of the tower but is too slow to be able to see the Oathgates in operation. Syl warns Kaladin of some scouts, and they realize that the scouts must be communicating with those operating the Oathgates using spanreeds. Kaladin resolves to steal a spanreed, so he sends Syl to follow them.

## Rowan's "If Asked" Notes

**Q: Who is Relador?**  
A: Relador was Syl's old knight—a previous Windrunner she was bonded to before Kaladin. Syl is processing Relador's death for the first time, which shows that spren experience grief and loss just like humans do.

**Q: How did the Sibling lead Dabbid to Kaladin?**  
A: Dabbid appears with broth, having been led there by the Sibling. This shows that the Sibling can guide people through the tower, likely using the same crystal veins and infrastructure that allowed Navani and Kaladin to communicate.

**Q: Why does Kaladin want to steal a spanreed?**  
A: Kaladin realizes that the scouts are communicating with those operating the Oathgates using spanreeds. If he can steal a spanreed, he might be able to intercept their communications or communicate with Navani and others outside the tower.

**Q: Why can't Kaladin see the Oathgates in operation?**  
A: Kaladin uses adhesion to climb the tower exterior, but he's too slow to catch the Oathgates in operation. This suggests the Oathgates are only active briefly, or that he needs to be in a better position to observe them.

**Q: What does Syl mean about processing Relador's death?**  
A: Syl is thinking about Relador and how she feels about his death. Kaladin realizes this is the first time she's really processing this grief. They talk about how they learn to deal with depressing thoughts, showing that both humans and spren struggle with loss and grief.

**Q: Why is Dabbid helping?**  
A: Dabbid brings broth and watches Teft while Kaladin goes to investigate the Oathgates. Dabbid has been communicating with the Sibling (from earlier chapters), and the Sibling led him to Kaladin, suggesting the Sibling is coordinating help for Kaladin.

**Q: What is the plan with the spanreed?**  
A: Kaladin resolves to steal a spanreed so he can potentially intercept communications about the Oathgates or communicate with others. He sends Syl to follow the scouts to find where they keep the spanreeds or how they use them.
