## Quick Summary
Dalinar is experimenting with his Bondsmith powers and has learned how to see someone's Connections to others. He leaves to talk to Taravangian. Dalinar asks why Taravangian betrayed them, and Taravangian explains that he knows that they are going to lose and this is the only way he can save at least Kharbranth. He says that Cultivation granted him the capacity to stop what was coming, and on his one transcendently brilliant day this was the path he saw. Dalinar tells him that he doesn't think that Taravangian could have seen the future perfectly and that he, Dalinar, has to believe that it is possible to defeat Odium. Taravangian wishes he were dealing with the Dalinar from before because he believes that that Dalinar would see the necessity of joining Odium. Taravangian believes that monarchs cannot allow themselves to be bound by oaths because they have to be able to engage in the kind of ignoble behavior that he has. Taravangian says that he could die happy if Dalinar saves mankind proving Taravangian wrong, but Dalinar believes that Taravangian's actions prove that he had just wanted power. After Dalinar leaves, Szeth's proffered opinion reinforces his feeling that they cannot trust Taravangian.

## Rowan's "If Asked" Notes

**Q: Why did Taravangian betray Dalinar?**  
A: Taravangian explains that he knows they are going to lose and this is the only way he can save at least Kharbranth. He says that Cultivation granted him the capacity to stop what was coming, and on his one transcendently brilliant day (when he created the Diagram), this was the path he saw. He believes betrayal is necessary to save something.

**Q: What is the Diagram?**  
A: The Diagram is a prophecy that Taravangian created on his one transcendently brilliant day. It showed him a path to save at least part of humanity, which involved betraying Dalinar. However, Dalinar points out that Taravangian couldn't have seen the future perfectly, suggesting the Diagram may be flawed.

**Q: What does Dalinar mean about seeing Connections?**  
A: Dalinar has learned how to see someone's Connections to others using his Bondsmith powers. This is a new ability that shows his growing understanding of his powers. Connections are the bonds between people, places, and things in the Cosmere.

**Q: Why does Taravangian wish he were dealing with the old Dalinar?**  
A: Taravangian believes that the old Dalinar (before he became a Bondsmith and changed) would see the necessity of joining Odium. The old Dalinar was more pragmatic and willing to do whatever was necessary, which Taravangian thinks would align with his own philosophy.

**Q: What is Taravangian's philosophy about monarchs?**  
A: Taravangian believes that monarchs cannot allow themselves to be bound by oaths because they have to be able to engage in ignoble behavior when necessary. He thinks that leaders must be willing to break promises and do terrible things for the greater good, which is why he betrayed Dalinar.

**Q: Can Taravangian be trusted?**  
A: After their conversation, Szeth's opinion reinforces Dalinar's feeling that they cannot trust Taravangian. Dalinar believes that Taravangian's actions prove he just wanted power, not that he was truly trying to save humanity. This suggests Taravangian may have ulterior motives.

**Q: What does Cultivation have to do with Taravangian?**  
A: Cultivation granted Taravangian his boon—the capacity to have days of transcendent brilliance (when he created the Diagram) alternating with days of high compassion. This boon/curse is what gave him the ability to see the path he believes will save humanity.

**Q: What does the epigraph mean about gods changing state?**  
A: The epigraph suggests that gods (Shards) cannot be destroyed, but they can change state, like spren or the various Lights. This relates to how Shards can be Splintered or combined, changing their form but not being truly destroyed. This may be relevant to ending the war or dealing with Odium.
