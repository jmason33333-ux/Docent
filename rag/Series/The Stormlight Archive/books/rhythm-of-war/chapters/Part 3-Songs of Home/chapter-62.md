## Quick Summary
**Eight years ago**

Venli and Eshonai are meeting with the Five, where Venli is telling them that she can create warform because she has trapped a painspren in a topaz. Eshonai is there because she has received word that Gavilar has extended an invitation to establish formal relations with the listeners. Eshonai wants to convince the elders that they should reach out to the other listeners to create a unified people. The elders say they will consider her proposal. She leaves and finds her mother, who can't remember where she is. Jaxlim eventually comes to lucidity and admits that she knows that her life is coming to an end. Venli joins them and tells them that they've been approved to try warform. Venli explains that she wants her mother to try to take on the form because it might heal her, and Eshonai is excited about that, though she is worried because her sister is humming an unusual rhythm.

## Rowan's "If Asked" Notes

**Q: How does Venli create warform?**  
A: Venli tells the Five that she can create warform because she has trapped a painspren in a topaz. This is the method she discovered earlier (in Chapter 45), and it allows listeners to take warform, which makes them stronger and more capable in combat.

**Q: What is Gavilar's invitation?**  
A: Gavilar has extended an invitation to establish formal relations with the listeners. This is part of his plan to learn more about them and their history, particularly their songs and stories about the Radiants. Eshonai wants to accept this, but she also wants to unify with other listeners first.

**Q: Why does Eshonai want to unify the listeners?**  
A: Eshonai wants to convince the elders to reach out to other listeners to create a unified people. This would make them stronger and better able to deal with the humans, and it aligns with Eshonai's desire for exploration and connection.

**Q: Why is Eshonai worried about Venli's rhythm?**  
A: Eshonai is worried because Venli is humming an unusual rhythm. This is likely related to Venli bonding Ulim (from Chapter 57), which has changed her in ways that affect her connection to listener culture and rhythms. Eshonai's concern shows that something is wrong with Venli.

**Q: Will warform heal Jaxlim?**  
A: Venli explains that she wants her mother to try warform because it might heal her. Jaxlim is suffering from memory loss and knows her life is coming to an end. Whether warform can actually heal her remains to be seen, but it's a source of hope for the family.

**Q: What does Jaxlim mean about her life ending?**  
A: Jaxlim eventually comes to lucidity and admits that she knows her life is coming to an end. Her memory loss is getting worse, and she's aware of her decline. This is an emotional moment that shows her acceptance of her condition.

**Q: Who are the Five?**  
A: The Five are the listener elders who make important decisions for their people. They consider Eshonai's proposal to unify with other listeners and approve Venli's method for creating warform. They're the leadership of the listener community.

**Q: Why is this chapter called "Keeper of Forms"?**  
A: The title "Keeper of Forms" likely refers to Venli's role in discovering and creating new forms for the listeners. She's the one who has figured out how to create warform, making her a keeper of this knowledge and ability.
