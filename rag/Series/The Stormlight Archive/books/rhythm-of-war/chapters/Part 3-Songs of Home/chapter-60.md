## Quick Summary
Venli goes to see Rlain. The guard tells her that he is considered a human sympathizer, but she manages to convince him to let her speak with Rlain alone. Rlain recognizes her, and as they exchange their stories, Venli realizes that they have overlapping goals. She tells him that she has saved Kaladin's family, and she promises to help get him out.

Venli finds Raboniel in Dalinar's former rooms accompanied by some Fused, some humans, and Mraize. Mraize is making a deal for access to the Oathgates, and he presents Lift in a cage as a gift, and reveals to her that Lift is an Edgedancer whom Cultivation made to use Lifelight instead of Stormlight. When Mraize is gone, Venli asks Raboniel if Rlain can be released into her care, as he is the only other of her kind left; Raboniel agrees. She leaves and arrives at her room, where she attempts to swear the next ideal. Her words are rejected, which makes sense to her as she realizes that she cannot be true to the ideal, having watched a human child carted away in a cage.

## Rowan's "If Asked" Notes

**Q: Who is Rlain?**  
A: Rlain is a listener (singer) who has been working with the humans. He's considered a "human sympathizer" and is imprisoned. Venli recognizes him, and they realize they have overlapping goals. Rlain is the only other listener left (besides Venli), making him significant.

**Q: What is Lifelight?**  
A: Lifelight is a type of Investiture that Lift uses instead of Stormlight. Mraize reveals that Cultivation made Lift to use Lifelight, which is why she can still use her powers when other Radiants are suppressed. This is a significant revelation about Lift's unique abilities.

**Q: Why does Venli's ideal get rejected?**  
A: Venli attempts to swear the next Radiant ideal, but her words are rejected. She realizes she cannot be true to the ideal because she watched a human child (Lift) carted away in a cage and didn't do anything to stop it. This shows that Radiant ideals require genuine commitment and action, not just words.

**Q: What is Mraize's deal?**  
A: Mraize is making a deal with Raboniel for access to the Oathgates. He presents Lift in a cage as a gift, showing that the Ghostbloods are willing to work with the Fused to achieve their goals. This is concerning because it suggests the Ghostbloods have their own agenda that may not align with the humans.

**Q: Why does Venli want Rlain released?**  
A: Venli asks Raboniel to release Rlain into her care because he is "the only other of her kind left." This shows Venli's desire to connect with another listener and her recognition that they share common ground. Raboniel agrees, which is significant.

**Q: What are Venli and Rlain's overlapping goals?**  
A: Venli and Rlain exchange their stories and realize they have overlapping goals. Both are listeners who have been working with or helping humans, and both seem to want to find a way for listeners and humans to coexist. This suggests potential for cooperation.

**Q: Who is El?**  
A: The epigraph asks "Who, then, is El?" This is a mysterious reference that may become clearer later. It's mentioned in connection with the Rhythm of War's emotional influence, suggesting El may be someone important to Odium or the Fused.

**Q: Why is Lift in a cage?**  
A: Mraize presents Lift in a cage as a gift to Raboniel. This shows that the Ghostbloods captured Lift (likely from the tower) and are using her as a bargaining chip. The fact that she's in a cage is disturbing and shows the Ghostbloods' willingness to treat people as objects.
