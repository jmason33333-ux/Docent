## Rowan's "If Asked" Notes

**Q: Why does Leshwi protect Kaladin's family?**  
A: Leshwi takes Kaladin's family into her protection after Venli informs her. Leshwi has shown respect for Kaladin before (in earlier books), and this act of compassion suggests she values honor and doesn't believe in harming non-combatants or using family as leverage.

**Q: Why does Venli tell Leshwi?**  
A: Venli informs Leshwi about Kaladin's family being identified and scheduled for execution. This shows Venli's growing compassion and her willingness to help, even when it means going against the plans of other Fused like the Pursuer.

**Q: How was Kaladin's family identified?**  
A: The reports indicate that Kaladin's family has been identified. This likely happened because Kaladin was seen or because someone recognized them. The fact that they're locked away and scheduled for execution shows the danger they're in.

**Q: What does the epigraph mean?**  
A: The epigraph suggests that humans, while originally from another world, have lived on Roshar for thousands of years and should be considered "cousins" rather than "aliens or interlopers." This reflects a theme of finding common ground and recognizing shared humanity.

**Q: Why is Kaladin running loose significant?**  
A: Kaladin being awake and active in the tower is significant because most Radiants are unconscious due to the suppression field. The fact that he's able to move around and has taken Teft suggests he may have some resistance to the suppression or has found a way around it.

**Q: What is the Pursuer's plan?**  
A: The Pursuer (Lezian) is after Kaladin. He's been hunting Kaladin since earlier in the book and wants to kill him as part of his tradition. The fact that Kaladin's family is to be executed may be part of a plan to draw Kaladin out or punish him.
