## Quick Summary
Dalinar discusses with the Stormfather his need to find out what is happening at Urithiru, and the Stormfather suggests he ride the current highstorm with him. As Dalinar rides the stormwall towards Urithiru, he notices wonders as well as villages destroyed by the Everstorm. He sees two men trapped out in the storm and begs the Stormfather to spare them. The Stormfather declines, stating that he would not be a storm if he did not rage. Dalinar calls him a coward and argues that having sympathy is important and that he should change. They arrive at Urithiru and at first do not see anything wrong. Dalinar pushes back to the tower, insisting he must investigate. He sees Kaladin clinging to the side of the tower, hanging on and badly wounded. Kaladin is able to explain to Dalinar that the forces of Odium now occupy the tower, Navani has been captured, the pillar is partially corrupted, and the Radiants other than himself are all unconscious. Kaladin himself loses consciousness, and Dalinar uses the winds to push him into the tower.

Dalinar wakes from the vision realizing the tower has been occupied by the enemy. He immediately goes to speak with Shalash and Taln to ask after Ishar, determining to go to the Bondsmith so he can learn about his powers in order to fight back.

## Rowan's "If Asked" Notes

**Q: How does Dalinar ride the highstorm?**  
A: The Stormfather suggests that Dalinar ride the current highstorm with him. This allows Dalinar to travel quickly to Urithiru and see what's happening there. It's a unique ability that shows Dalinar's connection to the Stormfather and his Bondsmith powers.

**Q: Why does the Stormfather refuse to spare the men?**  
A: The Stormfather declines to spare the two men trapped in the storm, stating that he would not be a storm if he did not rage. He sees himself as bound by his nature and cannot change. Dalinar calls him a coward and argues that having sympathy is important and that he should change, showing their philosophical disagreement.

**Q: What does Kaladin tell Dalinar?**  
A: Kaladin explains that the forces of Odium now occupy the tower, Navani has been captured, the pillar is partially corrupted, and the Radiants other than himself are all unconscious. This is crucial information that confirms the severity of the situation.

**Q: What is the pillar?**  
A: The pillar is the crystal pillar in the basement of the tower that the Sibling's heart is connected to. It's partially corrupted, which means the Fused have been able to corrupt at least one of the nodes, giving them some control over the tower.

**Q: Why does Dalinar want to go to Ishar?**  
A: Dalinar determines to go to the Bondsmith (Ishar) so he can learn about his powers in order to fight back. Ishar is one of the Heralds and was a Bondsmith, so he may have knowledge that Dalinar needs to understand his abilities better and potentially save the tower.

**Q: What happens to Kaladin?**  
A: Kaladin is badly wounded and clinging to the side of the tower. He loses consciousness, and Dalinar uses the winds to push him into the tower. His condition is serious, and it's unclear if he'll survive.

**Q: Why is this a vision?**  
A: Dalinar wakes from the vision, suggesting that what he experienced while riding the storm was a vision or Connection that allowed him to see Urithiru. This is similar to how he's used visions before to see distant places or events.

**Q: What does the epigraph mean about Light having Intent?**  
A: The epigraph suggests that if Light is Investiture, and all Investiture is deity, and deity has Intent, then Light must have Intent. This relates to Navani's research about Light and suggests that Light itself has desires or purposes, which may be important for understanding how to work with it.

**Q: Who are Shalash and Taln?**  
A: Shalash and Taln are two of the ten Heralds. Dalinar goes to speak with them to ask after Ishar, who is another Herald and was a Bondsmith. The Heralds may have information about Ishar's location or how to find him.
