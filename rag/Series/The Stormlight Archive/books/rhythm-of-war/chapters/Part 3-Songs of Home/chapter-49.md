## Quick Summary
**Navani:** Navani has her scholars doing busy work as they are being watched carefully. She periodically pauses to rest against the wall and speaks to the Sibling in private. The Sibling says that Raboniel does not yet know that she is awake; the Midnight Mother had told the Fused that the Sibling has been dead for a long time. Navani manages to convince the Sibling that they need to work together, though the Sibling is resistant because they are not interested in becoming bonded to a human. The Sibling reveals that there are three other nodes connected to their heart, and Navani needs to find a way to protect them from being corrupted. Navani asks why the Sibling did not activate the defenses that she had earlier, and the Sibling says that when the humans bound Ba-Ado-Mishram, it injured them. It caused them to lose the rhythm of their Light, and the tower stopped working. The Sibling tells her that there are two Radiants still awake in the tower that she can detect. One is Lift, awake because Cultivation gave her the power to use Light differently but in a cage surrounded with aluminum. The other is Kaladin, and she suspects that she can detect him because as a Windrunner he is close to Honor and to the surge of Adhesion. Navani asks the Sibling to contact him.

**Kaladin:** Kaladin and Syl are trying to get to the sixth floor, where he suspects he can find Stormlight. He avoids singer guards in Alethi uniforms, and Syl manages to make sure that Kaladin's family is safe. They find lanterns filled with infused gemstones left behind by workers, and Kaladin manages to break into them using a Reverse Lashing. After getting the Stormlight, they make their way to the Devotary of Mercy on the fourth floor, where he hopes to find food and water. He comes across the ardent Kuno, who provides him with various supplies and nervously agrees to help provide more in the future. As Kaladin nears the room where he left Teft, he is guided by a crystal vein in the rock that lights up; once inside, he touches it and hears Navani's voice.

**Navani:** Navani and Kaladin exchange information, and she tells him that aside from getting their spanreeds working somehow, they need to figure out how the Fused are making the Oathgates work. Kaladin agrees to scout them out and to keep an eye out for nodes before they say goodbye. The Sibling berates Navani for asking after fabrials so cavalierly but concedes that at least the Oathgate spren agreed to their transformation.

## Rowan's "If Asked" Notes

**Q: What are the nodes?**  
A: The Sibling reveals that there are three other nodes connected to their heart. These nodes are part of the tower's infrastructure and need to be protected from being corrupted by the Fused. If the nodes are corrupted, it could give the Fused control over the tower.

**Q: Why didn't the Sibling activate the tower's defenses earlier?**  
A: The Sibling explains that when the humans bound Ba-Ado-Mishram (one of the Unmade), it injured them. This caused them to lose the rhythm of their Light, and the tower stopped working. This is why the defenses weren't activated—the Sibling was too injured to do so.

**Q: Why is Lift still awake?**  
A: The Sibling tells Navani that Lift is still awake because Cultivation gave her the power to use Light differently. However, she's in a cage surrounded with aluminum, which blocks Investiture. This suggests that while Lift's powers work differently, she's still being held captive.

**Q: Why can the Sibling detect Kaladin?**  
A: The Sibling suspects they can detect Kaladin because as a Windrunner, he's close to Honor and to the surge of Adhesion. Adhesion is the surge of connection and pressure, which may relate to the Sibling's nature as a Bondsmith spren.

**Q: How do Navani and Kaladin communicate?**  
A: Navani asks the Sibling to contact Kaladin. When Kaladin nears the room where Teft is, a crystal vein in the rock lights up. When he touches it, he hears Navani's voice. This is the Sibling's infrastructure allowing communication through the tower.

**Q: What are Oathgate spren?**  
A: The Sibling reveals that Oathgates are spren that agreed to be transformed into fabrials. This is similar to how soulcasters were spren that chose to manifest in the Physical Realm. The Sibling notes that at least the Oathgate spren agreed to their transformation, suggesting this is important.

**Q: Why does the Sibling resist bonding with a human?**  
A: The Sibling is resistant to becoming bonded to a human. This may be because they're a Bondsmith spren (like the Stormfather) and bonding would be a significant commitment, or because they have concerns about what bonding would mean for them.

**Q: What is a Reverse Lashing?**  
A: A Reverse Lashing is a Windrunner ability that creates a pull toward an object. Kaladin uses it to break into the lanterns and get the Stormlight from the infused gemstones inside.

**Q: Who is Kuno?**  
A: Kuno is an ardent at the Devotary of Mercy on the fourth floor. He provides Kaladin with supplies (food and water) and nervously agrees to help provide more in the future. He's taking a risk by helping Kaladin, as the tower is under occupation.

**Q: Why is it important to figure out how the Fused are operating the Oathgates?**  
A: The Oathgates are how the Fused are bringing troops into the tower. If Navani and Kaladin can understand how they're doing this, they might be able to stop them or use the knowledge to their advantage. This is crucial for potentially retaking the tower.
