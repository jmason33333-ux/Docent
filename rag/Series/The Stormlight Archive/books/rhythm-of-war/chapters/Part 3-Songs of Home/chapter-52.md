## Quick Summary
**Eight and a half years ago**

Venli is with her mother, and it is becoming clear that Jaxlim is suffering from an Alzheimer's-type condition, as she continues to forget the songs that she has never forgotten before. Venli leaves and joins her sister on the scouting tower, and Eshonai says that she is leaving with a scout group to investigate some smoke in the distance which they think might be the humans returning for a second visit.

The humans are visiting the listeners, and Venli is frustrated with how much they are hounding her about the songs that they know. Axindweth surprises Venli by speaking to her in the listener language and by questioning Venli about the wisdom of following the path of her ancestors. Axindweth tells her that there are great things that the listeners could accomplish if they had the right forms of power, including the ability to heal her mother. Axindweth gives Venli a gemstone and tells her to break it in a storm.

## Rowan's "If Asked" Notes

**Q: What is wrong with Jaxlim?**  
A: Jaxlim is suffering from an Alzheimer's-type condition. She continues to forget the songs that she has never forgotten before, which is particularly concerning because she's the keeper of the listener songs. This memory loss is a significant problem for the listeners' cultural preservation.

**Q: Who is Axindweth?**  
A: Axindweth is a human who is visiting the listeners. She surprises Venli by speaking in the listener language and questions Venli about following her ancestors' path. She gives Venli a gemstone and tells her to break it in a storm. Her knowledge and actions suggest she has a specific agenda.

**Q: What is in the gemstone?**  
A: Axindweth gives Venli a gemstone and tells her to break it in a storm. The gemstone likely contains a spren (possibly Ulim, based on later chapters) that will be released when broken. This is a significant moment that will have major consequences for Venli.

**Q: Why does Axindweth promise healing for Jaxlim?**  
A: Axindweth tells Venli that if the listeners had the right forms of power, they could accomplish great things, including healing her mother. This is likely a manipulation tactic—she's offering Venli something she desperately wants (healing for her mother) to motivate her to take the gemstone and break it.

**Q: Why is Venli frustrated with the humans?**  
A: Venli is frustrated with how much the humans are hounding her about the songs. The humans are very interested in the listener songs (which contain their history), and their persistent questioning is annoying to Venli, who may feel like they're trying to take something sacred from her people.

**Q: What does Axindweth mean about "right forms of power"?**  
A: Axindweth suggests that the listeners could accomplish great things if they had the right forms of power. This likely refers to forms like warform or other forms that grant abilities beyond what the listeners currently have. She's essentially promising power in exchange for Venli taking the gemstone.

**Q: Why does Axindweth question following ancestors' path?**  
A: Axindweth questions Venli about the wisdom of following the path of her ancestors. This is part of her manipulation—she's trying to get Venli to question tradition and be open to change, specifically the change that breaking the gemstone will bring.
