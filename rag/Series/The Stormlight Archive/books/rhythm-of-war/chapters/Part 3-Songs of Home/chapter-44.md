## Quick Summary
Kaladin, carrying Teft and led by Syl, searches for a place to hide from the invaders. They narrowly avoid both the Pursuer (Lezian) and a search party. Realizing they are trapped, Syl notices a faint light coming from a vein in the stone, which they follow to a gemstone embedded in the wall. Kaladin infuses it, and it reveals a door to a hidden staircase. He takes the stairs to the eleventh floor and finds a dark room well away from the search parties, hidden behind another door fabrial. He collapses into sleep.

## Rowan's "If Asked" Notes

**Q: What is the light in the stone?**  
A: The faint light Syl notices comes from a vein in the stone, which leads to a gemstone embedded in the wall. This is connected to the Sibling, the tower's spren, and is part of Urithiru's hidden infrastructure.

**Q: How does Kaladin activate the hidden door?**  
A: Kaladin infuses the gemstone with Stormlight, which activates a door fabrial that reveals a hidden staircase. This is part of the tower's secret passage system.

**Q: Why is the room on the eleventh floor significant?**  
A: The eleventh floor is well away from the search parties, making it a safe hiding place. The room is also hidden behind another door fabrial, providing additional security.

**Q: What happened to Teft?**  
A: Teft collapsed when the suppression field activated in Chapter 39. Kaladin has been carrying him, trying to find a safe place to hide him while he's unconscious.

**Q: Who is the Pursuer?**  
A: The Pursuer is Lezian, a Fused who has been specifically hunting Kaladin. They narrowly avoid him in this chapter, adding to the tension.

**Q: Why is Kaladin so exhausted?**  
A: Kaladin has been carrying Teft, avoiding search parties and the Pursuer, and dealing with the stress of the invasion. His collapse at the end shows the physical and emotional toll of everything that's happened.
