## Quick Summary
**Nine years ago**

**Eshonai:** Eshonai is exploring the forest with a gathering party and leaves to wander on her own. She thinks about her people, how they live in scattered families with hopes limited to conquering ancient abandoned cities. She dreams of discovery and exploration—and then finds the humans.

**Venli:** Venli is with her mother reciting the listener songs. Their family is the "First-Rhythm" family, and though they have dwindled over years, they dream of claiming one of the cities their ancestors once controlled. Venli finds this too small of a goal and believes their responsibilities shouldn't involve just knowing and remembering the songs, but implementing them. She believes Eshonai is abandoning her duty by not learning the songs. She tells her mother that she thinks she has discovered a way to take a new form, warform, by taking a painspren into her gemheart. Jaxlim decides they need to seek the blessing of the Five. They do, but are interrupted by sounds from outside the town. At first Venli thinks it is a skirmish, but instead learns it is Eshonai and her discovery of the humans.

## Rowan's "If Asked" Notes

**Q: Who are the listeners?**  
A: The listeners (also called parshmen or singers) are the native people of Roshar. They can take different forms by bonding different spren in their gemhearts. This flashback shows them before they had extensive contact with humans.

**Q: What is the First-Rhythm family?**  
A: The First-Rhythm family is Eshonai and Venli's family. They are keepers of the listener songs, which contain the listeners' history and knowledge. The family has dwindled over the years, but they still maintain this important tradition.

**Q: What are the listener songs?**  
A: The listener songs are oral traditions that contain the listeners' history, knowledge, and culture. Venli believes they should be implemented (acted upon) rather than just remembered, which shows her ambition and desire for change.

**Q: How does warform work?**  
A: Venli discovers that taking a painspren into her gemheart allows her to take warform. This is a significant discovery because warform makes listeners stronger and more capable in combat. Listeners have gemhearts that can hold spren, which allows them to change forms.

**Q: Why is finding the humans significant?**  
A: This is the first contact between the listeners and humans in this era. It's a major turning point in listener history, as it will lead to trade, conflict, and eventually the events of the main series. Eshonai's discovery sets in motion many of the events that follow.

**Q: Who are the Five?**  
A: The Five are listener leaders who give blessings. When Venli wants to implement her discovery of warform, Jaxlim decides they need to seek the blessing of the Five, showing their importance in listener society.

**Q: What is the conflict between Eshonai and Venli?**  
A: Eshonai wants to explore and discover new things, while Venli focuses on learning and implementing the listener songs. Venli thinks Eshonai is abandoning her duty by not learning the songs, showing a tension between tradition and exploration.
