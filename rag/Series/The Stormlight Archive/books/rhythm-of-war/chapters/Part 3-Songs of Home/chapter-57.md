## Quick Summary
**Eight and a half years ago**

**Eshonai:** The humans are visiting the listeners a second time and are going to be leaving soon. Dalinar speaks with Eshonai and warns her against his brother, indicating that he is showing a dangerous degree of interest in them.

**Venli:** Venli and her family are in their storm shelter, and she decides to take the special sphere into the storm and break it. Those watching think that she is going to take mateform, the only other form available to them. The Stormfather speaks to her and cautions her against taking this action. She chooses to proceed and releases a spren. The spren says that he will live inside her, while also speaking of Shadesmar and a new storm.

**Eshonai:** Eshonai rejects Thude's notion that Venli has taken mateform, and she is relieved when Venli returns, as she was worried. When she speaks with her sister, Eshonai realizes that Venli, who claims she had been trying for warform, is not attuned to the rhythms. It takes Venli a couple of tries to attune them, and Eshonai is unnerved by her grin and the confidence in her walk.

## Rowan's "If Asked" Notes

**Q: Who is Ulim?**  
A: Ulim is a voidspren that was contained in the gemstone Axindweth gave Venli. When Venli breaks the gemstone in the storm, Ulim is released and says he will live inside her. This is a major moment—Ulim will become Venli's guide and connection to Odium.

**Q: Why does the Stormfather caution Venli?**  
A: The Stormfather speaks to Venli and cautions her against breaking the gemstone. This suggests he knows what's inside and understands the danger. However, Venli chooses to proceed anyway, showing her determination and perhaps her desperation to help her mother.

**Q: Why does Dalinar warn Eshonai about Gavilar?**  
A: Dalinar warns Eshonai that Gavilar is showing a dangerous degree of interest in the listeners. This foreshadows Gavilar's plans, which will have major consequences for both humans and listeners. Dalinar is trying to protect the listeners from his brother's ambitions.

**Q: Why is Venli not attuned to the rhythms?**  
A: After bonding Ulim, Venli has difficulty attuning to the rhythms. It takes her a couple of tries, and Eshonai is unnerved by this. This suggests that bonding Ulim has changed Venli in fundamental ways, affecting her connection to listener culture and the rhythms.

**Q: Why is Eshonai unnerved by Venli's grin and confidence?**  
A: Eshonai is unnerved by Venli's grin and the confidence in her walk. This suggests that Venli has changed in ways that are concerning—she seems different, more confident, perhaps more dangerous. This foreshadows the transformation Venli will undergo.

**Q: What does Ulim mean about Shadesmar and a new storm?**  
A: Ulim speaks of Shadesmar (the Cognitive Realm) and a new storm. The "new storm" likely refers to the Everstorm, which will be created later. This shows that Ulim is connected to Odium's plans and knows about future events.

**Q: Why do people think Venli is taking mateform?**  
A: Those watching think Venli is going to take mateform, which is the only other form available to the listeners at this time. However, Eshonai rejects this notion, and Venli claims she was trying for warform. The reality is that she's bonding Ulim, which is something entirely different.

**Q: What are the consequences of Venli bonding Ulim?**  
A: Bonding Ulim will have major consequences for Venli. It connects her to Odium, gives her access to voidlight and new forms, and changes her fundamentally. This is the moment that sets Venli on the path that leads to her role in the current story.
