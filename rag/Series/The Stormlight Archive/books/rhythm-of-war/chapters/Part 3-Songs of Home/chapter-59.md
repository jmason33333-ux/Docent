## Quick Summary
Kaladin follows the Sibling's light, fighting off guards as he encounters them, having to use different maneuvers than those to which he is accustomed. The Sibling leads him through a hidden passageway between two shops, and he runs into a woodworker. He comes out eventually at an open air tunnel to see Raboniel infusing the node and the Pursuer standing by. The Pursuer and Kaladin fight, and Syl stops the Pursuer from killing him by pretending to be voidspren. Eventually, the Pursuer has to take a brief break, and Kaladin has a short exchange with Raboniel. When the Pursuer returns, Kaladin tricks him into giving chase and enlists the Sibling's help to lock him in a room. Kaladin then returns to the node and destroys it with a rock. Suddenly exhausted, he is taken by surprise and Raboniel stabs him in the chest. As Kaladin realizes that he is unable to heal quickly, Raboniel manipulates him into not killing her and tells him to flee.

## Rowan's "If Asked" Notes

**Q: Why does Kaladin destroy the node?**  
A: Kaladin destroys the node with a rock to prevent Raboniel from corrupting it. If the node is corrupted, the Fused could gain control over the tower. Destroying it is a desperate measure, but it prevents the corruption.

**Q: Why can't Kaladin heal quickly?**  
A: After destroying the node and being stabbed, Kaladin realizes he is unable to heal quickly. This may be because he's low on Stormlight, exhausted from the fight, or because the suppression field is still affecting his powers to some degree.

**Q: How does Raboniel manipulate Kaladin?**  
A: After stabbing Kaladin, Raboniel manipulates him into not killing her and tells him to flee. She may be using psychological tactics, appealing to his honor, or exploiting his weakened state. Her manipulation shows her cunning and understanding of human psychology.

**Q: Why does Syl pretend to be voidspren?**  
A: Syl pretends to be voidspren to stop the Pursuer from killing Kaladin. This is a clever tactic that shows her ability to shapeshift or at least appear as different types of spren, and it saves Kaladin's life.

**Q: How does the Sibling help lock the Pursuer?**  
A: Kaladin enlists the Sibling's help to lock the Pursuer in a room. The Sibling can control doors and infrastructure in the tower, allowing them to trap the Pursuer temporarily so Kaladin can deal with the node.

**Q: Why does Kaladin have to use different maneuvers?**  
A: Kaladin has to use different maneuvers than he's accustomed to because his powers are still suppressed to some degree, and he's fighting in tight spaces with limited mobility. He can't use his full Windrunner abilities, so he has to adapt.

**Q: What happens after Kaladin flees?**  
A: Kaladin flees after being stabbed and manipulated by Raboniel. He's injured, exhausted, and needs to get to safety. This sets up his next actions, which will likely involve finding a place to recover and potentially getting help.

**Q: What is the Rhythm of War?**  
A: The epigraph mentions "the Rhythm of War's emotional influence," which may refer to a specific rhythm that affects emotions or behavior. The mention of "El" suggests this is important to someone specific, though their identity may not be clear yet.
