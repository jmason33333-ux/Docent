## Quick Summary
**Navani:** Navani meets with Raboniel and tells her that she has accepted Raboniel's offer to work under her. They discuss the science behind the Fourth Bridge, and in doing so, Navani learns that the soulcasters were spren that chose to manifest themselves as such in the Physical Realm. Raboniel says they have not been able to convince voidspren to sacrifice themselves that way. Raboniel reveals that her goal is to end the war once and for all using the information they can learn together. Navani goes to check on her scholars. She learns that two of her scholars were killed in the invasion, and tells them they will be cooperating with the invaders for now. She puts them to work drawing sketches and writing about the experiments they had been doing before the invasion.

**Kaladin:** Kaladin is in a nightmare, in a dark and stormy landscape. He sees three figures attack him, and he takes the spear from one of them and kills them—and then realizes that Tien was one of them. He drops the spear, turns, and finds himself on the Shattered Plains, his friends fighting and killing each other. His dying friends speak to him and tell him this is all his fault, and Moash appears, telling him that Kaladin is the reason for their suffering. He wakes in the tower, exhausted. He searches for Syl, and she arrives, letting him know that the guards seem to have given up the search. He asks her if she knows anything about the light that led them there, and she tells him what she knows about the Sibling, that they were the spren of one of the Bondsmiths. He touches the gemstone that opens the door, confirming that it will still work once infused.

## Rowan's "If Asked" Notes

**Q: What are soulcasters?**  
A: Soulcasters are fabrials that can transform materials. Navani learns that they were actually spren that chose to manifest themselves in the Physical Realm, sacrificing their ability to be spren to become tools. This is a significant revelation about how fabrials work.

**Q: Why won't voidspren sacrifice themselves?**  
A: Raboniel says they haven't been able to convince voidspren to sacrifice themselves the way other spren did to become soulcasters. This may be because voidspren are aligned with Odium and have different motivations, or because they're less willing to make such sacrifices.

**Q: Does Raboniel really want to end the war?**  
A: Raboniel claims her goal is to end the war once and for all using information she and Navani can learn together. Whether this is true or a manipulation remains to be seen, but it's an interesting claim that suggests she may have motivations beyond simple conquest.

**Q: What is Kaladin's nightmare about?**  
A: Kaladin has a traumatic nightmare where he kills Tien (his brother), sees his friends fighting and killing each other, and is told by Moash that he's the reason for everyone's suffering. This reflects his deep trauma and feelings of guilt and responsibility for the deaths of those he's tried to protect.

**Q: Who is the Sibling?**  
A: The Sibling is the spren of Urithiru itself. Syl tells Kaladin that they were the spren of one of the Bondsmiths. This is important because it means the tower has its own spren, which explains the hidden passages and gemstone mechanisms Kaladin discovered.

**Q: Why can Kaladin use the gemstone?**  
A: Kaladin touches the gemstone that opens the door and confirms it will still work once infused. This suggests he has some connection to the Sibling's infrastructure, possibly because as a Windrunner he's close to Honor and the surge of Adhesion, which may relate to the Sibling's nature.

**Q: What happened to Navani's scholars?**  
A: Two of Navani's scholars were killed in the invasion. She tells the remaining scholars they'll be cooperating with the invaders for now, and puts them to work documenting their previous experiments. This is both a way to preserve knowledge and to keep them busy under occupation.
