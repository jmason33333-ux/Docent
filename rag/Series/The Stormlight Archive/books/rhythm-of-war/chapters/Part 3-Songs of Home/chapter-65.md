## Quick Summary
Navani is using prisms to experiment with normal light, Stormlight, Voidlight, and Towerlight. She is able to recombine all of them except for Towerlight, which splits into Stormlight and what she thinks might be Lifelight. She reads one of her accounts of Gavilar's death, looking for her description of the spheres he had had on the table, and suspects that he was involved in this work as well. Raboniel and the insane Fused Essu enter, and Raboniel is amazed at the amount of work that she has achieved. She is fixated on the idea of combining Stormlight and Voidlight, which she believes is necessary to end the war forever. Navani asks if Raboniel has ever heard of spheres that warp the air around them, and Raboniel tells her of the theoretical anti-Light which, if combined with Light, would cause immediate and violent destruction. Navani suspects this is what happened to her scholars experimenting on Gavilar's strange sphere. When Navani asks Raboniel what Essu is saying, she learns that Essu is Raboniel's own daughter who has gone insane and no longer recognizes her.

## Rowan's "If Asked" Notes

**Q: What is anti-Light?**  
A: Anti-Light is a theoretical substance that is the opposite of Light. Raboniel explains that if anti-Light is combined with Light, it would cause immediate and violent destruction. This is a significant discovery that may have implications for ending the war or causing massive damage.

**Q: What happened to Navani's scholars?**  
A: Navani suspects that her scholars were killed when they experimented on Gavilar's strange sphere, which may have contained anti-Light. When they tried to combine it with regular Light, it caused the explosion that killed them (from Chapter 39).

**Q: Who is Essu?**  
A: Essu is Raboniel's own daughter who has gone insane and no longer recognizes her mother. This is a personal revelation that shows Raboniel's humanity and the cost of the long war. Essu is a Fused who has lost her mind, possibly due to the centuries of death and rebirth.

**Q: What does Navani discover about Towerlight?**  
A: Navani discovers that Towerlight splits into Stormlight and what she thinks might be Lifelight when she tries to recombine it with a prism. This confirms that Towerlight is a combination of different types of Light, which she suspected from earlier.

**Q: Was Gavilar involved in light research?**  
A: Navani reads her account of Gavilar's death and looks for her description of the spheres he had on the table. She suspects that Gavilar was involved in this work as well, which suggests he may have been researching anti-Light or other light-related phenomena before his death.

**Q: Why is Raboniel fixated on combining Stormlight and Voidlight?**  
A: Raboniel believes that combining Stormlight and Voidlight is necessary to end the war forever. This is her ultimate goal—to find a way to permanently end the conflict, possibly by creating something that would destroy both sides or neutralize the power of both Shards.

**Q: What are spheres that warp the air?**  
A: Navani asks Raboniel if she has ever heard of spheres that warp the air around them. These may be spheres containing anti-Light, which would have unusual properties. Gavilar's sphere may have been one of these.

**Q: Why is the epigraph about the nature of God?**  
A: The epigraph questions how a God can exist in all things yet have a substance that can be destroyed. This relates to the nature of Shards (Honor, Odium, Cultivation) and how they can be Splintered or destroyed, even though they are fundamental forces of the universe.
