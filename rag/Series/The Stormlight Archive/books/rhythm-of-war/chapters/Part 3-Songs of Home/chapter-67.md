## Quick Summary
Venli is taking the opportunity to practice her Radiant powers while Raboniel is visiting Navani. She goes to a section of the tower that is unoccupied, and after checking in Shadesmar to affirm that there are no watching spren, she presses her hand against the stone. She can't use Stormlight because of the tower's defenses but is able to use Voidlight instead. She communicates with the stone, and when the stone and her body align in an ancient rhythm set to a pure tone, she feels like she belongs. The stone reshapes to show her images of her ancient people, the Dawnsingers, shaping things with stone. She runs out of Voidlight and suddenly finds that there are three other spren like Timbre there. Timbre says they would like to bond her squires, but she tells them to wait because of the danger it would pose if her friends began using their powers while surrounded by Odium's forces.

## Rowan's "If Asked" Notes

**Q: Why can Venli use Voidlight instead of Stormlight?**  
A: Venli can't use Stormlight because of the tower's defenses (the suppression field), but she is able to use Voidlight instead. This is because she's a singer and has access to Voidlight through her connection to Odium, while Stormlight is suppressed.

**Q: What are the Dawnsingers?**  
A: The Dawnsingers are Venli's ancient people—the original listeners who could shape stone. When Venli communicates with the stone, it reshapes to show her images of the Dawnsingers shaping things with stone, connecting her to her heritage.

**Q: What is the ancient rhythm?**  
A: When Venli presses her hand against the stone, the stone and her body align in an ancient rhythm set to a pure tone, and she feels like she belongs. This is a connection to listener heritage and the rhythms that are fundamental to listener culture.

**Q: Who are the three other spren?**  
A: After Venli runs out of Voidlight, she suddenly finds that there are three other spren like Timbre there. Timbre says they would like to bond Venli's squires, which would create more singer Radiants.

**Q: Why does Venli tell them to wait?**  
A: Venli tells the spren to wait because it would be dangerous if her friends (squires) began using their powers while surrounded by Odium's forces. If they suddenly started using Radiant powers, they would be discovered and likely killed.

**Q: What are squires?**  
A: Squires are people who are connected to a Radiant and can use some of their powers. Venli's group members could become squires and potentially bond spren themselves, creating more singer Radiants.

**Q: Why does Venli check Shadesmar?**  
A: Venli checks in Shadesmar (the Cognitive Realm) to affirm that there are no watching spren before she practices her powers. This is to make sure she's not being observed by enemy spren or Fused.

**Q: What does this mean for the future?**  
A: The presence of more spren willing to bond suggests that there could be more singer Radiants in the future. However, Venli is being cautious about when to allow this, as it would be dangerous to reveal their powers while surrounded by enemies.
