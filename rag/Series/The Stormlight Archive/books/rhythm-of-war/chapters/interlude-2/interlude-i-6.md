## Quick Summary
Taravangian is with his armies, waiting for Odium to give him the signal to betray Dalinar's armies. He is having a dumb day, and as he wishes that he were smart, he realizes that smart Taravangian had failed. He considers that, contrary to what he had thought, the curse wasn't that he wasn't smart on some days, but instead that he could not be both smart and compassionate at the same time. He gets out some of the sheets from the Diagram and wonders why the Diagram cannot see Renarin Kholin. Odium pulls Taravangian into a vision and tells him it is time to send the orders. Odium continues to talk, telling Taravangian that he, Odium, prefers this honest and compassionate version of Taravangian. As he rambles about his plans, Taravangian realizes that Odium also failed, because he had expected Dalinar to turn. Taravangian manages to maneuver Odium into showing him his plans, and he looks for the blacked out place that reveals that Odium, like him, cannot see the future of Renarin and those that are close to him. Taravangian also realizes that Odium is terrified of Szeth and the sword that he carries and, finally, that Odium can be defeated; he commits to not giving up. Odium ends the vision, and Taravangian sends the message to his troops to betray Dalinar's soldiers. Minutes later, he is arrested.

## Rowan's "If Asked" Notes

**Q: What is Taravangian's curse?**  
A: Taravangian realizes that his curse isn't being dumb on some days, but rather that he cannot be both smart and compassionate at the same time. On days when he's highly intelligent, he lacks compassion, and on days when he's highly compassionate (like this one), he's less intelligent.

**Q: Why can't the Diagram or Odium see Renarin's future?**  
A: Renarin has some quality that makes him invisible to future sight. Neither Taravangian's Diagram (created on his smartest day) nor Odium can see what will happen with Renarin or those close to him. This is highly significant and may be related to Renarin's unique abilities or bond.

**Q: Why is Odium terrified of Szeth and Nightblood?**  
A: Nightblood is a powerful sword that Szeth carries. Odium is terrified of them, which suggests they pose a real threat to him. This is important because it shows Odium has weaknesses and can be threatened.

**Q: What does Taravangian realize about Odium?**  
A: Taravangian realizes several crucial things: (1) Odium also failed—he expected Dalinar to turn, but Dalinar didn't; (2) Odium cannot see Renarin's future, just like the Diagram; (3) Odium is terrified of Szeth and Nightblood; and most importantly, (4) Odium can be defeated.

**Q: Why does Taravangian send the betrayal order if he realizes Odium can be defeated?**  
A: This is a complex moment. Taravangian makes the crucial realization that Odium can be defeated, but he still sends the order to betray. This may be because he's already committed, or because he sees it as necessary for a larger plan. His arrest minutes later suggests there may be more to this story.

**Q: What is the significance of Odium preferring compassionate Taravangian?**  
A: Odium says he prefers this honest and compassionate version of Taravangian. This is interesting because it suggests Odium values honesty and compassion in some way, or perhaps he finds this version easier to manipulate or work with.

**Q: Why is Taravangian arrested?**  
A: Taravangian is arrested minutes after sending the betrayal order. This suggests that someone (possibly Dalinar's forces or coalition members) discovered his plan or intercepted the message. The timing is significant—he's caught very quickly after committing the betrayal.
