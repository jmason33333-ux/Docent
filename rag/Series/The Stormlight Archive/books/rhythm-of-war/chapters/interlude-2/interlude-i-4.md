## Quick Summary
Vyre (Moash) is in Kholinar, practicing with his Honorblade and trying to get it to respond to him the way he saw Adolin's blade respond. Khen, a singer, tells Vyre they're leaving because this isn't what they want to do, and points out that despite Vyre's claims of being unburdened, his obsession with Kaladin suggests otherwise. Vyre is taken by a vision with Odium, who says Vyre is becoming something unexpected. Odium orders Vyre to help operate the Oathgates to bring forces to Urithiru. Odium is worried about Kaladin's effect on Vyre and asks for help to defeat him. Vyre says Kaladin can't be killed, and when Odium suggests claiming Kaladin as he did Vyre, Vyre thinks he would rather Kaladin be dead. Odium asks how to hurt Kaladin, and Vyre says he needs to be isolated from his friends and made to fear. They plan to use Vyre's Connection to Kaladin through dreams to bring Kaladin visions.

## Rowan's "If Asked" Notes

**Q: Why is Vyre trying to get his Honorblade to respond like Adolin's blade?**  
A: Vyre saw Adolin's deadeye Shardblade (Mayalaran) respond to him in a way that normal deadeyes don't. He's trying to achieve something similar with his Honorblade, though it's unclear if this is possible or what he hopes to gain.

**Q: Why does Khen say they're leaving?**  
A: Khen and other singers are leaving because "this is not what they want to do." This suggests discontent among some singers with Odium's cause or methods.

**Q: What does Odium mean when he says Vyre is becoming something unexpected?**  
A: Odium notes that Vyre is transforming in ways he didn't anticipate. This suggests Vyre's path may be diverging from what Odium originally planned.

**Q: How will Vyre help operate the Oathgates?**  
A: Vyre will use his Honorblade to operate the Oathgates, allowing Odium's forces to be transported to Urithiru. This is a key part of the invasion plan.

**Q: Why does Odium want to use Vyre's Connection to Kaladin?**  
A: Odium lacks the Connection needed to directly influence Kaladin and show him visions. Since Vyre has a strong Connection to Kaladin (from their shared past in Bridge Four), they plan to use Vyre's dreams to bring Kaladin visions, essentially using Vyre as a conduit.

**Q: Why would Vyre rather Kaladin be dead than claimed by Odium?**  
A: This reveals the depth of Vyre's feelings toward Kaladin. Even though he serves Odium, he has complex emotions about Kaladin's fate, suggesting their relationship is more complicated than simple hatred.
