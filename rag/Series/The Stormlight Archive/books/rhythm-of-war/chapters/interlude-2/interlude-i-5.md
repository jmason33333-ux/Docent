## Quick Summary
Lift has made a home in the labyrinthine tunnels that make up the ceilings and walls of Urithiru. She continues to steal food, though primarily from baskets deliberately left for her. She continues to grow, which makes her angry. She talks with Wyndle, who confirms that she saw Cultivation, not the Nightwatcher. Lift says she had specifically asked not to change—she wanted to stay her mother's little girl and is heartbroken that her mother would not recognize her because she has grown. Wyndle wraps around her to comfort her, but then suddenly realizes something has happened to the tower. Lift realizes her powers are gone, and they watch a red chicken fight a green one. The red one is defeated, and Lift goes to it and tries to heal it but can't, feeling resistance. She takes it with her, goes inside to a safe place, and tries again. Taking extra effort, she finally pushes through the resistance and manages to heal it. Her "awesomeness" (Surgebinding), however, is not working at all. The chicken leads her away, and they hear battling in the tower. Eventually, the chicken leads them to a corpse of an old Alethi man in robes who has been killed by a knife and appears to be robbed, missing rings. Mraize, with the green chicken on his shoulder, arrives and says that he has always wanted an excuse to hunt her. Lift takes the red chicken and runs.

## Rowan's "If Asked" Notes

**Q: Why is Lift so upset about growing up?**  
A: Lift specifically asked Cultivation not to change her. She wanted to stay her mother's little girl, and she's heartbroken that her mother wouldn't recognize her now because she has grown. This reveals deep grief and loss in Lift's past.

**Q: What's the difference between seeing Cultivation and the Nightwatcher?**  
A: The Nightwatcher is the one who typically grants boons and curses on the Nightwatcher's Path. Lift clarifies that she actually saw Cultivation herself, not the Nightwatcher, which may be significant.

**Q: What are the red and green chickens?**  
A: The chickens appear to be spren or fabrials. The red chicken is defeated by the green one, and Lift heals the red one. Mraize has the green chicken. Their full significance may become clearer later, but they seem related to the tower or the invasion.

**Q: Why does Lift feel resistance when trying to heal the red chicken?**  
A: This is unusual—normally healing with Stormlight doesn't have resistance. The fact that Lift can push through it suggests she has unique capabilities, possibly related to her bond with Cultivation or her Edgedancer abilities.

**Q: Who is Gereh and why is he significant?**  
A: Gereh is an old Alethi man in robes who was killed by a knife and robbed (missing rings). His identity and significance may become clearer later, but his death seems connected to the invasion or tower secrets.

**Q: Why does Mraize want to hunt Lift?**  
A: Mraize is a Ghostblood, and he says he has always wanted an excuse to hunt Lift. The Ghostbloods have their own agenda, and Lift may be a threat or asset to them. This sets up future conflict.

**Q: Why can Lift still heal if her powers are suppressed?**  
A: Lift's Surgebinding ("awesomeness") isn't working, but she can still heal the red chicken by pushing through the resistance. This suggests her healing ability may work differently than standard Surgebinding, possibly related to her unique bond with Cultivation.
