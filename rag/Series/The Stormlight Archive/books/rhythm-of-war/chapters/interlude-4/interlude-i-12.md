## Quick Summary
Taravangian is having a dumb day, which are apparently increasingly common. He is still trying to figure out how to defeat Odium, but it's increasingly difficult to think. He focuses on Nightblood and what they know about it, which is that it feeds on the essence that makes up all things. Specifically, it feeds on either Stormlight, or the individual's soul holding the sword if they don't have enough, comparing it to larkin. He thinks about how Odium has a core self, and that while he is intelligent, his humanity means he can be tricked. He realizes that he can't manipulate Szeth into doing what he wants, but he thinks he might be able to get Szeth to come to him during one of Odium's visions. As he thinks on this, he has a vision of Sja-anat. They discuss his condition and how Cultivation is responsible for his, Dalinar's, and Lift's gifts. Taravangian had asked for "the capacity to stop what was coming" in his meeting with Cultivation. Sja-anat doesn't know if that's possible, though she states that because the mind and the power are not united, it leaves him vulnerable. Taravangian realizes that she seems like she wants Odium to fail, which he finds surprising. He says he wants to draw Odium to him, and Sja-anat says that she will help by sending two corrupted spren to him inside gemstones which should draw his attention. Then she leaves.

## Rowan's "If Asked" Notes

**Q: Why are Taravangian's dumb days increasing?**  
A: Taravangian is having a dumb day, which are apparently increasingly common. This is because of Cultivation's gift—he has days of extreme intelligence and days of extreme compassion, but the balance is shifting, and he's having more dumb days. This makes it harder for him to think and plan.

**Q: What does Taravangian know about Nightblood?**  
A: Taravangian focuses on Nightblood and what they know about it. It feeds on the essence that makes up all things—specifically, it feeds on either Stormlight, or the individual's soul holding the sword if they don't have enough. He compares it to larkin (creatures that feed on Stormlight).

**Q: Why is Odium vulnerable?**  
A: Taravangian thinks about how Odium has a core self, and that while he is intelligent, his humanity means he can be tricked. Sja-anat states that because the mind and the power are not united, it leaves him vulnerable. This is because Rayse (the Vessel) and Odium (the Shard) are not perfectly aligned, creating a weakness.

**Q: What does Sja-anat offer?**  
A: Sja-anat says she will help by sending two corrupted spren to Taravangian inside gemstones, which should draw Odium's attention. This is part of Taravangian's plan to draw Odium to him so he can attempt to defeat him.

**Q: Why does Sja-anat want Odium to fail?**  
A: Taravangian realizes that Sja-anat seems like she wants Odium to fail, which he finds surprising. This is because Sja-anat is one of the Unmade (Odium's spren), but she's been corrupted in a way that makes her work against Odium. She's been helping humans and working against her master.

**Q: What did Taravangian ask Cultivation for?**  
A: Taravangian had asked for "the capacity to stop what was coming" in his meeting with Cultivation. This is what she gave him—days of extreme intelligence to see the future and plan, and days of extreme compassion to care about saving people. However, the balance is shifting, making it harder for him.

**Q: How will Taravangian get Szeth?**  
A: Taravangian realizes he can't manipulate Szeth into doing what he wants, but he thinks he might be able to get Szeth to come to him during one of Odium's visions. This is part of his plan to use Szeth and Nightblood against Odium.

**Q: Why is this interlude called "Vulnerable"?**  
A: The title "Vulnerable" refers to Odium's vulnerability—because the mind (Rayse) and the power (Odium) are not united, it leaves him vulnerable to being tricked or defeated. It also refers to Taravangian's vulnerability as his dumb days increase and his ability to think diminishes.

**Q: What is the significance of the corrupted spren?**  
A: Sja-anat will send two corrupted spren to Taravangian inside gemstones, which should draw Odium's attention. This is because corrupted spren are connected to Sja-anat and Odium, so having them near Taravangian will make Odium notice him, allowing Taravangian to attempt his plan.
