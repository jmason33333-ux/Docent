## Quick Summary
Syl flies through the halls of Urithiru while Kaladin sleeps. She encounters Cord cooking and receives an offering, noting Cord's Shardplate. Syl struggles with distraction and reflects on Kaladin's depression, which she finds difficult to understand. She asks both the Stormfather and Dalinar to help strengthen her bond with Kaladin or make her feel what Kaladin feels, but both deny her request. Dalinar explains that Bondsmiths can Connect things and that this power created both the Oathpact and Nahel bonds, but reassures Syl that she can still help Kaladin without such intervention.

## Rowan's "If Asked" Notes

**Q: Why does Syl want to feel what Kaladin feels?**
A: Syl is struggling to understand Kaladin's depression and wants to truly empathize with what he's going through. She feels that if she could experience his emotions, she might be better able to help him.

**Q: Why do the Stormfather and Dalinar deny Syl's request?**
A: The Stormfather tells Syl that she's putting Kaladin's needs above her own, suggesting that feeling his depression might harm her. Dalinar explains that while Bondsmiths can Connect things, he doesn't think that's the right solution here. Both reassure her that she can help Kaladin without such extreme measures.

**Q: What are Bondsmith powers?**
A: Dalinar explains that Bondsmiths can Connect things. This power was used to create both the Oathpact (which bound the Heralds) and the Nahel bonds between spren and humans. It's a fundamental power of connection and unity.

**Q: Who is Cord?**
A: Cord is someone cooking in Urithiru who has Shardplate. She gives Syl an offering when Syl encounters her. Her full significance may become clearer later in the story.
