## Quick Summary
Sja-anat moves through the Kholinar palace, existing on the edge between the Physical and Cognitive Realms. She reflects on how she can only keep a few chosen secrets from Odium. As she senses Odium approaching, she meets with two touched windspren (ones humans would call "corrupted") and sends them away as a distraction. She also sends a touched Radiant spren to Urithiru to observe Mraize and potentially bond with him or someone else the spren chooses. When Odium arrives, he questions her about the spren she sent away. Sja-anat maintains a pretense of working for Odium, though both know she wants more freedom. Odium commands her to ignore Urithiru and instead observe Taravangian.

## Rowan's "If Asked" Notes

**Q: What are "touched" or "corrupted" spren?**
A: These are spren that Sja-anat has altered or "touched." Humans would call them "corrupted," but Sja-anat sees them as changed. She has the ability to modify spren, giving them different properties or capabilities.

**Q: Why does Sja-anat want freedom from Odium?**
A: Sja-anat serves Odium but desires more autonomy. She can only keep a few chosen secrets from him, showing the limits of her freedom. Both she and Odium know she wants more independence, creating a complex relationship.

**Q: Why is Sja-anat sending a touched Radiant spren to Urithiru?**
A: She's sending it to observe Mraize (a member of the Ghostbloods). The spren is instructed to either bond with Mraize or whomever else it chooses. This may be part of Sja-anat's own plans or part of gathering information.

**Q: Why does Odium tell Sja-anat to observe Taravangian instead of Urithiru?**
A: Odium is redirecting Sja-anat's attention away from Urithiru and toward Taravangian. This suggests that Taravangian is important to Odium's plans, and he wants Sja-anat to keep an eye on him rather than focusing on Urithiru.

**Q: Who is Sja-anat?**
A: Sja-anat is one of the Unmade, powerful spren-like entities that serve Odium. She has the ability to "touch" or alter spren, changing their nature. She serves Odium but desires more freedom.
