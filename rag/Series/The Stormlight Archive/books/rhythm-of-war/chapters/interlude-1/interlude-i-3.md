## Quick Summary
Taravangian gathers his friends from the Diagram in his chambers at Urithiru to say farewell. Later that day, he will leave for Emul with Dalinar and Jasnah, while the rest of the Diagram will return to Kharbranth. Taravangian reveals his plan: in Emul, he intends to have his armies betray the coalition as part of his agreement with Odium. Jah Keved will also turn against the coalition. He announces that the Diagram has served its purpose and must be disbanded. Kharbranth is to distance itself from Taravangian's actions so the city can stay out of the war. After his friends leave, Taravangian reassures himself that saving a single city was really all he could have done, and throws his copy of the Diagram into the fire.

## Rowan's "If Asked" Notes

**Q: Why is Taravangian betraying the coalition?**
A: Taravangian has made an agreement with Odium. As part of this agreement, he will have his armies betray the coalition in Emul. His motivation seems to be saving Kharbranth—by distancing the city from his actions, he hopes to keep it out of the war.

**Q: What is the Diagram?**
A: The Diagram is an organization/prophecy that Taravangian created. It has served its purpose, and he is now disbanding it. The Diagram has been a major force in the story, but Taravangian believes it's time for it to end.

**Q: Why is Taravangian throwing the Diagram into the fire?**
A: This represents the end of the Diagram and Taravangian letting go of the past. He's accepting that the Diagram has served its purpose and it's time to move on. It's also symbolic of his acceptance that saving a single city was all he could do.

**Q: What will happen in Emul?**
A: Taravangian plans to have his armies betray the coalition there as part of his agreement with Odium. This will be a significant event when they reach Emul. Jah Keved will also turn against the coalition.

**Q: Why does Kharbranth need to distance itself from Taravangian?**
A: By distancing itself from Taravangian's actions, Kharbranth may be able to stay out of the war. This seems to be Taravangian's way of protecting his city, even as he betrays the coalition.

**Q: What is Taravangian's agreement with Odium?**
A: The details aren't fully revealed here, but it involves Taravangian's armies betraying the coalition in Emul. This is part of a larger deal between Taravangian and Odium.
