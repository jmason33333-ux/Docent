## Quick Summary
Szeth watches Dalinar play with Gavinor, attempting to slouch so as to appear as a normal guard. He reminisces on his past and converses with Nightblood about it. Szeth's highspren shows itself to him, and Szeth moves away from Nightblood to speak to it. It tells him he must begin to progress in his Ideals, both by moving towards his crusade and beginning to rely on his own interpretation of justice rather than Dalinar's. Then he listens to a messenger read off a list of things Taravangian requested, and gets very angry upon hearing a request for a stone matching the description of his Oathstone. Thinking Taravangian plans to kill Dalinar, Szeth decides he needs to stop him.

## Rowan's "If Asked" Notes

**Q: What is Szeth's crusade?**  
A: Szeth's highspren tells him he must move towards his crusade, but the exact nature of it isn't fully explained in this interlude. It likely relates to his desire to bring justice to those who wronged him or to prevent further harm. Szeth's crusade is something he needs to pursue to progress in his Skybreaker Ideals.

**Q: What is an Oathstone?**  
A: An Oathstone is a stone that was used to bind Szeth when he was Truthless. Whoever held his Oathstone could command him, and he was forced to obey. Taravangian requesting a stone matching the description of Szeth's Oathstone is highly suspicious and suggests he may be trying to control Szeth again.

**Q: Why does Szeth get angry about the Oathstone request?**  
A: Szeth gets very angry because Taravangian requested a stone matching the description of his Oathstone. This suggests Taravangian may be trying to control Szeth again or use him to kill Dalinar. Szeth thinks Taravangian plans to kill Dalinar, which is why he decides to stop him.

**Q: What does Szeth's highspren tell him?**  
A: Szeth's highspren tells him he must begin to progress in his Ideals by moving towards his crusade and beginning to rely on his own interpretation of justice rather than Dalinar's. This suggests Szeth has been too dependent on Dalinar's judgment and needs to develop his own sense of justice.

**Q: Why does Szeth try to appear as a normal guard?**  
A: Szeth attempts to slouch so as to appear as a normal guard. This shows his desire to blend in and not draw attention, possibly because he's still uncomfortable with his role or because he wants to observe without being noticed.

**Q: What does Szeth decide to do?**  
A: Szeth decides he needs to stop Taravangian. He believes Taravangian plans to kill Dalinar (based on the Oathstone request), and as Dalinar's bodyguard, he feels responsible for protecting him. This sets up his next action, which will likely involve confronting Taravangian.

**Q: Who is Szeth's highspren?**  
A: Szeth's highspren is the spren that bonds with Skybreakers. It shows itself to Szeth in this interlude and gives him advice about progressing in his Ideals. Highspren are associated with the Skybreaker order and focus on law and justice.

**Q: What does Nightblood say to Szeth?**  
A: Szeth converses with Nightblood about his past. Nightblood is a sentient sword that can communicate, and their conversation likely relates to Szeth's history as Truthless and his journey to becoming a Skybreaker. The exact content isn't fully revealed, but it's part of Szeth's reminiscing.
