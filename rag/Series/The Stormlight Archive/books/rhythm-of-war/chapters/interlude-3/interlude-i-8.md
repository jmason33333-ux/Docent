## Quick Summary
Chiri-Chiri listens to Vstim and Rysn having a conversation while musing on how much she has grown. Chiri-Chiri thinks the humans have wet and flappy mouths and strains to make out words, managing to overhear a short exchange about Dawnshards. Chiri-Chiri then senses an ancient rhythm signifying danger. Once Vstim leaves, Chiri-Chiri decides to attempt to communicate this danger to Rysn by talking. She only manages to form the words "Rysn eat food," but this surprises Rysn greatly, as she did not know Chiri-Chiri was capable of talking.

## Rowan's "If Asked" Notes

**Q: What is Chiri-Chiri?**  
A: Chiri-Chiri is a larkin, a type of creature that is Rysn's companion. Larkin are rare and have unique abilities. Chiri-Chiri has grown significantly and is now capable of communicating, which surprises Rysn.

**Q: What are Dawnshards?**  
A: Dawnshards are powerful artifacts mentioned in the conversation between Vstim and Rysn. They are ancient and significant, though the exact details of what they are or do may not be fully explained in this interlude. They are important in the broader Cosmere mythology.

**Q: What is the ancient rhythm?**  
A: Chiri-Chiri senses an ancient rhythm that signifies danger. This suggests that larkin (or Chiri-Chiri specifically) can sense rhythms that warn of threats, similar to how listeners can attune to rhythms. The exact nature of the danger isn't revealed, but it's significant enough that Chiri-Chiri tries to warn Rysn.

**Q: Why is Rysn surprised that Chiri-Chiri can talk?**  
A: Rysn is greatly surprised because she did not know Chiri-Chiri was capable of talking. This suggests that larkin don't typically communicate verbally, or that Chiri-Chiri's ability to do so is a new development related to her growth.

**Q: What does "Rysn eat food" mean?**  
A: Chiri-Chiri only manages to form the words "Rysn eat food" when trying to communicate the danger. This is likely a very basic attempt at communication, and the actual message (about the danger) isn't successfully conveyed. It shows that Chiri-Chiri is just beginning to learn how to talk.

**Q: Why does Chiri-Chiri think humans have "wet and flappy mouths"?**  
A: Chiri-Chiri thinks humans have wet and flappy mouths, which is her perspective on how humans look when they talk. This shows her unique viewpoint as a non-human creature observing humans, and it explains why she has difficulty making out words.

**Q: What is the danger Chiri-Chiri senses?**  
A: Chiri-Chiri senses an ancient rhythm signifying danger, but the exact nature of the threat isn't revealed in this interlude. It's significant enough that she tries to warn Rysn, but her limited ability to communicate prevents her from fully explaining what the danger is.

**Q: Who is Vstim?**  
A: Vstim is Rysn's mentor, a trader who has been teaching Rysn. He has a conversation with Rysn that Chiri-Chiri overhears, which includes mention of Dawnshards. Once he leaves, Chiri-Chiri attempts to communicate with Rysn about the danger she sensed.
