## Quick Summary
Taravangian wakes up on a dumb day and moves around his prison. He reads instructions he left himself about what to say to Szeth, including "Don't talk to Dalinar," and copies them into a notebook. He eats stale bread before realizing fresh food has been left for him. Renarin visits and tries to convince Taravangian to work with them. Taravangian doesn't answer, overcome by emotion, and Renarin leaves. His next visitor is Szeth, who wants to know why Taravangian asked for an Oathstone. Taravangian tells Szeth several things: Odium fears Nightblood, Szeth's father is dead, and Szeth must give Nightblood to Dalinar. Szeth refuses to do it and leaves, thinking it's only more manipulation.

## Rowan's "If Asked" Notes

**Q: Why is Taravangian on a dumb day?**  
A: Taravangian wakes up on a dumb day, which is part of his boon/curse from Cultivation. On some days he has transcendent intelligence (when he created the Diagram), and on other days he has high compassion but low intelligence. This is a "dumb day," meaning he's less intelligent but more emotional.

**Q: What are Taravangian's instructions?**  
A: Taravangian reads instructions he left himself about what to say to Szeth, including "Don't talk to Dalinar." He copies them into a notebook. This suggests that on his brilliant days, he plans ahead for what to do on his dumb days, leaving instructions for important conversations.

**Q: Why does Renarin visit?**  
A: Renarin visits Taravangian and tries to convince him to work with them. This shows that despite Taravangian's betrayal, some people (like Renarin) still think there might be a way to work together. However, Taravangian doesn't answer, overcome by emotion, and Renarin leaves.

**Q: Why does Odium fear Nightblood?**  
A: Taravangian tells Szeth that Odium fears Nightblood. This is highly significant because it suggests that Nightblood is a weapon that could actually threaten or harm Odium. This is why Taravangian wants Szeth to give it to Dalinar—it may be crucial for defeating Odium.

**Q: Is Szeth's father really dead?**  
A: Taravangian tells Szeth that his father (Neturo-son-Vallano) is dead. This is important information for Szeth, but given Taravangian's history of manipulation, Szeth may be right to be suspicious about whether this is true or just another manipulation tactic.

**Q: Why does Taravangian want Szeth to give Nightblood to Dalinar?**  
A: Taravangian tells Szeth he must give Nightblood to Dalinar. This may be because Nightblood is a weapon that could help defeat Odium (since Odium fears it), and Dalinar would be the best person to wield it. However, Szeth refuses, thinking it's only more manipulation.

**Q: Why does Szeth refuse?**  
A: Szeth refuses to give Nightblood to Dalinar and leaves, thinking it's only more manipulation. Given Taravangian's history of manipulation and the fact that he requested an Oathstone (which would control Szeth), Szeth is rightfully suspicious of anything Taravangian says.

**Q: What is the significance of "Don't talk to Dalinar"?**  
A: Taravangian's instructions include "Don't talk to Dalinar." This may be because talking to Dalinar might reveal information or because Taravangian wants to avoid certain topics. It's part of his plan for how to handle conversations on his dumb days.

**Q: Why is this interlude called "The Sword"?**  
A: The title "The Sword" likely refers to Nightblood, which is central to the conversation between Taravangian and Szeth. Nightblood is a powerful sword that Odium fears, and Taravangian wants Szeth to give it to Dalinar, making it the focus of the interlude.
