## Quick Summary
**Shallan:** While Godeke binds their wounds, a shocked Notum resists the idea of traveling with Adolin and his group. Shallan goes to speak with her Lightweavers, who are searching the dead, and finally admits that she doesn't think Beryl is the spy any more than Veil or Radiant do. They load Notum onto a horse and trek back towards their camp, meeting those who were going more slowly halfway there. Adolin orders them to move out immediately, so Shallan decides to check her cube before leaving. Discovering that it has been touched, she realizes that Pattern is the only one who has had access to it. At the point where they can finally see Lasting Integrity, Veil broaches the subject of Shallan's childhood with Pattern and is shocked to hear him lie to her. As a group of honorspren approach, Veil promises Shallan she will keep her safe.

**Adolin:** Adolin puts up a strong front as three honorspren arrive on white steeds and immediately dismiss him. He hands over the letters from Dalinar and Jasnah, which the spren tear up without reading. Realizing that he has no options left, Adolin declares that he has come to face the judgement of the honorspren for his ancestors' actions in the Recreance. The spren agree to admit him on those terms and concede that he may bring two others with him, as well as Maya. Conferring with the others, who don't like the plan, Adolin directs a few to take word back to Dalinar and the rest to camp out nearby. He takes Shallan and Pattern with him and enters the fortress in chains.

## Rowan's "If Asked" Notes

**Q: Why does Shallan realize Pattern used the cube?**
A: Before leaving, Shallan checks her cube and discovers it has been touched. She realizes that Pattern is the only one who has had access to it. This suggests that Pattern has been using the cube to communicate with Mraize, not Beryl. This is a major revelation that changes everything about the spy investigation.

**Q: Why is Veil shocked that Pattern lies?**
A: At the point where they can see Lasting Integrity, Veil broaches the subject of Shallan's childhood with Pattern and is shocked to hear him lie to her. Cryptics are supposed to be drawn to lies and patterns, but typically they don't lie themselves—they expose lies. Pattern lying suggests something significant about his nature, Shallan's past, or their bond.

**Q: Why does Shallan finally admit Beryl isn't the spy?**
A: After speaking with her Lightweavers who are searching the dead, Shallan finally admits that she doesn't think Beryl is the spy any more than Veil or Radiant do. This contradicts Chapter 30's conclusion, but makes sense now that Pattern's use of the cube has been revealed. The investigation may have been misdirected.

**Q: Why does Adolin enter in chains?**
A: When the honorspren arrive and dismiss him, tearing up the letters from Dalinar and Jasnah, Adolin realizes he has no options left. He declares that he has come to face the judgement of the honorspren for his ancestors' actions in the Recreance. The spren agree to admit him on those terms, and he enters the fortress in chains as a symbolic acceptance of responsibility.

**Q: What happens to the others?**
A: Adolin confers with the others, who don't like the plan. He directs a few to take word back to Dalinar about what's happening, and the rest are to camp out nearby. He takes only Shallan and Pattern with him into the fortress, as well as Maya. This leaves most of the team outside while Adolin faces judgement.

**Q: Why do the honorspren tear up the letters?**
A: When Adolin hands over the letters from Dalinar and Jasnah, the honorspren tear them up without reading them. This shows their complete dismissal of human authority and their unwillingness to listen to human arguments. They're not interested in what humans have to say—they want judgement for the Recreance.
