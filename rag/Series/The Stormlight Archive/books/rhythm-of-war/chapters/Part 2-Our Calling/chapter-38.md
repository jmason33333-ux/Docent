## Quick Summary
**Navani:** Navani discovers that the explosion killed the two researchers who were running tests on the dark sphere of Gavilar's she had left with them. She leaves Rushu to catalogue everything from the remains of the explosion.

**Venli:** Venli and the invaders have entered the chamber in the basement of the tower that contains a large crystal pillar of gems. She and Raboniel approach the structure through the corpses of human soldiers and scholars. Raboniel touches the pillar, and Voidlight surges from her into it.

**Kaladin:** Teft and Kaladin are in Jez's Duty and are joined by Rlain. They ask him about his experience during the bridge runs when he was being targeted by his own people, and Kaladin apologizes for desecrating the bodies of the dead to create their armor. They talk about how they haven't seen Dabbid in a while, and how they would like him to join the groups that Kaladin has been forming. After prodding, Kaladin agrees to join the groups himself, something he has been avoiding. He admits to himself that the work he has been doing has been helping him put himself back together.

**Venli:** Venli watches as Raboniel continues to infuse the pillar with Voidlight. Just as Raboniel finishes the first step, succeeding in activating and inverting the tower's defenses to turn them against the Radiants instead of the Fused, an alarm sounds as the humans realize that they are being invaded. Raboniel gives the order to seize the city.

## Rowan's "If Asked" Notes

**Q: What happened in the explosion?**
A: Navani discovers that the explosion killed the two researchers who were running tests on the dark sphere of Gavilar's that she had left with them. She leaves Rushu to catalogue everything from the remains of the explosion. The dark sphere may have been dangerous or unstable, causing the explosion.

**Q: What is the crystal pillar?**
A: Venli and the invaders enter a chamber in the basement of the tower that contains a large crystal pillar of gems. Raboniel touches the pillar and Voidlight surges from her into it. This pillar appears to control the tower's systems, and by infusing it with Voidlight, Raboniel can control the tower's defenses.

**Q: How does Raboniel invert the tower's defenses?**
A: Raboniel infuses the crystal pillar with Voidlight and succeeds in activating and inverting the tower's defenses. The defenses that were meant to protect against the Fused are now turned against the Radiants instead. This is a major tactical advantage for the invaders.

**Q: Why does Kaladin apologize to Rlain?**
A: Kaladin, Teft, and Rlain are in Jez's Duty, and they ask Rlain about his experience during the bridge runs when he was being targeted by his own people. Kaladin apologizes for desecrating the bodies of the dead to create their armor. This shows Kaladin's growth and his recognition of past mistakes.

**Q: Why does Kaladin agree to join the groups?**
A: After prodding from Teft and Rlain, Kaladin agrees to join the groups that he has been forming, something he has been avoiding. He admits to himself that the work he has been doing has been helping him put himself back together. This shows his acceptance that he needs help too, not just to give it.

**Q: What happens when the alarm sounds?**
A: Just as Raboniel finishes inverting the tower's defenses, an alarm sounds as the humans realize that they are being invaded. Raboniel gives the order to seize the city. This marks the official beginning of the invasion of Urithiru.
