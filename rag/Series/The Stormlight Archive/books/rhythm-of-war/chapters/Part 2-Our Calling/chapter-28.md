## Quick Summary
Falilar, Isabi, and other scholars set up the spanreed, attaching it to a scale to measure the amount of decay when the fabrial is activated. Navani converses with the other party regarding the suffering to spren that her fabrial experiments cause while the others take measurements. Falilar, discovering that the writer is close enough to be inside the tower, requests they move to a second location so they can begin to triangulate the contact's location. Agreeing, Navani and Kalami meet the others on the plateau in front of the tower and reinitiate the conversation. The phantom writer reveals themself to be a spren, but Navani discounts Kalami's suggestion that it is another of the Unmade. The spren severs the connection, and Navani devises a plan to force them to show their hand. On her way back inside, she kicks the spanreed over the edge in such a way that makes it seem like an accident. Though she directs Falilar to search for it, she privately intends to make sure that it is unusable.

A: After the spren severs the connection, Navani devises a plan to force them to show their hand. On her way back inside, she kicks the spanreed over the edge in a way that makes it seem like an accident. Though she directs Falilar to search for it, she privately intends to make sure it's unusable. This may be part of her plan to force the spren to act or reveal themselves.

**Q: What does Navani mean about suffering to spren?**
A: Navani converses with the spren about the suffering that her fabrial experiments cause to spren. This suggests that her research involves using spren in ways that harm them, which may be a moral issue. The spren seems to be concerned about this suffering.

**Q: Why do they move to a second location?**
A: Falilar discovers that the writer is close enough to be inside the tower. He requests they move to a second location (the plateau in front of the tower) so they can begin to triangulate the contact's location. By having measurements from two different locations, they can determine where the spren actually is.

**Q: Why does Kalami suggest it might be an Unmade?**
A: When the spren reveals themselves, Kalami suggests it might be another of the Unmade (like Sja-anat, Nergaoul, etc.). However, Navani discounts this suggestion. The Unmade are powerful spren-like entities that serve Odium, so if this spren were one, it would be significant.

**Q: What is Navani's plan?**
A: After the spren severs the connection, Navani devises a plan to force them to show their hand. The exact details aren't fully explained, but kicking the spanreed over the edge (and making it unusable) may be part of this plan. By cutting off their communication method, she may force the spren to reveal themselves or take action in another way.
