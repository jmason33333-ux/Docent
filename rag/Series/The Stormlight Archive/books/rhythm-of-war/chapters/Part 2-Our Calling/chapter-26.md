## Quick Summary
On the fourth day on the barge, Shallan tries to sketch while all four Cryptics crowd around her. She starts to panic as they get excited over a mistake, so Radiant emerges to excuse herself from the spren and wait until Shallan recovers. Veil wants to walk Shallan through finding the spy, but Radiant insists they make sure the communication cube couldn't have been moved accidentally first. As the three determine that none of them moved it but someone must have, Pattern approaches and discusses the nature of Cryptics with Shallan. Veil decides the best method is to feed each Lightweaver a distinct piece of misinformation and see which one Mraize hears about. Shallan approaches Ishnah, overhears Ua'pam and Unativi arguing, then manipulates Ishnah into thinking she already knows her secret. Ishnah confesses she unwittingly funded illegal enterprises. Mentioning the Ghostbloods elicits no reaction, so Shallan moves on to Beryl, who is practicing Soulcasting. Shallan feeds her misinformation about a corrupted gloryspren. The conversation overwhelms Shallan, so Radiant approaches Vathah. After Radiant's poor attempt, Shallan takes over and tells Vathah the Lightweavers are like family, then slips in misinformation about a corrupted fearspren. When Veil tries to bring up what's really bothering Shallan, Shallan retreats and forces Radiant into control.

## Rowan's "If Asked" Notes

**Q: Why are there four Cryptics?**
A: In Shadesmar, there are four Cryptics crowding around Shallan while she sketches. This may be because Cryptics are drawn to lies and patterns, and they're excited about her drawing (especially when she makes a mistake, which creates an interesting pattern). Pattern is one of them, and the others are likely just curious or drawn to the activity.

**Q: What is the misinformation strategy?**
A: Veil decides the best way to find the spy is to feed each Lightweaver a distinct piece of false information and see which one Mraize hears about. This way, if Mraize mentions one of the false stories, they'll know which Lightweaver reported it to him. Shallan feeds Ishnah information about seeing a corrupted windspren, Beryl about a corrupted gloryspren, and Vathah about a corrupted fearspren.

**Q: Why does Shallan get overwhelmed?**
A: The conversations with the Lightweavers, especially with Beryl, overwhelm Shallan. This causes her personas to take over—first Radiant when she needs to excuse herself, then Radiant again when she can't handle talking to Vathah. When Veil tries to bring up what's really bothering Shallan about the conversations, Shallan retreats and forces Radiant into control, suggesting she's avoiding something difficult.

**Q: What does Ishnah confess?**
A: Shallan manipulates Ishnah into thinking she already knows her secret. Ishnah confesses that she unwittingly funded illegal enterprises among her former acquaintances in the underground. However, when Shallan mentions the Ghostbloods, Ishnah shows no suspicious reaction, suggesting she's not the spy (or is very good at hiding it).

**Q: Why does Veil want to discuss what's bothering Shallan?**
A: After the conversations with the Lightweavers, Veil tries to bring up what's really bothering Shallan about the day's conversations. However, Shallan retreats and forces Radiant into control instead of dealing with it. This suggests there's something about the interactions that's troubling Shallan, but she's avoiding thinking about it.

**Q: What is Pattern's discussion about Cryptics?**
A: Pattern approaches Shallan and discusses the nature of Cryptics with her. The exact content isn't fully detailed, but it happens after they determine someone must have moved the cube. This may be Pattern trying to help Shallan understand something about herself or about how to find the spy.
