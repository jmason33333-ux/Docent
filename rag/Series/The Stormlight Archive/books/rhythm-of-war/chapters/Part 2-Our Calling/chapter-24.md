## Quick Summary
On the second day on the barge, Radiant sets assignments for her Lightweavers, then sits down to look through Ialai's book again. Radiant and Shallan discuss the ramifications of the Ghostbloods' search for Ba-Ado-Mishram in conjunction with their goal of taking Stormlight offworld. When Radiant tries to get Shallan to emerge, Shallan instead votes to end Veil's punishment and lets Veil take control. Veil immediately starts making terrible attempts at sketching. When she draws Adolin very badly, she finally goads Shallan into taking control. Shallan draws for five hours, at the end of which Adolin brings her a meal and sits down with her. They talk about Adolin's worries for the mission, which leads into Shallan confessing the real reason she sought to become Jasnah's ward: to rob her. Adolin just laughs incredulously, which lightens Shallan's feeling of dread. They trade bad jokes, and then Shallan asks for help getting the peakspren's clothing right. When Dreaming-though-Awake approaches, Shallan begins to sketch her. The mistspren investigates the drawing and is disappointed to see it's just her, having overheard their earlier talk of the Unmade. They ask her what she knows about Ba-Ado-Mishram, but she just tells them to ask the Heralds.

## Rowan's "If Asked" Notes

**Q: Why does Shallan confess she wanted to rob Jasnah?**
A: Shallan reveals the real reason she sought to become Jasnah's ward: to rob her. This is a significant moment of honesty with Adolin. When Adolin just laughs incredulously instead of judging her, it lightens Shallan's feeling of dread and shows his acceptance of her, flaws and all. This moment of vulnerability may strengthen their relationship.

**Q: What is Ba-Ado-Mishram?**
A: Ba-Ado-Mishram is one of the Unmade (powerful spren-like entities that serve Odium). The Ghostbloods are searching for her, and this search is connected to their goal of taking Stormlight offworld. When Shallan and the others ask Dreaming-though-Awake about Ba-Ado-Mishram, the mistspren tells them to ask the Heralds, suggesting the Heralds might have important knowledge about this Unmade.

**Q: Why is Dreaming-though-Awake disappointed by Shallan's drawing?**
A: Dreaming-though-Awake approaches Shallan while she's sketching and investigates the drawing. She's disappointed to see it's just her, because she had overheard their earlier talk of the Unmade and was hoping the drawing might be of an Unmade. This shows her interest in or connection to the Unmade.

**Q: Why does Veil make terrible sketching attempts?**
A: When Veil takes control, she immediately starts making terrible sketching attempts, including drawing Adolin very badly. This seems to be a deliberate attempt to goad Shallan into taking control, since Shallan is the skilled artist. It works—Shallan takes over and draws for five hours.

**Q: What is the connection between Ba-Ado-Mishram and taking Stormlight offworld?**
A: Radiant and Shallan discuss how the Ghostbloods' search for Ba-Ado-Mishram is connected to their goal of taking Stormlight offworld. The exact connection isn't fully explained, but it suggests that Ba-Ado-Mishram might be key to understanding how to transport Stormlight off Roshar, which is one of the Ghostbloods' major objectives.

**Q: Why does Shallan end Veil's punishment?**
A: When Radiant tries to get Shallan to emerge, Shallan instead votes to end Veil's punishment and lets Veil take control. This suggests that Veil was being punished for something (possibly related to the cube incident in Chapter 22), and Shallan decides to forgive her or let her back in control. The punishment system shows how Shallan's personas can be disciplined or restricted.
