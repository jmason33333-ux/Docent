> "I have begun searching for a pathway out of this conundrum by seeking the ideal person to act on my behalf. Someone who embodies both Preservation and Ruin. A … sword, you might say, who can both protect and kill."

---

## Quick Summary
**Adolin:** Adolin is with Gallant when land is finally sighted. Moving to the prow with the others, he tells them they need to let a couple of Lightweavers investigate the town before entering. The humans convene under the tarp, where Adolin wakes a hungover Shallan, who agrees to take Vathah and scout the settlement. While they, Ua'pam, and Unativi are gone, Godeke and Adolin discuss their waning Stormlight stores. Needing to calm his nerves, Adolin begins teaching Maya a new kata. He gets lost in the training exercise until Shallan's group returns and reports nothing unusual. As Adolin and Maya enter the town with a small group, he is transfixed by the different varieties of spren. After a brief exchange with Maya about why Shallan would choose to stay behind, Adolin realizes Maya is staring at another deadeye standing in front of a shop. Thinking it best for Maya to move on, Adolin tries to leave, but her insistent focus causes him to approach the shop and engage the shopkeeper. The inkspren tells Adolin the cryptic deadeye's story, disturbing him greatly when he realizes she was killed recently. As he turns to go, Godeke jogs up to tell him about a caravan of humans.

**Shallan:** Realizing this is a chance to be alone, Shallan tells Adolin she wants to rest as the others leave for Nameless. He reluctantly leaves her, so she goes to tell Unativi that she will watch over the barge if he and his crew wish to visit the town. Pattern stays behind and, correctly intuiting her intentions, wishes to hear her conversation with Mraize. She directs him to station himself where he can both hear the exchange and watch for anyone returning. After confirming the cube has been moved again, Shallan asks the cube if she can speak with Mraize and shortly hears his voice coming from the device.

## Rowan's "If Asked" Notes

**Q: Why does Maya stare at the other deadeye?**
A: Adolin realizes Maya is staring at another deadeye standing in front of a shop—a cryptic deadeye. Her focus is so insistent that Adolin approaches the shop to find out more. The inkspren shopkeeper tells Adolin the deadeye's story, and Adolin is disturbed to realize she was killed recently. This may be significant because deadeyes are Shardblades whose Radiants broke their oaths, and seeing another one, especially one killed recently, may affect Maya or reveal something important.

**Q: Who is Testament?**
A: Testament is mentioned in the chapter, and may be connected to the deadeye Maya stares at or to Shallan. Testament was Shallan's first Cryptic spren, who became a deadeye when Shallan broke her oaths. If the deadeye is Testament, this would explain why Maya (another deadeye) is so focused on her, and why it would be significant to Shallan.

**Q: What does Mraize say to Shallan?**
A: Shallan uses the communication cube to speak with Mraize, and she hears his voice coming from the device. The exact content of their conversation isn't revealed in this chapter, but Pattern stays behind to hear it, and Shallan directs him to watch for anyone returning. This conversation may relate to the spy investigation or Ghostblood plans.

**Q: Why does the cube keep getting moved?**
A: Shallan confirms that the cube has been moved again since the last time she checked. This continues the mystery from earlier chapters about who is using it to communicate with Mraize. It confirms that someone (possibly the spy) has been accessing it.

**Q: Why does Shallan stay behind?**
A: Shallan realizes that the others going to the town gives her a chance to be alone. She tells Adolin she wants to rest, and when he reluctantly leaves, she uses the opportunity to contact Mraize via the cube. She also tells Unativi she'll watch the barge, giving her privacy for the conversation.

**Q: What is the significance of waning Stormlight stores?**
A: Godeke and Adolin discuss that their Stormlight stores are waning. This may become a problem for the mission, as Stormlight is needed for Surgebinding and other abilities. They may need to find a way to replenish their stores or be more careful about usage.

**Q: What is the caravan of humans Godeke mentions?**
A: As Adolin is turning to leave after learning about the deadeye, Godeke jogs up to tell him about a caravan of humans. This may be significant—it could be other travelers, potential allies or enemies, or something else important to the mission. The details aren't fully explained yet.
