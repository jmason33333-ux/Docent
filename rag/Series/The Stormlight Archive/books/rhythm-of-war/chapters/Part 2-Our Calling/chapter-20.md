## Quick Summary
Radiant (one of Shallan's personas) visits her Lightweavers who are practicing in an underground chamber. Though Radiant disapproves of the Shadesmar mission, she respects the decision and tries to ease Shallan's burden by handling arrangements for which agents to bring. After giving sparring tips to Beryl and Darcira, Radiant decides to leave the most likely spy behind. She interviews the Lightweavers and settles on Ishnah, Vathah, Beryl, and Stargyle as potential spies. When Radiant and Shallan decide to leave them behind, Veil takes control and informs Ishnah, Vathah, and Stargyle that they are coming on the mission. Veil remains in control for the rest of the day and finds a note from Mraize instructing her to communicate via a strange cube he left in her dresser.

## Rowan's "If Asked" Notes

**Q: Why does Radiant think some Lightweavers might be spies?**
A: Radiant is being cautious about who to bring on the mission to Shadesmar. She identifies Ishnah, Vathah, Beryl, and Stargyle as the most likely to be spies based on her interviews and observations. However, Veil ultimately decides to bring some of them anyway.

**Q: How do Shallan's personas work?**
A: Shallan has multiple personas—Radiant, Veil, and Shallan herself. They can "vote" on decisions and take control of her body. In this chapter, Radiant takes control to make arrangements, but then Veil takes over and does the opposite of what they decided. There's a moment of nausea when control shifts, and Shallan herself can "pound weakly" trying to regain control.

**Q: What is Mraize's cube?**
A: Mraize left a strange cube in Shallan's dresser that allows communication with him. The note instructs Veil to use it to keep in touch during the mission. This suggests the Ghostbloods want to stay connected to Shallan/Veil even while she's in Shadesmar.

**Q: Why does Veil take control and do the opposite?**
A: Veil seems to have her own agenda and doesn't always agree with Radiant or Shallan. When they decide to leave potential spies behind, Veil takes control and tells them they're coming on the mission instead. This shows the conflict between Shallan's different personas and their different priorities.

**Q: Why does Radiant disapprove of the Shadesmar mission?**
A: The chapter doesn't fully explain her reasoning, but she respects the decision made by the others (presumably Shallan and Veil). Her disapproval may relate to concerns about safety, the mission's purpose, or other strategic considerations.
