## Quick Summary
**Kaladin:** Teft begins to seize at the same moment that Kaladin feels something strong press on him. He shakes it off and goes to check on Teft, whom he presumes had a stroke. They go to take him to a nearby clinic; Kaladin tries to Lash him to make him easier to carry, and it doesn't work.

**Navani:** Navani is with her soldiers and the Lightweaver Red to talk to the individual who had the gemstone to replace in the spanreed. They discover that it is Dabbid, who makes the Windrunner salute. They try to get answers from him, and he motions to the wall, while indicating in response to queries that he is not working with the enemy. Someone brings Navani the spanreed from the stranger, who writes that the tower is under attack. As the writer introduces themself as the Sibling, the spren of the tower, Red collapses.

**Kaladin:** Kaladin is stunned at his failed Lashing, and he searches for Syl or Phendorana but can't find either. Rlain says that the rhythms are going crazy, and as they arrive at the Edgedancer clinic, they find the Edgedancers unconscious. They decide to take Teft to Kaladin's father. As they try to find a way to carry him, Kaladin discovers that he can still do full Lashings, which use the surge of Adhesion. As they make their way, they learn that this is affecting all the Radiants except Kaladin, and Kaladin says the effect reminds him of the device that they found in Hearthstone. They arrive at Lirin's clinic and find it full of other Radiants. He calls again for Syl, whom he can feel distantly, and she emerges, describing feeling sleepy. Rlain arrives and says that Fused are in the tower and that spanreeds aren't working.

**Navani:** The Sibling tells Navani that the Fused are doing something to them, and Navani sends runners with instructions to scouting offices to carry out a predefined defensive plan. She and her scribes go to the map room, bringing an unchained Dabbid with her. Dabbid is able to communicate that he has been talking with the Sibling through garnet veins in the strata of the wall.

**Kaladin:** Kaladin panics and begins to disassociate and have flashbacks. He studies a scalpel, which he finally picks up. He tries to make his way toward the screams and fighting that he can hear, but he is overwhelmed with a cold sweat and a heavy weight. He is brought out of his detachment by Syl, who says that while she could not go as far as usual from him without losing herself, she learned that they are bringing in troops via the Oathgate. Lirin finds Kaladin and helps talk him down from fighting, saying that he is in no shape for it and that if they discover him, they will likely kill the rest of his family.

## Rowan's "If Asked" Notes

**Q: Why are the Radiants affected?**
A: When Raboniel inverted the tower's defenses in Chapter 38, it created a suppression field that affects all Radiants except Kaladin. Most Radiants collapse unconscious, and their Surgebinding doesn't work. Kaladin can still use Full Lashings (which use Adhesion), but other Surges don't work. This is similar to a device they found in Hearthstone.

**Q: Who is the Sibling?**
A: The Sibling is the spren of the tower Urithiru itself. They introduce themself to Navani via spanreed, writing that the tower is under attack. The Sibling can communicate through garnet veins in the strata of the walls. They tell Navani that the Fused are doing something to them, which is causing the suppression of Radiant powers.

**Q: How has Dabbid been communicating with the Sibling?**
A: When Navani brings Dabbid to the map room, he is able to communicate that he has been talking with the Sibling through garnet veins in the strata of the wall. This explains why Dabbid was missing—he was communicating with the tower's spren. He indicates he is not working with the enemy, showing his loyalty.

**Q: Why is Kaladin not affected?**
A: While all other Radiants are unconscious or can't use their powers, Kaladin is not affected. He can still use Full Lashings (Adhesion), though other Surges don't work. Kaladin says the effect reminds him of a device they found in Hearthstone. His immunity may be related to his unique bond with Syl or something about his nature.

**Q: Why does Kaladin panic?**
A: Kaladin panics and begins to disassociate and have flashbacks. He studies a scalpel and picks it up, trying to make his way toward the screams and fighting. He's overwhelmed with cold sweat and a heavy weight. This is his PTSD and trauma response to the invasion and the sounds of battle. Syl and Lirin help bring him out of it.

**Q: Why does Lirin tell Kaladin not to fight?**
A: Lirin finds Kaladin and helps talk him down from fighting. He says that Kaladin is in no shape for it (referring to his panic and trauma) and that if the invaders discover him, they will likely kill the rest of his family. Lirin is trying to protect Kaladin and the family by keeping him hidden and safe.
