## Quick Summary
Adolin takes a long time deciding what to wear into Shadesmar, and Shallan gives him a hard time about it before giving him an hour to be at the Oathgate. He chooses a uniform he had designed four years before, then visits Kaladin at his clinic. Adolin kids Kaladin about lighteyed women feigning illness to see him, and Kaladin's reaction shows this is already happening without him realizing it. Despite Syl saying Kaladin's job as a surgeon is helping, Kaladin reacts instinctively when Adolin makes a sudden movement. Adolin gives Kaladin a coin from Zahel to remind him that while war shouldn't be necessary, they live in an imperfect world. Syl gives Adolin advice about dealing with honorspren. At the Oathgate, Adolin gives instructions about his Shardplate, speaks with team members, and watches Jasnah observe the Lightweavers Soulcasting obsidian into lavis grain. After Dalinar, Jasnah, and Taravangian speak, Adolin and Dalinar have a tense conversation where Dalinar tries to tell Adolin not to make his mistakes, while Adolin feels misunderstood and avoids expressing his anger about his mother's death. The group then transfers into Shadesmar.

## Rowan's "If Asked" Notes

**Q: Why is there tension between Adolin and Dalinar?**
A: Dalinar tries to tell Adolin not to make his same mistakes, but Adolin feels misunderstood and belittled. Adolin has blocked-up anger about his mother Evi's death that he avoids expressing. Dalinar also expects Adolin to become a Radiant, which may add pressure. The conversation is tense and doesn't resolve their issues.

**Q: Why does Kaladin react instinctively when Adolin makes a sudden movement?**
A: Despite Syl saying that Kaladin's job as a surgeon is helping him, he still has soldier instincts. When Adolin makes a sudden movement to test him, Kaladin reacts instinctively, showing that his transition from soldier to surgeon isn't complete. He's still wired for combat even in a peaceful setting.

**Q: What is the significance of Zahel's coin?**
A: Zahel gave Adolin a coin to give to Kaladin. The message is that while war should not be necessary, they live in an imperfect world. This seems to be advice about accepting that sometimes violence is unavoidable, even when you'd prefer peace.

**Q: Why are they testing Soulcasting obsidian into lavis grain?**
A: Jasnah observes the Lightweavers performing this test before the mission. This may be preparation for something they'll need to do in Shadesmar, or it could be testing their abilities before the journey. Lavis grain is important for food, so this might be about ensuring they can create supplies.

**Q: What advice does Syl give Adolin about honorspren?**
A: Syl gives Adolin last-minute advice about dealing with honorspren. This is likely important for the Shadesmar mission, where they'll encounter honorspren. The specific advice isn't detailed, but it suggests they'll need to interact with honorspren in Shadesmar.

**Q: Why does Zu suggest Adolin should become a Radiant?**
A: When Adolin is showing off his swords, Zu suggests he should just get a Shardblade by becoming a Radiant instead. This highlights that Adolin doesn't have a Shardblade (he has Mayalaran, but she's a deadeye) and isn't a Radiant, unlike many others around him. Dalinar also expects him to become a Radiant.
