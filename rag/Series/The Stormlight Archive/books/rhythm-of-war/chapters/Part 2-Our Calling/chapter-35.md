## Quick Summary
**Adolin:** Adolin and Shallan return to camp, where they separate to pack up. Adolin loads his gear onto Gallant, then helps Maya up into the saddle. Around midday, Felt brings him news that the Tukari caravan has split off from trailing them. Calling for a break, Adolin follows the scout onto an outcropping and discusses the situation with him, telling him to keep a watch on them during their break. While on the way back to Gallant, he realizes what they must really be after.

**Shallan:** After confirming that the cube hasn't been touched and then grabbing a bowl of food from Ishnah, Radiant looks up when Adolin sprints past, making for Felt's outcropping again. She catches up to him on top, and he directs her to get everyone to follow him. After watching Adolin leap up onto Gallant's back as the horse gallops by, Radiant sets Pattern to watch their camp and follows after with the rest of the group.

**Adolin:** As they gallop past the human's caravan and see only a handful of humans, Adolin's suspicions are confirmed. They approach Notum's group, and, noting the signs of violence ahead, Adolin directs Gallant to stay out of the fight when they get there. After riding in close enough to see the Tukari stabbing Notum repeatedly, Adolin again tells Gallant to stay away and asks Maya if she would be able to cut Notum free if he distracted the assailants. Receiving what he takes as a negative, Adolin confronts the Tukari alone with his greatsword. He drives them away from Notum, using his momentum to intimidate them and thin their numbers. As soon as they back off, however, their leader organizes them so they can surround him. Adolin receives a spear through the abdomen, but he keeps fighting and soon finds that Maya has brought a sword and come to help him. He starts her going through the stances of his morning kata, and with his back now guarded and with Gallant's looming presence helping as well, he is able to kill or scare off the remaining Tukari. A group from the diplomatic mission arrive to find the Tukari gone and the wounded Adolin holding Notum.

## Rowan's "If Asked" Notes

**Q: What does Adolin realize the Tukari are after?**
A: When Felt brings news that the Tukari caravan has split off from trailing them, Adolin follows him to an outcropping to investigate. While on the way back to Gallant, Adolin realizes what they must really be after—Notum and the honorspren. This is confirmed when they see the Tukari attacking Notum.

**Q: How does Maya help in the fight?**
A: After Adolin asks if Maya can cut Notum free and receives what he takes as a negative, he confronts the Tukari alone. However, Maya brings a sword and comes to help him. Adolin starts her going through the stances of his morning kata (the training exercises he's been teaching her). With his back now guarded by Maya and Gallant's looming presence helping, Adolin is able to kill or scare off the remaining Tukari.

**Q: How badly is Adolin wounded?**
A: Adolin receives a spear through the abdomen during the fight. Despite this serious injury, he keeps fighting and is able to defeat or drive off the Tukari. When the rest of the group arrives, they find the wounded Adolin holding Notum. This shows his incredible determination and strength.

**Q: Why does Adolin tell Gallant to stay away?**
A: When Adolin sees the signs of violence and realizes the Tukari are attacking Notum, he directs Gallant to stay out of the fight. He does this again when they get closer. However, Gallant's looming presence still helps by intimidating the Tukari. Adolin may be trying to protect his horse, or he may want to fight on foot for better control.

**Q: What is the significance of Maya fighting?**
A: Maya bringing a sword and helping Adolin in combat is significant because she's a deadeye Shardblade. Typically, deadeyes are thought to be unresponsive, but Maya has been showing increasing awareness and ability. Her fighting alongside Adolin, going through kata stances, shows she's more than just a weapon—she's a partner.

**Q: Why do the Tukari attack Notum?**
A: The Tukari's true purpose is revealed—they weren't just following the mission, they were planning to attack Notum and the honorspren. This may be part of a larger plan, or they may have their own reasons for targeting honorspren. Adolin's realization and quick action saves Notum's life.
