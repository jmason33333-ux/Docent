## Quick Summary
Dalinar approaches Navani as she contemplates troops being transferred to Azir. She tells him again to watch for treachery from Taravangian before Gavinor walks in with his governess, ready to leave. Dalinar exits after him, and Navani gives her daughter Jasnah an awkward hug. After a short discussion, Jasnah heads to the Oathgate as well, and Sebarial walks up. They talk about Taravangian's inevitable betrayal, and then Sebarial obliquely solicits advice regarding Palona when Navani mentions her. Navani directs him to bring the warcamps back under Alethi control, then suggests he try to be more sincere with Palona. Navani and Brightness Anesa go over various reports regarding the tower as they ride to the atrium. Once there, she inspects a hidden room to which a Stoneward has created access. Falilar promises to conduct a thorough study of the scale model hidden in the room as well as to try to uncover the secrets of the gemstone found in the doorway. As she reenters the palanquin, Isabi runs up holding a blinking spanreed that indicates her mysterious contact wants to speak with her again.

## Rowan's "If Asked" Notes

**Q: What is the hidden room?**
A: A Stoneward has created access to a hidden room in the atrium of Urithiru. The room contains a scale model (which Falilar promises to study) and a gemstone in the doorway (whose secrets also need to be uncovered). This room may contain important information about the tower or its history.

**Q: Who is Navani's mysterious contact?**
A: Isabi runs up with a blinking spanreed that indicates Navani's mysterious contact wants to speak with her again. The identity isn't revealed in this chapter, but this contact has been communicating with Navani via spanreed in previous chapters. They may be a source of information or a potential ally.

**Q: Why does Navani keep warning about Taravangian?**
A: Navani tells Dalinar again to watch for treachery from Taravangian. This connects to Interlude I-3, where Taravangian revealed his plan to betray the coalition in Emul. Navani is aware of the threat and keeps warning Dalinar, though he may not fully believe her or may have his own plans.

**Q: What advice does Navani give Sebarial about Palona?**
A: When Sebarial obliquely asks for advice about Palona (his relationship with her), Navani first directs him to bring the warcamps back under Alethi control, then suggests he try to be more sincere with Palona. This suggests that Sebarial's relationship issues may stem from not being genuine or honest.

**Q: What is the scale model?**
A: The hidden room contains a scale model that Falilar promises to conduct a thorough study of. What it represents isn't clear yet, but it may be a model of Urithiru, the tower's structure, or something else significant. This could reveal important information about the tower's design or purpose.

**Q: What is the gemstone in the doorway?**
A: There's a gemstone found in the doorway of the hidden room. Falilar promises to try to uncover its secrets. This gemstone may be important for understanding the room, the tower, or may have some other significance. Gemstones are often used in fabrials and may have magical properties.
