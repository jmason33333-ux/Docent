## Quick Summary
Navani and her scribes reach the map room and find officers there, including a darkeyed battalionlord named Teofil. They discuss strategy and decide that the best thing they can do is to try to reverse what has happened by getting to the basement. Navani communicates with the Sibling through a garnet vein in the wall. She learns that the Sibling was asleep until they felt a Bondsmith in the tower but has subsequently stayed hidden. The Sibling recognizes Raboniel, calling her the Lady of Pains, and knows that she intends to go farther than just reversing the suppression abilities of the tower, that she wants to change who they are. The Sibling agrees to help Navani prevent that from happening and tells her to gather Stormlight.

## Rowan's "If Asked" Notes

**Q: What is Raboniel's true plan?**
A: The Sibling tells Navani that Raboniel (whom they call the Lady of Pains) intends to go farther than just reversing the suppression abilities of the tower. She wants to change who the Sibling is. This is much more dangerous than just seizing control—it's an attempt to fundamentally alter the tower's spren.

**Q: Why was the Sibling asleep?**
A: The Sibling tells Navani that they were asleep until they felt a Bondsmith in the tower (presumably Dalinar when he first arrived). They have subsequently stayed hidden. This suggests the Sibling was dormant until Dalinar's presence awakened them, but they've been hiding since then.

**Q: What does the Sibling want Navani to do?**
A: The Sibling agrees to help Navani prevent Raboniel from changing them. They tell Navani to gather Stormlight. This suggests they need Stormlight to power something or to help in the fight against Raboniel's plan.

**Q: What is their strategy?**
A: Navani and the officers discuss strategy and decide that the best thing they can do is to try to reverse what has happened by getting to the basement. This is where the crystal pillar is (from Chapter 38), so they need to reach it to undo what Raboniel has done.

**Q: Who is Teofil?**
A: Teofil is a darkeyed battalionlord who is in the map room with Navani and the officers. They discuss strategy together. He appears to be a capable military leader helping to plan the defense.

**Q: Why is Raboniel called the Lady of Pains?**
A: The Sibling recognizes Raboniel and calls her the Lady of Pains. This appears to be a title or epithet for her, possibly related to her nature as a Fused or her methods. It suggests she is known for causing suffering or pain.
