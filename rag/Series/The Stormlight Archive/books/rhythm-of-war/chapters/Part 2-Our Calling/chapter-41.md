## Quick Summary
**Venli:** Venli and Raboniel make their way through the tower and encounter the Pursuer. Raboniel gives him leave to carry out his tradition and kill Kaladin, though she gives instructions to kill only the humans that resist. Venli feels conflicted; she is part of an invading Fused force while becoming a Radiant, but she really just wants to stay neutral and only help her people. She realizes that Raboniel is primarily there because she is interested in the discoveries of the humans and their fabrials, wanting to partner with them to end the war for good.

**Navani:** Navani and a group of scholars follow Teofil and his soldiers through the ground level to the location of their planned ambush. A group of human soldiers who had been sent ahead retreat into the room, followed by a pursuing force of Regals. When the Regals enter the room, they notice the soldiers waiting in the room and release lightning at them. However, the humans allow the lightning to be drawn to two metal rods instead and kill the Regals with crossbows. The group then heads down towards the basement.

**Kaladin:** Kaladin brings blankets and rations to a Herdazian family. On his way to retrieve more, a singer confronts him; seeing Kaladin's build, the singer doesn't believe that he is a surgeon. After automatically blocking the singer's attempt to hit him, Kaladin realizes that the Regal is spoiling for a fight and makes the decision to back down. After knocking Kaladin to the ground and getting no more defiance from him, the singer walks away and leaves Kaladin to continue delivering blankets.

## Rowan's "If Asked" Notes

**Q: Why does Venli feel conflicted?**
A: Venli is part of an invading Fused force while also becoming a Radiant (bonded with Timbre). She really just wants to stay neutral and only help her people. This creates a moral conflict—she's helping invade Urithiru while also being bonded to a spren that makes her a Knight Radiant, who are supposed to protect humans.

**Q: What is Raboniel's true interest?**
A: Venli realizes that Raboniel is primarily there because she is interested in the discoveries of the humans and their fabrials. Raboniel wants to partner with them to end the war for good, not just conquer them. This suggests she sees value in human innovation and wants collaboration rather than just destruction.

**Q: How does the ambush work?**
A: Navani and her group set up an ambush. When Regals enter the room and release lightning at the waiting soldiers, the humans allow the lightning to be drawn to two metal rods instead (likely a fabrial). This protects the soldiers, who then kill the Regals with crossbows. This shows innovative use of fabrials in combat.

**Q: Why does Kaladin back down from the fight?**
A: When a singer confronts Kaladin and doesn't believe he's a surgeon, Kaladin automatically blocks an attempt to hit him. However, he realizes the Regal is spoiling for a fight and makes the decision to back down. After the singer knocks him down and gets no more defiance, he leaves. This shows Kaladin's restraint and his commitment to Lirin's advice about not fighting.

**Q: What is the Pursuer's tradition?**
A: The Pursuer (Lezian) has a tradition of hunting and killing Kaladin. Raboniel gives him leave to carry out this tradition, though she gives instructions to kill only humans that resist. This sets up a potential confrontation between the Pursuer and Kaladin.

**Q: Why are they heading to the basement?**
A: After the successful ambush, Navani's group heads down towards the basement. This is where the crystal pillar is (from Chapter 38), and their plan is to reverse what Raboniel has done by reaching it. This is their counterattack strategy.
