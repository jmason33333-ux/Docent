## Quick Summary
**Shallan:** As Veil begins to speak with Mraize, a sense that she has knelt like this before, communicating via a similar glowing cube, overtakes her. Veil tries to make Shallan remember, but Shallan has Veil take over again. Veil tries to trade for information on the cube, then reports they are being watched by corrupted spren. After telling Mraize there is nothing to report on events at Urithiru or in Emul, Mraize tells her to keep watching the corrupted gloryspren—confirming to Shallan that Beryl is the spy.

**Adolin:** Adolin, Godeke, Archinal, and Maya investigate a caravan of humans camped outside Nameless. After trying several languages, one speaks in broken Alethi to say they don't want to talk. Adolin and Godeke agree the group is definitely Tukari and leave pondering the implications. As they approach the barge, Adolin recognizes Notum speaking to Unativi.

**Shallan:** Pattern finds Shallan sketching the cube over and over. Shallan explains she has figured out that Beryl has been using the device to communicate with Mraize. Veil, suspicious the process was too easy, questions their conclusion. Shallan begins sketching the cube from her childhood memory, and Pattern tells her he is worried about her. In distress, Shallan forces Veil to take over again. Veil, hearing Adolin's voice, realizes his presence is what they need and goes to him but remains silent to avoid causing Shallan more pain.

**Adolin:** Adolin and Maya step up to speak with Notum. Adolin tells the honorspren about their diplomatic mission, and Notum tries to get him to see how futile he thinks it will be. They argue over the Recreance, and Maya growls when Adolin states that other honorspren should listen to Syl since they think she is so precious. Adolin calls out to Notum as he leaves, referring to him as a ship's captain. Notum tells him about his punishment following his decision to let their group go free before the Battle of Thaylen Field. He asks if they at least saved Dalinar, and Adolin is forced to admit it had actually ended up being the other way around. Notum promises to counsel against their mission should they meet again, but Adolin's resolve is only strengthened.

## Rowan's "If Asked" Notes

**Q: How does Shallan discover Beryl is the spy?**
A: When Veil speaks with Mraize via the cube, Mraize tells her to "keep watching the corrupted gloryspren." This is the exact piece of misinformation Shallan fed to Beryl in Chapter 26. Since Mraize knows about it, it confirms that Beryl reported it to him, making her the spy.

**Q: Why does Shallan feel familiar with the cube?**
A: As Veil begins speaking with Mraize, a sense overtakes her that she has knelt like this before, communicating via a similar glowing cube. Veil tries to make Shallan remember, but Shallan avoids it. Later, Shallan begins sketching the cube from her childhood memory, suggesting she had one as a child. This may connect to her past with the Ghostbloods or her family.

**Q: What is Notum's punishment?**
A: Notum tells Adolin about his punishment following his decision to let their group go free before the Battle of Thaylen Field. He was punished by other honorspren for this decision. He asks if they at least saved Dalinar, and Adolin is forced to admit it was actually the other way around—Dalinar saved them. This shows the consequences Notum faced for helping them.

**Q: Why does Maya growl?**
A: When Adolin states that other honorspren should listen to Syl since they think she is so precious, Maya growls. This may be because she's a deadeye (a Shardblade whose Radiant broke their oaths) and the discussion of honorspren and the Recreance may be painful or triggering for her. It shows she has feelings and reactions even as a deadeye.

**Q: Who are the Tukari?**
A: Adolin and Godeke investigate a caravan of humans camped outside Nameless. After trying several languages, one speaks in broken Alethi to say they don't want to talk. Adolin and Godeke agree the group is definitely Tukari. The Tukari are a people from the region, and their presence may be significant—they're following the mission, which could be a threat or complication.

**Q: Why is Pattern worried about Shallan?**
A: Pattern finds Shallan sketching the cube over and over, and when she begins sketching it from her childhood memory, Pattern tells her he is worried about her. This suggests Shallan's mental state is deteriorating, possibly due to the stress of discovering the spy, the memories the cube triggers, or her ongoing struggle with her personas. Her distress causes her to force Veil to take over.
