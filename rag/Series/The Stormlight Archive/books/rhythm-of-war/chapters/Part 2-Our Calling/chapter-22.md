## Quick Summary
**Adolin:** After thanking the gatekeepers, Adolin takes count of everyone and Shallan points out Maya to him. He hurries over to greet her, then takes her around introducing her to everyone, defending his actions to Dreaming-though-Awake. While redistributing load on Gallant, Adolin notices the horse has a slight glowing afterimage. He puts an arm around Shallan as they set off down the ramp, and Pattern hugs them both, excited about having feet again. Adolin spends the trip speaking with team members, getting details on Zu's past, discussing the Almighty with Godeke, and speaking with Arshqqam via her spren's translation. After discussing plans with Shallan, Ua'pam points out his cousin's barge. They pay the peakspren and board, then Adolin grooms Gallant while teaching Maya how to groom the Ryshadium.

**Shallan:** Checking her trunk, Shallan panics after recognizing that someone has used her cube, presumably to communicate with Mraize.

## Rowan's "If Asked" Notes

**Q: Why can Adolin interact with Maya in Shadesmar?**
A: In Shadesmar (the Cognitive Realm), deadeye Shardblades like Maya can interact more directly. Adolin can introduce her to people, teach her things, and she seems more present than in the Physical Realm. This suggests that in Shadesmar, the connection between a Shardblade and its bearer (or former bearer) is different.

**Q: Why does Pattern have feet in Shadesmar?**
A: Shadesmar is the Cognitive Realm, which is the native realm of spren. In Shadesmar, spren have physical forms, so Pattern has feet and a body. He's excited about this because in the Physical Realm, spren are more ethereal. Being in Shadesmar is like being home for spren.

**Q: Why is Shallan panicked about her cube being used?**
A: Shallan discovers that someone has used her communication cube (the one Mraize left for Veil in Chapter 20) to communicate with Mraize. This panics her because it means either someone else used it (possibly a spy) or one of her personas (Veil) used it without her knowledge. Either way, it suggests her communication with the Ghostbloods isn't as private as she thought.

**Q: What is Gallant's glowing afterimage?**
A: When Adolin is redistributing load on Gallant in Shadesmar, he notices the Ryshadium has a slight glowing afterimage. This may be how living things appear in the Cognitive Realm, or it could be something specific to Ryshadium. It suggests that physical beings have a different appearance or presence in Shadesmar.

**Q: Who used Shallan's cube?**
A: The chapter doesn't reveal who used it, but it was presumably used to communicate with Mraize. It could have been Veil (one of Shallan's personas), or it could have been someone else who found it. This creates suspicion and tension.

**Q: Why does Adolin introduce Maya to everyone?**
A: Adolin cares about Maya and wants others to know her. He defends his actions when Dreaming-though-Awake questions him, showing that he sees Maya as more than just a weapon. This continues the theme of Adolin's relationship with his Shardblade being different from typical Shardbearers.
