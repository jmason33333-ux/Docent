## Quick Summary
Adolin conducts a meeting with the Radiants to discuss how they should approach the honorspren. The Stump rejects all the ideas he comes up with, and Godeke and Zu are quick to agree with her. After Radiant asks Beryl if she has an opinion, Arshqqam chips in again to suggest that they somehow appeal to the spren's honor. When Adolin dismisses the meeting, Radiant speaks further with Beryl and concludes that she agrees with Veil's assessment that Beryl is not the spy. While everyone else is breakfasting, Adolin brings Radiant a ration bar and suggests they take a walk. Veil and Adolin don't go very far before they see a spectacular sight: Notum riding up to them on a brilliant white horse-like spren. Notum bids them goodbye after one last attempt to dissuade them from going to the fortress, as he must turn aside before he gets any closer.

Adolin and Veil continue to walk and talk, climbing onto a ridge that Ua'pam had told Adolin about. Adolin tries to reassure Shallan that he won't leave her no matter what, but it is the emergence of a starspren that finally draws her out. Shallan immediately begins sketching, exclaiming over the sight. They banter for a while, and a mention of Shallan's disastrous relationship with Kabsal leads to Adolin confessing the story of his mishap with Idani as a fourteen-year-old. A comment of Adolin's regarding how Idani treated Renarin causes Shallan to realize that he is feeling a mixture of emotions centered around Dalinar's expectations of him. Shallan shows him that she really understands how he feels, for which he is grateful.

## Rowan's "If Asked" Notes

**Q: Why does Radiant conclude Beryl is not the spy?**
A: After the meeting, Radiant speaks further with Beryl and concludes that she agrees with Veil's assessment that Beryl is not the spy. This contradicts Chapter 30, where Mraize's mention of the corrupted gloryspren seemed to confirm Beryl was the spy. This contradiction may indicate that either the conclusion was wrong, or there's more complexity to the situation.

**Q: What is the starspren?**
A: As Adolin and Veil walk and talk, a starspren emerges. This spectacular sight finally draws Shallan out from her retreat. She immediately begins sketching and exclaims over the sight. The starspren seems to have a positive, calming effect on Shallan, helping her emerge from her distressed state.

**Q: What is Adolin's story about Idani?**
A: A mention of Shallan's disastrous relationship with Kabsal leads to Adolin confessing the story of his mishap with Idani as a fourteen-year-old. The details aren't fully explained, but Adolin comments on how Idani treated Renarin. This story helps Shallan understand Adolin's feelings about Dalinar's expectations of him.

**Q: What does Shallan realize about Adolin?**
A: Adolin's comment about how Idani treated Renarin causes Shallan to realize that Adolin is feeling a mixture of emotions centered around Dalinar's expectations of him. Adolin feels pressure to live up to his father's expectations, especially regarding becoming a Radiant. Shallan shows him that she really understands how he feels, for which Adolin is grateful.

**Q: Why does Notum appear?**
A: Notum rides up to them on a brilliant white horse-like spren. He bids them goodbye and makes one last attempt to dissuade them from going to the fortress (Lasting Integrity). He must turn aside before he gets any closer, suggesting there's a boundary or rule preventing him from going further. This is his final warning before they reach their destination.

**Q: What happens in the meeting?**
A: Adolin conducts a meeting with the Radiants to discuss how to approach the honorspren. The Stump rejects all his ideas, and Godeke and Zu agree with her. Arshqqam suggests appealing to the spren's honor. The meeting doesn't produce a clear solution, showing how difficult it will be to convince the honorspren to help.
