## Quick Summary
Kaladin binds Adin's ankle and tells his father to bring him back after a week. Hawin leads Mil in next, and Kaladin forgets she's gone when he asks her to read the list of "migraine prodromes." Syl instead finds the right page and reads the list to Kaladin while he chats with Mil. After sending Mil away, Kaladin and Syl discuss further uses for their powers that don't involve fighting. Kaladin notices Syl is acting more somber than usual. Walking out to the balcony, Kaladin is spotted by Windrunners who salute him before flying to the Oathgates. He treats patients for the rest of the day, taking special note of Cressa's request to find her uncle. Going to ask Lirin about the man, Kaladin instead encounters Teft speaking with Hesina. Knowing Teft was mobilized with Bridge Four, Kaladin asks why he's still there. Teft explains he got approval from Dalinar to step down from active duty. Kaladin tries to tell him to go back, but quickly realizes Teft is dead set on his decision. Kaladin thanks Teft for sticking with him and tells him about their search for Noril.

## Rowan's "If Asked" Notes

**Q: Why is Teft stepping down from active duty?**
A: Teft got approval from Dalinar to step down from active duty even though the rest of Bridge Four was mobilized. He's dead set on this decision. Kaladin initially tries to tell him to go back, but quickly realizes Teft is committed. Teft seems to be choosing to stay with Kaladin rather than go with the rest of Bridge Four, showing his loyalty and perhaps his own need to step back from active combat.

**Q: Why is Syl acting more somber than usual?**
A: Kaladin notices that Syl is acting more somber than usual, but the chapter doesn't fully explain why. This may be related to concerns about Kaladin, the situation with Bridge Four, or something else. It's a detail that may become significant later.

**Q: What are "migraine prodromes"?**
A: These are early warning signs or symptoms that occur before a migraine headache. Kaladin is treating patients and needs to reference a list of these symptoms. When he forgets that Hawin has left, Syl steps in to read the list to him, showing how she helps with his medical work.

**Q: Why do the Windrunners salute Kaladin?**
A: When Kaladin goes out to the balcony, a group of Windrunners spots him and salutes him before flying down to the Oathgates. This shows that despite his transition to being a surgeon, he's still respected as a leader and member of the Windrunners. They're likely heading out on a mission.

**Q: What is the search for Noril?**
A: Kaladin tells Teft about their search for Noril. Noril was mentioned in Chapter 1 as a one-armed Alethi man who Lirin treated. The search for him may be related to helping him or finding out what happened to him. This may become a plot point later.

**Q: Why does Kaladin forget that Hawin left?**
A: Kaladin is still adjusting to his new role as a surgeon. He's used to having Bridge Four around to help, and he forgets that Hawin (who leads patients in and out) has left the room. This shows he's still learning the routines of his new job and adapting to working without his usual support system.
