## Quick Summary
**Adolin:** Zu points out to Adolin that the Tukari caravan is still trailing them. She proposes confronting them, but as Adolin has already informed Notum of the group and been told they aren't doing anything illegal, he just gets everyone moving forward again. After noticing Gallant's care for Maya, Adolin finds Shallan to speak with her.

**Shallan:** With the aim of confirming that Beryl is the spy, Shallan discusses Lightweaving with her for a while, ostensibly trying to help her master the art. After a brief internal argument, Veil takes over just as Adolin comes up and asks to talk privately, soliciting her opinion on how to approach the honorspren. They dismiss a couple of faulty approaches, causing Adolin to realize the spren will be ready for any argument they can bring up. He hopes to surprise them but isn't sure how. Wanting to speak with Shallan, he asks after her; Veil is relieved that he has noticed something is wrong, but Shallan is panicked. When Veil tries to explain a little of what is bothering Shallan, she causes Shallan to retreat even further even though Adolin is more determined than ever to find a way to help her.

## Rowan's "If Asked" Notes

**Q: Why doesn't Adolin confront the Tukari?**
A: Zu proposes confronting the Tukari caravan that's still trailing them, but Adolin has already informed Notum about the group. Notum told him they aren't doing anything illegal, so Adolin just gets everyone moving forward again instead of confronting them. This suggests the Tukari are within their rights to follow, even if it's suspicious.

**Q: What does Adolin notice about Gallant and Maya?**
A: Adolin notices Gallant's care for Maya. This shows that the Ryshadium has developed a relationship with the deadeye Shardblade, caring for her even though she's not a living spren. This demonstrates the depth of connection possible between bonded partners and their associated entities.

**Q: Why does Shallan retreat when Veil tries to explain?**
A: When Veil tries to explain a little of what's bothering Shallan to Adolin, it causes Shallan to retreat even further. This suggests that Shallan is not ready to face or discuss her problems, even with Adolin. The attempt to help actually makes things worse, showing how delicate the situation is.

**Q: Why is Adolin more determined to help?**
A: When Adolin asks after Shallan and Veil is relieved he's noticed something is wrong, Adolin becomes more determined than ever to find a way to help her. However, he isn't sure how to do it. This shows his care for her and his commitment to supporting her, even when he doesn't know the right approach.

**Q: What do they realize about approaching the honorspren?**
A: Adolin and Veil discuss how to approach the honorspren, dismissing a couple of faulty approaches. They realize that the spren will be ready for any argument they can bring up. Adolin hopes to surprise them but isn't sure how. This suggests they need a novel approach, not the standard arguments.

**Q: Why does Shallan discuss Lightweaving with Beryl?**
A: With the aim of confirming that Beryl is the spy, Shallan discusses Lightweaving with her, ostensibly trying to help Beryl master the art. This is a cover for Shallan's real purpose—to observe Beryl and confirm her suspicions. Since Beryl is confirmed as the spy, this interaction may be part of Shallan's investigation or an attempt to understand Beryl better.
