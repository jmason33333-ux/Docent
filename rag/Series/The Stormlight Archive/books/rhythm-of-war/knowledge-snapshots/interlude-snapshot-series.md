# Interlude Knowledge Snapshot Series: Rhythm of War

**Book:** Rhythm of War  
**Coverage:** All Interludes (I-1 through I-12)  
**Note:** Interludes may contain spoilers for main narrative chapters. Spoiler callouts are included where applicable.

---

## Overview of Interludes

The interludes in "Rhythm of War" offer diverse perspectives from characters across Roshar, often providing insights into events happening concurrently with the main narrative. They enrich the story by revealing character motivations, exploring lesser-known parts of the world, and providing critical foreshadowing for future events. These snapshots serve as narrative bridges and expand the lore of the series.
