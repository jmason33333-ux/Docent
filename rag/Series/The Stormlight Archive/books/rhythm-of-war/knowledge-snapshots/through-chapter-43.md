## Common Reader Questions (Up to Chapter 43)

**Q: How did the invasion happen?**  
A: Venli's group approached Urithiru and breached security systems. Raboniel infused the crystal pillar with Voidlight, inverting the tower's defenses to work against Radiants instead of Fused. Moash operated the Oathgates with an Honorblade to bring in troops (Ch 37-39, 42).

**Q: Why is Kaladin immune to the suppression?**  
A: While all other Radiants collapse unconscious or can't use their powers, Kaladin is not affected. He can still use Full Lashings (which use Adhesion). The reason isn't fully explained, but it may be related to his unique bond with Syl or something about his nature (Ch 39).

**Q: What is the blue shield?**  
A: After Navani and the Sibling infuse a sapphire, a blue light forms from the pillar creating an impenetrable shield as hard as glass. This blocks Raboniel from accessing the crystal pillar. The Sibling tells Navani she must destroy the gemstone to prevent reversal (Ch 42).

**Q: Why does Navani surrender?**  
A: After the Sibling tells her that her men have fallen and Raboniel will return to kill her, Navani surrenders to protect the people. The people will be allowed to live and work, but unconscious Radiants are to be delivered to the Fused (Ch 42).

**Q: What is Raboniel's offer to Navani?**  
A: Raboniel offers to hire Navani to work on fabrial projects together. Navani considers this because it would let her keep an eye on what Raboniel is doing and give her the opportunity to uncover Raboniel's ancient secrets (Ch 42).

**Q: Why does Kaladin kill the Regal?**  
A: When soldiers come to take Radiants into custody, Kaladin cannot bear the thought of them taking Teft. He tells them to leave Teft, but the Regal refuses. They fight brutally, and Kaladin kills the Regal in Lirin's clinic. This happens in his father's home, which makes it worse for Lirin (Ch 43).

**Q: Why does Lirin call Kaladin a monster?**  
A: After Kaladin kills the Regal in Lirin's clinic (his home), Lirin is in shock and furious. He cannot believe Kaladin murdered someone in his clinic. He tells Kaladin to leave, saying he believes his son has become a monster. This is a fundamental disagreement about violence and morality (Ch 43).

**Q: What is Raboniel's true plan?**  
A: The Sibling reveals that Raboniel (called the "Lady of Pains") wants to do more than just reverse the suppression abilities of the tower. She wants to change who the Sibling is—a fundamental alteration of the tower's spren, not just control (Ch 40).

**Q: Who is operating the Oathgates?**  
A: The Sibling tells Navani that there is a human with an Honorblade operating the Oathgates to bring in troops. Navani realizes it must be Moash. This reveals Moash is actively helping the invaders (Ch 42).

**Q: What happens to the Radiants?**  
A: When the suppression field activates, most Radiants collapse unconscious. Their Surgebinding doesn't work (except Kaladin can still use Full Lashings). The unconscious Radiants are to be delivered to the Fused, likely for experiments (Ch 39, 42, 43).

**Q: Why does Adolin enter Lasting Integrity in chains?**  
A: When the honorspren arrive and dismiss him, tearing up letters from Dalinar and Jasnah, Adolin realizes he has no options left. He declares he has come to face the judgement of the honorspren for his ancestors' actions in the Recreance. They agree to admit him on those terms (Ch 36).

**Q: What is the Sibling?**  
A: The Sibling is the spren of the tower Urithiru itself. They can communicate through garnet veins in the walls. They were asleep until they felt a Bondsmith (Dalinar) in the tower, but have stayed hidden since. They're helping Navani protect the tower (Ch 39, 40, 42).

**Q: Why does Venli struggle with killing?**  
A: When Venli and her company kill a patrol of soldiers, Venli doesn't handle it well. Raboniel is surprised, given her people's involvement in the War of Reckoning. Venli responds that she had been a scholar, not a fighter. She doesn't want to kill anymore (Ch 37).

**Q: What is group therapy and why does it help?**  
A: Kaladin sets up group therapy sessions where people with similar experiences talk together. He realizes that what actually helps is having a support group who has experienced the same feelings, not pity from those who haven't. This is revolutionary compared to locking people in dark rooms (Ch 33).

**Q: Why does Raboniel want to partner with humans?**  
A: Venli realizes that Raboniel is primarily interested in the discoveries of the humans and their fabrials. Raboniel wants to partner with them to end the war for good, not just conquer them. This suggests she sees value in collaboration (Ch 41).
