## World-Building / Lore (Chapters 11-19)

### Bondsmith Powers
- **First explained:** Interlude I-1
- **What we know:** Bondsmiths can Connect things
- **How it works:** This power was used to create both the Oathpact and Nahel bonds
- **Key examples:** Dalinar's abilities, Syl's request for stronger bond
- **Related concepts:** Connection, Nahel bonds, Oathpact
- **Unanswered questions:** Full extent of Bondsmith capabilities

### The Diagram
- **First mentioned:** Interlude I-3
- **What we know:** Organization/prophecy created by Taravangian
- **How it works:** Has served its purpose, now being disbanded
- **Key examples:** Taravangian's plan for betrayal, disbanding
- **Related concepts:** Taravangian's motivations, Kharbranth
- **Unanswered questions:** What was the Diagram's true purpose?

### Corrupted/Touched Spren
- **First explained:** Interlude I-2
- **What we know:** Spren that Sja-anat has altered
- **How it works:** Sja-anat can "touch" spren, changing their nature
- **Key examples:** Touched windspren, touched Radiant spren sent to Urithiru
- **Related concepts:** Sja-anat, the Unmade, spren bonds
- **Unanswered questions:** What are the full effects of being "touched"?

### Lifting Fabrials
- **First mentioned:** Chapter 19
- **What we know:** Prototype device for labor efficiency
- **How it works:** Can lift or move objects
- **Key examples:** Tomor and Falilar's prototype
- **Related concepts:** Fabrial technology, labor efficiency
- **Unanswered questions:** How will this be applied?

## Common Reader Questions (Up to Chapter 19)

**Q: What is the honorspren diplomatic mission?**  
A: The coalition decides to send a mission to the honorspren capital (Lasting Integrity) to seek their support. Shallan and Adolin volunteer for this mission (Ch 19).

**Q: What is Taravangian planning?**  
A: Taravangian plans to have his armies betray the coalition in Emul as part of his agreement with Odium. He disbands the Diagram and distances Kharbranth from his actions (Interlude I-3).

**Q: What are Bondsmith powers?**  
A: Bondsmiths can Connect things. This power was used to create both the Oathpact (binding the Heralds) and the Nahel bonds between spren and humans (Interlude I-1).

**Q: Who is Sja-anat and what does she do?**  
A: Sja-anat is one of the Unmade, powerful spren-like entities that serve Odium. She can "touch" or alter spren, changing their nature. She serves Odium but desires more freedom (Interlude I-2).

**Q: What is a corrupted or touched spren?**  
A: These are spren that Sja-anat has altered. She sends touched windspren and a touched Radiant spren to Urithiru. The touched spren may have different properties or capabilities (Interlude I-2, Ch 19).

**Q: Why does Venli feel conflicted?**  
A: Venli is secretly bonded to Timbre (making her a Knight Radiant) while serving the Fused. This creates a moral conflict as she's helping the enemy while being a Radiant (Ch 11).

**Q: What is the Diagram?**  
A: The Diagram is an organization/prophecy created by Taravangian. It has served its purpose, and Taravangian is now disbanding it. Members are returning to Kharbranth (Interlude I-3).

**Q: What did Navani discover about the suppression fabrial?**  
A: Navani discovers that the captured suppression fabrial contains a corrupted spren. This may be significant for understanding Sja-anat's activities or fabrial mechanics (Ch 19).

**Q: Why does Syl want to strengthen her bond with Kaladin?**  
A: Syl is concerned about Kaladin's depression and wants to better understand and help him. She asks both the Stormfather and Dalinar, but both deny her request while reassuring her she can still help (Interlude I-1).

**Q: What is the attack on Emul?**  
A: The coalition plans an attack on Emul as part of their military strategy. However, Taravangian plans to betray them there as part of his agreement with Odium (Ch 19, Interlude I-3).
