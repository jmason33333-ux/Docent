## World-Building / Lore

### Surgebinding Suppression in Urithiru
- **First explained:** Chapter 47
- **What we know:** A device, possibly created by Taravangian, suppresses Radiant powers and disables Oathgates
- **How it works:** Not fully explained; affects Radiants within Urithiru
- **Limitations/Rules:** Specific to Urithiru; unclear if reversible or extendable
- **Key examples:** Kaladin's powers are suppressed, Chapter 44; mentioned in communication to Dalinar, Chapter 47
- **Related concepts:** Ties into fabrial technology and Odium's tactics
- **Unanswered questions:** Can the suppression be lifted? How widespread is this technology?

### Listener Songs
- **First explained:** Chapter 45
- **What we know:** Cultural and historical songs that preserve listener knowledge and guide their actions
- **How it works:** Recited by listeners to maintain connection with their heritage
- **Limitations/Rules:** Songs are limited to what has been passed down; not all-encompassing
- **Key examples:** Venli and her mother recite songs, Chapter 45
- **Related concepts:** Connects to listener identity and resistance
- **Unanswered questions:** How accurate and complete are the songs? What secrets might they hold?
