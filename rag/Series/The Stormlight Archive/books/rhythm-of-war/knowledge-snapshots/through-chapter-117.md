## World-Building / Lore

### Surgebinding
- **First explained:** Ongoing, expanded in Chapter 110
- **What we know:** A magic system allowing manipulation of fundamental forces through bonds with spren.
- **How it works:** Surgebinders use Stormlight to fuel their abilities, which are tied to specific oaths and ideals.
- **Limitations/Rules:** Requires Stormlight, dependent on oaths, and specific to types of spren bonds.
- **Key examples:** Kaladin's Lashing in Chapter 110
- **Related concepts:** Tied to Honor and spren bonding
- **Unanswered questions:** Full extent of powers and potential new abilities

### Anti-Voidlight
- **First explained:** Expanded in Chapter 113
- **What we know:** A form of light that counteracts Voidlight, used against Fused.
- **How it works:** Created through fabrial experimentation, requires precise manipulation and energy.
- **Limitations/Rules:** Dangerous, difficult to control
- **Key examples:** Navani's use against Raboniel in Chapter 113
- **Related concepts:** Fabrials, Voidlight
- **Unanswered questions:** Potential broader applications and risks
