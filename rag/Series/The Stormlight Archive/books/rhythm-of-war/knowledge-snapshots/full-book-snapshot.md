## Book Overview
"Rhythm of War" continues the epic saga of Roshar, focusing on the war between humans and the Fused, as well as the inner conflicts within the Knights Radiant. The book explores themes of trust, identity, and the struggle for power, featuring complex character arcs and deepening the lore of the world with the exploration of fabrials and the rhythms of Roshar.

The narrative is split into multiple perspectives, with significant focus on characters like Kaladin, Shallan, Navani, and Venli. Kaladin faces his own mental health challenges while leading the Windrunners. Navani discovers the secrets of the Tower and the true nature of the Sibling. Shallan battles internal and external threats as she uncovers secrets about the Ghostbloods. Venli's journey highlights the struggle between personal loyalty and cultural duty. The book culminates in a thrilling climax within Urithiru, where bonds are tested and critical secrets are uncovered.

Ultimately, "Rhythm of War" sets the stage for future conflicts, leaving several threads unresolved while resolving key character arcs and deepening the understanding of Roshar's magical systems.

## Book Structure (Book > Parts > Chapters)

### Part 1: Burdens (Chapters 1-19)
- **Overview:** The story begins with the aftermath of the Battle of Thaylen Field, focusing on rebuilding efforts and the internal struggles of key characters.
- **Key Events:** Kaladin is relieved from active duty due to his mental health; Shallan investigates the Ghostbloods; Navani works on fabrial technology.
- **Themes:** Burdens of leadership, identity crisis, mental health.

### Part 2: Our Calling (Chapters 20-43)
- **Overview:** Characters are faced with defining their roles and callings as the war with the Fused intensifies.
- **Key Events:** Navani's research into the Tower's secrets; Kaladin's fight against his mental struggles; Venli's dual loyalties.
- **Themes:** Duty versus desire, the quest for knowledge, internal conflict.

### Part 3: Songs of Home (Chapters 44-72)
- **Overview:** The narrative delves into the cultural and historical aspects of Roshar, emphasizing the Parshendi's perspective.
- **Key Events:** Venli's inner transformation and connections with spren; Shallan's deepening involvement with the Ghostbloods.
- **Themes:** Cultural identity, transformation, the power of unity.

### Part 4: A Knowledge of Storms (Chapters 73-97)
- **Overview:** Characters face the storm of war both literally and metaphorically, pushing towards significant revelations.
- **Key Events:** Navani's discovery of the Sibling; Kaladin's stand against the Fused; Shallan's confrontation with her past.
- **Themes:** Knowledge as power, confronting the past, resilience.

### Part 5: Knowing a Home of Songs, Called Our Burden (Chapters 98-117)
- **Overview:** The climax and resolution of key plotlines, set against the backdrop of a major battle in Urithiru.
- **Key Events:** The battle for the Tower; Navani's bonding with the Sibling; Venli's decision to aid the humans.
- **Themes:** Home and belonging, the weight of legacy, victory through understanding.
