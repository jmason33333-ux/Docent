## Part Overview
In Part 2 of "Rhythm of War," titled "Our Calling," the narrative follows several key storylines centered around the characters' missions and personal struggles. Shallan, Adolin, and their group venture into Shadesmar on a diplomatic mission to gain the support of the honorspren, while Shallan grapples with internal conflicts and espionage within her ranks. Meanwhile, Kaladin faces challenges back in Urithiru, dealing with his mental health and the complexities of leadership as the tower comes under siege. Navani is embroiled in scholarly pursuits and the sudden need to strategize against an unexpected invasion, while Venli navigates her dual loyalties as a Fused and a budding Radiant.

The part explores themes of identity, leadership, and trust against the backdrop of an impending invasion. The characters must confront their pasts, their allegiances, and their roles in the larger struggle against Odium. As the invasion of Urithiru progresses, the stakes are raised, forcing each character to adapt and respond to rapidly changing circumstances.

A: The Sibling, introduced in Chapter 40, is a spren crucial to Urithiru's function and defenses, integral to the tower's survival.

**Q: How does Kaladin's role change during the invasion?**  
A: Despite the suppression of Radiants in Chapter 39, Kaladin remains active, using his unique abilities and leadership to navigate the crisis.

**Q: What internal conflicts does Venli face?**  
A: Throughout Part 2, Venli struggles with her identity as a Fused and a budding Radiant, particularly highlighted in Chapter 38.

**Q: How does Adolin plan to gain the honorspren's support?**  
A: In Chapter 36, Adolin offers to face judgment for his ancestors' actions, hoping to secure the honorspren's aid through honor and sacrifice.

**Q: What are the implications of Navani's surrender?**  
A: In Chapter 43, Navani's surrender underlines the gravity of the invasion and sets up a new phase of resistance under singer rule.

**Q: How does Shallan manage her multiple personas?**  
A: Shallan's personas, including Radiant and Veil, manage different tasks and conflicts, as seen in Chapters 20 and 26, but create internal tension and confusion.

**Q: What is the Tukari caravan's purpose in Shadesmar?**  
A: The caravan, observed in Chapters 29 and 32, adds external threat dynamics to the Shadesmar mission, complicating Adolin and Shallan's journey.
