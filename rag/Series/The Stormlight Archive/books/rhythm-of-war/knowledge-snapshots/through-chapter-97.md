## World-Building / Lore

### Rhythms and Tones
- **First explained:** Chapter 97
- **What we know:** Navani is exploring the connection between rhythms and fabrials, as well as their potential to affect the world.
- **How it works:** Rhythms are fundamental to the functioning of fabrials and the wider magic system.
- **Limitations/Rules:** Still under exploration by Navani, with potential unknowns.
- **Key examples:** Navani's research in Chapter 97.
- **Related concepts:** Fabrials, Surgebinding
- **Unanswered questions:** What is the full potential of rhythms, and how do they interact with other magic systems?

### Honorspren
- **First explained:** Chapter 94
- **What we know:** They are key to the trial of Adolin and are significant in the alliance with humans.
- **How it works:** Honorspren judge the worthiness and honor of individuals.
- **Limitations/Rules:** Highly selective and principled, often at odds with human actions.
- **Key examples:** Sekeir's role in the trial in Chapter 94.
- **Related concepts:** Nahel bond, Radiant spren
- **Unanswered questions:** What will it take to sway them to ally with humans?
