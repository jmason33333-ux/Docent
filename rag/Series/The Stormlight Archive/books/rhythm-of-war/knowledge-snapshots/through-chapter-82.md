## Major Characters (Introduced So Far)

### Venli
- **First appearance:** Chapter 73
- **Role:** A listener who has been secretly aiding Ulim in bringing voidspren into the physical realm.
- **Key relationships:**

  - **Ulim:** A voidspren who influences her actions, often mocking her and highlighting her ignorance about humans.
- **Motivations/Goals:** Initially driven by ambition and a desire for power, Venli's motivations are complicated by her growing awareness of the consequences of her actions.
- **Character Arc (through Ch 82):**

  - **Key moments:** Flashbacks reveal her involvement in the events leading to the unification of listener families and her growing doubt about Ulim's plans.
  - **Current state:** Torn between her past decisions and the looming consequences.
- **Key scenes/chapters:** Chapters 73, 74, 77, 81

## World-Building / Lore

### Voidbinding and Voidspren
- **First explained:** Chapter 73
- **What we know:** Voidspren like Ulim are being brought into the physical realm to assist in Odium’s goals.
- **How it works:** Requires a willing host or a gemheart to manifest.
- **Limitations/Rules:** Not all listeners are willing to host voidspren, and there are risks involved.
- **Key examples:** Venli’s interactions with Ulim and the presence of voidspren in spheres.
- **Related concepts:** Tied to Odium’s influence and the larger cosmere magic systems.
- **Unanswered questions:** What are the full capabilities of voidbinding, and how can it be countered?

### Honorspren and Shadesmar
- **First explained:** Chapter 75
- **What we know:** Honorspren are crucial for forming bonds with Radiants and are currently skeptical of humans.
- **How it works:** They reside in Shadesmar, where they govern their own city, Lasting Integrity.
- **Limitations/Rules:** They are bound by strict honor codes and wary of betrayal.
- **Key examples:** Adolin’s attempts to negotiate with them during the diplomatic mission.
- **Related concepts:** Connected to the broader spren-human alliances and the Radiants.
- **Unanswered questions:** What will convince the honorspren to support the humans?
