## Part Overview

Part 3: Songs of Home in "Rhythm of War" explores the ongoing invasion and occupation of Urithiru by Odium's forces, focusing on key characters like Kaladin, Navani, and Venli as they navigate perilous situations. The narrative is interspersed with flashbacks that delve into the pasts of Eshonai and Venli, providing context for the present events and exploring the history of the listeners and their interactions with humans.

The themes in this part revolve around resistance, the struggle for power, and the exploration of identity and belonging. Characters are pushed to their limits, revealing their inner strengths and vulnerabilities. The occupation forces individuals like Kaladin and Navani to adapt and find new ways to fight back, while Venli grapples with her dual identity and the implications of her past actions. The part culminates in a series of tense confrontations and realizations that set the stage for future conflicts and resolutions.
