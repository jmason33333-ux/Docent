# Part-Level Knowledge Snapshot: Our Calling

**Book:** Rhythm of War  
**Part:** 2: Our Calling  
**Coverage:** Chapters 20-43  
**Safe for readers up to:** Chapter 43 (end of this part)

---

## Part Overview
Part 2: Our Calling delves into the preparations and initial stages of the mission to Shadesmar, interweaving with the defensive efforts at Urithiru. The narrative arc follows two primary threads: Adolin and Shallan's journey through Shadesmar to seek the support of the honorspren, and the escalating tensions at Urithiru as they prepare for and experience an invasion. Key themes include identity and the burden of leadership, as seen through Shallan's struggles with her multiple personas and Adolin's quest for redemption. The part also explores mental health and its stigmatization, primarily through Kaladin's efforts to provide treatment for mental illness and his own struggles with PTSD.

A: She uses misinformation to see what reaches Mraize, eventually suspecting Beryl (Ch 26).

**Q: What is the significance of the deadeye spren in Shadesmar?**  
A: It highlights the ongoing effects of the Recreance and the tensions between spren and Radiants (Ch 29).

**Q: Why can't Kaladin use his Lashings during the invasion?**  
A: Raboniel has manipulated the tower's defenses, suppressing Radiant powers (Ch 39).

**Q: What motivates Venli during the invasion?**  
A: She is torn between her loyalty to her people and her growing identity as a Radiant (Ch 41).

**Q: What does Adolin hope to achieve by confronting the honorspren?**  
A: He seeks their support for Dalinar's coalition by accepting judgment for the Recreance (Ch 36).

**Q: How does Navani plan to counter Raboniel's control of Urithiru?**  
A: She communicates with the Sibling to devise a strategy to reverse the changes (Ch 40).

**Q: What role does the Sibling play in Urithiru's defense?**  
A: The Sibling is integral to the tower's functions and may help restore its defenses (Ch 40).

**Q: What are the implications of Navani's fabrial discoveries?**  
A: They could shift the balance of power and provide new tools against the Fused (Ch 28).

**Q: How does Adolin's interaction with Maya reflect his character growth?**  
A: His care for Maya shows his empathy and understanding of spren, indicating growth beyond traditional Radiant-spren dynamics (Ch 29).

**Q: What challenges does Kaladin face with his father during the invasion?**  
A: Kaladin and Lirin clash over resistance and morality, highlighting their differing views on survival and honor (Ch 43).
