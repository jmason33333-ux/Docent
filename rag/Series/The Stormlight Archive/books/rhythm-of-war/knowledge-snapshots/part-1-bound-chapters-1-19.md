# Part-Level Knowledge Snapshot: Burdens

**Book:** Rhythm of War  
**Part:** 1: Burdens  
**Coverage:** Chapters 1-19  
**Safe for readers up to:** Chapter 19 (end of this part)

---

## Part Overview
In Part 1: Burdens, the narrative explores the impact of leadership, trauma, and responsibility on key characters. Kaladin struggles with his mental health and the heavy burden of leadership amidst the ongoing war against Odium's forces. Shallan faces her fractured identity and the complex politics surrounding the Ghostbloods. Navani delves into fabrial technology but is confronted by the mysteries and dangers it holds. Venli juggles her dual role as a servant to the Fused while secretly nurturing her Knight Radiant potential. The part sets the stage for strategic maneuvers, personal challenges, and the looming threat of the Fused's plans.

Themes of burden and responsibility are prevalent as characters navigate their roles in a war-torn landscape. The narrative arc focuses on setting up significant character development and plot threads that will drive the larger story forward. The part concludes with strategic discussions among the coalition, highlighting the importance of unity and innovation in the face of impending conflict.
