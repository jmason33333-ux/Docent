## World-Building / Lore

### Surgebinding
- **First explained:** Chapter 100
- **What we know:** Magic system allowing users to manipulate natural forces through a Nahel bond with a spren
- **How it works:** Involves manipulating gravity, pressure, and other forces
- **Limitations/Rules:** Requires Stormlight, limited by the user's skills and oaths
- **Key examples:** Kaladin’s use in battles (Ch 100, 105)
- **Related concepts:** Connects to the broader Cosmere magic systems
- **Unanswered questions:** Full extent of the abilities and interactions with other magic systems

### Oathgates
- **First explained:** Chapter 102
- **What we know:** Ancient transportation devices used to travel between cities instantly
- **How it works:** Requires a Radiant to activate with Stormlight
- **Limitations/Rules:** Currently under control of occupation forces, limiting access
- **Key examples:** Venli’s plans to use them (Ch 102)
- **Related concepts:** Part of the larger fabrial technology in Roshar
- **Unanswered questions:** How to regain control and use effectively
