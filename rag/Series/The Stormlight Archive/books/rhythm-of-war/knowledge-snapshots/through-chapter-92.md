## World-Building / Lore

### Investiture and Fabrial Science
- **First explained:** Chapter 84
- **What we know:** Investiture can enhance minds and memories, and fabrials can be used creatively in warfare.
- **How it works:** Investiture is harnessed through fabrials, which can be disguised or used strategically.
- **Limitations/Rules:** Requires expertise and understanding of the underlying mechanics
- **Key examples:** Navani's disguised fabrial weapons (Ch 84)
- **Related concepts:** Connects to broader themes of magical technology and its applications
- **Unanswered questions:** How far can fabrial science be pushed, and what are its ultimate limits?

### Shadesmar and the Spren Trial
- **First explained:** Chapter 87
- **What we know:** Shadesmar is the cognitive realm where spren reside, and trials here can affect real-world dynamics.
- **How it works:** Trials are conducted by spren to judge human actions and intentions.
- **Limitations/Rules:** Governed by spren laws and customs
- **Key examples:** Adolin's trial in Lasting Integrity (Ch 87, 90)
- **Related concepts:** Spren-human relationships, cognitive realm politics
- **Unanswered questions:** What are the long-term implications of Adolin's trial on human-spren relations?
