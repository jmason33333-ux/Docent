# Part-Level Knowledge Snapshot: Songs of Home

**Book:** Rhythm of War  
**Part:** 3: Songs of Home  
**Coverage:** Chapters 44-72  
**Safe for readers up to:** Chapter 72 (end of this part)

---

## Part Overview

In Part 3: Songs of Home, the narrative shifts focus to the complexities of war and the personal struggles within it. The occupation of Urithiru by the Fused becomes a central conflict, with characters like Navani and Kaladin working from within to undermine the invaders. Flashbacks provide insight into the history and motivations of the listeners, particularly through Venli and Eshonai’s perspectives. Themes of resilience, identity, and the struggle for autonomy are woven throughout, as characters grapple with the consequences of past decisions and the harsh realities of their current situations.

This part also delves into the technological and magical aspects of the world, exploring the interactions between different forms of Light and the underlying mechanics of the tower's defenses. Relationships between characters are tested, alliances form and fracture, and the stakes are raised with each passing chapter, setting the stage for the conflicts to come.

A: Navani covertly communicates with the Sibling and experiments with Light, as detailed in Chapters 49 and 69.

A: Dalinar explores his powers’ potential, aiming to reforge the Oathpact or bolster alliances, discussed in Chapters 47 and 71.
