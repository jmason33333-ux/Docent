## World-Building / Lore

### Fabrials
- **First explained:** Chapter 3
- **What we know:** Devices powered by trapping spren in gemstones, used for various purposes.
- **How it works:** Different spren and metals create distinct effects, such as augmenters (Ch 9) and diminishers (Ch 10).
- **Limitations/Rules:** Requires specific metals and gemstones; Stormlight consumption varies.
- **Key examples:** Fourth Bridge's flight, Radiant suppression fabrial (Ch 9).
- **Related concepts:** Surgebinding, as both utilize Stormlight.
- **Unanswered questions:** How can these devices be improved or weaponized further?

### Surgebinding
- **First explained:** Not explicitly detailed in Chapters 1-10, but implied through characters like Kaladin.
- **What we know:** Magic system allowing manipulation of physical and spiritual forces via Stormlight.
- **How it works:** Based on ten Surges, with each Radiant Order accessing two.
- **Limitations/Rules:** Requires a Nahel bond and Stormlight; strain on mental health evident.
- **Key examples:** Kaladin’s combat abilities (Ch 2, 5).
- **Related concepts:** Radiant Ideals, fabrials.
- **Unanswered questions:** What are the specific effects of all ten Surges?
