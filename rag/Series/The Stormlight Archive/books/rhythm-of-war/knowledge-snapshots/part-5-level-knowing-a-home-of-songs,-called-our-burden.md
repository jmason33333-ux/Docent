# Part-Level Knowledge Snapshot: Knowing a Home of Songs, Called Our Burden

**Book:** Rhythm of War

**Coverage:** Chapters 98-117  
**Safe for readers up to:** Chapter 117 (end of this part)  

---

## Part Overview
Part 5 of "Rhythm of War" brings together multiple narratives converging on critical confrontations and revelations. Dalinar Kholin navigates the complex political and military landscape, seeking alliances and strategies against Odium while contending with new challenges posed by Ishar and the Heralds. Meanwhile, Urithiru's occupation reaches a climax as Kaladin, Navani, and others face intense personal trials and battles. Kaladin's journey is central as he grapples with grief, guilt, and the need for healing, ultimately reaching a pivotal moment of growth.

Themes of unity, sacrifice, and the struggle for identity permeate this part, as characters confront their weaknesses and strengths in the face of overwhelming odds. The narrative arc builds towards a resolution at Urithiru while setting the stage for future conflicts and alliances, especially through the contest of champions and the shifting balance of power with Taravangian's transformation into Odium.

A: It provides emotional closure and clarity for Kaladin, allowing him to embrace his new role (Ch 108).

**Q: How does Navani's bond with the Sibling affect the battle at Urithiru?**  
A: It gives her new abilities that are crucial in defending the tower and reclaiming control (Ch 106).

**Q: What are the terms of the contest of champions?**  
A: Dalinar and Odium agree on a battle of champions in eleven days, with significant stakes for both sides (Ch 112).

**Q: How does Taravangian become Odium?**  
A: After a confrontation with Szeth, Taravangian's mind is pulled into Odium's realm, leading to his transformation (Ch 113).

**Q: What role does the Rhythm of War play in the conflict?**  
A: It represents a potential key to ending the war, as discussed by Navani and Raboniel (Ch 106).

**Q: Why is Kaladin focusing on mental health?**  
A: After his experiences, Kaladin sees the importance of addressing mental health, both for himself and others (Ch 116).

**Q: How does Lezian's story conclude?**  
A: Lezian is permanently killed by El with anti-Voidlight (Ch 116).

**Q: What happens to Eshonai in her final moments?**  
A: Eshonai finds peace and transformation in the Cognitive Realm after her death (Ch 117).
