## World-Building / Lore

### Surgebinding
- **First explained:** Ongoing exploration in these chapters
- **What we know:** It's the ability to manipulate natural forces through bonded spren.
- **How it works:** Radiants use Stormlight to perform various abilities, like flying or healing.
- **Limitations/Rules:** Can be suppressed by certain conditions, like in Chapter 61 where Kaladin can't fly.
- **Key examples:** Kaladin's struggle to use his powers in occupied Urithiru (Ch 61).
- **Related concepts:** Fabrials, Voidbinding.
- **Unanswered questions:** Full extent of abilities under suppression.

### Voidlight and Stormlight
- **First explained:** Chapter 54 Epigraph
- **What we know:** They are opposite forces; their interaction is not straightforward.
- **How it works:** Unclear if they can destroy each other.
- **Limitations/Rules:** Not fully understood; experimentation ongoing.
- **Key examples:** Mentioned in chapter 54 epigraph.
- **Related concepts:** Rhythm of War, emotional influence.
- **Unanswered questions:** How can they be combined or nullified?
