## Part Overview
Part 1: Burdens opens with multiple storylines running concurrently, focusing on the characters' internal and external conflicts as they navigate the mounting pressures of war and personal demons. Kaladin struggles with his mental health and the burdens of leadership, while Shallan juggles her fractured identity and covert missions. Navani's scientific endeavors with fabrials reveal deeper mysteries, and Venli grapples with her allegiance among the singers. Overarching themes of responsibility and the toll of war loom large as the characters confront their personal and collective burdens.

The narrative arc of Burdens builds through the exploration of the characters' internal struggles and their roles in the broader conflict against Odium's forces. Several key events, including battles, espionage, and strategic planning, set the stage for the deeper involvement of each character in the unfolding war. The part concludes with strategic discussions among the coalition of monarchs, setting up the next phase of their resistance.
