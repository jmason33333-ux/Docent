## Common Reader Questions (Up to Chapter 30)

**Q: Who is the spy among the Lightweavers?**  
A: Beryl is confirmed as the spy when Mraize mentions the corrupted gloryspren (the misinformation Shallan fed to Beryl). However, Pattern also used the communication cube, complicating the situation (Ch 30).

**Q: Why can Maya interact in Shadesmar?**  
A: In Shadesmar (the Cognitive Realm), deadeye Shardblades can interact more directly. Maya can be introduced to people, taught things, and shows emotional responses. This is different from the Physical Realm where deadeyes are less responsive (Ch 22, 29).

**Q: Why did Pattern lie to Veil?**  
A: Pattern lies to Veil about Shallan's childhood, which is shocking because Cryptics don't typically lie—they expose lies. This suggests something significant about Shallan's past or Pattern's nature that he's protecting (Ch 30).

**Q: What is Ba-Ado-Mishram?**  
A: Ba-Ado-Mishram is one of the Unmade. The Ghostbloods are searching for her, and this search is connected to their goal of taking Stormlight offworld. Dreaming-though-Awake says to ask the Heralds about her (Ch 24).

**Q: Why does Kaladin take Noril into his care?**  
A: Kaladin finds Noril at the Devotary of Mercy, where patients are locked in dark rooms. Shocked by the poor treatment, Kaladin takes Noril into his care and promises to return for the others. This shows his commitment to helping those with mental illness (Ch 25).

**Q: What is the Sibling?**  
A: The Sibling is the spren of the tower Urithiru itself. They can communicate through garnet veins in the walls. They were asleep until they felt a Bondsmith (Dalinar) in the tower, but have stayed hidden since (Ch 27, 28).

**Q: Why does Notum warn against the mission?**  
A: Notum is an honorspren who was punished for letting their group go free before the Battle of Thaylen Field. He warns that the honorspren are angry about the Recreance and will be difficult to convince. He promises to counsel against the mission (Ch 30).

**Q: What is group therapy and why does Kaladin use it?**  
A: Kaladin begins group therapy sessions where people with similar experiences talk together. He realizes that what helps is having a support group who has experienced the same feelings, not pity from those who haven't. This is revolutionary compared to the old treatment of locking people in dark rooms (Ch 25, 33).

**Q: Why does Shallan confess she wanted to rob Jasnah?**  
A: Shallan reveals the real reason she sought to become Jasnah's ward: to rob her. When Adolin just laughs incredulously instead of judging her, it lightens her feeling of dread and strengthens their relationship (Ch 24).

**Q: What is the Tukari caravan?**  
A: A caravan of humans (Tukari) that follows the mission through Shadesmar. Adolin and Godeke investigate them, and Notum says they're not doing anything illegal, but their presence is suspicious (Ch 30).

**Q: Why does Adolin's conversation with Dalinar become tense?**  
A: Dalinar tries to tell Adolin not to make his same mistakes, but Adolin feels misunderstood and belittled. Adolin has blocked-up anger about his mother Evi's death that he avoids expressing. Dalinar also expects Adolin to become a Radiant, which adds pressure (Ch 21).

**Q: What is the significance of Maya seeing another deadeye?**  
A: Maya stares intently at another deadeye (possibly Testament, Shallan's first Cryptic spren who became a deadeye). The inkspren shopkeeper tells Adolin the deadeye was killed recently. This may connect to Shallan's past (Ch 29).
