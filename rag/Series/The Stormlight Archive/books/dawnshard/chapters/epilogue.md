## Rowan's "If Asked" Notes

**Q: What does Lopen do?**  
A: As the ship begins its return journey, Lopen and Huio discuss the implications of Huio swearing the Third Ideal with an oath to protect Lopen. The resulting introspection leads to Lopen swearing his own Third Ideal, and Rua becomes a Sharddagger. This is a major moment of growth for Lopen.

**Q: What is a Sharddagger?**  
A: When Lopen swears the Third Ideal, Rua becomes a Sharddagger. This is a smaller version of a Shardblade, shaped like a dagger. It's still incredibly sharp and can cut through almost anything, but it's smaller and more practical for certain situations.

**Q: What does Drlwan do?**  
A: Drlwan approaches Rysn to thank her for standing by the crew and to request that she take the helm of the Wandersail at the beginning of their next expedition. This is a gesture of respect and recognition of Rysn as a worthy rebsk (ship owner/captain).

**Q: What is a rebsk?**  
A: A rebsk is a Thaylen term for a ship owner or captain. By offering Rysn the helm, Drlwan is recognizing her as worthy of being a rebsk—someone who owns and commands a ship. This is a great honor and shows how much respect Rysn has earned.

**Q: What does Rysn notice?**  
A: Rysn notes that she seems to be suddenly more attuned to color and musical tone. This is likely an effect of becoming a Dawnshard. The Dawnshard is changing her, enhancing her senses and perception in ways that relate to its nature as a Command.

**Q: Why is this chapter important?**  
A: This epilogue shows the growth and changes in both Lopen and Rysn. Lopen advances as a Radiant, and Rysn is recognized for her leadership and begins to experience the effects of the Dawnshard. It's a satisfying conclusion that shows how the journey has changed them.

**Q: What are the implications of the Dawnshard?**  
A: Rysn's increased attunement to color and musical tone suggests that the Dawnshard is changing her in fundamental ways. This is just the beginning of the effects—the Dawnshard is incredibly powerful and will likely have more significant impacts on Rysn's life going forward.
