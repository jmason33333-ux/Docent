## Quick Summary
Rysn sits in her office and notes how much Chiri-Chiri has grown and how sickly she appears. She also takes note of how her office and the Wandersail have been customized to facilitate her mobility, including the arrival of a new porter Nikli. Talik, with whom Rysn has been in contact regarding Chiri-Chiri, arrives to assess the larkin and recommends that she be taken to the ancestral home of the larkin at the Aimian city Akinah--long thought to be lost. Rysn recalls that Navani had put out a call for vessels to sail to Aimia to investigate the return of the First Dreams.

## Rowan's "If Asked" Notes

**Q: What is Chiri-Chiri?**  
A: Chiri-Chiri is a larkin, a creature that Rysn has been caring for. Larkin are creatures that can feed on Stormlight. Chiri-Chiri has grown but appears sickly, which is why Talik recommends taking her to Akinah, the ancestral home of the larkin.

**Q: Why is Rysn's office customized?**  
A: Rysn's office and the Wandersail have been customized to facilitate her mobility. This suggests that Rysn has a disability that affects her ability to move, and accommodations have been made to help her. Nikli is a new porter who helps with this.

**Q: What is Akinah?**  
A: Akinah is an Aimian city that was long thought to be lost. It's the ancestral home of the larkin, and Talik recommends taking Chiri-Chiri there. This is a mysterious and legendary location that will be the destination of the expedition.

**Q: What is the connection to the First Dreams?**  
A: Rysn recalls that Navani put out a call for vessels to sail to Aimia to investigate the return of the First Dreams. This connects the prologue (where Yalb found the abandoned First Dreams) to Rysn's story. The expedition will investigate what happened to the First Dreams and why it returned.

**Q: Who is Talik?**  
A: Talik is someone Rysn has been in contact with regarding Chiri-Chiri. He arrives to assess the larkin and recommends taking her to Akinah. He seems to have knowledge about larkin and their needs.

**Q: Why is this chapter important?**  
A: This chapter sets up the main plot of Dawnshard—the expedition to Aimia to investigate the First Dreams and take Chiri-Chiri to Akinah. It introduces Rysn as the main character and establishes her relationship with Chiri-Chiri and her need to help the larkin.
