## Quick Summary
Rysn travels to Urithiru in the company of Nikli and Chiri-Chiri to discuss terms of an expedition with Navani. She admires the fabrial lifts created by the Alethi artifabrians. They negotiate and agree to terms including: generous monetary compensation for the crew, Queen Fen providing a royal mandate to motivate the crew, two Windrunners (Lopen and Huio) ordered to accompany the mission, and that the scribe (Rushu) to accompany the mission would be one familiar with fabrial lift technology.

## Rowan's "If Asked" Notes

**Q: What are the fabrial lifts?**  
A: Rysn admires the fabrial lifts created by the Alethi artifabrians. These are fabrials (magical devices) that create lifts/elevators in Urithiru, allowing people to move between floors. This is important technology that helps with mobility in the tower.

**Q: What are the terms of the expedition?**  
A: They agree to terms including: generous monetary compensation for the crew, Queen Fen providing a royal mandate to motivate the crew, two Windrunners (Lopen and Huio) ordered to accompany the mission, and that the scribe (Rushu) to accompany the mission would be one familiar with fabrial lift technology.

**Q: Why are Lopen and Huio assigned?**  
A: Two Windrunners (Lopen and Huio) are ordered to accompany the mission. Windrunners can fly using their Surgebinding abilities, which will be useful for the expedition. They're also experienced Radiants who can provide protection and support.

**Q: Why is Rushu needed?**  
A: The scribe (Rushu) to accompany the mission would be one familiar with fabrial lift technology. This suggests that fabrial technology will be important on the expedition, and Rushu's expertise will be needed. She's also a scholar who can document and study what they find.

**Q: Why does Queen Fen provide a mandate?**  
A: Queen Fen provides a royal mandate to motivate the crew. This gives the expedition official authority and support, which helps convince the crew to participate in what might be a dangerous mission. It shows the importance of the expedition.

**Q: Why is this chapter important?**  
A: This chapter finalizes the terms of the expedition and establishes who will be going. It sets up the crew and the mission parameters, which will be important for the rest of the story. It also shows the cooperation between different groups (Thaylens, Alethi, Radiants).
