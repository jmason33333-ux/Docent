## Quick Summary
**Rysn:** Cord, filling in for the chef Nlan who stayed ashore, discovers that the ship's grain stores have been infested by grubs. Once again, the crew assumes that this is a bad omen and that the voyage is cursed. Using notes she took while studying under Vstim, Rysn averts the crisis by directing Drlwan to dock and initiate trade with a group of Hexi nomads. Since the Hexi are strict vegetarians (but consider grubs to be plants), they are willing to trade the bad grain for jerky made from the nomad's dead animals. Nikli recounts his success warming up to the crew and converses with Rysn about how he ended up among the Thaylen people.

**Nikliasorm:** Separately, Nikli finds a private moment to contact the other Sleepless without revealing his true form to Rysn. He reports on the status of his efforts to dissuade the expedition. Although working to maintain the secrecy of Aimia, Nikli argues that he should be allowed a last attempt to dissuade the crew, before being forced to kill everyone aboard--as had happened on the First Dreams.

## Rowan's "If Asked" Notes

**Q: What are the Hexi nomads?**  
A: The Hexi are a group of nomads that the Wandersail docks with to trade. They are strict vegetarians but consider grubs to be plants, which allows them to trade the infested grain for jerky made from their dead animals. This is a creative solution that Rysn comes up with using her knowledge from studying under Vstim.

**Q: Why is Nikli a Sleepless?**  
A: Nikli finds a private moment to contact the other Sleepless without revealing his true form to Rysn. This reveals that Nikli is not human—he's a Sleepless, a creature made of many smaller creatures (hordelings). The Sleepless are working to maintain the secrecy of Aimia.

**Q: What happened on the First Dreams?**  
A: Nikli reports that he should be allowed a last attempt to dissuade the crew before being forced to kill everyone aboard, as had happened on the First Dreams. This reveals that the Sleepless killed the crew of the First Dreams to protect Aimia's secrets. This is why the ship was found empty in the prologue.

**Q: Why does Nikli want to dissuade the expedition?**  
A: Nikli is working to maintain the secrecy of Aimia. The Sleepless want to keep Aimia hidden and protected, so they're trying to prevent the expedition from reaching it. Nikli argues he should be allowed one more attempt to dissuade the crew before resorting to killing them.

**Q: How does Rysn avert the crisis?**  
A: Using notes she took while studying under Vstim, Rysn directs Drlwan to dock and initiate trade with a group of Hexi nomads. Since the Hexi are vegetarians but consider grubs plants, they're willing to trade the infested grain for jerky. This solves the food problem and shows Rysn's resourcefulness.

**Q: Why is this chapter important?**  
A: This chapter reveals Nikli's true nature as a Sleepless and his mission to protect Aimia's secrets. It also shows Rysn's leadership and problem-solving skills, and it connects to the mystery of the First Dreams from the prologue. It's a turning point that reveals the stakes of the expedition.
