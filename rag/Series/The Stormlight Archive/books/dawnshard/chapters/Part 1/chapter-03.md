## Quick Summary
Lopen and Rua fly through Urithiru to meet with a Reshi delegation that has arrived to meet with Dalinar. Talik greets him and is immediately lashed upward to the peak of the atrium, where Lopen shows him the view of mountains surrounding Urithiru. The two hold an impromptu summit in the air before returning to the ground to find Ral-na (a newly bonded Dustbringer) and Kaladin, who is irritated at Lopen's lack of decorum. Kaladin assigns Lopen and Huio to Rysn's expedition, and they decide to take along Cord, who can see spren.

## Rowan's "If Asked" Notes

**Q: Who is Ral-na?**  
A: Ral-na is a newly bonded Dustbringer. Dustbringers are an Order of Knights Radiant who can use the Surges of Division and Abrasion. Ral-na is a new Radiant who appears in this chapter.

**Q: Why does Lopen lash Talik upward?**  
A: Talik greets Lopen and is immediately lashed upward to the peak of the atrium, where Lopen shows him the view of mountains surrounding Urithiru. This is Lopen's playful and unconventional way of greeting someone—he takes them flying to show them the view. It's part of his personality and shows his lack of formality.

**Q: Why is Kaladin irritated?**  
A: Kaladin is irritated at Lopen's lack of decorum. Lopen is very informal and playful, which can be seen as disrespectful in formal situations. Kaladin, as a leader, expects more decorum and professionalism, especially when meeting with delegations.

**Q: Who is Cord?**  
A: Cord is Rock's (Lunamor's) daughter. They decide to take her along on the expedition because she can see spren. This is a useful ability that will help them on their mission, as spren are important to understanding the world and magic of Roshar.

**Q: Why is this chapter important?**  
A: This chapter finalizes the expedition crew by assigning Lopen and Huio, and deciding to bring Cord. It also shows Lopen's personality and establishes the tone of the story. It's a lighter chapter that sets up the adventure to come.

**Q: What is the summit in the air?**  
A: Lopen and Talik hold an impromptu summit in the air before returning to the ground. This is Lopen's unconventional way of conducting business—flying up to talk while showing the view. It's both practical (they can talk privately) and playful (showing off the view).
