## Quick Summary
Despite not having a corpse to burn, the crew goes ashore for a bonfire and a night of rest. Rysn communicates via spanreed with Vstim, who reveals Navani's hidden agenda to find and possibly activate the Oathgate in Akinah. The two theorize that a Fused Lightweaver may be responsible for the Santhid corpse and the other omens; Jasnah agrees via another spanreed. Nikli shares an account of the scouring of Aimia with Rysn, and Cord shares her past, also explaining about her people's stories of the Sleepless. Building on Huio's accidental discovery of aluminum-modified spanreeds, Rushu demonstrates how she can turn Rysn's chair into a levitating mobility device.

## Rowan's "If Asked" Notes

**Q: What is Navani's hidden agenda?**  
A: Vstim reveals Navani's hidden agenda to find and possibly activate the Oathgate in Akinah. This is a major revelation—the expedition isn't just about investigating the First Dreams or taking Chiri-Chiri to Akinah, but also about finding and potentially using an Oathgate. This adds another layer of purpose to the mission.

**Q: What is the scouring of Aimia?**  
A: Nikli shares an account of the scouring of Aimia with Rysn. This is a historical event where Aimia was destroyed or devastated. The details aren't fully revealed, but it's significant history that explains why Aimia is lost and why the Sleepless want to protect it.

**Q: What does Cord share?**  
A: Cord shares her past and explains about her people's stories of the Sleepless. This reveals that Cord's people (the Horneaters) have knowledge and stories about the Sleepless, which is important information. It also develops Cord's character and background.

**Q: What is the levitating chair?**  
A: Building on Huio's accidental discovery of aluminum-modified spanreeds, Rushu demonstrates how she can turn Rysn's chair into a levitating mobility device. This is a practical application of the aluminum discovery—using it to create a fabrial that can help Rysn move around more easily.

**Q: Who is the Fused Lightweaver?**  
A: Rysn and Vstim theorize that a Fused Lightweaver may be responsible for the Santhid corpse and the other omens. Jasnah agrees via spanreed. This suggests that the "bad omens" might be illusions created by a Fused (enemy) Lightweaver, rather than being created by the Sleepless. This adds another layer of complexity.

**Q: Why is this chapter important?**  
A: This chapter reveals multiple important pieces of information: Navani's hidden agenda with the Oathgate, the scouring of Aimia, Cord's knowledge of the Sleepless, and the practical application of the aluminum discovery. It's an information-dense chapter that advances the plot and deepens the worldbuilding.

**Q: What is an Oathgate?**  
A: An Oathgate is a transportation device that can move people and objects between locations instantly. There are Oathgates in major cities, and Navani wants to find and possibly activate the one in Akinah. This would be a major strategic advantage.
