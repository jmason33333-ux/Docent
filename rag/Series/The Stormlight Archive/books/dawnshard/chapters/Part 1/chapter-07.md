## Quick Summary
Cord and Lopen converse about the expedition so far and Cord's motivation for joining it. They are interrupted for a bit when Huio comes to deliver a letter to Cord. Huio, next going to Rushu's cabin to deliver some aluminum, tinkers with one of her spanreeds and remarks to Lopen when he comes to find him about how aluminum appears to affect the spren captive inside the spanreed's ruby. They assume it broken when the paired spanreed only moves laterally, making unintelligible scribbles, and Huio says he will pay for it.

## Rowan's "If Asked" Notes

**Q: What does Cord discuss with Lopen?**  
A: Cord and Lopen converse about the expedition so far and Cord's motivation for joining it. This is a bonding moment where they get to know each other better and understand why Cord wanted to come on the mission.

**Q: What happens with the spanreed?**  
A: Huio, going to Rushu's cabin to deliver aluminum, tinkers with one of her spanreeds and discovers that aluminum appears to affect the spren captive inside the spanreed's ruby. When they test it, the paired spanreed only moves laterally, making unintelligible scribbles. They assume it's broken, but this is actually a discovery about how aluminum affects fabrials.

**Q: Why is aluminum's effect important?**  
A: Aluminum's effect on spren and fabrials is significant because it can block or interfere with Investiture. This discovery shows that aluminum can modify how fabrials work, which relates to Rushu's mission to understand aluminum's impact. This could have important applications.

**Q: What does the lateral movement mean?**  
A: When aluminum affects the spanreed, the paired spanreed only moves laterally, making unintelligible scribbles. This suggests that aluminum is interfering with the normal communication function of the spanreed, possibly blocking or distorting the spren's ability to transmit messages properly.

**Q: Why is this chapter important?**  
A: This chapter shows the discovery of aluminum's effect on fabrials, which is part of Rushu's research mission. It also develops the relationships between characters and shows their curiosity and experimentation. It's a lighter chapter that builds on the ongoing research and exploration.

**Q: What is a spanreed?**  
A: A spanreed is a fabrial device that allows long-distance communication. It works by having a spren captive inside a ruby that can move a pen to write messages. The paired spanreed moves in sync, allowing two people to communicate across distances. Aluminum appears to interfere with this function.
