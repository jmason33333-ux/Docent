## Quick Summary
Rysn and Cord follow a swarm of luckspren underwater to a cave beneath the island surface. Contained in the cave are a set of Shardplate, eight Soulcasters, and a mural depicting the Shattering of Adonalsium. When Rysn examines the mural, she hears the voice of the Dawnshard contained within it; she accepts it, and it exits the mural and joins with her. Thus, Rysn becomes a Dawnshard just before hordelings begin entering the cavern.

## Rowan's "If Asked" Notes

**Q: How do Rysn and Cord find the cave?**  
A: Rysn and Cord follow a swarm of luckspren underwater to a cave beneath the island surface. This is where Chiri-Chiri was leading them—the luckspren were guiding them to the hidden cave where the Dawnshard is kept.

**Q: What is in the cave?**  
A: Contained in the cave are a set of Shardplate, eight Soulcasters, and a mural depicting the Shattering of Adonalsium. These are the real treasures of Akinah, hidden away from the fake gemhearts that were used as a distraction.

**Q: What is the Dawnshard?**  
A: When Rysn examines the mural, she hears the voice of the Dawnshard contained within it. She accepts it, and it exits the mural and joins with her. Thus, Rysn becomes a Dawnshard. A Dawnshard is one of four powerful Commands that were used to Shatter Adonalsium. They are incredibly powerful and significant.

**Q: What is the Shattering of Adonalsium?**  
A: The Shattering of Adonalsium is the event where Adonalsium (the original god) was broken into sixteen Shards. The mural depicts this event, and the Dawnshard was one of the Commands used to do it. This is cosmere-wide history.

**Q: Why is this chapter important?**  
A: This is the climactic moment where Rysn becomes a Dawnshard, which is one of the most significant events in the cosmere. It's the culmination of the expedition and changes Rysn's life forever. The hordelings approaching also create immediate danger.

**Q: What does becoming a Dawnshard mean?**  
A: Becoming a Dawnshard means Rysn now holds one of the four most powerful Commands in the cosmere. This gives her incredible power but also makes her a target. The Dawnshard is what the Sleepless were protecting, and now it's bonded to Rysn instead of being in the mural.

**Q: Why are the hordelings coming?**  
A: The hordelings begin entering the cavern just after Rysn becomes the Dawnshard. This is likely Nikli and other Sleepless trying to stop her or retrieve the Dawnshard. They're the immediate threat that Rysn and Cord must face.
