## Rowan's "If Asked" Notes

**Q: What happens as they approach Akinah?**  
A: The Wandersail breaches the storm surrounding Akinah. Rysn and Huio stay below while Lopen remains topside interacting with the crew. This is a dangerous moment as they navigate through the storm to reach the island.

**Q: What does Lopen do?**  
A: Lopen dives into the water to rescue Turlm, who has fallen overboard. He also adheres Drlwan's foot to the deck with Stormlight to keep him safe during the storm. This shows Lopen's quick thinking and use of his powers to help the crew.

**Q: What are the shadows?**  
A: Right as the ship breaks through the storm, Rua points out to Lopen large shadows in the sea that he thinks are the same creatures as the ones that drained Leyten and the others of Stormlight on their reconnaissance mission. These are likely the same creatures that attacked the earlier expedition.

**Q: What is Akinah?**  
A: Finally in the center of the storm, they get their first glimpse of Akinah. This is the lost island they've been searching for, the destination of their expedition. It's been hidden and protected, which is why it's surrounded by a storm.

**Q: Why is this chapter important?**  
A: This chapter marks the arrival at Akinah, the main destination of the expedition. It shows the danger they face (the storm, the shadows) and Lopen's heroism in rescuing crewmembers. It also sets up the mystery of what they'll find on the island and what the shadows represent.

**Q: What happened to Leyten?**  
A: Leyten and others were on a reconnaissance mission and were drained of Stormlight by creatures in the sea. These are likely the same shadows that Rua points out to Lopen. This explains why the expedition is dangerous and why they need to be careful.
