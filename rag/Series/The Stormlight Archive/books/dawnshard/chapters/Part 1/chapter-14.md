## Rowan's "If Asked" Notes

**Q: What do Cord and Rysn discuss?**  
A: Cord and Rysn discuss Cord's desire for Shards and the political status of the Horneater Peaks in reference to the status of the war on Roshar. This develops Cord's character and motivations, showing why she joined the expedition.

**Q: What is Cultivation's Perpendicularity?**  
A: Cord acknowledges that Cultivation's Perpendicularity is going to become a major draw for forces both allied and malicious. A Perpendicularity is a connection between the Physical, Cognitive, and Spiritual Realms. Cultivation's Perpendicularity is located in the Horneater Peaks, making it strategically important.

**Q: What does Rysn realize?**  
A: Rysn realizes that the gemhearts on the beach might have been deliberately planted there as a distraction. This is a crucial insight—the valuable gemhearts aren't natural, but were placed there to draw attention away from something else on the island.

**Q: Why is this significant?**  
A: This realization suggests that someone (likely the Sleepless) has set up the gemhearts to distract the expedition from finding something more important. This connects to Nikli's mission to protect Akinah's secrets and suggests there's something hidden that they don't want discovered.

**Q: Why is this chapter important?**  
A: This chapter develops Cord's character and motivations while revealing Rysn's intelligence and suspicion. The realization that the gemhearts are a distraction is a key plot point that changes how we understand what's happening on Akinah.

**Q: What are Shards?**  
A: Shards are Shardblades (magical swords) and Shardplate (magical armor). They're extremely powerful and valuable. Cord wants them, likely to help protect her people or gain status. This shows her ambition and goals.
