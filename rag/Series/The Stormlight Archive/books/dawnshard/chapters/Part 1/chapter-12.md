## Rowan's "If Asked" Notes

**Q: What does Rysn see?**  
A: Rysn first sights Akinah. The lookouts with spyglasses report seeing greatshell corpses with gemhearts lying on the shore. This is the first real view of what's on the island—dead greatshells with valuable gemhearts.

**Q: What does Cord say about the shadows?**  
A: Cord tells Rysn about the shadows Lopen saw underwater, though she thinks they are not what Rua determined them to be. This suggests Cord has different knowledge or perspective about what these creatures are, possibly from her Horneater background.

**Q: What is the landing party?**  
A: Rysn and Drlwan authorize a landing party to examine the beach, accompanied by the Windrunners (Lopen and Huio) and Rushu, who want to set out toward the city ruins at the island's center. This is the first step in exploring Akinah.

**Q: Why does Chiri-Chiri dive overboard?**  
A: Cord reports seeing luckspren heading toward the island, and Chiri-Chiri dives overboard, leaving Rysn's side to follow the mandras (luckspren). This suggests Chiri-Chiri is following some instinct or connection to the luckspren, which may lead to something important.

**Q: Why is this chapter important?**  
A: This chapter marks the actual arrival and first exploration of Akinah. It sets up the mystery of the greatshell corpses, the landing party's mission, and Chiri-Chiri's departure. It's the beginning of the real adventure on the island.

**Q: What are greatshells?**  
A: Greatshells are large creatures in the world of Roshar (like chasmfiends). They have gemhearts, which are valuable gems that can hold Stormlight. The fact that there are corpses with gemhearts on the beach is unusual and significant.
