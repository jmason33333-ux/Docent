## Quick Summary
**Lopen:** The Windrunners continue to fight the large hordeling, trying to buy time for the crew to escape back to the ship. However, specialized hordelings arrive and drain the Stormlight from Lopen, rendering him unable to use Lashings. Lopen falls to the ground and breaks his leg.

**Rysn:** Nikli refuses to negotiate with Rysn, instead explaining his previous deception and how the Sleepless had planned to entice her crew to leave before discovering the caverns. Cord attempts to bind Nikli to an oath grounded in Horneater mythology but is unsuccessful. After protracted discussion, Nikli agrees to attempt a negotiation, despite his claims that Rysn cannot provide anything that the Sleepless desire.

**Lopen:** In order to protect his cousin, Huio swears the Third Ideal and summons Caelinora as a Shardhammer.

**Rysn:** While explaining that the Sleepless guard Akinah in the place of the extinct lanceryn, Nikli discovers that the Dawnshard is now bonded to Rysn and is enraged. His hordelings begin swarming all over Rysn and Cord.

## Rowan's "If Asked" Notes

**Q: What happens to Lopen?**  
A: The Windrunners continue to fight the large hordeling, trying to buy time for the crew to escape. However, specialized hordelings arrive and drain the Stormlight from Lopen, rendering him unable to use Lashings. Lopen falls to the ground and breaks his leg. This is the same type of creature that drained Leyten's Stormlight.

**Q: What does Huio do?**  
A: In order to protect his cousin, Huio swears the Third Ideal and summons Caelinora as a Shardhammer. This is a major moment—Huio advances as a Radiant and gains the ability to summon his spren as a weapon. The Third Ideal is typically about protecting those who cannot protect themselves.

**Q: What does Nikli explain?**  
A: Nikli refuses to negotiate with Rysn, instead explaining his previous deception and how the Sleepless had planned to entice her crew to leave before discovering the caverns. This reveals that all the "bad omens" were attempts to get them to turn back before finding the real treasures.

**Q: What does Cord attempt?**  
A: Cord attempts to bind Nikli to an oath grounded in Horneater mythology but is unsuccessful. After protracted discussion, Nikli agrees to attempt a negotiation, despite his claims that Rysn cannot provide anything that the Sleepless desire. This shows Cord's knowledge of the Sleepless from her culture.

**Q: What is the lanceryn?**  
A: While explaining that the Sleepless guard Akinah in the place of the extinct lanceryn, Nikli discovers that the Dawnshard is now bonded to Rysn and is enraged. The lanceryn were the original guardians of Akinah, but they're now extinct, so the Sleepless took over that role.

**Q: Why is Nikli enraged?**  
A: Nikli discovers that the Dawnshard is now bonded to Rysn and is enraged. His hordelings begin swarming all over Rysn and Cord. The Sleepless were protecting the Dawnshard, and now it's been taken and bonded to Rysn, which is exactly what they were trying to prevent.

**Q: Why is this chapter important?**  
A: This chapter has multiple major moments: Huio swearing the Third Ideal, Nikli discovering the Dawnshard, and the immediate danger of the swarming hordelings. It's a turning point where the negotiation breaks down and the situation becomes more dangerous.
