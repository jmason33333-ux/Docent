## Rowan's "If Asked" Notes

**Q: What happens on the beach?**  
A: Lopen arrives back at the beach in time to rescue Huio from the jaws of a large hordeling. Lopen teases Huio about being the one to save his cousin, and as the two try to fight the hordeling off, Lopen complains to Rua that he can't turn into a Blade yet. This shows the ongoing fight against the Sleepless.

**Q: What does Rysn do?**  
A: In the cavern, Rysn falls back on her training from Vstim and attempts to open negotiations with the three Sleepless who have come to kill her. She points out their need of her, attempting to offer herself as a secret keeper regarding the treasures of Akinah. This shows Rysn using her merchant skills to negotiate rather than fight.

**Q: Why can't Lopen summon a Blade?**  
A: Lopen complains to Rua that he can't turn into a Blade yet. This suggests that Lopen hasn't sworn a high enough Ideal to allow Rua to become a Shardblade. He's still a lower-level Windrunner, which limits his abilities in the fight.

**Q: What is a secret keeper?**  
A: Rysn offers herself as a secret keeper regarding the treasures of Akinah. This means she would protect the secret of what's in the cave (the Shardplate, Soulcasters, and Dawnshard) rather than revealing it to the world. This is her bargaining position.

**Q: Why is this chapter important?**  
A: This chapter shows the two parallel conflicts: Lopen and Huio fighting on the beach, and Rysn negotiating in the cave. It demonstrates Rysn's intelligence and diplomatic skills, using her merchant training to try to resolve the situation peacefully rather than through violence.

**Q: What are the three Sleepless?**  
A: Three Sleepless have come to kill Rysn in the cavern. These are likely the leaders or representatives of the Sleepless who guard Akinah. They're responding to Rysn taking the Dawnshard, which they were protecting.
