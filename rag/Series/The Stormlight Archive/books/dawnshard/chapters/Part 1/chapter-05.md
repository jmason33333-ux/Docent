## Rowan's "If Asked" Notes

**Q: How do Rysn and Lopen bond?**  
A: Rysn and Lopen bond over shared experience with disability. Lopen lost an arm (which he regrew as a Radiant), and Rysn has mobility issues. They connect over their experiences with disability and how they've adapted. This creates a bond between them and shows understanding and empathy.

**Q: What are conjoiner fabrials?**  
A: Rushu demonstrates conjoiner fabrial technology as it relates to Rysn's mobility. Conjoiner fabrials are a type of fabrial that work together or in conjunction with each other. They're being used to help with Rysn's mobility, showing how fabrial technology can accommodate disabilities.

**Q: Why is aluminum important?**  
A: The group discusses Rushu's separate mission to understand the impact of aluminum on fabrials. Aluminum has special properties in the cosmere—it can block Investiture and interfere with fabrials. Understanding this impact is important for fabrial technology and may be relevant to the expedition.

**Q: What is Rushu's separate mission?**  
A: Rushu has a separate mission to understand the impact of aluminum on fabrials. This is research she's conducting alongside the main expedition. It shows that there are multiple goals and purposes for the voyage, and that Rushu is there both to help with the expedition and to conduct her own research.

**Q: Why is this chapter important?**  
A: This chapter shows the voyage beginning and establishes relationships between the characters. It also introduces important concepts about fabrial technology and aluminum that may be relevant later. It's a bonding and learning chapter that sets up the adventure.

**Q: How does fabrial technology help Rysn?**  
A: Rushu demonstrates conjoiner fabrial technology as it relates to Rysn's mobility. This shows how fabrials can be used to create accommodations for people with disabilities, allowing Rysn to move around the ship more easily. It's an example of technology being used for inclusion and accessibility.
