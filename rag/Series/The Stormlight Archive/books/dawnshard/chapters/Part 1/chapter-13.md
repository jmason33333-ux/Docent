## Quick Summary
Lopen, Huio, and Rushu land on Akinah. Rushu speculates that the gemhearts on the beach are a result of an ancestral instinct in the greatshells coming to die in the same place. They and the rest of the disembarked crew begin to examine, sketch, and gather the gemhearts in order to ensure that the crew gets paid. Lopen notes how odd it is that there is no crem buildup on the beach, wondering if highstorms do not reach this island. Huio remains with the sailors on the beach while Rushu and Lopen go search for the Oathgate.

## Rowan's "If Asked" Notes

**Q: What do they do on the beach?**  
A: Lopen, Huio, and Rushu land on Akinah. Rushu speculates that the gemhearts on the beach are a result of an ancestral instinct in the greatshells coming to die in the same place. They and the rest of the disembarked crew begin to examine, sketch, and gather the gemhearts to ensure that the crew gets paid.

**Q: What does Lopen notice?**  
A: Lopen notes how odd it is that there is no crem buildup on the beach, wondering if highstorms do not reach this island. This is significant because crem is normally deposited by highstorms, so its absence suggests something unusual about Akinah's weather or protection.

**Q: What do Rushu and Lopen do?**  
A: Huio remains with the sailors on the beach while Rushu and Lopen go search for the Oathgate. This is part of Rushu's mission to find and potentially activate the Oathgate in Akinah, as revealed in Chapter 9.

**Q: Why is the lack of crem significant?**  
A: The lack of crem buildup suggests that highstorms don't reach Akinah, or that something is protecting the island from them. This is unusual and hints at the island's special nature or protection. It also suggests the island might be magically protected.

**Q: Why is this chapter important?**  
A: This chapter shows the actual exploration of Akinah, with the crew gathering gemhearts and Rushu and Lopen searching for the Oathgate. It also introduces the mystery of why there's no crem, which hints at the island's special nature. It's the practical work of the expedition.

**Q: What are gemhearts?**  
A: Gemhearts are valuable gems found inside greatshells. They can hold Stormlight and are very valuable. The crew is gathering them to ensure they get paid for the expedition, as promised by Navani.
