## Rowan's "If Asked" Notes

**Q: What is a Santhid?**  
A: A Santhid is a large sea creature in the world of Roshar. The crew discovers a floating corpse of a Santhid that has washed over to the hull. This is seen as another bad omen by the superstitious crew, continuing the pattern of omens they've been experiencing.

**Q: Why does Rysn order a proper funeral?**  
A: Rysn turns the mood and wins some approval from her crew by ordering that the ship tow the corpse to shore to give it a proper funeral. This shows respect for the creature and demonstrates Rysn's leadership and compassion, which helps her gain the crew's respect.

**Q: What are hordelings?**  
A: The corpse disintegrates into hordelings before they have a chance to give it a proper funeral. Hordelings are small creatures that are part of the Sleepless. This reveals that the "Santhid corpse" wasn't actually a real Santhid, but was made of hordelings—likely created by Nikli or another Sleepless as part of their efforts to create omens and dissuade the expedition.

**Q: Why is this significant?**  
A: This chapter reveals that the "bad omens" (Screech's death, the grubs, the Santhid corpse) are likely being created by the Sleepless (through Nikli) to try to dissuade the expedition. It shows that the omens aren't natural but are deliberate attempts to turn the crew back. This connects to Nikli's mission from Chapter 6.

**Q: Why does Rysn win approval?**  
A: Rysn wins some approval from her crew by ordering a proper funeral for the Santhid. This shows her respect for life and tradition, and her willingness to do the right thing even when it's inconvenient. It demonstrates her leadership qualities and helps build trust with the crew.

**Q: Why is this chapter important?**  
A: This chapter continues the pattern of "bad omens" while revealing that they're likely fabricated. It shows Rysn's leadership skills and her ability to turn negative situations into positive ones. It also hints at the true nature of the omens and connects to Nikli's mission to dissuade the expedition.
