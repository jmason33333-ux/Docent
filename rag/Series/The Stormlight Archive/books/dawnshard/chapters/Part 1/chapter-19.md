## Quick Summary
**Lopen:** Huio takes the hordeling down with a Shardspear through the head and, running low on Stormlight, escapes with Lopen.

**Rysn:** Nikli's outburst is cut short by the arrival of luckspren and Chiri-Chiri to the cavern. The larkin lands on the negotiating table and roars protectively. The revelation of the Dawnshard's bonding gives Rysn the leverage needed to turn the negotiation. The crew of the Wandersail is permitted safe return with information about Akinah in addition to the Shardplate and Soulcasters found in the cavern. In return for their safety, Rysn vows to be the protector of the Dawnshard so that it is not in Akinah when forces from the cosmere eventually come in search of it. The Sleepless agree to this plan under the condition that they be permitted to follow Rysn permanently. A two-layer cover story is created: it would be said publicly that the return of a larkin convinced the Sleepless to let the ship go; while the rulers of Roshar, particularly Navani and Queen Fen, would be told that the exchange included Sleepless remaining with Rysn for training on impersonating humanity.

## Rowan's "If Asked" Notes

**Q: How does the fight end?**  
A: Huio takes the hordeling down with a Shardspear through the head and, running low on Stormlight, escapes with Lopen. This resolves the beach fight, allowing the Windrunners to escape.

**Q: What happens in the cave?**  
A: Nikli's outburst is cut short by the arrival of luckspren and Chiri-Chiri to the cavern. The larkin lands on the negotiating table and roars protectively. The revelation of the Dawnshard's bonding gives Rysn the leverage needed to turn the negotiation.

**Q: What is the deal?**  
A: The crew of the Wandersail is permitted safe return with information about Akinah in addition to the Shardplate and Soulcasters found in the cavern. In return for their safety, Rysn vows to be the protector of the Dawnshard so that it is not in Akinah when forces from the cosmere eventually come in search of it.

**Q: What do the Sleepless get?**  
A: The Sleepless agree to this plan under the condition that they be permitted to follow Rysn permanently. This means they'll stay with Rysn to help protect the Dawnshard and ensure she keeps her vow.

**Q: What is the cover story?**  
A: A two-layer cover story is created: it would be said publicly that the return of a larkin convinced the Sleepless to let the ship go; while the rulers of Roshar, particularly Navani and Queen Fen, would be told that the exchange included Sleepless remaining with Rysn for training on impersonating humanity. This protects the secret of the Dawnshard.

**Q: Why is this chapter important?**  
A: This chapter resolves the conflict and establishes the deal that allows everyone to survive. It sets up Rysn's new role as protector of the Dawnshard and the Sleepless following her. It's the climax and resolution of the main conflict.

**Q: What does protecting the Dawnshard mean?**  
A: Rysn vows to protect the Dawnshard so that it's not in Akinah when forces from the cosmere come searching for it. This means she'll keep it hidden and safe, preventing others from finding and using it. The Dawnshard is incredibly powerful and dangerous if it falls into the wrong hands.
