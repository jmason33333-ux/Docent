## Quick Summary
Rysn admires the Wandersail and explains the command structure since being given the ship by Vstim. Captain Drlwan curtly suggests that Rysn remain ashore for the voyage. They discuss the role of superstition and Passions among Thaylen naval crews after learning that the crew had found the ship's pet skyeel, Screech, dead that morning. The crew is offered a chance to remain ashore for the duration of the expedition. After getting confirmation of her sudden suspicion that Drlwan had expected to inherit the ship herself, Rysn confides in Nikli that she suspects the crew resents her being assigned as rebsk. They brainstorm methods to garner favor with both crew and captain, and she suggests to Nikli that he take part in the crew's hazing ritual as a way to feel more like one of them.

## Rowan's "If Asked" Notes

**Q: What is a rebsk?**  
A: Rysn suspects the crew resents her being assigned as rebsk. A rebsk is likely a Thaylen naval title or position, possibly meaning owner or commander of the ship. Rysn was given the ship by Vstim, but the crew (especially Drlwan) may have expected someone else to inherit it.

**Q: Why does Drlwan resent Rysn?**  
A: After getting confirmation of her suspicion, Rysn learns that Drlwan had expected to inherit the ship herself. This explains why Drlwan curtly suggests that Rysn remain ashore—she resents Rysn for getting the ship instead of her. This creates tension between Rysn and the captain.

**Q: Why is Screech's death significant?**  
A: The crew found the ship's pet skyeel, Screech, dead that morning. This is seen as a bad omen by the superstitious Thaylen crew. They discuss the role of superstition and Passions among Thaylen naval crews, and Screech's death adds to the crew's unease about the voyage.

**Q: What is the hazing ritual?**  
A: Rysn suggests to Nikli that he take part in the crew's hazing ritual as a way to feel more like one of them. This is likely an initiation ritual that new crewmembers go through to be accepted by the crew. It's a way for Nikli to integrate and gain the crew's acceptance.

**Q: Why does Rysn need to garner favor?**  
A: Rysn confides in Nikli that she suspects the crew resents her being assigned as rebsk. They brainstorm methods to garner favor with both crew and captain. This is important because Rysn needs the crew's cooperation and respect for the voyage to be successful, especially given the dangerous nature of the mission.

**Q: Why is this chapter important?**  
A: This chapter establishes the interpersonal dynamics and tensions on the ship. It shows the challenges Rysn faces as a leader and sets up potential conflicts that will need to be resolved during the voyage. It also adds depth to the Thaylen naval culture and traditions.
