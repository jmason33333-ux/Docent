## Quick Summary
Back on the Wandersail, Nikli attempts to frame Cord for the failed omens by going to search her room for evidence while she talks to Rysn on deck. He comes back with blackbane leaves, and Rysn immediately summons Kstled to arrest him--Rysn had found Nikli suspicious, so she had had Kstled search Cord's room first so that it became clear that Nikli's evidence was fabricated. Nikli pleads for Rysn to turn back from Aimia before he and Plamry are removed from the ship by Lopen and Huio.

## Rowan's "If Asked" Notes

**Q: How does Rysn catch Nikli?**  
A: Rysn had found Nikli suspicious, so she had Kstled search Cord's room first before Nikli went to search it. When Nikli comes back with blackbane leaves claiming to have found them in Cord's room, Rysn immediately knows the evidence is fabricated because Kstled had already searched and found nothing. This shows Rysn's intelligence and suspicion.

**Q: What are blackbane leaves?**  
A: Blackbane leaves are likely a poisonous plant. Nikli claims to have found them in Cord's room as evidence that she was responsible for the omens. However, since Rysn had the room searched first, she knows Nikli planted the evidence.

**Q: Why does Nikli try to frame Cord?**  
A: Nikli attempts to frame Cord for the failed omens as part of his mission to dissuade the expedition. This is his "last attempt" (as he mentioned in Chapter 6) to turn the crew back before resorting to killing them. By framing Cord, he hopes to create conflict and turn the expedition around.

**Q: Who is Plamry?**  
A: Plamry is removed from the ship along with Nikli by Lopen and Huio. This suggests that Plamry is also a Sleepless, working with Nikli. She's been part of the crew but is actually an agent trying to protect Aimia's secrets.

**Q: What does Nikli plead?**  
A: Nikli pleads for Rysn to turn back from Aimia before he and Plamry are removed. This is his final attempt to dissuade the expedition peacefully. He's trying to convince Rysn to abandon the mission rather than forcing him to kill everyone (as happened on the First Dreams).

**Q: Why is this chapter important?**  
A: This chapter resolves the tension with Nikli and reveals his true nature and mission. It shows Rysn's intelligence and leadership in catching his deception. It also removes a major obstacle/threat from the expedition, allowing them to continue toward Aimia. It's a turning point in the story.

**Q: What happens to Nikli and Plamry?**  
A: Nikli and Plamry are removed from the ship by Lopen and Huio. They're likely set ashore or otherwise removed from the expedition. This eliminates the immediate threat of the Sleepless trying to kill the crew, though there may be other Sleepless or threats ahead.
