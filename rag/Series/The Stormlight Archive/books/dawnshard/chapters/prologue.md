## Quick Summary
Having survived the sinking of the Wind's Pleasure, Yalb serves on a ship led by Captain Smta. Yalb and the new crewmember Dok discuss Thaylen naval traditions and the Wind's Pleasure before spotting the abandoned First Dreams sailing over the horizon. Yalb and a group board and find the ship to be empty of crew and floating with no direction.

## Rowan's "If Asked" Notes

**Q: What happened to the Wind's Pleasure?**  
A: Yalb survived the sinking of the Wind's Pleasure. This ship was from earlier in the Stormlight Archive series—it was Jasnah's ship that sank. Yalb was a crewmember who survived and now serves on a different ship.

**Q: What is the First Dreams?**  
A: The First Dreams is an abandoned ship that Yalb and the crew spot sailing over the horizon. When they board it, they find it empty of crew and floating with no direction. This is a mysterious discovery that sets up the story of Dawnshard.

**Q: Why is the ship empty?**  
A: The ship is found empty of crew, which is mysterious and concerning. This raises questions about what happened to the crew and why the ship is floating aimlessly. This mystery is part of what drives the story forward.

**Q: What are Thaylen naval traditions?**  
A: Yalb and Dok discuss Thaylen naval traditions. The Thaylens are a seafaring people with their own customs and practices related to sailing and naval operations. This adds cultural depth to the story.

**Q: Why is this prologue important?**  
A: This prologue introduces the mystery of the First Dreams, which is likely connected to the main plot of Dawnshard. It also reconnects with Yalb, a character from earlier books, and sets up the expedition that will follow.
