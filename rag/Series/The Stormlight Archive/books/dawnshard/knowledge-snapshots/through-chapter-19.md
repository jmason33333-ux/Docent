## World-Building / Lore

### Dawnshard
- **First explained:** Chapter 16
- **What we know:** A powerful ancient force contained within a mural that Rysn accepts, becoming its new bearer
- **How it works:** The Dawnshard is a central element of power, capable of influencing great events
- **Limitations/Rules:** Specific powers and limitations are not fully explained
- **Key examples:** Rysn's bonding with the Dawnshard (Ch 16)
- **Related concepts:** Tied to the Shattering of Adonalsium and other cosmere elements
- **Unanswered questions:** The full extent of its powers and responsibilities

### Sleepless
- **First explained:** Chapter 15
- **What we know:** A group of creatures capable of disguising themselves as humans, composed of hordelings
- **How it works:** They control hordelings and can alter their appearance
- **Limitations/Rules:** Must maintain their guise and protect Akinah's secrets
- **Key examples:** Nikli's deception and negotiation with Rysn (Ch 15, 17, 19)
- **Related concepts:** Linked to Horneater mythology and the cosmere's secrets
- **Unanswered questions:** The full scope of their intentions and history

## Common Reader Questions (Up to Chapter 19)

**Q: What is the Dawnshard and how does Rysn become one?**  
A: The Dawnshard is a powerful force contained within a mural in an underwater cave beneath Akinah. Rysn becomes a Dawnshard by accepting it after hearing its voice during her exploration in Chapter 16.

**Q: Who are the Sleepless and what do they want?**  
A: The Sleepless are a group of creatures who can disguise themselves as humans. They aim to protect the secrets of Akinah from outsiders, as revealed in Chapter 15.

**Q: Why is Akinah important?**  
A: Akinah holds ancient secrets, including a Dawnshard and other artifacts, making it a significant location within the cosmere, as explored in Chapters 11-19.

**Q: How do Lopen and Huio's dynamics evolve?**  
A: Lopen and Huio share a close familial bond, with Lopen saving Huio from danger and supporting his growth as a Windrunner, as depicted in Chapters 11 and 17.

**Q: What challenges does the expedition face on Akinah?**  
A: The expedition encounters deceptive traps, hostile Sleepless, and the challenge of negotiating their safe return, highlighted in Chapters 15-19.

**Q: How does Rysn manage to negotiate with the Sleepless?**  
A: Rysn leverages the bonding with the Dawnshard and her training from Vstim to negotiate with the Sleepless for the crew's safe departure in Chapter 19.

**Q: What are the implications of Rysn becoming a Dawnshard?**  
A: While the full implications are not yet clear, it suggests a significant role for Rysn in future cosmere events, as introduced in Chapter 16.

**Q: How does the expedition plan to leave Akinah safely?**  
A: Through negotiation with the Sleepless, Rysn secures conditions for the crew's safe departure, including a cover story, as concluded in Chapter 19.

**Q: What is the significance of Chiri-Chiri?**  
A: Chiri-Chiri is a larkin that plays a protective role and influences the negotiation outcome by appearing in the cavern, as seen in Chapter 19.

**Q: What tactics do the Sleepless use against the expedition?**  
A: The Sleepless use deception, threats, and their hordelings to protect Akinah's secrets, as demonstrated in Chapters 15-19.

**Q: How does Huio progress as a Windrunner?**  
A: Huio swears the Third Ideal and summons a Shardhammer during the conflict, marking significant progress in Chapter 18.

**Q: What is the significance of the mural in the cave?**  
A: The mural depicts the Shattering of Adonalsium and contains the Dawnshard, central to Rysn's transformation in Chapter 16.

**Q: What role does Rushu play in the expedition?**  
A: Rushu is a scholar assisting in locating the Oathgate and analyzing Akinah's layout, contributing to the expedition's goals as seen in Chapters 13 and 15.

**Q: How do the Sleepless view their role in guarding Akinah?**  
A: They see themselves as protectors of ancient secrets, with a long-standing duty to prevent discovery by outsiders, as explained in Chapter 18.

**Q: What is the role of luckspren in the story?**  
A: Luckspren guide Rysn and Cord to the underwater cave, playing a crucial role in the discovery of the Dawnshard in Chapter 16.
