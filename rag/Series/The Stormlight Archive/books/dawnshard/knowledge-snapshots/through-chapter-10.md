## Major Characters (Introduced So Far)

### Rysn Ftori
- **First appearance:** Chapter 1
- **Role:** Thaylen merchant, owner of the Wandersail, tasked with leading an expedition to Aimia.
- **Physical description:** Often noted for her mobility challenges, uses a wheelchair.
- **Key relationships:**
  - **Chiri-Chiri:** Her pet larkin, whom she cares deeply for, motivating her journey.
  - **Nikliasorm (Nikli):** Initially seen as a helpful porter, later revealed to be a Sleepless with hidden motives.
- **Abilities/Powers:** Skilled in trade and negotiation, adept at navigating Thaylen merchant culture.
- **Motivations/Goals:** To heal Chiri-Chiri by taking her to Akinah and to succeed in Navani's mission to investigate Aimia.
- **Character Arc (through Ch 10):**
  - **Starting point:** Managing Wandersail and dealing with her disability.
  - **Key moments:** Negotiating terms with Navani (Ch 2), averting crisis with Hexi trade (Ch 6), exposing Nikli's deception (Ch 10).
  - **Current state:** More confident, having gained crew's respect after thwarting Nikli's plans.
- **Key scenes/chapters:** Ch 1 (introduction and setup), Ch 6 (grain crisis), Ch 10 (Nikli's deception).
- **Notable quotes:** "To see the world from a different perspective is to learn."

### Lopen
- **First appearance:** Chapter 3
- **Role:** Windrunner, assigned to Rysn's expedition.
- **Physical description:** Known for his one-armed humor and jovial nature.
- **Key relationships:**
  - **Huio:** Cousin and fellow Windrunner, works closely with him.
  - **Cord:** Shares camaraderie and mutual respect, discusses expedition goals.
- **Abilities/Powers:** As a Windrunner, can perform Lashings, allowing him to manipulate gravity.
- **Motivations/Goals:** To protect the crew and fulfill his duties as a Windrunner.
- **Character Arc (through Ch 10):**
  - **Starting point:** Light-hearted and somewhat irresponsible.
  - **Key moments:** Bonding with Rysn over shared experiences (Ch 5), investigating spanreed anomalies (Ch 7).
  - **Current state:** More focused and responsible, aiding in removing Nikli (Ch 10).
- **Key scenes/chapters:** Ch 3 (introduction and mission assignment), Ch 5 (bonding with Rysn), Ch 10 (action against Nikli).

### Nikliasorm (Nikli)
- **First appearance:** Chapter 1
- **Role:** Initially a porter on the Wandersail, later revealed as a Sleepless.
- **Physical description:** Appears human but is a Sleepless, an Aimian species with control over hordelings.
- **Key relationships:**
  - **Rysn:** Pretends to assist her, but actually seeks to sabotage the mission.
  - **Other Sleepless:** Communicates secretly to report progress and receive instructions.
- **Abilities/Powers:** Can manipulate hordelings and disguise himself as human.
- **Motivations/Goals:** To protect the secrets of Aimia and dissuade the expedition from reaching Akinah.
- **Character Arc (through Ch 10):**
  - **Starting point:** Helpful and unassuming crew member.
  - **Key moments:** Secret communication with Sleepless (Ch 6), framing Cord (Ch 10).
  - **Current state:** Exposed and removed from the Wandersail.
- **Key scenes/chapters:** Ch 6 (revealed as Sleepless), Ch 10 (exposed by Rysn).

## World-Building / Lore

### Sleepless
- **First explained:** Chapter 6
- **What we know:** A species capable of controlling hordelings and disguising themselves as humans.
- **How it works:** Sleepless use hordelings, tiny creatures, to form bodies and interact with the world.
- **Limitations/Rules:** Maintaining human disguise requires concentration; they must protect their secrets.
- **Key examples:** Nikli's manipulation of hordelings and communication with other Sleepless.
- **Related concepts:** The scouring of Aimia, the mysteries they protect.
- **Unanswered questions:** Full extent of their abilities and their history with Aimia.

### Fabrials
- **First explained:** Chapter 2
- **What we know:** Devices that use spren to perform various functions, such as mobility aids.
- **How it works:** Spren are captured and manipulated to produce effects like levitation or conjoining.
- **Limitations/Rules:** Aluminum impacts spren interaction, causing malfunctions.
- **Key examples:** Rysn's wheelchair, Rushu's spanreed experiments.
- **Related concepts:** Navani's fabrial advancements, implications for mobility and communication.
- **Unanswered questions:** Future fabrial developments and aluminum's full impact.

## Common Reader Questions (Up to Chapter 10)

**Q: Who is Rysn and what is her role on the Wandersail?**  
A: Rysn is a Thaylen merchant and the owner of the Wandersail, leading an expedition to Aimia to investigate the return of the First Dreams and to heal her pet larkin, Chiri-Chiri. (Ch 1)

**Q: What happened to the First Dreams?**  
A: The First Dreams was found abandoned at sea, with no crew aboard, prompting an investigation led by Navani and Rysn. (Prologue, Ch 1)

**Q: How does the conjoiner fabrial work?**  
A: Conjoiner fabrials use spren to link objects, allowing actions performed on one to affect the other, demonstrated by Rushu for Rysn's mobility. (Ch 5)

**Q: What are the Sleepless and their goals?**  
A: Sleepless are a species that use hordelings to form bodies and protect Aimia's secrets, as seen with Nikli's sabotage attempts. (Ch 6)

**Q: How do Lopen and Rysn relate to each other?**  
A: Lopen and Rysn bond over shared experiences with disability, fostering mutual respect during the expedition. (Ch 5)

**Q: Why did Nikli try to frame Cord?**  
A: Nikli aimed to sabotage the expedition to protect Aimia's secrets, using deception to create mistrust among the crew. (Ch 10)

**Q: What is the significance of Chiri-Chiri to the plot?**  
A: Chiri-Chiri's health is a driving factor for Rysn's journey to Akinah, where larkins originate, adding personal stakes. (Ch 1)

**Q: How does aluminum affect fabrials?**  
A: Aluminum disrupts the interaction of spren within fabrials, causing malfunctions, as seen with spanreed issues. (Ch 7)

**Q: What was the scouring of Aimia?**  
A: An event in history where Aimia was devastated, its details and implications remain a mystery tied to the current expedition. (Ch 9)

**Q: What are the omens faced by the crew?**  
A: The crew encounters bad omens like grain infestation and a Santhid corpse, fueling superstitions about the voyage. (Ch 6, Ch 8)

**Q: How does Rysn earn the crew's respect?**  
A: By managing crises effectively, such as trading with Hexi nomads and exposing Nikli's deception. (Ch 6, Ch 10)

**Q: What is the Dawnshard and why is it important?**  
A: The Dawnshard is a powerful artifact linked to Aimia, its discovery and implications are central to the mission's success. (Ch 1)

**Q: Why is Aimia significant to the expedition?**  
A: Aimia holds historical and magical secrets, including the Dawnshard and potentially an Oathgate, making it the expedition's focal point. (Ch 9)

**Q: What is Navani's hidden agenda regarding Aimia?**  
A: Navani seeks to find and possibly activate the Oathgate in Akinah, adding strategic depth to the mission. (Ch 9)
