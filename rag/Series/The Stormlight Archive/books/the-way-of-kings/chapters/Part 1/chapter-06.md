## Quick Summary
Kaladin and the slave caravan arrive at Highprince Sadeas's warcamp on the Shattered Plains. Kaladin tries to argue for a fighting position but is labeled a deserter and placed in a bridge crew. During a grueling bridge run, he endures immense pain and is the only survivor from the front row. The windspren, Sylphrena, revives him, and he makes his way back to camp.
