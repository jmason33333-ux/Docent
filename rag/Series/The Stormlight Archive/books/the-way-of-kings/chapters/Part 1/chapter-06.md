## Quick Summary
Kaladin, now part of a slave caravan, arrives at the warcamp of Highprince Sadeas on the Shattered Plains. He is assigned to a bridge crew, a grueling and dangerous role, and experiences his first bridge run. After surviving an attack that kills most of his crew, he is aided by a windspren named Sylphrena.

---

## Key Beats
- Kaladin arrives at the Shattered Plains with the slave caravan.
- The slaves are presented to a woman who decides their fates; Kaladin's hope to fight is dashed when Tvlakv labels him a deserter.
- Kaladin and his companions are assigned to bridge crews.
- Kaladin meets Gaz, who dislikes him and assigns him to a dangerous position.
- On their first bridge run, Kaladin's crew is decimated by enemy arrows.
- Kaladin survives, regaining consciousness hours later with the help of Sylphrena.
- Exhausted, Kaladin returns to the camp with the bridge crew.
