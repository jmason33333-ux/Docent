## Quick Summary
Shallan Davar meets Jasnah Kholin and witnesses her negotiating with King Taravangian. During a conversation, Shallan discovers that her acceptance as a ward is uncertain. Jasnah demonstrates her Soulcaster abilities to save Taravangian's granddaughter, but ultimately rejects Shallan as her ward due to her inadequate education in history and philosophy. Shallan's true motive is revealed: she intends to steal Jasnah's Soulcaster to aid her family.

---

## Key Beats
- Shallan observes Jasnah Kholin in conversation with King Taravangian.
- Jasnah offers to hear Shallan's petition to become her ward, surprising Shallan.
- An evaluation of Shallan's education reveals her strengths and weaknesses.
- Jasnah uses her Soulcaster to transform a fallen stone into smoke, saving Taravangian’s granddaughter.
- King Taravangian agrees to grant Jasnah access to the Palanaeum.
- Jasnah rejects Shallan as her ward due to her lack of historical and philosophical knowledge.
- Shallan resolves to persevere, revealing her plan to steal Jasnah's Soulcaster.
