## Quick Summary
Shallan Davar meets Jasnah Kholin in Kharbranth and witnesses her using a Soulcaster to rescue King Taravangian's granddaughter. Despite Shallan's hopes, Jasnah rejects her as a ward due to Shallan's deficient historical and philosophical knowledge. Shallan is determined to persist because her true goal is to acquire Jasnah's Soulcaster to save her family.

---

## Key Beats
- Shallan meets Jasnah Kholin and is struck by her imposing presence and beauty.
- Jasnah is engaged in a conversation with King Taravangian of Kharbranth.
- Shallan learns that Jasnah will consider her petition to become her ward, despite Shallan's assumption of acceptance.
- A discussion ensues about Shallan's educational background, highlighting her strengths and weaknesses.
- Jasnah uses a Soulcaster to transform a fallen stone into smoke, rescuing the trapped granddaughter of Taravangian.
- Jasnah refuses to take Shallan as her ward, citing her lack of education in history and philosophy.
- Shallan resolves to continue her pursuit, revealing her intention to steal Jasnah's Soulcaster to aid her family's financial recovery.
