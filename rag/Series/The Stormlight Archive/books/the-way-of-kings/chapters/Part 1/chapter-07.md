## Quick Summary
Shallan attempts to win over Jasnah Kholin as her ward by waiting for her at the Palanaeum. While waiting, she encounters Kabsal, an ardent, who attempts to convert Jasnah to Vorinism. After Kabsal leaves, Shallan prepares to depart but is interrupted by an unimpressed Jasnah.

---

## Key Beats

- Shallan travels to the Palanaeum with the hope of convincing Jasnah Kholin to reconsider her as a ward.
- She is permitted to wait in Jasnah's reading alcove but is not allowed access to the Palanaeum itself.
- While waiting, Shallan sketches and writes a letter to Jasnah, arguing her case.
- Kabsal, an ardent, introduces himself and praises Shallan's sketches, revealing his intention to convert Jasnah to Vorinism.
- Kabsal departs, asking Shallan to inform Jasnah of his visit.
- Shallan begins to lacquer her drawings and is about to leave when Jasnah arrives, displeased.
