## Quick Summary
Kaladin struggles with his new life as a member of the bridge crews. Haunted by memories of his brother Tien, he becomes despondent, causing Syl to leave him. During a bridge run, Kaladin witnesses the death of a boy who reminds him of Tien, deepening his despair.

---

## Key Beats
- Kaladin has been part of the bridge crews for two to four weeks.
- Only one man from Kaladin's first bridge run is still alive.
- New recruits arrive, including a boy who reminds Kaladin of Tien.
- Kaladin struggles with his mental state and snaps at Syl.
- Syl, distressed by Kaladin's despondency, decides to leave him.
- During a bridge run, the boy resembling Tien and the last remaining man from Kaladin's initial crew are killed.
- Kaladin is left feeling dead inside and breaks down in tears.
