## Quick Summary
Kaladin has been part of the bridge crews for several weeks and is struggling with the harsh realities and losses. He becomes despondent and snaps at Syl, who leaves him. During another bridge run, a new young recruit who reminds Kaladin of his brother Tien is killed, deepening Kaladin's despair.

---

## Key Beats
- Kaladin reflects on his time as a bridge crew member, noting the high mortality rate.
- He notices a new recruit who resembles his younger brother, Tien.
- Kaladin's mental state deteriorates, leading to a confrontation with Syl, who leaves him.
- During a bridge run, the young recruit and the last survivor from Kaladin's first run are killed.
- Kaladin feels emotionally dead inside and breaks down crying.
