## Quick Summary
Kaladin assists his father, Lirin, in amputating a young woman's finger. The procedure prompts a discussion between them about career paths, with Lirin advocating for Kaladin to pursue a future in medicine rather than soldiering. Lirin reveals his hopes of sending Kaladin to Kharbranth for further training, emphasizing Kaladin's natural talent for surgery.
