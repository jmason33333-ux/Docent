## Quick Summary
Nine years ago, young Kaladin, nicknamed Kal, assists his father, Lirin, in performing surgery on a woman named Sani. After the procedure, a discussion ensues between Kaladin and Lirin about the virtues of being a surgeon versus a soldier. Lirin expresses his hope for Kaladin to train as a surgeon in Kharbranth.

---

## Key Beats

- Kaladin helps his father, Lirin, perform surgery to amputate Sani's middle finger.
- Lirin questions Kaladin's tardiness, learning he was with Jam practicing quarterstaff skills.
- A debate arises between Kaladin and Lirin regarding the value of soldiering compared to surgery.
- Lirin argues that saving lives through killing is futile, akin to fighting a storm by blowing harder.
- Lirin quizzes Kaladin on surgical knowledge and reveals plans to send him to Kharbranth for further training.
- Lirin praises Kaladin's natural talent for surgery and discourages him from pursuing soldiering.
