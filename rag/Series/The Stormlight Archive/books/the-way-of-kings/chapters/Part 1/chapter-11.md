## Quick Summary
Kaladin, feeling hopeless, heads to the Honor Chasm intending to end his life. He is stopped by Syl, who brings him a blackbane leaf and persuades him to keep fighting. Kaladin confronts Gaz, demanding to become the bridgeleader of Bridge Four. He begins to learn the names of his fellow bridgemen, noting their desperate situation.

---

## Key Beats
- Kaladin walks to the Honor Chasm with the intent of committing suicide.
- Gaz accuses Kaladin of trying to steal spheres left out in the storm.
- At the Honor Chasm, Syl appears with a blackbane leaf and encourages Kaladin.
- Kaladin reflects on his past failures and is convinced by Syl to try saving the bridgemen.
- Kaladin confronts Gaz and demands to be made bridgeleader, offering part of his wages.
- Kaladin returns to the barracks and begins learning the names of the other bridgemen.
