## Quick Summary
Kaladin, despondent after a highstorm, heads towards the Honor Chasm to end his life. Gaz confronts him, suspecting theft, but Kaladin ignores him. Just before taking the fatal step, Syl appears with a blackbane leaf, persuading him to reconsider. Inspired, Kaladin returns to camp, confronts Gaz, and secures leadership of Bridge Four.
