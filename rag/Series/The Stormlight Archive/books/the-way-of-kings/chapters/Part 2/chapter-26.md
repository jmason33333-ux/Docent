## Quick Summary
**Dalinar Kholin:** Dalinar is in his quarters, listening to Brightness Litima read from "The Way of Kings" while Renarin accompanies him. Normally a source of comfort, the reading now unsettles him, reminding him of his disagreements with Adolin and the disturbing nature of his visions. Dalinar confides in Renarin about the lack of support from Highprinces Aladar and Roion for his call for unity. As horns signal a forthcoming battle, Dalinar moves to the war room, where he orders Teleb to prepare the troops and grants permission to experiment with faster bridges carried by men.

Sadeas arrives to conduct interviews with Dalinar's soldiers, undeterred by the impending battle. Dalinar and Sadeas discuss the Thrill, a battle fervor that empowers warriors but is rarely spoken of openly. As the battle begins, Dalinar summons his Shardblade, Oathbringer, and joins Adolin in combat against the Parshendi. The Thrill invigorates him until a wave of revulsion overcomes him, forcing him to fight through nausea, prompted by a mysterious voice uttering "Life before death."

After the battle, Adolin secures the gemheart, marking victory. Dalinar, gazing eastward, spots a retreating group of Parshendi with a Shardbearer who did not join the fight, sparking his curiosity about their motives.
