## Quick Summary
Dalinar listens to Litima read from The Way of Kings, feeling unsettled by his visions and recent conflicts. Despite the lack of alliances with other highprinces, Dalinar prepares for battle. During the fight, the Thrill empowers him, but he is disturbed by a sudden revulsion. After the battle, Dalinar notices a Parshendi Shardbearer who did not join the fight, raising questions about the Parshendi's strategy.
