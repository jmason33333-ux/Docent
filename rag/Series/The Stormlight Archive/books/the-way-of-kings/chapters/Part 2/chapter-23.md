## Quick Summary
Kaladin and members of Bridge Four gather stones to Soulcast into food and search for knobweed to use its sap as an antiseptic. While working, they discuss their pasts, with Rock sharing how he became a bridgeman and Kaladin cryptically alluding to his own history. Despite Kaladin’s efforts, he realizes that the bridgecrew remains distant and he needs to find a way to truly inspire and lead them.

---

## Key Beats
- Bridge Four is tasked with gathering stones for Soulcasting.
- Kaladin sends Rock and Teft to find knobweed, guided by Syl.
- Syl plays a prank, leading Rock to a pile of dung.
- Kaladin attempts to engage the bridgecrew in conversation, but struggles to change their attitudes.
- The group retrieves knobweed from where it was hidden and heads to the Honor Chasm.
- While extracting knobweed sap, Rock recounts his demotion to bridgeman status.
- Kaladin hints at his own past and why he became a bridgeman.
