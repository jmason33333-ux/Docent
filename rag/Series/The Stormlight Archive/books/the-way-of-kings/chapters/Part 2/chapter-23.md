## Quick Summary
Kaladin and Bridge Four are tasked with gathering rocks to be Soulcast into food. During this duty, Kaladin enlists Rock and Teft to search for knobweed, which contains a valuable antiseptic sap. Syl, who is visible to Rock, assists them in finding the knobweed by playfully leading Rock to it, though she also leads him to a pile of dung as a joke. Kaladin discreetly attaches bundles of knobweed to the bottom of their cart to avoid detection.

Later, Kaladin, Rock, and Teft sneak into the wagonyard to retrieve the knobweed and find discarded liquor bottles to store the sap. They proceed to the Honor Chasm to extract the sap. During the extraction process, Teft questions Kaladin about his determination to lead Bridge Four. Kaladin explains that he feels responsible as the bridgeleader to improve their lives, though he realizes that saving their lives is not enough to earn their loyalty.

Rock shares his backstory, revealing that his leader, the nuatoma, dueled Highprince Sadeas for Shardplate and lost, resulting in Rock's servitude to Sadeas. Rock was demoted to a bridgeman after putting chull dung in Sadeas’s food. Teft then asks Kaladin about his own past. Kaladin cryptically mentions that he killed a man, which was not murder, and that his refusal of a gift from a lighteyes led to his current situation.
