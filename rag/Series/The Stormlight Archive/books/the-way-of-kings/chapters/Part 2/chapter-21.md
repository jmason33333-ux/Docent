## Quick Summary

Kaladin wakes up in the barracks, grappling with his motivation to continue his rigorous training routine. Observing the bridgemen's expectation, he decides to get up, realizing they had been watching him to see if he would continue his leadership and training from the previous day. Kaladin then checks on the injured bridgemen from the previous bridge run. Two of them are mostly fine, but one, Hobber, is in dire need of antiseptic, which is scarce. After washing Hobber's wounds with water, Kaladin heads out to perform his morning exercises while carrying a plank, accompanied by Sylphrena, the spren.

During their conversation, Syl questions why men lie, a topic Kaladin struggles to answer. Syl notes that not all men lie, mentioning that the king's uncle doesn't, but Kaladin retorts that lighteyes are inherently dishonest. Gaz, the bridge sergeant, approaches Kaladin to inform him that Sadeas has ordered that the wounded bridgemen will not receive payment or food until they can run bridges again, leaving Kaladin to find a way to support them.

Determined, Kaladin appeals to the men of Bridge Four to share their food and contribute their pay for medical supplies. Despite initial laughter and mockery, Rock agrees to share his food with Hobber, feeling indebted to Kaladin for sparing him the deathline. Interestingly, Rock reveals he can see Syl, although she hasn't shown herself to anyone else.

Kaladin negotiates with Gaz to have his bridge crew assigned to stone-gathering duty for the day. He then enlists Rock and Teft's help to gather knobweed reeds, crucial for the antiseptic properties their sap provides, demonstrating his resourcefulness and leadership despite dire circumstances.
