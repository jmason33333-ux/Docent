## Quick Summary
Kaladin reluctantly gets out of bed, conscious of his role as a leader. After checking on the wounded bridgemen, he performs his morning training routine and discusses the nature of lying with Syl. Gaz informs Kaladin that Sadeas has ordered that the wounded will not receive food or pay, prompting Kaladin to ask Bridge Four to share resources. Later, he convinces Gaz to let the crew gather knobweed reeds, essential for antiseptic, during their duty.
