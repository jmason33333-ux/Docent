## Quick Summary
**Seven years ago** in the village of Hearthstone, young Kaladin overhears villagers speaking negatively about his father, Lirin. They accuse Lirin of stealing spheres from the recently deceased Brightlord Wistiow. The villagers are suspicious of Lirin's ability to write, something uncommon for a man of his standing. Kaladin meets his mother, Hesina, who advises him not to harbor hatred towards the villagers for their ignorance, emphasizing that they respect Lirin but are intimidated by his status as second nahn.

Kaladin reflects on his parents' plans to arrange a marriage for him with Laral, the daughter of a wealthy landowner. He is unsure of his future, torn between pursuing a path as a soldier or following in his father's footsteps to become a surgeon.

The chapter introduces the new citylord, Brightlord Roshone, who arrives with disdain for the village, feeling banished to this rural place. He blames Lirin for his predicament, accusing him of letting Wistiow die. Lirin and Hesina are uncertain whether Roshone's arrival will improve or worsen their situation, feeling the weight of his grudge against them.
