## Quick Summary
Dalinar attends a feast with his sons and discusses the king's paranoia and the investigation into the attempted assassination. Dalinar is frustrated with the nobles' disregard for the Alethi War Codes and is warned by Wit about rumors spreading. Lady Navani arrives unexpectedly, and King Elhokar appoints Sadeas as Highprince of Information to handle the investigation, sidelining Dalinar.

## Key Beats
- Dalinar, Adolin, and Renarin attend the king's feast.
- Adolin reports the girth strap incident as potentially intentional, increasing the king's paranoia.
- Dalinar reflects on the importance of truth over rumors regarding his highstorm episode.
- Dalinar is annoyed by the nobles' fashion and drunkenness, which violate the Alethi War Codes.
- Wit warns Dalinar about rumors concerning retreat from the Vengeance Pact.
- Lady Navani's unexpected arrival causes Dalinar to suppress his attraction to her.
- Elhokar appoints Sadeas as Highprince of Information, sidelining Dalinar's influence.
