## Quick Summary

**Dalinar Kholin:** Dalinar attends a feast with his sons Adolin and Renarin in the Alethi warcamp on the Shattered Plains. Adolin updates Dalinar on the investigation into the king's saddle strap, suggesting it was cut deliberately, though it might have been an accident. Dalinar is concerned about King Elhokar's increasing paranoia and refuses to discuss his own recent highstorm vision, suggesting that the rumors might actually be worse than what his men witnessed.

At the feast, Dalinar feels discomfort with the sloppy fashion and drunkenness of the nobles, which violate the Alethi War Codes he holds dear. Wit, the king's jester, makes pointed insults at the attendees, including a veiled warning to Dalinar about the spreading rumor of his suggestion to abandon the Vengeance Pact.

Lady Navani Kholin, the widow of Dalinar's late brother, Gavilar, unexpectedly arrives at the feast. Dalinar struggles with his feelings for her, trying to maintain propriety by addressing her as a sister.

During the feast, King Elhokar publicly announces the appointment of Torol Sadeas as Highprince of Information to investigate the attempt on his life. This appointment is a clear snub to Dalinar, suggesting the king's lack of confidence in him. Dalinar realizes that Sadeas is maneuvering against his idea of a unified Highprince of War, indicating a political struggle within the Alethi leadership.
