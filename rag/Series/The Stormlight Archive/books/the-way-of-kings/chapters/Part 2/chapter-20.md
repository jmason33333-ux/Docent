## Quick Summary

**Seven years ago**

Kaladin finds himself in a tense situation as he performs an unexpected surgery on a young girl from his village. The girl has suffered a severe injury, and Kaladin, who was nearby, steps in to help. Despite his efforts to stop the bleeding, he ultimately realizes that his actions were not the cause of her survival; instead, the girl succumbs to her injuries and dies.

Kaladin is left to grapple with the emotional weight of the situation, feeling the sting of failure. He watches as the girl's father grieves, understanding the gravity of not being able to save her. This moment becomes a learning experience for Kaladin, as his father, Lirin, approaches him with words of wisdom. Lirin reassures Kaladin that he did everything right and that the girl's death was not his fault. He imparts a valuable lesson, telling Kaladin that he must learn when to care deeply and when to let go, a concept that will shape Kaladin's future actions and decisions.
