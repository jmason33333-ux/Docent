## Quick Summary
Kaladin negotiates the sale of knobweed sap with the apothecary, who tries to cheat him. Later, Bridge Four is assigned chasm duty as punishment. As they work, Kaladin retrieves a spear and performs a kata, impressing his men. Afterward, Rock cooks a stew for Bridge Four, fostering camaraderie among the group.
