## Quick Summary
Kaladin revisits the apothecary to sell knobweed sap he and his men collected. The apothecary attempts to deceive him by claiming the "wild" knobweed is less effective. With Syl's assistance, Kaladin negotiates a fairer price, displaying his growing confidence and leadership skills. Despite contemplating escape, Kaladin remains committed to helping his fellow bridgemen.

Gaz assigns Bridge Four to chasm duty, where they are tasked with scavenging from corpses. This is a punishment for their unconventional actions, such as saving the wounded, which has earned them resentment from other bridge crews. During the chasm duty, Kaladin, Teft, and Rock engage in conversation, and they manage to involve Dunny, who reveals his ability to sing, helping to ease the tension among them.

As they progress, the group encounters many corpses. Kaladin picks up a spear and, overwhelmed by memories of his past, performs a kata. His display of skill captivates the other bridgemen, who initially mock him but are ultimately impressed. Kaladin quickly refocuses on the task at hand, downplaying the incident to Teft.

Syl, exploring alongside Rock and Dunny, discovers Parshendi corpses with their unique armor that grows from their bodies, hinting at the mysterious nature of Parshendi biology. After completing their duties, Kaladin contemplates unifying the crew and devises a plan. Back at the barracks, he collaborates with Rock to prepare a stew, hoping to foster camaraderie. Gradually, the men of Bridge Four gather around the fire, enjoying the meal together.

The next morning, Kaladin notices an increase in morale as many bridgemen rise early for exercise, indicating the success of his efforts to build team spirit.
