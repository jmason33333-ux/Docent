## Quick Summary
Kaladin wakes up early with a renewed resolve to improve the conditions for Bridge Four. He tries to motivate the other bridgemen to train in the mornings to reduce casualties, but they refuse after Gaz informs them they are not obligated. Kaladin bribes Gaz with a sphere to secure some leeway and spends the morning training alone. Sylphrena reveals she is changing, gaining new understanding, which mirrors Kaladin’s own transformation.
