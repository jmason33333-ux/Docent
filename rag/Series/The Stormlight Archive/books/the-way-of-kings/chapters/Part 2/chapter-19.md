## Quick Summary
Dalinar experiences a vision during a highstorm, finding himself in a barn with a young girl, Seeli, who believes he is her father. A monstrous creature attacks, forcing Dalinar to flee with Seeli to a nearby house where a woman named Taffa, who also thinks he is her husband, awaits. Dalinar fights off more creatures called Midnight Essence, aided by two Knights Radiant, who invite him to train at Urithiru. A voice that Dalinar suspects is The Almighty instructs him to "Unite them." Dalinar awakens back in the barracks, reassured of his clarity.

---

## Key Beats
- The storm transports Dalinar to a barn with a frightened girl, Seeli, who believes Dalinar is her father.
- A creature bursts in, prompting Dalinar to escape with Seeli to a house where Taffa, the woman who thinks Dalinar is her husband, is waiting.
- Dalinar barricades the door, but more creatures enter, leading to a fight where he uses an iron poker as a weapon.
- Upon killing the creatures, Dalinar notices their wounds emit smoke instead of blood.
- The group attempts to flee along a river but is attacked again by the creatures.
- Two Knights Radiant appear, assist in defeating the creatures, and clarify that they are Midnight Essence, not Voidbringers.
- The Knights invite Dalinar to Urithiru and leave to assist others.
- Taffa speaks with a voice recognized by Dalinar as one from his previous visions, giving him cryptic advice and confirming trust in Sadeas.
- Dalinar awakens in the barracks on the Shattered Plains, calms the soldiers holding him, and exits with Renarin.
