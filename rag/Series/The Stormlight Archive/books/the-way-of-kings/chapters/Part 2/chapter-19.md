## Quick Summary
Dalinar Kholin experiences a vision during a highstorm, finding himself in an unfamiliar barn at night. A small, terrified girl named Seeli believes Dalinar to be her father. As they converse, a creature crashes through the barn wall. Dalinar grabs Seeli and escapes to a nearby house where a woman, Taffa, mistakes him for her husband. After barricading themselves, more creatures attack, and Dalinar fights them off with an iron poker. The creatures release smoke instead of blood when wounded.

Dalinar, Taffa, and Seeli attempt to flee to a river but are ambushed by more creatures. As Dalinar struggles, two Knights Radiant appear, help him defeat the creatures, and inform him they are not Voidbringers but Midnight Essence. They invite Dalinar to Urithiru to train as a Radiant.

Taffa speaks with a voice not her own, which Dalinar recognizes as that of The Almighty. The voice urges him to "Unite them" and advises trust in Sadeas. Before Dalinar can ask more, he wakes back in the barracks on the Shattered Plains, soldiers holding him down during the vision. Dalinar assures them he is clear-minded and leaves with his son, Renarin.
