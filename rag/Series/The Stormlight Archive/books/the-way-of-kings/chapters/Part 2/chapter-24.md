## Quick Summary
Dalinar Kholin meets with Highprince Roion in the king's Gallery of Maps to propose a joint plateau assault, hoping to unite the highprinces. Roion is skeptical of Dalinar’s intentions and accuses him of losing his sanity. After Roion leaves, Adolin arrives and expresses concern over Sadeas investigating their warcamp, fearing a setup. Dalinar insists on trusting Sadeas, leading to a heated argument with Adolin.
