## Quick Summary

**Dalinar:**

Dalinar Kholin stands alone within the king's Gallery of Maps, contemplating his plans to unite the highprinces as per his visions. His current aim is to forge an alliance with Highprince Roion for a joint plateau assault. Roion, known for having the fewest gemhearts among the highprinces, arrives but is wary and suspicious of Dalinar's intentions. Dalinar offers a compromise, suggesting they split any gemhearts acquired and allow Roion to claim the first set of Shardplate. Despite this, Roion remains distrustful, insinuating that Dalinar's mental stability is compromised by his highstorm-induced visions. Roion exits the meeting, leaving Dalinar disappointed but not deterred.

**Adolin:**

Adolin Kholin arrives shortly after, curious about the results of Dalinar's meeting. Upon learning that it was unproductive, Adolin informs Dalinar that Sadeas seeks permission to enter their warcamp under the pretense of investigating threats to King Elhokar. Adolin is skeptical of Sadeas's motives, fearing he might manufacture evidence against Dalinar. However, Dalinar insists that they should trust Sadeas, as advised by his visions. This leads to a heated argument between father and son, with Adolin accusing Dalinar of relying too heavily on what he views as hallucinations. The confrontation ends with Dalinar asking Adolin to leave, underscoring the growing tension between them.

## Rowan's "If Asked" Notes

**Q: Why does Dalinar want to unite the highprinces?**  
A: Dalinar believes that the kingdom's survival depends on unity, as implied by his highstorm visions. He sees cooperation as essential to overcoming external threats and internal divisions.

**Q: What is the significance of the Gallery of Maps?**  
A: The Gallery of Maps is a strategic location where military plans are discussed, symbolizing Dalinar's efforts to navigate the political landscape and his ambitions for unifying the highprinces.

**Q: Why is Roion suspicious of Dalinar's offer?**  
A: Roion is wary because he suspects Dalinar might use the joint assault to seize control of resources or assert dominance, reflecting the distrust among the highprinces.

**Q: How does Adolin view Dalinar's visions?**  
A: Adolin is skeptical and frustrated, viewing them as hallucinations that undermine practical decision-making. He worries about their impact on their house's future.

**Q: What are the potential implications of allowing Sadeas into the warcamp?**  
A: Allowing Sadeas entry could lead to false evidence being fabricated against Dalinar, deepening political tensions and increasing the risk to the Kholin family.

**Q: How does Dalinar justify trusting Sadeas?**  
A: Dalinar's visions have urged him to trust Sadeas, suggesting a deeper plan or destiny at work, despite Sadeas's dubious history and Adolin's concerns.

**Q: Why is Dalinar's mental stability questioned?**  
A: Dalinar's reliance on visions, seen as hallucinations by others, leads to doubts about his leadership capabilities and mental health, especially after his lapses during highstorms.

**Q: What role does Adolin play in challenging Dalinar's decisions?**  
A: Adolin acts as a voice of reason and concern, questioning Dalinar's reliance on visions and advocating for more conventional, evidence-based strategies.

**Q: How does this chapter contribute to character development?**  
A: The tension between Dalinar and Adolin highlights their differing approaches to leadership and the strain on their relationship, providing insight into their characters and future challenges.

**Q: How does Dalinar's offer to Roion reflect his strategy?**  
A: Dalinar's offer to share spoils and Shardplate demonstrates his willingness to compromise and his strategic thinking in fostering alliances for greater unity.
