## Quick Summary
Adolin oversees the aftermath of a chasmfiend attack while reflecting on the political dynamics surrounding his father, Dalinar. A tense exchange occurs in Elhokar's pavilion, involving accusations and taunts between Sadeas and the Kholins. Dalinar investigates a potential assassination attempt on Elhokar and discusses Sadeas's role during Gavilar's assassination, while Elhokar remains paranoid about threats to his life.

---

## Key Beats
- Adolin supervises cleanup after the chasmfiend attack and notes a shift in how others treat Dalinar.
- In Elhokar's pavilion, a heated discussion between Elhokar and Dalinar is interrupted by Adolin and Sadeas exchanging insults.
- Wit intervenes, mocking Sadeas and complimenting Renarin.
- Adolin and Dalinar investigate Elhokar's saddle strap, suspecting it might have been cut, indicating a potential assassination attempt.
- Dalinar and Adolin manipulate Vamah into using Elhokar's Soulcasters, increasing Elhokar's income.
- Sadeas reveals his cooperation with Dalinar in manipulating Vamah, sparking Adolin's realization of their political maneuvering.
- Dalinar confides in Adolin about Sadeas's role as a decoy during Gavilar's assassination, ensuring Elhokar's protection.
- Elhokar argues with Dalinar over his paranoia about assassination attempts.
- Dalinar reflects on his failure to interpret Gavilar's last words and his commitment to protecting Elhokar.
