## Quick Summary
Adolin and Dalinar Kholin conduct inspections of their battalions, and Dalinar reflects on potential changes in leadership and his waning interest in battle. Adolin questions his father's delegation of responsibilities, and Dalinar considers abdicating his position. A messenger from Thanadal delivers disappointing news, and Dalinar is left to contemplate his future, while Navani introduces new intrigue regarding Jasnah's research.

---

## Key Beats
- Adolin observes Dalinar's behavior during an inspection and expresses concern.
- Teleb's portable bridge demonstration fails, but Adolin encourages further development.
- Dalinar contemplates the political and economic situation on the Shattered Plains and discusses the importance of soldiers' families being present.
- After inspecting the 5th Battalion, interrogation of soldiers reveals their loyalty may have worsened a situation with Sadeas.
- Thanadal cancels a meeting with Dalinar, leaving Sadeas as the only highprince willing to work with him.
- Dalinar grapples with the decision to abdicate and seeks solitude to ponder his choices.
- Navani reminds Dalinar of an appointment, leading to a conversation about Jasnah's research and a drawing of a chasmfiend.
- Dalinar reveals his intention to abdicate to Navani, who believes it's a mistake.
