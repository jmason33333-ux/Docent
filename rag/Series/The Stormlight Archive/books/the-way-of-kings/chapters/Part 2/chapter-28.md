## Quick Summary

**Adolin:**
Adolin accompanies Dalinar on an inspection of their troops, observing his father's behavior with growing concern. During the inspection, Teleb demonstrates a prototype of a new portable bridge, which fails. Despite the failure, Adolin encourages further development of this innovation. As they proceed, Dalinar questions Adolin about the Thrill, a battle frenzy that many soldiers experience, and notes the absence of Shards for ordinary people. Adolin supports Dalinar's decision to allow soldiers to bring their families to the Shattered Plains, a move that Dalinar reflects on as having significant political and economic implications. After inspecting the 5th Battalion, Dalinar delegates the interrogation of soldiers questioned by Sadeas to Adolin, who discovers the men's loyalty may have complicated matters. Dalinar assigns Tadet to conduct further interviews.

Their inspection is interrupted by a messenger from Thanadal, who cancels a meeting with Dalinar and declines to participate in a joint plateau assault. With all other highprinces refusing his offer, Dalinar is left with only Sadeas as a potential ally. He leaves Adolin to continue inspections, hinting at something he needs to do.

**Dalinar:**
Dalinar contemplates abdication, confident in Adolin's ability to lead. He orders Niter to fetch his war hammer and begins working on a latrine, using the manual labor to think through his decision. He reflects on his changing nature, influenced by the Codes, the book, and his visions, which have distanced him from his past warrior self. Navani interrupts, reminding him of an appointment and informing him that Jasnah's Spanreed is flashing. As they walk to the viewing room, Navani hints at a possible relationship, but Dalinar declines, citing tradition.

In the sitting chamber, Dalinar finds Adolin introducing his new love interest, Danlan Morakotha. Jasnah requests Dalinar recount his first encounter with the Parshendi and inquires about their possession of Shardblades, to which he replies they appeared after Gavilar's death. Jasnah then shows a drawing by her ward, Shallan, of a creature referred to as a Voidbringer, which Dalinar identifies as a chasmfiend. Dalinar urges Jasnah to return to the Shattered Plains. Alone with Navani, he reveals his decision to abdicate, which she argues is a mistake.

## Rowan's "If Asked" Notes

**Q: Why is Dalinar considering abdication?**
A: Dalinar is troubled by his changing perspective due to his visions and the Codes. He worries about his effectiveness as a leader and considers stepping down to allow Adolin to lead, whom he believes is ready for the responsibility.

**Q: What is the significance of the failed bridge prototype?**
A: The failed bridge prototype represents innovation and the potential for tactical advancements. Adolin's encouragement to continue its development shows a commitment to improving their strategies.

**Q: Why does Dalinar refuse Navani's advances?**
A: Dalinar adheres to Alethi tradition, which views a widowed sister-in-law as a sister in truth. Despite his feelings, he prioritizes tradition over personal desires.

**Q: How does Dalinar feel about the Thrill?**
A: Dalinar reflects on the Thrill as a once-welcomed battle frenzy that now feels monstrous to him. This change illustrates his evolving mindset and discomfort with violence.

**Q: What does the epigraph suggest?**
A: The epigraph hints at an urgent plea for support in a significant conflict. It emphasizes the importance of unity to prevent disaster.

**Q: Who are the highprinces mentioned, and what is their role?**
A: Thanadal, Hatham, and Bethab are highprinces who have declined to join Dalinar in a joint assault, highlighting the political isolation Dalinar faces.

**Q: What is the purpose of the Spanreed conversation with Jasnah?**
A: The Spanreed conversation allows Jasnah to gather information on past events, specifically the Parshendi and Shardblades, aiding her research and understanding of current events.

**Q: Why is Shallan's drawing important?**
A: Shallan's drawing is significant because it depicts a creature thought to be a Voidbringer. Jasnah uses it to explore misconceptions and to further her investigation into historical and current threats.

**Q: What are Dalinar's concerns about the Shattered Plains?**
A: Dalinar is concerned about the long-term political and economic impact of their presence on the Shattered Plains, particularly the de facto colonization and resource exploitation.
