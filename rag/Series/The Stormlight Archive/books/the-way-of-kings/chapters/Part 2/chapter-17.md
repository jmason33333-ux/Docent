## Quick Summary
Kaladin visits an apothecary for supplies but can only afford basic items. He returns to camp just as a horn summons the bridgemen for a bridge run. During the run, Kaladin takes Rock's position at the front, facing significant danger. Despite injuries, he tends to wounded bridgemen, negotiating with Gaz to transport them back on the bridge.

---

## Key Beats
- Kaladin attempts to purchase antiseptic at an apothecary but lacks funds.
- The horn sounds, signaling a bridge run, and Kaladin quickly returns to camp.
- Kaladin chooses to run at the front of the bridge, risking his life.
- The deathline is hit by arrows; Kaladin sustains minor injuries.
- Kaladin prioritizes treating the wounded over personal safety.
- He bribes Gaz to allow wounded men to be carried back on the bridge.
