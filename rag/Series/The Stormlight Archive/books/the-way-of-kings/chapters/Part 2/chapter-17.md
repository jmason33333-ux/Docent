## Quick Summary

Kaladin, a leader among the bridgemen, visits an apothecary in hopes of acquiring antiseptic for the wounded. However, the prices are exorbitant, forcing him to settle for cheaper supplies like bandages and sewing materials. This reflects Kaladin's ongoing struggle with limited resources as he tries to care for his men.

As Kaladin returns to camp, a horn signals the call for a bridge run. Despite his exhaustion, Kaladin maintains his resolve. During the run, he chooses to stand at the front of the bridge, replacing Rock and demonstrating his leadership and commitment to his men. This position, known as the "deathline," is perilous, and Kaladin narrowly escapes with only minor injuries while his companions suffer more severe wounds.

After the bridge is set, Kaladin immediately shifts his focus to treating the injured bridgemen, displaying his medical skills and deep care for his team. He organizes his men to carry the wounded back to camp on the bridge, a decision that Gaz, the overseer, initially opposes. Kaladin manages to persuade Gaz by offering him a bribe, illustrating the corrupt environment in which he operates.

Throughout the chapter, Kaladin's actions underscore his leadership qualities and the burdens he bears as he fights for the survival and morale of his men, despite the oppressive circumstances.
