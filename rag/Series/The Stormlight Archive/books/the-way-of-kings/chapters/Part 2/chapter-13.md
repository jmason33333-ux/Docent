## Quick Summary
**Dalinar:** Dalinar, Adolin, and Elhokar all immediately begin to race for the chasmfiend, hoping to kill it before it can harm any bystanders. Sadeas uses his grandbow, weakening it from a distance while Dalinar and Adolin go for its many legs and Elhokar distracts it. While doing so, however, the strap on Elhokar's saddle breaks, causing him to be thrown to the ground. Just as he is about to be crushed by the chasmfiend, Dalinar hurls himself beneath the descending claw and, with Shardplate-enhanced strength, catches it, giving Elhokar time to escape.

**Adolin:** Adolin continues to cut off its legs, and the chasmfiend is eventually unable to support its own weight. Elhokar summons his Shardblade once again and uses it to kill the felled chasmfiend, then harvest its gemheart.

## Rowan's "If Asked" Notes

**Q: What are the "ten heartbeats"?**  
A: The chapter title refers to the fact that Shardblades take ten heartbeats to summon. When Elhokar needs his Shardblade to kill the chasmfiend, he must wait for ten heartbeats before it appears. This is a limitation of Shardblades that bonded spren don't have.

**Q: How does Dalinar save Elhokar?**  
A: When Elhokar's saddle strap breaks and he's thrown to the ground, the chasmfiend is about to crush him. Dalinar hurls himself beneath the descending claw and, using his Shardplate-enhanced strength, catches it, giving Elhokar time to escape. This is an incredibly heroic and selfless act.

**Q: Why does the saddle strap break?**  
A: The strap on Elhokar's saddle breaks during the fight, causing him to be thrown to the ground. This is suspicious and will be investigated in the next chapter, as it may have been cut as an assassination attempt.

**Q: How do they kill the chasmfiend?**  
A: Sadeas uses his grandbow to weaken it from a distance, while Dalinar and Adolin cut off its many legs. Once the chasmfiend can no longer support its own weight, Elhokar summons his Shardblade (after ten heartbeats) and uses it to kill the felled creature, then harvests its gemheart.

**Q: What is a grandbow?**  
A: A grandbow is a powerful weapon used by Sadeas to weaken the chasmfiend from a distance. It's likely a large, powerful bow designed for hunting or combat against large creatures.

**Q: Why is this chapter important?**  
A: This chapter shows Dalinar's heroism and protective nature, especially toward Elhokar. It also demonstrates the teamwork required to take down a chasmfiend and introduces the suspicious breaking of Elhokar's saddle strap, which will be important in the next chapter.
