## Quick Summary
Dalinar, Adolin, and Elhokar race to confront a chasmfiend threatening bystanders. Sadeas aids from a distance with his grandbow. During the fight, Elhokar's saddle strap breaks, nearly causing his demise under the chasmfiend's claw. Dalinar heroically intervenes, saving Elhokar. The battle concludes with Elhokar delivering the final blow and harvesting the chasmfiend's gemheart.
