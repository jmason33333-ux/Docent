## Quick Summary
Adolin investigates the damaged saddle strap and learns it might have been cut. Dalinar works to influence other highprinces and proposes to Elhokar that he be made Highprince of War. Renarin discusses his future with Dalinar, and they take shelter from a highstorm together.

## Key Beats
- Adolin examines Elhokar's saddle strap with the leatherworkers and learns it might have been cut.
- A chasmfiend sighting occurs, but Dalinar decides not to pursue it, disappointing Adolin.
- Dalinar discusses military strategy with his scribes and decides to increase patrols.
- Renarin speaks with Dalinar about acquiring Shardplate and a Shardblade.
- Adolin talks to Kadash about Dalinar's visions, and Janala leaves him due to his distraction.
- Dalinar proposes to Elhokar to end the war or appoint him Highprince of War.
- Dalinar and Renarin take shelter in a barracks to avoid a highstorm.
