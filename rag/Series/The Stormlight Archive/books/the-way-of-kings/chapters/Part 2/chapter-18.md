## Quick Summary

**Adolin:**

Adolin is walking with Janala, his current romantic interest, and visits the leatherworkers to inspect a strap from Elhokar's saddle. The leatherworkers reveal that the strap was likely cut, though possibly by a buckle. The arrival of a chasmfiend is announced by a horn, but Adolin is disappointed when Dalinar decides not to pursue it, indicating a shift in priorities.

**Dalinar:**

Dalinar is engaged in strategizing with his scribes, aiming to guide the other highprinces to genuinely support Elhokar. Teshav, an officer's wife, questions the troop deployments, to which Dalinar responds by increasing patrols due to rising bandit activity. Renarin approaches Dalinar, who promises him Shardplate and a Shardblade if Dalinar captures new ones, hoping to enable Renarin to overcome his physical limitations.

**Adolin:**

Seeking insight into Dalinar's highstorm visions, Adolin consults Kadash, an ardent. Kadash suggests that Dalinar's visions might be signs of madness or vivid dreams. Meanwhile, Janala leaves Adolin, frustrated by his inattentiveness.

**Dalinar:**

Dalinar reports to Elhokar and questions the prolonged nature of their war with the Parshendi, hinting at withdrawal. Elhokar is taken aback, but Dalinar clarifies that a new strategy is needed to win. Dalinar proposes to be appointed as Highprince of War, a suggestion Elhokar considers.

Dalinar and Renarin attempt to return to their camp but are caught by an approaching highstorm. They seek refuge in a barracks just before the storm arrives.
