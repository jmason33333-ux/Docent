## Quick Summary

**Wit:** In Kholinar, Wit engages in a philosophical conversation with a group of guardsmen. He poses the question, "What is the talent that men value most?" The guards offer various answers, including art, but Wit dismisses each suggestion with his characteristic wit and humor. This interaction reflects Wit's role as a philosopher and observer, often prompting deeper contemplation among those he encounters.

The scene is interrupted by the dramatic arrival of Talenel, also known as Taln, who stumbles into the courtyard. Taln, appearing disheveled and exhausted, bears a dire message about the impending Everstorm, a prophecy that has significant implications for the world. As he collapses, an Honorblade clatters to the ground beside him, a symbol of his identity as one of the Heralds.

Wit concludes the scene with a poignant observation on timeliness, pointing out that despite Taln's return, he has arrived "too late." This moment underscores the urgency and gravity of the events to come, foreshadowing the challenges that lie ahead for the characters and the world.
