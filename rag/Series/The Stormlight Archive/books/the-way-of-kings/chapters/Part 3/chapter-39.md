## Quick Summary
Shallan is consumed by thoughts of the deaths of four robbers, drawing their scenes despite not having intentionally memorized them. She reflects on philosophical concepts related to her theft of Jasnah's Soulcaster. After a disturbing moment with her sketches, she talks to her brother Balat via spanreed. Later, in the palace gardens, Shallan attempts to figure out how to use the Soulcaster but finds herself unsuccessful.

---

## Key Beats
- Shallan draws scenes of the robbers' deaths, though she hadn't consciously remembered them.
- She reflects on Jasnah's philosophical teachings and her own theft of the Soulcaster.
- Disturbed by an unexpected sketch of a man in blood, Shallan rushes out and encounters a servant.
- Shallan speaks with Balat via spanreed, discussing the Soulcaster theft and their father's associates.
- Concerned about the Soulcaster's safety, she secures it in her safe-pouch.
- In the gardens, Shallan calms herself by drawing snails and plants, and attempts to use the Soulcaster.
