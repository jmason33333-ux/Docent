## Quick Summary
**Shallan:** Shallan discusses the moral implications of Jasnah's actions with her. She acknowledges that while Jasnah may have been technically justified in killing the footpads, her actions were still unethical. Jasnah is pleased with Shallan's learning and dismisses her for the day. Puzzled by Jasnah's lack of reaction to the stolen Soulcaster, Shallan attempts unsuccessfully to use it herself, wondering if it's a fake. While taking a break to sketch, she hears a mysterious voice, which she dismisses as a trick of her imagination. A maid enters, and Shallan uses the opportunity to search Jasnah's room for notes on Soulcasting. She finds notebooks about Natanatan, Urithiru, and Voidbringers, questioning Jasnah's interest in these seemingly mythical topics.

Kabsal visits Shallan, bringing bread and jam for a picnic. While discussing the origins of the Truthberry jam, Kabsal flirts with Shallan, hinting he might leave the ardentia for her. Shallan, though not fully reciprocating, encourages him. Kabsal reveals his original plan to involve Shallan in stealing the Soulcaster, but his superiors feared it might incite a war. Shallan inquires about Soulcasting techniques, and Kabsal suggests a simple method that Jasnah does not seem to use. After he leaves, Shallan finds a note from Captain Tozbek, informing her of his arrival in a week. Determined to learn more before departing, Shallan returns to study under Jasnah.

**Q: Why is Kabsal interested in Shallan?**

**Q: What is the importance of the mysterious voice Shallan hears?**

**Q: Why hasn't Jasnah reacted to the stolen Soulcaster?**

**Q: What does Kabsal reveal about his plans?**
A: Kabsal initially intended to involve Shallan in a plan to steal the Soulcaster, but his superiors deemed it too risky due to potential political repercussions.
