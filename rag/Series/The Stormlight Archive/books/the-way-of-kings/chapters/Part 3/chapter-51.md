## Quick Summary
Kaladin waits in Amaram's waiting room, reflecting on the recent battle where only four of his twenty-six men survived. Although he killed a Shardbearer, he refused to take the Shards, a decision Amaram questions. Amaram then betrays Kaladin, ordering the execution of his surviving men and branding Kaladin as a slave, claiming credit for the Shardbearer's death for himself.

---

## Key Beats
- Kaladin waits numbly in Amaram's waiting room, haunted by the loss of his men.
- He reflects on his decision to refuse the Shards, considering it foolish but morally right.
- Amaram enters with a Stormwarden, discussing the Shardbearer’s identity.
- Amaram questions Kaladin about his actions in the battle and his refusal of the Shards.
- Without warning, Amaram’s soldiers kill Kaladin's surviving men as he is restrained.
- Amaram, feeling guilty, rationalizes his betrayal, citing the Shards' importance.
- Kaladin is branded a slave as Amaram fabricates a story about the battle.
