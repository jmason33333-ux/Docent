## Quick Summary
Kaladin wakes up to find himself tied upside down outside the barrack, awaiting judgment by the Stormfather. Syl informs him of recent events, including Lamaril's execution. Rock, Teft, and Moash visit Kaladin, offering support and a dun sphere for luck. As the stormwall approaches, Kaladin remains determined to survive.
