## Quick Summary
Kaladin regains consciousness to find himself suspended upside down by his ankles outside the barracks, a punishment meant for death by exposure to the coming highstorm. Syl, his spren companion, informs him that Lamaril has been executed, and Gaz remains in his position, implying the instability and brutality within the camp's hierarchy. The punishment is to be judged by the Stormfather—essentially, survival of the storm will determine his fate.

Syl leaves and shortly returns with Bridge Four members Rock, Teft, and Moash, who risk punishment themselves to visit Kaladin. They inform him of the chaos caused by the side carry maneuver he implemented, which led to Lamaril's execution. The bridge crew expresses their gratitude and solidarity, ensuring Kaladin that his leadership has transformed them, and they will not regress to their previous state.

Despite Kaladin's precarious situation, he reassures his friends that he will survive the storm. Although the odds are slim, they choose to believe in him. Before leaving, Teft hands Kaladin a dun sphere as a token, a kind of talismanic gesture for luck. As his friends retreat to the barracks for safety, the stormwall arrives, marking the onset of the highstorm and the critical test of Kaladin's resilience.
