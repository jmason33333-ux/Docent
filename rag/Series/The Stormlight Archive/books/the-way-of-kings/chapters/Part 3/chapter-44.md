## Quick Summary
Kaladin repairs the roof of his home and receives a carved wooden horse from his brother, Tien. They are later joined by their mother, Hesina, who reassures Kaladin about their family's financial situation. The family attends a gathering where Laral is engaged to Roshone. Amaram arrives to recruit young men for the army, and when the conscription list is read, Tien's name is called. Kaladin volunteers to protect his brother, promising to return with him in four years.
