## Quick Summary
**Five years ago**

Kaladin is on the roof of his home, making temporary repairs. His younger brother, Tien, joins him and gives Kaladin a beautifully carved wooden horse. Kaladin is worried because his father, Lirin, had to spend another sphere, which could indicate financial trouble. Their mother, Hesina, comes out to reassure him, explaining that Lirin spent the sphere to bluff Toralin Roshone, the citylord, into thinking they were becoming desperate. They discuss Kaladin's future, and although other professions are suggested, Kaladin is determined to become a surgeon like his father.

Lirin soon calls them to a gathering in the town square. There, they discover that Laral, a childhood friend of Kaladin, is engaged to Roshone. This news shocks Kaladin, who is appalled and wants to intervene, but Lirin advises him against it. During the gathering, Roshone announces that Meridas Amaram is present to recruit soldiers, and several young men volunteer. However, the quota is not met, leading Amaram to ask Roshone to read a list of conscripts. The last name called is Tien, which devastates Kaladin. Amaram, familiar with the situation, asks for a different name, but Roshone insists on Tien's conscription.

In an effort to protect his brother, Kaladin volunteers to take Tien's place, but Roshone refuses to allow it. Instead, Kaladin volunteers to join the army alongside Tien, hoping to keep him safe. Their parents are heartbroken and leave the scene in tears. Kaladin makes a solemn vow to bring Tien back in four years.
