## Quick Summary
Shallan communicates with her brothers and Eylita via spanreed about obtaining Jasnah's Soulcaster. Balat shares troubling news about their father's associates demanding the Soulcaster back. Later, Shallan studies with Jasnah, discussing the Alethi monarchy and the nature of scholarship. Taravangian visits, requesting a drawing, leading to an unsettling moment for Shallan when she sketches mysterious creatures. The chapter concludes with discussions on faith and Jasnah's potential interest in a Devotary.
