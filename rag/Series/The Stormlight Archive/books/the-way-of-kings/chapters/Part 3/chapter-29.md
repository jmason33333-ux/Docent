## Quick Summary

**Shallan Davar:** In this chapter, Shallan is in her quarters communicating with her brothers via spanreed. They discuss how to obtain Jasnah's Soulcaster. Shallan reflects on her situation and the allure of her new life, but her brother Balat informs her of Luesh's death. He shares concerns about the people who visited after Luesh's death, suggesting they are the true owners of the Soulcaster and potentially dangerous. Balat describes a symbol seen on a pendant and tattoo worn by these individuals. After burning the transcript of their conversation, Shallan returns to her studies with Jasnah.

Later, while studying the Alethi monarchy, Shallan criticizes the authors for being "errorgant," which Jasnah explains is part of the "Assuredness Movement" characterized by overconfidence in their assertions. They discuss Shallan's tendency to speak her mind and Jasnah's views on scholarship and youth. Shallan ponders Jasnah's secretive research, particularly concerning King Gavilar's murder.

Taravangian joins them for lunch, during which he inquires about Jasnah's Soulcaster. Jasnah deflects the question. Taravangian asks Shallan to draw him, intending the portrait as a gift for his granddaughter. While drawing, Shallan unintentionally sketches strange creatures, which she conceals by crumpling the paper, offering to redo the drawing. After Taravangian departs, Jasnah and Shallan discuss him, leading to a broader conversation about faith and Devotaries. Jasnah expresses a tentative openness to joining one someday, then encourages Shallan to complete the sketch for the King.
