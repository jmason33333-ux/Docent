## Quick Summary
Brightlord Roshone and his son Rillir are attacked by a whitespine during a hunt. Rillir is mortally wounded, and Lirin, a surgeon, assesses that he cannot be saved, so he focuses on Roshone instead. Despite Roshone's insistence to help his son, Lirin saves Roshone's life, adhering to his ethical duty as a surgeon. Kaladin later questions Lirin's decision not to let Roshone die, leading to a conversation about morality and the value of life.
