## Quick Summary

**Kaladin:**
Kaladin is in a dire state, lying feverish and wounded in the barracks of Bridge Four. The injuries he sustained from being lashed to the highstorm have left him barely conscious for most of the time. In his few moments of lucidity, he sees deathspren gathering around him, signaling his precarious hold on life. Syl, appearing as a figure of pure light, fights off these deathspren with a sword of light. Struggling between life and death, Kaladin is tempted to give up, haunted by memories of failing his brother Tien and other comrades like Dallet. Despite his exhaustion, he clings to life for the sake of Bridge Four, determined not to let them down as he believes he has with others.

**Teft:**
While Kaladin battles internally, Teft, a member of Bridge Four, takes a risk to test a long-held theory from his past with the Envisagers—a belief he once rejected. As Skar watches over Kaladin, Teft secretly places three diamond spheres into Kaladin's hand, hoping for a reaction. To his amazement, Kaladin begins to breathe in Stormlight, which temporarily heals his wounds and revitalizes him. Teft is both relieved and frustrated, cursing the timing of this revelation. He realizes Kaladin has the ability to heal using Stormlight, but this must be managed discreetly to avoid unwanted attention and danger.
