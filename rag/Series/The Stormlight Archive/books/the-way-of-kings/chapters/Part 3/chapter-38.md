## Quick Summary
Kaladin is delirious with fever in Bridge Four's barrack, battling to survive after being exposed to a highstorm. Syl combats deathspren that appear around him, while Skar and Teft take turns guarding him. Teft tests a theory by placing diamond spheres in Kaladin's hand, leading to Kaladin unconsciously drawing in Stormlight and beginning to heal, confirming Teft's long-dismissed beliefs.
