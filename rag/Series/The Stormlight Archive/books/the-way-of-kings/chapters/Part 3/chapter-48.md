## Quick Summary
**Shallan:** 

Shallan is in a hospital in Kharbranth, recovering from a suicide attempt. During her stay, she sketches, finding comfort in the activity while under suicide watch. She contemplates the presence of strange creatures she calls "symbolheads" in her drawings, suspecting a connection to Soulcasting. Shallan is in a state of reflection, likening her current situation to the stifling environment of her father's estate, where she had every physical need met but felt trapped.

King Taravangian visits Shallan, granting her the permission to receive visitors. Jasnah Kholin, her mentor, comes to see her and attempts an awkward apology for the events leading up to Shallan's current state. Jasnah gives Shallan a copy of the "Book of Endless Pages," explaining its metaphorical significance.

Kabsal, a friendly ardent Shallan has interacted with before, enters with a basket of bread and strawberry jam. Jasnah is intrigued by the jam, examines it but doesn't consume any. Instead, she partakes in the bread. Kabsal encourages Shallan to try the jam, although she finds the smell repulsive. Despite her reluctance, she reluctantly tastes it. Shortly thereafter, Kabsal consumes a large amount himself, then suddenly collapses. Shallan rushes to his aid but starts feeling dizzy and ill as well. Jasnah quickly diagnoses that Shallan has been poisoned and urgently asks for a garnet, a key component for healing via Soulcasting.

In a desperate attempt to save herself, Shallan reveals the stolen Soulcaster hidden in her safepouch. As she attempts to use it, she experiences an intense heat within her before losing consciousness.
