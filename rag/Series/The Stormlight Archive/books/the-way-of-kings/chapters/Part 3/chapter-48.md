## Quick Summary
Shallan, recovering in a hospital room and on suicide watch, sketches and contemplates her situation, drawing connections between the Symbolheads and Soulcasting. King Taravangian visits and permits her visitors. Jasnah attempts to apologize and gifts Shallan a book. Kabsal brings bread and jam; however, the jam smells off, and after consuming it, Kabsal collapses, leading to a revelation about Shallan's stolen Soulcaster.
