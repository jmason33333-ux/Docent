## Quick Summary
Lopen joins Bridge Four and helps bring a water cart for the first time. During a bridge run, Kaladin realizes the Parshendi are anticipating them, threatening the safety of Bridge Four. He orders a side-carry maneuver, saving his crew but inadvertently causing a failure in Sadeas’s larger assault. Kaladin is nearly executed but manages to convince Lamaril and Gaz to spare him, resulting in a brutal beating instead.
