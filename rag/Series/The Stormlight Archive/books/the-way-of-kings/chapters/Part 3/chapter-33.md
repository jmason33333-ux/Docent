## Quick Summary
Shallan Davar navigates the Palanaeum, focusing on her secret mission to steal Jasnah's Soulcaster. She encounters Kabsal, who demonstrates the concept of Cymatics and attempts to recruit her to his Devotary. Jasnah later dismisses the significance of Kabsal's demonstration and warns Shallan about his intentions.
