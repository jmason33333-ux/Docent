## Quick Summary

**Shallan Davar:** As Shallan navigates the vastness of the Palanaeum, she reflects on her dual objectives: pursuing her intellectual curiosity and her secret mission to steal Jasnah's Soulcaster. Accompanied by a Parshman servant, she seeks a copy of "Dialogues" for her studies but is also intrigued by a book titled "Shadows Remembered," which turns out to be a collection of children's stories, much to her surprise.

Her thoughts shift to her family and the pressing need to secure their future by obtaining a Soulcaster. She hides in an alcove, contemplating the real Jasnah against her preconceptions of the heretic scholar, and wonders if she can muster the courage to discuss Soulcasting openly with Jasnah.

Upon returning to her study area, Shallan encounters Kabsal, who has brought bread and jam to share. Their conversation meanders through topics of Shallan's self-perception, Jasnah's heresy, and Kabsal's interest in converting Shallan to his Devotary. Kabsal introduces the concept of Cymatics, demonstrating how sound can create patterns in sand and suggesting these patterns are proof of the Almighty’s work. He uses this to segue into a discussion about saving Shallan's soul, indicating a deeper motive.

Jasnah arrives, unimpressed by Kabsal's Cymatics demonstration, and warns Shallan that Kabsal's apparent interest in her may merely be a pretext to access Jasnah and her Soulcaster. Shallan, caught between admiration, curiosity, and her duty to her family, faces the complexity of her situation.
