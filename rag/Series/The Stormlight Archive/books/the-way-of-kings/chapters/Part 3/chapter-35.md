## Quick Summary

**Kaladin:**
As the chapter begins, Kaladin is caught in the midst of a highstorm. The storm's ferocity flings him around, and he struggles to maintain his grip on the sphere he clutches, which seems to be his lifeline. Syl, his spren companion, remains by his side, urging him to grab hold of the roof. Despite the storm's overwhelming power, Kaladin manages to grasp a ring tied with ropes, fighting to stay anchored. Amidst the chaos, he catches glimpses of Syl standing before him, appearing to hold back the storm itself. Just when his grip begins to falter, he sees a vision of an enormous face and feels a chilling sensation coursing through him. Before losing consciousness, he notices the sphere glowing brightly.

**Teft:**
After the highstorm passes, Teft and Rock emerge from the safety of the barracks to find Kaladin. Despite their hopes, they are surprised to find him alive, though in a dire state. As Kaladin's eyes snap open, the other bridgemen are shocked and fall to the ground, overwhelmed by the sight. Rock calls for assistance to help Kaladin down from the roof. Teft notices that the sphere Kaladin had been holding is now dun, indicating it has expended its Stormlight.
