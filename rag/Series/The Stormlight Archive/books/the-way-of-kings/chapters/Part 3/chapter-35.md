## Quick Summary
Kaladin braces against a highstorm, clutching a glowing sphere and clinging to the barracks roof as the storm rages. He experiences visions and loses consciousness. After the storm, Teft and Rock discover him injured but alive, with the sphere now dulled.

## Key Beats
- Kaladin is struck by the stormwall and struggles to maintain his grip on the barracks roof.
- Syl advises Kaladin to hold onto the roof to avoid being swept away.
- During the storm, Kaladin sees a vision of an enormous face and feels an intense chill.
- The sphere he holds glows brightly during the storm and then becomes dun.
- After the storm, Rock and Teft find Kaladin's battered but conscious body.
- The bridgemen are shocked when Kaladin's eyes snap open.
