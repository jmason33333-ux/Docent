## Quick Summary

**Six years ago**

Kaladin is in Hearthstone, observing his father Lirin, who is inebriated and advising Kal not to return to their village after completing his studies at Kharbranth. The villagers' mistrust of Lirin is palpable, possibly due to his profession as a surgeon, which requires him to literally look beneath the skin. Kaladin feels anger towards the villagers who, under the influence of Roshone, stopped providing donations to their family. He suggests to his father that they use the spheres they have, but Lirin opposes this idea.

Lirin and Kaladin discuss the differences between Roshone, the current citylord, and Wistiow, the former citylord. Despite the hardships, Kaladin returns to his medical studies, carrying a rock that reminds him of his brother, Tien. Tien, unable to stand the sight of blood, has been learning carpentry from Ral instead of surgery.

Kaladin's thoughts drift towards the idea of becoming a soldier, but he is abruptly interrupted by the sound of banging on the door. A mob of villagers, including Luten, Horl, and Balsas, has gathered outside, accusing Lirin of stealing spheres. Unfazed, Lirin confronts them, daring the mob to attack. His boldness causes the villagers to disperse back into the night.
