## Quick Summary
Kaladin confronts his father, Lirin, who is drunk and bitter about the villagers' treatment of their family. Kaladin reflects on the past struggles his family faced and Lirin's profession as a surgeon. A mob of villagers, led by Luten, Horl, and Balsas, demand the return of stolen spheres, but Lirin stands his ground, ultimately dispersing the mob.

---

## Key Beats
- Kaladin finds Lirin drunk, who tells him not to return home after his studies.
- Kaladin considers the villagers' mistrust due to Lirin's surgical profession.
- Lirin laments the villagers' cessation of donations following Roshone's influence.
- Kaladin recalls his parents' attempt to get him early admission to Kharbranth's surgeons.
- A mob demands the return of spheres, accusing Lirin of theft.
- Lirin confronts the mob, daring them to act on their threats.
- The mob disperses, leaving Lirin and Kaladin in tense solitude.
