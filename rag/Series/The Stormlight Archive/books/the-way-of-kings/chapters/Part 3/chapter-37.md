## Quick Summary
**Five and a half years ago**

Kaladin, as a young boy, helps his mother Hesina with cooking while his younger brother Tien shows off another stone he has found. Kaladin quickly leaves when he sees a carriage arriving from the citylord Roshone, which is there to take his father Lirin for a meeting. Kaladin insists on accompanying his father, despite Lirin's surprise, as he wasn't supposed to know about the meeting.

The meeting involves a dispute over a large sum of spheres that Lirin obtained from Wistiow, the former citylord. Roshone offers a settlement where he would take nine-tenths, leaving only a small portion for Lirin. During the meal, Lirin refuses to eat, and an argument ensues. Kaladin, feeling indignant, is dismissed from the table by his father.

In the kitchen, Kaladin encounters Laral, Roshone's ward, and Rillir, Roshone's son. Rillir treats Kaladin as a servant, and Laral initially plays along. When Kaladin refuses to serve Rillir, Laral pleads with him to stop, and they leave together. This experience with the arrogant lighteyes reignites Kaladin's desire to become a surgeon rather than a soldier.

On the way home, Lirin explains his strategy to make Roshone focus on negotiating a deal rather than proving theft. Kaladin is shocked by the implication that his family might be involved in theft, but Lirin rationalizes it by saying Wistiow would have given them the spheres anyway. That night, Kaladin decides to go by his full name, Kaladin, symbolizing his transition towards adulthood.
