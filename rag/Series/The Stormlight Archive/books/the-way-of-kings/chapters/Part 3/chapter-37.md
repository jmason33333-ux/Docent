## Quick Summary
Kaladin helps his mother in the kitchen while Tien shows off a stone. When Roshone's carriage arrives for Lirin, Kaladin insists on accompanying him. At Roshone's manor, a tense negotiation about the spheres from Wistiow ensues. Kaladin later decides he wants to be a surgeon and chooses to embrace his full name, Kaladin.

---

## Key Beats
- Kaladin assists his mother with cooking; Tien shows him a pretty stone.
- A carriage from Roshone arrives to take Lirin for a meeting about Wistiow's spheres.
- Kaladin insists on joining his father despite Lirin's surprise.
- At Roshone's manor, Lirin and Kaladin sit down for a meal, but Lirin refuses to eat.
- Roshone offers a settlement on the spheres: nine-tenths for him, the rest for Lirin.
- Kaladin is dismissed from the table and meets Laral and Rillir in the kitchen.
- Rillir taunts Kaladin, treating him like a servant; Laral initially plays along but later asks Rillir to stop.
- On the way home, Lirin explains his tactic to make Roshone focus on a deal.
- Kaladin is shocked by the revelation of his family's involvement with the spheres.
- Kaladin decides he wants to be a surgeon and starts using his full name, Kaladin.
