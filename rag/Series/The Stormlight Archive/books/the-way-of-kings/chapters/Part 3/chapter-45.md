## Quick Summary
Shallan researches Gavilar's meetings with the Parshendi, noting inconsistencies in his scholarly pursuits. She is preparing to return to Jah Keved with the Soulcaster. A conversation with Kabsal reveals her plans to leave, and a disturbing drawing session leads to a frightening encounter with mysterious creatures. In her panic, Shallan inadvertently transports herself to Shadesmar, where she Soulcasts a goblet into blood. Jasnah arrives, prompting Shallan to cover up her actions by injuring herself.
