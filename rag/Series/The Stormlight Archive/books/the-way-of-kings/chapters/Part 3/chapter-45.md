## Quick Summary
**Shallan Davar:** Shallan continues her study in the Palanaeum, focusing on Gavilar's interactions with the Parshendi. She discovers an unusual interest Gavilar had in scholarly pursuits, which is inconsistent with his known character. Shallan plans to return to Jah Keved with the stolen Soulcaster to assist her family. While pretending to search for another book, she investigates the Voidbringers, aligning with Jasnah's research interests.

During her research, Kabsal approaches Shallan, engaging her in a discussion about the Voidbringers. He asks Shallan for a drawing of himself and later requests one including both of them. As she sketches, Shallan inadvertently draws creatures with symbol heads. Frightened by the appearance of these creatures in her sketches, she flees to her room. The creatures seem to follow her, manifesting in her artwork as she runs.

In her room, overwhelmed and trapped, Shallan begins to summon her Shardblade. One of the creatures questions her identity, to which she responds, "I'm terrified." This response transports her to Shadesmar, a mysterious realm where she inadvertently Soulcasts a goblet of wine into blood. Jasnah's voice from outside prompts Shallan to injure herself to explain the blood, fearing discovery of her Soulcasting abilities.
