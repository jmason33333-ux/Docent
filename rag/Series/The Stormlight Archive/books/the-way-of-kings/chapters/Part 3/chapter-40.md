## Quick Summary
Kaladin, recovering from his highstorm ordeal, rejoins Bridge Four as they prepare for a bridge run. Observing the battle, he realizes the true purpose of bridgemen as bait for the Parshendi. Later, Bridge Four gives Rock a razor as a gift, which moves him to tears. Kaladin grapples with despair, knowing the harsh reality of their situation.
