## Quick Summary
Kaladin ventures outside the barracks for the first time since his miraculous survival of the highstorm judgment, still weak but mostly healed. Bridge Four, engaged in their daily bridge training, reacts with surprise and awe at his appearance, noting that it's only been ten days since the storm. Moash jokes about Kaladin having bones like granite, while Rock comments on his resilience.

As the horns signal a bridge run, Kaladin joins Bridge Four, assisting Lopen and Dabbid with the water duties. Observing the battle, he reflects on the brutal truth of the bridgemen's role: they are bait for the Parshendi, expendable and cheap compared to trained soldiers.

Later, the camaraderie within Bridge Four is highlighted when they gift Rock a razor in appreciation for his culinary efforts. This gesture brings Rock to tears, prompting him to retreat to the barracks. Dunny then takes over serving the stew. Kaladin notices Sigzil's absence from the gathering and seeks him out, learning about the grim customs of Marabethia involving execution.

Rock returns, freshly shaved and in high spirits, offering to extend his grooming services to others. Despite this moment of levity, Kaladin is burdened by the grim reality of their situation, fearing the bridgemen's chances of survival remain slim.
