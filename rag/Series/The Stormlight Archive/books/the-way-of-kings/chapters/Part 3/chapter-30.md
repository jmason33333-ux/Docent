## Quick Summary

**Kaladin:** At the break of dawn, Kaladin leaves the barracks feeling optimistic as he is joined by all twenty-nine members of Bridge Four. He notices that the last holdout, Bisig, may have been coerced by Teft and Rock, but he chooses to overlook this. Kaladin leads his men in military exercises, drawing the attention and laughter of nearby carpenters and soldiers. Spotting Gaz watching them, Kaladin decides they have time to practice bridge hauling before breakfast.

**Gaz:** Gaz reflects on the loss of his eye and the darkness it brings. He meets with Lamaril to pay a bribe but only has half the amount. Lamaril is more concerned about Kaladin, who has become a potential problem. Gaz and Lamaril discuss Bridge Four’s unusual training and the possibility that Kaladin might have military experience. Lamaril is wary of making Kaladin a martyr by killing him and instead wants him to fall during a bridge run. He threatens Gaz with becoming a bridgeman if he fails to manage his debts.

**Kaladin:** Kaladin continues training Bridge Four in setting down the bridge from a raised position. Despite the short training period of two weeks, he is impressed with their progress. He contemplates practicing on a real chasm and reflects on their recent bridge runs, which resulted in two deaths (Amark and Koolf) and two injuries (Narm and Peet). Syl warns Kaladin after seeing Gaz and Lamaril together, prompting Kaladin to consider alternative ways to protect his men. He develops a strategy for a "side carry" to use different muscles, but doesn't reveal his real intention of using the bridge as a shield. Moash questions his appointment as squad leader, and after Kaladin explains it’s due to his strong will, Moash admits his distrust but remains curious.

**Gaz:** Gaz observes the unusual "side carry" method and calls Kaladin over for an explanation. Kaladin claims it’s to utilize different muscles. Gaz realizes that this technique might endanger Kaladin during an actual assault and suggests trying it on a run.
