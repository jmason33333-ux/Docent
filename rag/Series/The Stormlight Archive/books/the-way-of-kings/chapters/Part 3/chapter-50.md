## Quick Summary

**Shallan:**

Shallan wakes up in a private hospital room with a surprising sense of well-being, despite her recent brush with death. As she regains consciousness, a woman quickly leaves the room, presumably to inform someone of her awakening. Confused and with fragmented memories of recent events, Shallan struggles to recall what happened to her. Her memories gradually return, triggering a realization: she was poisoned. She also recalls Kabsal, the man who had shown interest in her.

In her room, a guard peeks in, and Shallan asks him about Kabsal's condition. The guard's silence and disapproving glare discourage her from leaving the bed. During this period of reflection, Shallan remembers returning the Soulcaster she had stolen to Jasnah.

Jasnah enters the room half an hour later, demanding to know the identity of the person who ordered Shallan to steal the Soulcaster. Shallan confesses that she acted alone, motivated by the financial despair facing her family. Jasnah reveals the disturbing truth: Kabsal had been trying to poison Shallan through the bread, with the jam serving as an antidote to ensure her survival. Jasnah expresses her profound disappointment, attributing Shallan's suicide attempt to the guilt of her theft. She informs Shallan that no one will take her as a ward now, and she has squandered a promising future. Jasnah leaves, leaving Shallan to confront her isolation and the consequences of her actions.
