## Quick Summary
Shallan wakes up in a private hospital room, struggling to remember her recent poisoning. She recalls returning the stolen Soulcaster to Jasnah and the events leading up to her current condition. Jasnah confronts Shallan about the theft, revealing that Kabsal had intended to poison her and expressing her disappointment in Shallan's actions.
