## Quick Summary

Kaladin leads Bridge Four through the chasms, contemplating the possibility of escaping to the east, inspired by the mysterious water flows. His thoughts are interrupted by Teft, who inquires if Kaladin has experienced surges of strength or other unusual phenomena, suggesting a subtle awareness of Kaladin's developing abilities. Kaladin reflects on his rapid healing and wonders if he might be cursed, like those who sought the Old Magic, suspecting that Syl might not be the only spren following him.

At a crossroads in the chasms, Kaladin decides this will be a training spot for Bridge Four. He imparts a key lesson: a soldier should care, contradicting the typical training that aims to humble recruits. Demonstrating the importance of balance over brute strength, Kaladin resists being pushed over by Skar and other bridgemen, illustrating the value of a solid stance. Teft, revealing his past military experience, is tasked with organizing the training.

Kaladin then discusses with Rock, who explains why he can't fight due to cultural reasons. Kaladin sends Rock, Lopen, Dabbid, and Shen to gather salvage, emphasizing the need to maintain their quota to avoid suspicion. Syl aids the group by becoming visible to help with the search, showcasing her unique abilities. Kaladin reassures Lopen of his future training while prioritizing their immediate needs. As the day progresses, Kaladin observes the rapid progress of Bridge Four, attributing their physical readiness to the demanding nature of bridge duty.
