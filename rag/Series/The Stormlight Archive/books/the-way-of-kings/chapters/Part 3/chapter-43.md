## Quick Summary
Kaladin wakes up to the grim reality of the bridgemen's fate as mere bait in battle. Despite new leadership under Brightlord Matal and his wife Hashal, Kaladin feels despondent. When ordered to train, he initially resists but eventually decides to teach his men to fight in hopes of escaping. Syl encourages him to hold onto hope, while Teft reminds Kaladin of the Radiant oath, "journey before destination."

---

## Key Beats
- Kaladin awakens with a sense of dread regarding the fate of the bridgemen.
- New leadership is introduced: Brightlord Matal and his wife, Hashal.
- Hashal dismisses Kaladin's defiance, ordering him beaten, but he restrains himself from fighting back.
- The bridgemen are relegated to chasm duty; Kaladin descends with Syl questioning his lack of joy in survival.
- Rock inquires about their next move, leading Kaladin to express his hopelessness.
- Teft's encouragement and a reminder of the Radiant oath prompt Kaladin to reconsider.
- Kaladin decides to train the men as spearmen with the hope of escaping.
- Rock opts out of fighting but agrees to cook for the group.
