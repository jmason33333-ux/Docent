## Quick Summary
Kaladin experiences a surreal vision during a highstorm, observing distant places and people, including Szeth, the Assassin in White. A booming voice identifies Kaladin as a child of Tanavast and Honor, warning of Odium's reign. Awakening from his vision, Kaladin interacts with his bridge crew and encounters Adolin Kholin, who intervenes in a street altercation.
