## Quick Summary

Kaladin experiences a surreal vision while riding the highstorm, which transports him across Roshar. During this vision, he observes various unfamiliar landscapes and cities, eventually arriving at a city configured in a triangular pattern. Here, he witnesses Szeth, the Assassin in White, immediately after committing an assassination. Kaladin perceives that Szeth may have sensed his presence. As the storm progresses toward the western ocean, Kaladin hears a booming voice identifying him as a child of Tanavast and Honor, warning that Odium comes. Kaladin questions the prevalence of war, and the voice replies, "Odium reigns."

Awakening from the vision, Kaladin finds himself restrained by his bridge crew, who had held him down to prevent him from running into the storm. They proceed to bathe in the riddens, and during this time, Rock shaves Kaladin and reveals that Sigzil is a Worldsinger. A new member joins Bridge Four, a Parshman who is dubbed Shen because he doesn't provide his name.

Later, as Kaladin wanders the camp, he encounters an officer assaulting a woman. Before Kaladin can intervene, a high-ranking officer, Adolin Kholin, arrives and diffuses the situation, offering to escort the woman away. Adolin gives Kaladin an emerald chip to deliver a message, but Kaladin chooses not to pass it along.
