## Quick Summary
Shallan Davar, while serving as Jasnah Kholin's bathing attendant, considers stealing Jasnah's Soulcaster but ultimately refrains. Jasnah decides to provide Shallan with a hands-on philosophy lesson by walking through a dangerous part of town at night. When they are attacked, Jasnah demonstrates her formidable Soulcasting abilities by transforming one attacker into fire, another into crystal, and using Stormlight to kill the remaining two. Shaken by the experience, Shallan returns to their rooms and decides to swap Jasnah's functional Soulcaster with her own broken one.
