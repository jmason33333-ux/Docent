## Quick Summary
**Shallan Davar:** Shallan is serving as Jasnah Kholin's bathing attendant and contemplates stealing Jasnah's Soulcaster. However, despite her desperation, she finds herself unable to go through with the theft. Her internal conflict is palpable, as she struggles between her duty to her family and her growing respect for Jasnah.

Later, Jasnah decides to give Shallan a lesson in philosophy through a practical experience. They venture out into a dangerous area of Kharbranth at night. During their walk, they are ambushed by a group of murderers. Jasnah showcases her formidable abilities by Soulcasting one of the attackers into fire, causing immediate panic among the remaining assailants. As they flee in terror, Jasnah Soulcasts another into crystal and uses Stormlight to kill the last two with a burst of energy.

Shallan is deeply shaken by the violence and power she witnesses. Upon returning to their rooms, Shallan is troubled by the morality of Jasnah's actions. She rationalizes that Jasnah, despite her apparent righteousness, should not possess such a powerful and sacred artifact. This leads Shallan to swap Jasnah's functioning Soulcaster with her own broken one, committing the theft she previously could not.
