## Quick Summary
Kaladin, nearing the end of his enlistment, decides to reenlist due to his guilt over Tien's death. He bribes a squadleader to have a new recruit, Cenn, transferred to his squad. During a battle, Kaladin's squad fights fiercely, but they are decimated by a Shardbearer. Kaladin manages to kill the Shardbearer but chooses not to claim the Shardblade, offering it to Coreb instead.

---

## Key Beats
- Kaladin decides to reenlist, unable to face his parents after Tien's death.
- He arranges for Cenn, a new recruit, to join his squad by bribing Gare.
- Kaladin reflects on the senselessness of Alethi internal conflicts.
- During battle, Kaladin saves Cenn and dispatches multiple enemies.
- A Veden Shardbearer attacks, killing many, including Cenn.
- Kaladin's squad fights the Shardbearer, but only four survive.
- Kaladin kills the Shardbearer by striking through the eye slit.
- He refuses the Shardblade, associating it with corrupt lighteyes, and assigns it to Coreb.
