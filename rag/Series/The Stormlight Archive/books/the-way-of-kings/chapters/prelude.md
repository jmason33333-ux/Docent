## Quick Summary
Kalak, one of the ten Heralds, walks through a battlefield filled with the dead and reaches a meeting place where only Jezrien, their leader, awaits. Kalak learns that the surviving Heralds have abandoned the Oathpact, leaving Talenel, who died in battle, to bear the burden alone. Kalak and Jezrien relinquish their Honorblades and go their separate ways.

---

## Key Beats
- Kalak traverses a desolate battlefield, observing the aftermath of a fierce battle.
- He arrives at the prearranged meeting place to find only Jezrien there.
- Kalak initially suspects the other Heralds have died but sees their seven Honorblades, realizing they have abandoned the Oathpact.
- Jezrien explains the decision to leave Talenel to uphold the Oathpact alone.
- Both Jezrien and Kalak relinquish their Honorblades, symbolizing the end of their duty.
- They part ways, each resigning to never seek the others or return to their previous roles.
