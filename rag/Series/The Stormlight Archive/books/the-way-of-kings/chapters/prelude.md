## Quick Summary
Kalak, one of the ten Heralds, traverses a ravaged landscape after a brutal battle, heading to a designated meeting point for surviving Heralds. Upon arrival, he finds only Jezrien, another Herald, present. Jezrien explains that the other Heralds have abandoned the Oathpact, leaving only Talenel to uphold it. Kalak and Jezrien agree to lie, claiming victory over the enemy, and depart separately, abandoning their duties and leaving Talenel to suffer alone.

- Kalak surveys a battlefield filled with dead combatants and monstrous foes.

- Despite their fear and guilt, Kalak and Jezrien decide to tell humanity they have won.

- Kalak reflects on their betrayal and feels sorrow for Talenel as he walks away.

## Rowan's "If Asked" Notes

**Q: Who are the Heralds?**
A: The Heralds are ten powerful individuals bound by the Oathpact to protect humanity from Desolations.

**Q: What is the Oathpact?**
A: The Oathpact is a binding agreement among the Heralds to fight against Desolations and protect humanity.

**Q: Why do Jezrien and Kalak leave?**
A: They are overwhelmed by the cycle of endless battles and suffering, choosing to abandon their duties.

**Q: What happens to Talenel?**
A: Talenel is left alone to bear the burden of the Oathpact, continuing to suffer in the waiting realm.

**Q: What are Honorblades?**
A: Honorblades are powerful weapons wielded by the Heralds, symbolizing their duties and powers.

**Q: Why is there a missing sword?**
A: The missing sword represents Talenel, who has not yet returned and continues to uphold the Oathpact alone.

**Q: What is the significance of the rocky spire?**
A: It is the preordained meeting place for the Heralds to regroup after a Desolation.

**Q: How does this prelude relate to the rest of the book?**
A: It sets the stage for understanding the world's history and the burdens faced by those sworn to protect it.
