## Quick Summary
Joshor, the Head of His Majesty's Silent Gatherers, reflects on a unique death quote from a dying Herdazian. The quote forms a ketek—a complex, palindromic form of poetry often associated with deep philosophical or spiritual meaning. Despite the Herdazian's limited grasp of the Alethi language and apparent lack of the education required to compose such a sophisticated verse, the ketek is crafted expertly.

The Silent Gatherers, who are responsible for collecting and interpreting the last words of the dying for King Taravangian, are intrigued by this anomaly. Joshor decides that this particular death quote should be left to Taravangian to decipher, as it might hold a significant, hidden meaning.

The endnote highlights the mysterious nature of the ketek and suggests that even those who seem unlikely to produce profound thoughts can sometimes surprise with their insights. This incident underscores the enigma surrounding the Silent Gatherers' work and Taravangian's unique interest in these last words as potential sources of hidden truths.
