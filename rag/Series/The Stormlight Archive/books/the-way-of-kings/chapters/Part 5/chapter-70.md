## Quick Summary

**Shallan Davar:** Shallan lies in her hospital bed, reflecting on her recent actions and the surprising death of Kabsal. As she revisits the events surrounding the poisoning, inconsistencies disturb her. Questions arise about why the antidote failed Kabsal and why Jasnah wasn't affected by the poisoned bread she also consumed. These recollections lead Shallan to examine her sketchpad. She draws Jasnah observing the strawberry jam and notes Jasnah's lack of revulsion to its smell. Another drawing of Jasnah eating the bread reveals an oddity: the bread appears to be melting. This realization propels Shallan to leave the hospital, clad only in her robe, and make her way through the darkness to the Conclave.

Confronting Jasnah, Shallan declares that she knows Jasnah's Soulcaster is a fake. She presents a picture she drew of Shadesmar, stunning Jasnah, and concludes that Jasnah never possessed a functional Soulcaster. Jasnah dismisses Shallan's claims as delusions born from exhaustion. Determined, Shallan attempts to prove her assertions by returning to Shadesmar. Holding a partially dun garnet sphere, she communicates with the twisted, symbol-headed spren who demand a truth to form a bond. In a moment of vulnerability, Shallan admits to killing her father. This confession plunges her into the sea of beads in Shadesmar, where she starts to drown.

Jasnah intervenes, rescuing Shallan and reprimanding her for entering Shadesmar with insufficient resources, such as only a single dim sphere. Shallan pleads to become a genuine part of Jasnah's work and to receive training in Soulcasting. Though Jasnah initially resists, Shallan's apology and offer to share Jasnah's burdens sways her. Jasnah agrees, extracting a promise from Shallan to never lie or steal again and entrusts her with notes on the Voidbringers.
