## Quick Summary
Shallan reflects on the circumstances surrounding Kabsal's death and her own poisoning, leading to a revelation about Jasnah's Soulcaster. She confronts Jasnah, attempting to prove her theory by venturing into Shadesmar. As Shallan reveals a secret truth about her past, she establishes a bond with the spren and learns more about Soulcasting. Ultimately, Shallan persuades Jasnah to train her in Soulcasting.

---

## Key Beats
- Shallan, from her hospital bed, questions the events surrounding Kabsal's death and Jasnah's lack of poisoning.
- She sketches Jasnah with the poisoned bread and jam, triggering realizations about the Soulcaster.
- Determined to confront Jasnah, Shallan leaves the hospital to find her.
- Shallan accuses Jasnah of using a fake Soulcaster and shows her a drawing of Shadesmar.
- To prove her claim, Shallan attempts to enter Shadesmar and interacts with the spren.
- She admits to killing her father to form a bond with the spren.
- Shallan nearly drowns in the sea of beads in Shadesmar but is saved by Jasnah.
- Jasnah agrees to train Shallan in Soulcasting after Shallan promises not to lie or steal.
