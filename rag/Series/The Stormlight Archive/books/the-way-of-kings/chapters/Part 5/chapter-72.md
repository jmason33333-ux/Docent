## Quick Summary
Shallan Davar engages in an in-depth discussion with Jasnah Kholin, focusing on the nature of Voidbringers and the process of Soulcasting. She begins by questioning Jasnah's proficiency in Soulcasting, particularly how she managed to Soulcast blood multiple times despite her admitted lack of skill with organic materials. Jasnah clarifies that blood, as one of the Ten Essences, is relatively simple to Soulcast, unlike more complex organic materials such as the strawberry jam she struggled with earlier.

The conversation shifts to the topic of Voidbringers, with Shallan expressing skepticism over their existence, given that many scholars consider them mythical. Jasnah, however, argues that legends often have roots in reality, and she seeks to find natural explanations for seemingly supernatural events. She guides Shallan through her research, revealing her hypothesis that the Voidbringers were not destroyed or banished by humans but were instead enslaved as the Parshmen. This revelation challenges Shallan's understanding of Roshar's history and the nature of the Parshmen, setting the stage for further exploration of these themes.
