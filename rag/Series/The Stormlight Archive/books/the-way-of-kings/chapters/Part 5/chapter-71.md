## Quick Summary
Szeth, tasked with a series of assassinations, arrives in Kharbranth to kill King Taravangian. Upon confronting the king, Szeth discovers that Taravangian is his mysterious employer. Taravangian explains the assassinations are necessary for the future of Roshar, showing Szeth a secret hospital where the dying's last words are recorded. He adds Dalinar Kholin to Szeth's list.
