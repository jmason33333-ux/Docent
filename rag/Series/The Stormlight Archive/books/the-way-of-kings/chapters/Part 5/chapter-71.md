## Quick Summary

**Szeth:** The chapter opens with Szeth operating under the guise of a porter, moving through the streets of Kharbranth, heading towards the king’s palace. He reflects on the series of assassinations he has completed, all part of a list given to him by his unseen master. His final target is King Taravangian, and he prepares to fulfill this deadly task.

Upon reaching the palace, Szeth uses his Shardblade to cut through the servants' privy, gaining access to the king's private chambers with ease. In a swift and efficient manner, he subdues the guards present, demonstrating his lethal skills honed through years of being Truthless. Szeth confronts King Taravangian, ready to complete his mission. However, the encounter takes an unexpected turn when Taravangian reveals himself as Szeth's mysterious master.

Taravangian explains that his own name was added to the list as a precaution, in case Szeth failed in his task, to deflect suspicion from himself. The king justifies the orchestrated assassinations as a necessary step to prepare Roshar for future challenges, claiming it is for the greater good. Szeth, grappling with the moral implications of his actions, accuses the king of exploiting him for nefarious purposes.

Taravangian then leads Szeth to a hidden hospital within the palace, where Szeth witnesses people on the verge of death, their blood being siphoned. The king reveals that he records the dying words of these individuals, believing that in their final moments, they see visions that could contain vital knowledge for the salvation of the world. Szeth is shown the extent of the king's machinations, realizing that Taravangian's hands are bloodied too.

The chapter concludes with Taravangian adding a new name to Szeth's list: Dalinar Kholin. Despite his initial reluctance, the king deems it necessary due to recent developments, setting the stage for further turmoil.
