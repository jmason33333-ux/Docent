## Quick Summary
During a highstorm, Dalinar experiences another vision where he is in a white rocky landscape. The Almighty's voice speaks to him, urging him to unite the people against the coming Everstorm. Despite Dalinar's questions about Sadeas's betrayal, the voice cannot hear him. The vision reveals a future of destruction and void, commanding Dalinar to rebuild the Knights Radiant and declaring that God is dead, killed by Odium.
