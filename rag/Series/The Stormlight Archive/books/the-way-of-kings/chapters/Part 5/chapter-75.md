## Quick Summary
Dalinar Kholin experiences another vision during a highstorm, finding himself in a surreal landscape filled with smokey figures. In this vision, a voice, often believed to be that of the Almighty, commands him to "unite them." Dalinar, feeling betrayed by Sadeas, demands to know why the voice misled him. Instead of addressing Dalinar's questions, the voice manifests in human form and warns of the Everstorm, a catastrophic event depicted as a wall of darkness advancing on the city of Kholinar.

Dalinar realizes that the voice cannot hear him and never could. The vision presents the destruction of Kholinar and the land around it, which falls into a void, leaving Dalinar standing alone. The voice insists that someone must lead and protect the people, urging Dalinar to rebuild the Knights Radiant. This unity, the voice suggests, is crucial to facing "The True Desolation." The vision concludes with the voice revealing that it is the Almighty, who claims to have been killed by Odium.

A: The voice is more of a recording or echo, meant to convey messages rather than interact directly with Dalinar. It has never been able to hear him.

**Q: What is the Everstorm?**
A: The Everstorm is depicted as a catastrophic event that threatens the world, symbolized by a wall of darkness in Dalinar's vision.

**Q: Who is the Almighty?**
A: The Almighty is a god-like figure believed to guide the world, revealed in this chapter to have been killed by Odium.

**Q: Why does the vision focus on Kholinar?**
A: Kholinar represents a central point of civilization and power, making its destruction symbolic of broader threats.

**Q: What does "unite them" mean?**
A: This phrase suggests that Dalinar's role is to bring together disparate groups or factions to face an impending threat.

**Q: What is the significance of the Knights Radiant?**
A: The Knights Radiant were an ancient order of protectors, and their rebirth is seen as crucial to defending against coming dangers.

**Q: How does Dalinar feel about Sadeas's betrayal?**
