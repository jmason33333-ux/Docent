## Quick Summary
Shallan Davar completes her study of Jasnah Kholin's notes, which explore the nature of the Voidbringers. After thorough analysis, Shallan reaches the same startling conclusion as Jasnah: the seemingly docile parshmen are the legendary Voidbringers. This realization carries grave implications, prompting a discussion between Shallan and Jasnah about the potential consequences of their discovery.

They decide to travel to the Shattered Plains. Their goal is to delve deeper into the circumstances of King Gavilar's death and understand the true nature of the Parshendi, who may have the ability to transform into warriors. As they plan their journey, the topic of the Ghostbloods arises. Jasnah reveals her suspicion that Kabsal, a man Shallan previously interacted with, was a member of this secretive organization.

Shallan experiences a moment of revelation when she recognizes the symbol of the Ghostbloods, realizing that her father, Lin Davar, was likely involved with the group. This connection to her family adds a personal stake to the mystery surrounding the Ghostbloods and their objectives.
