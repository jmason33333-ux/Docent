## Quick Summary
Kaladin waits at the staging ground as requested by Dalinar, pondering the trustworthiness of Dalinar and the moral implications of his actions. Dalinar proposes that Bridge Four replace his honor guard and the rest of the bridgemen be trained as spearmen, offering Kaladin a captaincy. Upon returning to the barracks, Kaladin's men question him about his abilities, and they agree to experiment with them, swearing secrecy.
