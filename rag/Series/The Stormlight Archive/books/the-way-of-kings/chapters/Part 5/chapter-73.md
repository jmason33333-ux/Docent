## Quick Summary

Kaladin stands at Dalinar's warcamp staging ground, reflecting on the trustworthiness of Dalinar and the moral implications of his recent actions. Syl, his spren companion, believes Dalinar is indeed honorable, while Kaladin remains conflicted about his role in the deaths of three of his bridge crew members compared to the lives they saved.

As the middle moon rises, Dalinar arrives to discuss the future of the bridge crews. Dalinar proposes that Kaladin and Bridge Four replace his honor guard, lost earlier that day, and the rest of the bridgemen be trained as spearmen. Kaladin agrees, stipulating that the men consent and that he be made a captain. As a sign of respect and appreciation, Dalinar gifts Kaladin his cloak from the day's battle.

Returning to the barracks, Kaladin finds Bridge Four enjoying Rock's stew. The men express interest in Kaladin's seemingly extraordinary abilities and ask him to teach them. Though unsure if his abilities can be taught, Kaladin agrees to experiment and requires their silence on the matter. He feels a sense of peace, having successfully protected and saved others.
