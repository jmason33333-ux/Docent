## Quick Summary
Geranid and Ashir, two ardents, are engaged in research and domestic life. Geranid is focused on studying spren, while Ashir experiments with chemistry through cooking. During their experiments, they discover that a flamespren remains fixed in its form once measured, indicating that it can somehow detect when it's being observed.
