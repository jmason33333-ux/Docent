## Quick Summary
Baxil and his cousin Av follow their mistress as she vandalizes artwork in the palace of Ashno of Sages. While she destroys the art, Baxil and Av discuss the Nightwatcher and the potential consequences of seeking a boon from her, including the risk of receiving a curse. Baxil expresses a desire for courage, invoking the Old Magic.
