## Quick Summary
**Szeth:** Szeth-son-son-Vallano, a Truthless assassin from Shinovar, infiltrates a king's banquet in Jah Keved. He swiftly eliminates two guards and bursts into the opulent hall where King Hanavanar is hosting a gathering. Chaos erupts as Szeth begins his deadly mission. King Hanavanar cries out for aid while the guests panic and attempt to flee.

In the ensuing mayhem, Szeth methodically slaughters many attendees. His primary target is the king, and as he approaches, he faces resistance from two Shardbearers. Additionally, a squad armed with half-shards, a form of lesser Shardplate, confronts him. Szeth drops his Shardblade, utilizing his Windrunner abilities to levitate and maneuver around the room. He skillfully uses Stormlight to enhance his physical capabilities, enabling him to dispatch the attackers with ease.

During the battle, Szeth infuses a stone with Stormlight multiple times, lashing it toward one of the Shardbearers, crushing him under its weight. The remaining honor guard is quickly overwhelmed by his prowess. The chapter culminates with Szeth pinning King Hanavanar to the floor and executing him by driving his Shardblade through the king's face, completing his grim task.

## Rowan's "If Asked" Notes

**Q: How does Szeth manage to use Windrunner abilities?**
A: Szeth is able to use Windrunner abilities because he possesses an Honorblade, a powerful artifact that grants him these powers despite not being a Knight Radiant.

**Q: What are half-shards, and how are they different from Shardplate?**
A: Half-shards are a new military technology that provides some protective benefits similar to Shardplate but are less powerful. They are used by the honor guard in this chapter.

**Q: Why does Szeth kill King Hanavanar?**
A: Szeth is following orders from those who control him as a Truthless, a role that compels him to obey commands without question.

**Q: What is the significance of the Shardblade?**
A: A Shardblade is a mystical weapon capable of cutting through any material and even severing the soul from the body, making it incredibly deadly.

**Q: Why are the Shardbearers unable to stop Szeth?**
A: Szeth's mastery of Windrunner abilities and strategic use of Stormlight make him a formidable opponent, allowing him to overcome even Shardbearers.

**Q: Are Szeth's actions purely mercenary, or is there more to his character?**
A: Szeth's actions are driven by his role as a Truthless, which forces him to obey orders, but he internally struggles with the morality of his actions.

**Q: How does Szeth's use of Stormlight work?**
A: Szeth uses Stormlight to infuse objects and himself, manipulating gravity to perform feats like Lashing, which enables flight and enhanced strength.

**Q: What is the emotional tone of this interlude?**
A: The tone is tense and chaotic, underscored by Szeth's efficient but morally conflicted execution of his task.
