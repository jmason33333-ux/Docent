## Quick Summary
Baxil and his cousin Av accompany their enigmatic employer, known only as "the mistress," through the palace of Ashno of Sages. The mistress is on a mission to destroy art, and Baxil is tasked with aiding her by carrying a bag of tools she uses to deface paintings and sculptures. As they walk, Baxil is torn between his loyalty to the mistress and his discomfort with their task. While the mistress works, Baxil and Av discuss the Nightwatcher and the Old Magic, which can grant boons but also inflict curses. Baxil expresses a belief that he can cleverly word his request to avoid a curse, revealing his desire for courage. Av, more skeptical, insists that a curse is inevitable, no matter how the boon is phrased.
