## Quick Summary
Szeth assassinates two guards and disrupts a royal banquet in Jah Keved, creating chaos as he kills many attendees. King Hanavanar calls for help, and Szeth faces opposition from Shardbearers and guards with half-shards. Using his Windrunner abilities, Szeth defeats his opponents and subdues the king, threatening him with a Shardblade.

---

## Key Beats
- Szeth kills two guards before crashing into King Hanavanar's banquet.
- King Hanavanar shouts for assistance amidst the chaos.
- Szeth targets the fleeing guests and approaches the king.
- Two Shardbearers and a squad with half-shards attempt to defend the king.
- Szeth drops his sword, relying on his Windrunner powers to defeat his opponents.
- He lashes a stone multiple times, using it to kill one Shardbearer.
- Szeth easily dispatches the remaining honor guard.
- He pins King Hanavanar to the floor, placing a Shardblade at his throat.

## Rowan's "If Asked" Notes

**Q: What are Windrunner abilities?**
A: Windrunner abilities allow manipulation of gravity and adhesion, enabling feats like flying and enhanced combat maneuvering.

**Q: What is a Shardbearer?**
A: A Shardbearer is someone who wields a Shardblade and/or wears Shardplate, powerful magical artifacts.

**Q: Why does Szeth drop his sword?**
A: Szeth drops his sword to better utilize his Windrunner abilities, which are more effective against multiple opponents in close quarters.
