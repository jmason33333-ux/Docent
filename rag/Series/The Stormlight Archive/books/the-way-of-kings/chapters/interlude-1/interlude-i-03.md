## Quick Summary
Szeth, now under the ownership of a man named Took, is used as a tool to entertain and impress mine workers in small towns. After Took is murdered by thugs, Szeth's Oathstone is picked up by one of the attackers, obligating Szeth to serve them.

---

## Key Beats
- Szeth is serving Took, who exploits him to gain attention and free drinks by having Szeth perform various acts of obedience.
- Took orders Szeth to harm himself, revealing Szeth's inability to disobey without exception except for commands to kill himself.
- Szeth reflects on how his refined demeanor and speech often unsettle his masters.
- As the night progresses, Took realizes the crowd is losing interest and decides to leave with Szeth.
- On their way out, Took is ambushed and killed by thugs.
- A thug picks up Szeth's Oathstone, and Szeth explains his obligation to serve the stone's holder.
- Szeth internally notes that he cannot be ordered to give up his Shardblade, though he does not disclose this to the thugs.
