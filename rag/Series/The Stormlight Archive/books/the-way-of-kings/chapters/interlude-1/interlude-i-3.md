## Quick Summary
Szeth, currently serving a man named Took, is used for his obedient nature to entertain and gain favor in small towns. After Took orders Szeth to perform various acts, including harming himself, their relationship abruptly ends when Took is killed by thugs. These thugs, upon discovering Szeth's Oathstone, realize they can command him.

---

## Key Beats
- Szeth obediently follows Took's commands to entertain townsfolk.
- Took orders Szeth to harm himself, showcasing his control over Szeth.
- Szeth reflects on his refined speech and mannerisms, which unsettle his masters.
- Took is killed by thugs as they leave the town.
- A thug picks up Szeth's Oathstone, realizing he now controls Szeth.
- Szeth informs the thug of his obligation and limitations in obedience.

## Rowan's "If Asked" Notes

**Q: Why does Szeth obey the thugs?**  
A: Szeth is bound to serve whoever holds his Oathstone, a cultural and magical obligation.

**Q: What are Szeth’s limits in following orders?**  
A: Szeth cannot be ordered to kill himself or give up his Shardblade, though he doesn't reveal the latter to the thugs.

**Q: Why do people find Szeth’s speech unsettling?**  
A: His refined speech and manners contrast sharply with his role as a servant, causing discomfort among less refined individuals.
