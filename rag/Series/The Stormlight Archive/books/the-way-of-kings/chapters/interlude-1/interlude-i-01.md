## Quick Summary
Ishikk, a fisherman in the Purelake, returns home after a day of fishing. He interacts with Maib, who attempts to keep him in her debt by providing him with food. Later, Ishikk meets with a group of foreigners seeking information about a man named Hoid. Despite traveling to many villages, Ishikk reports that no one knows about Hoid's whereabouts.
