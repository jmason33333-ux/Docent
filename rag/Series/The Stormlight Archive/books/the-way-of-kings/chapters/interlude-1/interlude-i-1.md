## Quick Summary
Ishikk, a fisherman in the Purelake, returns home after a day of fishing. He interacts briefly with Thaspic and Maib, a local woman trying to win his affection through favors. Ishikk then meets with a group of foreigners seeking information about a man named Hoid. Ishikk reports no new findings, and the foreigners debate before leaving him.

## Key Beats
- Ishikk returns home from fishing in the Purelake.
- He speaks with Thaspic, a local acquaintance.

- Ishikk meets with a group of foreigners who seek information about Hoid.
