## Quick Summary

**Dalinar:** Dalinar Kholin and Navani Kholin continue their secret relationship, discussing their past and Dalinar's feelings. Dalinar reveals to Navani that although he was interested in her initially, he stepped aside when his brother, Gavilar, showed interest. This was typical of the brothers' relationship dynamics. Navani had always believed Dalinar hated her due to his cold demeanor, but she learns it was a facade to hide his true feelings. During their conversation, Dalinar reflects on his current personality, striving to maintain control over his once tyrannical tendencies. Their meeting is cut short by the sound of alarms, signaling a chasmfiend sighting at the Tower plateau—a strategic location where Dalinar plans to conduct a dual assault with Sadeas.

**Kaladin:** Kaladin prepares for the assault as Bridge Four lines up. The team is now equipped with carapace armor, crafted and decorated by Leyten, and each member wields a shield made from Parshendi bones. These shields have been adorned to create a rattling sound, symbolizing unity and intimidation. Bridge Four's transformation impresses the other bridgemen, who look on with admiration, and they cheer as Bridge Four lifts their bridge onto their shoulders, ready for battle.

**Adolin:** Adolin Kholin processes his newfound trust in his father, Dalinar, after realizing he had misjudged him. This revelation strengthens their bond, and Adolin is relieved to fully trust his father's leadership. At the staging area, Dalinar, Adolin, and Sadeas strategize, deciding to amass fifteen thousand troops to potentially crush the Parshendi army in a decisive move.
