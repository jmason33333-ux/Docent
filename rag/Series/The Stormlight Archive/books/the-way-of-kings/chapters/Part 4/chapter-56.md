## Quick Summary
**Dalinar Kholin:**

Dalinar and Torol Sadeas continue their joint military campaign against the Parshendi on the Shattered Plains. They execute another successful assault on a plateau, but to their surprise, a second Parshendi army emerges, adapting to the Alethi's new strategy of combined forces. In the chaos, Dalinar formulates a new tactic to counter this unexpected development. However, he notices that Sadeas is caught in the thick of the battle, surrounded by the Parshendi.

Without hesitation, Dalinar mounts his Ryshadium, Gallant, and charges across a chasm, cutting through Parshendi forces to reach Sadeas. As he arrives, he finds his old friend overpowered and at risk of being overwhelmed. Demonstrating his combat prowess, Dalinar single-handedly slays the Parshendi attacking Sadeas, defending him until Adolin and his guard manage to break through and join the fight to relieve the Highprinces.

After securing the area, Sadeas questions why Dalinar took such a significant risk to save him. Dalinar cites the principles from "The Way of Kings," emphasizing the importance of honor and responsibility. Sadeas, skeptical of these ideals, warns Dalinar that adhering too closely to such principles might lead to his downfall, dismissing the book's influence with the remark, "That storming book."
