## Quick Summary
Dalinar decides against abdicating his position to Adolin and spends time with his family as they await a highstorm. During the storm, Dalinar experiences a vision where he converses with Nohadon, a historical figure, about leadership and responsibility. Upon returning to reality, Navani discovers that Dalinar's vision might provide proof that they are real through a phrase in the Dawnchant.

---

## Key Beats
- Dalinar decides not to abdicate his position to Adolin.
- Navani demonstrates a new fabrial, a painrial, to Adolin.
- Dalinar enters a vision during the highstorm, meeting Nohadon.
- Nohadon discusses the aftermath of a Desolation and contemplates leadership.
- Dalinar suggests writing a book, but Nohadon dismisses the idea.
- Returning from the vision, Navani realizes Dalinar spoke in the Dawnchant.
- Navani believes this discovery could prove the reality of Dalinar's visions.
