## Quick Summary
Kaladin and Bridge Four return from a bridge run where Maps succumbs to a fatal arrow wound. The loss weighs heavily on Kaladin, who insists on tending to Teft's injured arm despite his own grief. Teft, probing for any signs of unusual activity from Kaladin, suddenly attempts to hit him. Kaladin instinctively draws Stormlight from nearby spheres to defend himself, revealing his ability to absorb Stormlight. Teft notices all the spheres are now dun and points it out, causing Kaladin to panic and flee in confusion about his powers.

While reflecting alone, Kaladin encounters Hoid, who is playing a flute. Hoid shares a tale about Derethil and the Wandersail, a story with a moral about taking responsibility. This inspires Kaladin to embrace his powers to protect Bridge Four. He returns to his friends and questions Teft about his knowledge of Kaladin's abilities. Teft explains that his family belonged to a sect anticipating the Knights Radiant's return. Kaladin resolves to explore his powers and their implications further.
