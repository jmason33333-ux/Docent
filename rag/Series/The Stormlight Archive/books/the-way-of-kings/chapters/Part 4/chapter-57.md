## Quick Summary
Kaladin faces the death of Maps during a bridge run and begins to realize the extent of his mysterious powers when Teft provokes him into drawing Stormlight. Overwhelmed by the implications, Kaladin seeks solitude and encounters Hoid, who imparts a lesson on responsibility through a story about Derethil and the Wandersail. Determined to protect Bridge Four, Kaladin resolves to explore his powers further.
