## Quick Summary
Kaladin orders Leyten to craft carapace armor for all members of Bridge Four, excluding Shen, the Parshendi bridgeman. This decision underscores the growing unity and identity within Bridge Four while simultaneously highlighting the tension surrounding Shen's integration. Kaladin observes the bridgemen's spear practice, noting Moash's exceptional skill. Curious about Moash's motivation, Kaladin inquires about his purpose. Moash reveals a desire for vengeance but withholds the target of his vendetta.

While discussing escape plans with Rock, Kaladin grapples with the responsibility of leadership and the pressure it brings. Teft approaches Kaladin, urging him to teach the other bridgemen. Kaladin declines, fearing that his impatience and eagerness could lead to failure, as he believes it did in the past. This internal conflict reveals Kaladin's struggle with self-doubt and his fear of repeating past mistakes.
