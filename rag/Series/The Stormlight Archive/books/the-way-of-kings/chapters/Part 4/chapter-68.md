## Quick Summary

**Adolin:** Adolin leads the remaining Kholin forces in a daring charge through the Parshendi lines towards the bridge at the western side of the plateau. Despite the maneuver's recklessness, his well-trained troops manage the dangerous sprint for freedom, showcasing Adolin's leadership and the soldiers' discipline.

**Teft:** Teft and the other members of Bridge Four watch in awe as Kaladin engages the Parshendi with inhuman speed and precision, enhanced by the glow of Stormlight. Teft quickly organizes the crew into a defensive formation to support Kaladin and secure the bridge's strategic position on the plateau.

**Dalinar:** Dalinar fights valiantly, making his way to the bridgemen he believes have perished. To his surprise, he finds them holding their ground, which inspires him. As he tries to maintain a retreat corridor for his men, he faces a formidable Parshendi Shardbearer. Despite his Shardplate being damaged, Dalinar uses his tactical acumen to topple the Shardbearer, but he is unable to capitalize on the advantage due to his own injuries.

**Kaladin:** Kaladin and Bridge Four hold their position for what feels like hours, earning respect for the Parshendi's battlefield ethics. He appreciates the moral code he observes, which contrasts with the Alethi warcamps. After the Kholin army secures the bridge, Kaladin learns of the deaths of some of his men and begins organizing a retreat. He promotes a young lighteyed soldier to help with the evacuation and urges Adolin to cross the bridge, promising to support his father Dalinar during the retreat.

**Dalinar:** Dalinar, struck multiple times, loses his helm, leaving him dazed. The Parshendi Shardbearer confronting him does not strike the killing blow but instead speaks cryptically. A disturbance among the Parshendi reveals Kaladin approaching with a glowing spear, which surprises and frightens them.

**Kaladin:** Kaladin exploits a weakness in the Shardbearer's armor, using Stormlight-enhanced strength to strike. The Parshendi chant "Neshua Kadal" and retreat in fear. Kaladin finds Gallant, Dalinar's horse, and helps Dalinar escape. Dalinar then acknowledges Kaladin's honor and courage, offering him a place in his army to protect him from Sadeas.
