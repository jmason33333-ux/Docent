## Quick Summary
Adolin leads a daring charge through Parshendi lines to secure a retreat path. Teft and Bridge Four support Kaladin, who fights with Stormlight-enhanced abilities. Dalinar battles a Parshendi Shardbearer but ultimately retreats with Kaladin's help. Kaladin gains the respect of the Parshendi, who fearfully recognize him as "Neshua Kadal."
