## Quick Summary
Navani confronts Sadeas about Dalinar's fate, defying his account with a prayer for justice. Dalinar returns, confronting Sadeas, who admits to his betrayal. Dalinar trades his Shardblade for the freedom of Sadeas's bridgemen. He confronts Elhokar, uncovers his role in recent sabotage, and resolves to take charge as Highprince of War.

---

## Key Beats
- Navani hears rumors of Dalinar's defeat and confronts Sadeas, who claims Dalinar is dead.
- Navani prays for justice, leaving a scorch mark with a large glyph as news of Dalinar's survival arrives.
- Dalinar returns with his forces, determined to hold Sadeas accountable.
- Dalinar and Sadeas have a private confrontation where Sadeas admits he wanted Dalinar gone.
- Dalinar offers his Shardblade to Sadeas in exchange for the freedom of the bridgemen.
- Dalinar confronts Elhokar about sabotaging his own saddle, leading to Sadeas's betrayal.
- Dalinar decides to embrace his warlord persona and plans to become Highprince of War.

## Rowan's "If Asked" Notes

**Q: Why did Dalinar give up his Shardblade?**  
A: Dalinar values the lives of his men and his honor more than the Shardblade, believing it a fair trade for their freedom.

**Q: What does Navani's glyph signify?**  
A: It represents a prayer for justice, a significant act in response to Sadeas's betrayal.

**Q: Why did Elhokar sabotage his own saddle?**  
A: Elhokar sought to manipulate perceptions and ensure his importance, revealing his insecurity and poor leadership judgment.
