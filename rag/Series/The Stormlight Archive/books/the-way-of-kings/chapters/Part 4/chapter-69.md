## Quick Summary

**Navani:**
Navani Kholin, distressed by rumors about Dalinar’s fate, forces her way into Sadeas's staging area. She confronts Sadeas, who claims that Dalinar was overwhelmed by Parshendi forces and has fallen in battle. Deeply skeptical, Navani performs a ritual of drawing a Thath glyph in burn ink, praying for justice. Her ritual is dramatically interrupted with news of Dalinar's survival.

**Dalinar:**
Dalinar, leading his remaining forces, is consumed with anger over Sadeas’s betrayal but prioritizes Alethkar’s protection over personal revenge. As he approaches his warcamp, he releases his wounded soldiers but allows Kaladin and the bridgemen to accompany him into Sadeas's camp. Upon arrival, he is reunited with Renarin and Navani, sharing a heartfelt moment. He then confronts Sadeas, who privately admits his intentions to remove Dalinar due to perceived insanity and weakness. Dalinar recognizes that honor will not secure Sadeas’s loyalty.

**Kaladin:**
Kaladin observes the tense exchange between Dalinar and Sadeas. When Sadeas refuses to release the bridgemen, even for an exorbitant price, Dalinar shocks everyone by offering his Shardblade in exchange for their freedom. Sadeas accepts, mocking Dalinar’s decision. Kaladin, astounded by this act, learns from Dalinar that the lives he saved are worth more than a Shardblade, and Dalinar’s actions stem from honor.

**Dalinar (continued):**
Dalinar confronts Elhokar, engaging in a staged brawl to demonstrate his power and resolve. He demands to know if Elhokar sabotaged his own saddle, leading to Sadeas’s betrayal. Elhokar admits to the deception. Dalinar explains the repercussions and asserts that he intends to become Highprince of War, seeking to unify Alethkar. He declares he will no longer be held back by the ideals of Nohadon and will embrace his formidable past as the Blackthorn. Before departing, he informs Elhokar of his relationship with Navani.

## Rowan's "If Asked" Notes

**Q: Why did Dalinar offer his Shardblade for the bridgemen?**
A: Dalinar believes the lives saved by Kaladin and his men are worth more than a weapon, emphasizing his commitment to honor and valuing human life over material possessions.

**Q: What was Navani's ritual with the glyph?**
A: Navani performed a ritual by drawing a Thath glyph in burn ink, symbolizing a prayer for justice following the news of Dalinar's supposed demise.

**Q: Why did Sadeas betray Dalinar?**
A: Sadeas feared Dalinar’s influence on Elhokar, perceiving him as insane and weak, and wanted to remove him as a threat to Elhokar’s rule.

**Q: How did Dalinar confront Elhokar about the saddle incident?**
A: Dalinar engaged Elhokar in a mock brawl to demonstrate his strength and demanded the truth, leading to Elhokar admitting to cutting the saddle girth.

**Q: What is the significance of Dalinar becoming Highprince of War?**
A: It represents Dalinar’s commitment to unifying Alethkar and taking a more direct role in the political and military affairs of the kingdom.

**Q: What does Dalinar’s confrontation with Sadeas reveal about their relationship?**
A: It highlights the deep mistrust and conflicting ideologies between them, with Sadeas prioritizing political expediency over loyalty.

**Q: How do Kaladin and Bridge Four react to being freed?**
A: They are initially in disbelief and overwhelmed by the unexpected act of honor and sacrifice by Dalinar.

**Q: What are the implications of Dalinar’s relationship with Navani?**
A: It underscores a personal shift for Dalinar, embracing personal desires and relationships despite potential political complications.
