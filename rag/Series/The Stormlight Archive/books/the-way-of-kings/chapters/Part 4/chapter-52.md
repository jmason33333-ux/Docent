## Quick Summary

**Adolin Kholin:**

The chapter opens with a conversation between Adolin and Dalinar Kholin. Dalinar informs Adolin of his decision to abdicate, a move that would make Adolin the Highprince. Adolin is taken aback and expresses his uncertainty and reluctance about assuming such a critical leadership role. Their discussion is interrupted by the approach of a highstorm, and the brothers prepare Dalinar for his vision by tying him to a post.

**Dalinar Kholin:**

In his vision, Dalinar finds himself atop Feverstone Keep, playing the role of a guard. He surveys the landscape and is the first to spot an approaching army. As they draw nearer, Dalinar identifies them as Radiants, specifically from the orders of the Stoneward and Windrunners. Sensing something amiss, Dalinar rushes outside the keep's gates. As he approaches, he witnesses a shocking sight: the Radiants begin abandoning their Shardblades and Shardplate, leaving them behind as they silently walk away. Dalinar is overwhelmed with a profound sense of loss and tries to understand the reason behind their actions. One Radiant, in a voice Dalinar recognizes from previous visions, tells him that "They were the first, and they were also the last," revealing to Dalinar that he has witnessed the Day of Recreance.

Returning to consciousness, Dalinar resumes his conversation with Adolin. Adolin expresses a wish for Dalinar to dismiss the visions as untrue and continue leading the princedom. Dalinar, however, insists that he must follow his conscience and trust the visions to avoid leading his house to ruin. Renarin suggests a method to verify the truth of the visions, proposing they compare the details with known history. Dalinar agrees and asks Navani to record the vision, with instructions to communicate with Jasnah Kholin to investigate further.
