## Quick Summary
Dalinar discusses his decision to abdicate with Adolin, who is unprepared for leadership. As a highstorm approaches, Dalinar enters a vision, witnessing the Day of Recreance where Radiants abandon their Shardblades and Shardplates. Upon returning, Dalinar insists on trusting his visions despite Adolin's skepticism, leading to Renarin suggesting a method to verify the visions' truth.

## Key Beats
