## Quick Summary
Adolin Kholin is at a wineshop with his date and friends, discussing Dalinar's leadership and the potential threat from Sadeas. As Adolin prepares for a duel, he reflects on the state of the Alethi armies and his father's reforms. Meanwhile, Dalinar, watching the duel, discusses strategy with Elhokar and Sadeas, who mocks Dalinar's ideals. Adolin wins his duel, and Elhokar agrees to a joint military experiment proposed by Dalinar.
