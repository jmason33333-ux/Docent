## Quick Summary
Dalinar Kholin is grappling with the implications of his most recent vision, which confirmed the reality of the visions he has been experiencing. As the chapter opens, Dalinar is sitting with Navani Kholin and his son Renarin. Adolin has gone to assess the damage caused by the recent highstorm, visibly shaken by the confirmation that the visions are indeed real. Navani questions Dalinar about why he believes the man in his vision was Nohadon. Dalinar admits he has no definitive proof, but he is convinced of its truth.

Navani shares what she knows about the Desolations, though she emphasizes that her daughter Jasnah is better versed in historical matters. They deliberate on various topics, briefly considering the Old Magic before dismissing it as irrelevant to their current concerns. Despite his certainty about the visions, Dalinar is unsettled and requests solitude. Renarin quickly obliges, but Navani stays behind.

Navani then reveals her true motivations for returning to the Shattered Plains. She confesses that she felt sidelined and wanted to be involved in the events unfolding around them. As they talk, they share a moment of intimacy, leading to a passionate kiss. Following this, Navani discloses a significant concern: something is amiss on Roshar, something more significant than the ongoing War of Reckoning. She shares intelligence about the assassination of the King of Jah Keved, carried out by a Shin Shardbearer dressed in white.
