## Quick Summary

In this chapter, Kaladin faces the harsh reality of leadership during a bridge run on the Shattered Plains. Dunny, one of the young members of Bridge Four, is tragically trampled during the chaos. Kaladin is deeply affected by the loss, feeling a personal failure for not being able to save him. This event strengthens his resolve to protect his men at all costs.

In response to Dunny's death, Kaladin decides to take action by helping wounded bridgemen from other crews, despite the potential risk and resource drain this poses. Initially, the men of Bridge Four are hesitant, fearing the consequences and questioning the logic of expending their limited resources on others. Kaladin delivers a compelling lecture on honor, contrasting their actions with the behavior of hypocritical lighteyes, and challenges them to rise above the expectations set by their oppressors.

Kaladin's speech moves the bridgemen, who join him in aiding the wounded. Their efforts result in the survival of four injured bridgemen who might otherwise have perished. During the operation, Teft advises Kaladin to always keep his pouch of infused spheres with him, alluding to the good fortune Bridge Four has had, noting the absence of casualties in recent runs. This suggestion points to the mysterious luck surrounding their bridge crew.
