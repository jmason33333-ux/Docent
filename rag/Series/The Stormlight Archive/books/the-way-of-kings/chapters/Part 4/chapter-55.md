## Quick Summary
Kaladin observes a successful joint plateau assault by Sadeas and Dalinar. Following the battle, Bridge Four is tasked with a chasm duty where they discover a dead lighteyed officer with valuable spheres. Kaladin devises a plan to retrieve the spheres without surrendering them to the army, enlisting Rock's unexpected archery skills to execute the plan.

## Key Beats
- Kaladin witnesses the first joint assault led by Sadeas and Dalinar.
- Bridge Four is sent into the chasms shortly after returning from the run.
- They find a dead lighteyed officer with an emerald broam and lesser spheres.
- Kaladin plans to retrieve the spheres without alerting authorities.
- Rock admits to archery skills and successfully executes Kaladin's plan by shooting an arrow with the pouch attached to the underside of a bridge.
