## Quick Summary
In this chapter, Kaladin leads Bridge Four during the first joint plateau assault between Highprinces Sadeas and Dalinar. The assault is executed efficiently, showcasing the combined might of both forces. Bridge Four performs their duties with precision, earning brief respite upon returning to camp. However, Kaladin and his men are immediately reassigned to a chasm duty, much to their dismay.

While scavenging in the chasms, Kaladin discovers the body of a dead lighteyed officer. Among the officer's belongings is an emerald broam, a highly valuable gem, and a pouch of lesser spheres. Recognizing the potential of this find, Kaladin devises a plan to smuggle the pouch from the chasms without surrendering it to their superiors, who would undoubtedly confiscate their find.

Kaladin's plan involves tying the pouch to an arrow and shooting it onto the underside of a permanent bridge, a feat requiring exceptional skill. Despite his reluctance, Rock admits he possesses this skill and successfully executes the shot, ensuring the spheres' retrieval at a later time. Rock insists, however, that he will not use a bow in battle, maintaining his personal code even as he aids the team.
