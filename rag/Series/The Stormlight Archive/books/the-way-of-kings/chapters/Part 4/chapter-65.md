## Quick Summary
Dalinar and Sadeas observe a Parshendi army while preparing for battle. Sadeas suggests a joint attack, but Dalinar is concerned about the bridgemen's safety. Kaladin leads a decoy team to protect the bridge crews. As the battle progresses, Dalinar experiences doubts and notices that Sadeas's forces have withdrawn, leaving his army surrounded.

---

## Key Beats
- Dalinar and Sadeas strategize against the Parshendi, who number around ten thousand.
- Dalinar is concerned about the high casualty rate among bridgemen and adheres to the Codes.
- Kaladin and his team act as decoys to protect bridge crews from Parshendi arrows.
- Once the bridges are in place, Dalinar leads his army into battle alongside Adolin.
- Dalinar feels the Thrill during combat but loses it when facing a young Parshendi.
- Dalinar reflects on the Codes, Gavilar's death, and his visions, questioning his path.
- Adolin alerts Dalinar to another group of Parshendi; they realize Sadeas has retreated.
- Dalinar's army is left surrounded and cut off from retreat.
