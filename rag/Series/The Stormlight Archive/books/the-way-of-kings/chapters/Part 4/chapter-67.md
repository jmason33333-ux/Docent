## Quick Summary
Kaladin and Bridge Four, lagging behind Sadeas's army, choose to rest and plan an escape. However, Kaladin is torn by the plight of Dalinar's army and Syl's revelation of her true nature as an honorspren. Despite the risk, Kaladin leads Bridge Four back to aid Dalinar. Meanwhile, Dalinar's forces are in dire straits, but the sight of Bridge Four offers a glimmer of hope. Kaladin utilizes a powerful surge of Stormlight to protect his men and, realizing the Words of the Radiants, leads a fierce counterattack.

- Kaladin and Bridge Four decide to rest instead of crossing with the army, planning their escape.

- Empowered by the Words, Kaladin leads a powerful charge against the Parshendi.

**Q: Why does Kaladin decide to help Dalinar's army despite the risk?**
A: Kaladin is influenced by Syl's revelation and his father's teachings about the importance of protecting life, leading him to act selflessly.

**Q: What are "the Words" that Kaladin speaks?**
A: The Words are an oath of the Knights Radiant, expressing a commitment to protect others, which empowers Kaladin.

**Q: How does Syl's revelation affect Kaladin?**
A: Syl's revelation as an honorspren reinforces Kaladin's sense of duty and his connection to the ideals of the Radiants.
