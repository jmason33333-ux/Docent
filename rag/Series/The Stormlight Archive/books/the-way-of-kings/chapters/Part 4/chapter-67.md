## Quick Summary

**Kaladin:**
As Sadeas's forces withdraw from the battlefield, Bridge Four lags behind due to their wounded state and reduced numbers. Kaladin decides to rest his men and catch up later, using this as an opportunity to plan their escape. However, his conscience is troubled by the thought of leaving behind the wounded soldiers and the thousands of darkeyed soldiers left to die. Syl, appearing as a regular-sized woman, reveals that she is an honorspren, embodying oaths, promises, and nobility. Despite his resentment towards lighteyes from past betrayals, particularly by Amaram, Kaladin recalls his father's teachings about valuing life. Inspired by these ideals, he convinces Bridge Four to return and assist Kholin's army, leading them back to the Tower.

**Dalinar:**
Dalinar's Shardplate is weakening due to numerous cracks, but he persists in leading his army alongside Adolin. Noticing a lone bridge crew moving towards the chasm, Dalinar and Adolin suspect a trap but grasp onto the hope that the bridge crew might be allies. They rally their men to push towards the bridge.

**Kaladin:**
As Bridge Four approaches, the Parshendi prepare to attack with arrows. Kaladin orders a side carry move and uses an extraordinary amount of Stormlight to attract the arrows to his shield, saving his men. This display of power causes the Parshendi to retreat, exclaiming "Neshua Kadal!" Drained of energy, Kaladin feels he has failed again, reminiscent of his brother Tien's death.

**Kaladin (Flashback):**
In a flashback, Kaladin recalls the chaos of battle where his squad is dead, and he is reassigned. Desperate to protect his brother, Tien, who is a messenger boy placed at the front, Kaladin is too late to save him.

**Kaladin (Back in the present):**
Prompted by Syl, Kaladin stands and seizes a spear. He notices Parshendi with infused gems and draws in Stormlight, rejuvenating himself. A voice urges him to speak the Words, and he instinctively knows them: "I will protect those who cannot protect themselves."

**Teft:**
As Kaladin lights up with energy, Teft and the other bridgemen watch in awe as Kaladin fights with newfound vigor and resolve.
