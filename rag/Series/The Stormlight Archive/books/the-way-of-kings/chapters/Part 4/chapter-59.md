## Quick Summary
Kaladin and his men face increased danger as Brightness Hashal assigns Bridge Four to relentless bridge and chasm duties. Amidst this, Kaladin experiences a breakthrough in his Surgebinding abilities, accidentally inhaling Stormlight and using it to perform extraordinary feats, including creating a stone ladder and surviving a fall.

## Key Beats
- Kaladin struggles and fails to draw Stormlight from spheres while in the barracks.
- Teft discusses the First Oath of the Knights Radiant, referencing "The Way of Kings."
- Brightness Hashal assigns Bridge Four to full-time bridge and chasm duty, which Kaladin interprets as a death sentence.
- In the chasms, Kaladin and his team practice spear fighting and examine Parshendi corpses.
- Kaladin successfully uses Stormlight to create a makeshift stone ladder and survives a significant fall.
- Syl encourages Kaladin to trust his abilities, leading to a moment of self-discovery.
