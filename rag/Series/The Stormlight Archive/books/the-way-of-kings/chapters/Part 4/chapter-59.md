## Quick Summary
Kaladin is in the Bridge Four barracks with Teft, while Rock runs drills outside and Lopen stands watch. Kaladin attempts to draw Stormlight from an infused sphere but fails, raising doubts about his potential as a Surgebinder and Knight Radiant, despite Teft's insistence. Teft advises Kaladin on the First Oath and references the book "The Way of Kings," linking it to the Ideals of the Radiants. Brightness Hashal appears, informing them that Bridge Four will be on full-time bridge duty by day and chasm duty by night, a schedule Kaladin recognizes as a death sentence for his men.

After Hashal's departure, Kaladin and his team discuss their dire situation. During this conversation, Kaladin accidentally inhales Stormlight and begins to glow faintly, confirming his abilities. He leads his crew into the chasms for their shift, where they practice spear techniques. While Teft directs the drills, Kaladin examines Parshendi corpses with Syl, cutting off carapace and gathering bones with Lopen's help.

Kaladin uses Stormlight to create a stone ladder, allowing him to climb up the chasm wall and attach a sack of Parshendi remains to the underside of a bridge for later retrieval. Despite his fear, Syl encourages him to cut the rope he is hanging from, assuring him he will survive the fall. Kaladin lands safely, using the remaining Stormlight to heal himself, illustrating his growing mastery. He instructs Lopen to retrieve the sack on their next bridge run.
