## Quick Summary

**Kaladin:** The chapter opens with Kaladin and Bridge Four preparing for another bridgerun. Kaladin is anxious because he plans to use a new tactic: distracting the Parshendi archers by wearing Parshendi armor and using a shield he crafted himself. His aim is to draw their fire away from the bridge crews, improving their chances of survival. As they head to the battlefield, an impatient soldier tries to take water from Bridge Four’s water skin, unaware that it conceals Kaladin's armor. The bridge crew manages to defuse the situation without revealing the hidden armor.

During the bridgerun, Kaladin's plan works — dressed in Parshendi armor, he manages to attract the attention of the Parshendi archers, drawing their fire. He uses Stormlight to enhance his speed and reflexes, protecting himself and the crew by blocking arrows with his shield. Afterwards, Torol Sadeas arrives and, impressed by the tactic, promotes Matal, the leader of the bridge crews, to a higher position, believing it was his idea. However, the use of so much Stormlight leaves Kaladin in shock, though he quickly recovers with the help of his crew.

As usual, Bridge Four continues their routine of searching for and tending to the wounded from other crews. Suddenly, Lopen spots a group of Parshendi archers returning and aiming at them. As the crew tries to retreat, they are surprised and relieved when Dalinar Kholin charges into the Parshendi forces, scattering them. After the skirmish, Dalinar raises his Shardblade in salute to Bridge Four, acknowledging their efforts and bravery.
