## Quick Summary
Kaladin and Bridge Four participate in another bridgerun, during which Kaladin attempts a risky strategy to distract Parshendi archers using self-made Parshendi armor and Stormlight. Their plan succeeds, drawing the attention of Sadeas, who promotes Matal for the success. Afterward, Bridge Four rescues wounded soldiers, but they are surprised by a Parshendi counterattack. Dalinar Kholin dramatically intervenes, saving them and acknowledging their efforts with a salute.

- Kaladin prepares to distract the Parshendi with his makeshift Parshendi armor.

- Sadeas promotes Matal for the strategy's success, mistakenly attributing the idea to him.

- Dalinar Kholin heroically intervenes, defeating the Parshendi and saluting Bridge Four.
