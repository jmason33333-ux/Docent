## Quick Summary

**Kaladin:**
Kaladin is tending to Skar, assuring him that he will recover and walk normally once healed. While in the medical tent, Kaladin observes Sadeas's army retreating, despite the battle progressing well. This raises alarm, and Kaladin quickly deduces that Sadeas is betraying Dalinar Kholin. He overhears Sadeas remarking that Dalinar's honor would one day lead to his death, confirming Kaladin's suspicion about the Highprince's treachery. Realizing the gravity of the situation, Kaladin is filled with urgency and dread.

**Dalinar:**
On the battlefield, Adolin confronts Dalinar, expressing frustration that Sadeas's betrayal was predictable. Despite the dire circumstances, Adolin refuses to place the blame on Dalinar, acknowledging that his father’s honor would not have allowed him to act differently. This sentiment surprises Dalinar, but he is moved by Adolin’s faith in him. With waves of Parshendi attacking, Dalinar attempts to inspire his soldiers with a speech about dying with glory and honor. He internally laments leaving his son Renarin in a dangerous political landscape, surrounded by potential enemies, and silently bids farewell to his family.
