## Quick Summary

**Dalinar Kholin** attends a feast hosted by King Elhokar. During the event, Wit (Hoid) sits next to Dalinar and subtly warns him that Sadeas plans to make a significant announcement. Wit subsequently makes an enigmatic exit, leaving Dalinar to ponder what the announcement might entail. Concerned about potential danger, Dalinar collects Adolin and his guards to be on alert.

At the feast, Sadeas stands to make his announcement. He reveals to the gathered nobility that the strap of Elhokar's saddle was deliberately cut while the horse was within the king's palace complex. However, Sadeas notably states that Dalinar is an "unlikely suspect" in this act of sabotage. This public declaration puts an end to the rumors about Dalinar's potential involvement, though it leaves the true saboteur unidentified.

After the announcement, Sadeas and Dalinar engage in a serious conversation about their past friendship and the ongoing war. Dalinar confesses that he had previously considered retreating but now wishes to find a strategy to decisively win the war. He proposes an alliance with Sadeas, suggesting that they could complement each other in their military efforts. Sadeas agrees to Dalinar's proposal, marking a potential shift in their strained relationship and setting the stage for future collaboration.

A: Sadeas publicly clears Dalinar of suspicion in the saddle strap incident, which helps alleviate some of the political pressure and rumors against Dalinar, allowing him to focus on other matters.

**Q: How does the alliance between Dalinar and Sadeas affect the war effort?**
A: The potential alliance suggests a unified front against the Parshendi, combining their military strengths, although the true impact will depend on their ability to trust and cooperate.

**Q: What is the significance of the epigraph?**
A: The epigraph reflects Dalinar's burden of leadership and the weight of others' madness he feels compelled to carry, hinting at his internal struggles and responsibilities.

**Q: Why is the setting of the announcement important?**
A: The king's palace complex is a symbol of Elhokar's power and security, making the revelation of the sabotage there particularly impactful, as it suggests vulnerabilities at the heart of the kingdom.

**Q: How does Dalinar’s proposal of an alliance show his growth?**
A: Dalinar's willingness to propose an alliance with Sadeas, despite past tensions, shows his focus on the greater good and his strategic thinking in seeking a decisive victory.

**Q: What could be the consequences of Sadeas's agreement to the alliance?**
A: While the agreement could strengthen their position, it also introduces the risk of betrayal or conflict over strategies, given their complex history.
