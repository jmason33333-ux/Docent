## Quick Summary
Szeth is in a gambling den as Makkek, his owner, flaunts his wealth. Makkek sends Szeth on a mission to assassinate Gavashaw, a rival. Szeth executes the plan only to discover Gavashaw already dead. A new master reveals Makkek's head and takes possession of Szeth's Oathstone, giving him a new list of targets.
