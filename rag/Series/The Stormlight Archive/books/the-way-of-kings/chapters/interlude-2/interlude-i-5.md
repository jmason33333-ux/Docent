## Quick Summary
Axies the Collector wakes up in an alley in the city of Kasitor, amidst rotting garbage, after a night of drinking. He is naked and manages to find a ratty blanket to cover himself. Axies heads to the docks, where he observes Cusicesh the Protector, a large spren, rise from the water. A street urchin steals his blanket, leaving him naked again, and he is subsequently taken to jail.
