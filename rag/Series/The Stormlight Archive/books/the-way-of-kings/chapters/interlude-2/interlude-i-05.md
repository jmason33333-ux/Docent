## Quick Summary

**Axies the Collector:** Axies wakes up in an alley in the city of Kasitor, lying amidst rotting garbage. He recalls getting drunk the previous night, an attempt to observe a type of spren known as Alespren. Now, stripped of his clothing, he is left with nothing but his wits. He manages to acquire a ratty blanket from a beggar after a tense exchange, covering himself as he navigates the streets.

Drawing attention due to his peculiar appearance and lack of attire, Axies heads towards the docks, where a crowd has gathered. They await the appearance of Cusicesh the Protector, a massive spren known to appear at the same time each day. Axies is eager to witness and document this event for his research on spren.

As Cusicesh emerges from the waters, Axies feels an overwhelming sense of fatigue. This sensation is typical of those who witness Cusicesh, yet Axies is more fascinated than deterred. During this encounter, a street urchin steals his blanket, leaving him exposed once more.

Despite the chaos, Axies is content, having succeeded in observing two new types of spren. However, his peculiarities lead to his arrest. As he is taken away, he remains optimistic, hoping to encounter the elusive captivityspren during his time in jail, adding to his extensive research on spren.
