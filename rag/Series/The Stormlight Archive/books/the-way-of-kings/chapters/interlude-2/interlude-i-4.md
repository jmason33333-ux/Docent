## Quick Summary
Rysn accompanies her mentor Vstim to Shinovar to engage in trade with the Shin people. During their visit, she is bewildered by the unique aspects of the Shin landscape and culture, such as grass that does not retract. Vstim negotiates with Thresh-son-Esan, offering Soulcast metal for exotic goods. Thresh mentions a past servant, Szeth, referred to as Truthless, indicating his worthlessness.

## Rowan's "If Asked" Notes

**Q: Why doesn't the grass retract in Shinovar?**
A: The grass in Shinovar is adapted to its environment, unlike the rest of Roshar, where grass retracts.

**Q: What is the significance of being Truthless?**
A: In Shin society, being Truthless denotes a person of low status who has committed a grave transgression.

**Q: Why are farmers valued over warriors in Shinovar?**
A: Shin society uniquely values the cultivation of land and agriculture, placing farmers at the top of their social hierarchy.
