## Quick Summary

**Szeth:** Szeth, a Truthless Shin assassin, is currently owned by Makkek, a man who has become wealthy due to Szeth's deadly skills. They are in a gambling den, where Makkek flaunts his newfound wealth and status with women on each arm. Despite his apparent success, Makkek's demeanor is one of arrogance and indulgence. Szeth remains passive, waiting for his master's command. 

When Makkek gives a subtle signal, Szeth departs on an assassination mission targeting Gavashaw, a competitor who recently arrived in the city to challenge Makkek's gambling business. Szeth infiltrates Gavashaw's residence with practiced stealth. Using his surgebinding abilities, he sends a decoy—a simple wooden knob dressed in a curtain robe—into the room where Gavashaw is expected to be. This careful approach ensures that any potential traps or ambushes are triggered without harming him.

Finding no threat, Szeth enters the room only to discover Gavashaw's severed head. Another man, a new master, reveals himself. This stranger presents Szeth with Makkek's severed head and Szeth's Oathstone, indicating that this new master now controls Szeth. The new master provides Szeth with a list of high-ranking nobles to assassinate, setting the stage for further lethal tasks.

## Rowan's "If Asked" Notes

**Q: What is the significance of the Oathstone in Szeth's life?**
A: The Oathstone is a physical object that symbolizes Szeth's enslavement. Whoever holds it can command Szeth, forcing him to follow their orders regardless of his personal desires or moral considerations.

**Q: Why does Szeth use a decoy made of a wooden knob and a curtain?**
A: Szeth employs a decoy to safely test for traps or ambushes in the room. This cautious approach highlights his experience and skill as an assassin, ensuring his safety during dangerous missions.

**Q: How did Makkek become wealthy and influential?**
A: Makkek gained wealth and status by leveraging Szeth's abilities as a powerful assassin. Szeth's successful missions brought Makkek significant financial and social gains within the gambling community.

**Q: Why is Gavashaw targeted for assassination?**
A: Gavashaw is targeted because he poses a threat to Makkek's gambling operations. As a new competitor, his elimination ensures Makkek's continued dominance in the gambling scene.

**Q: What does the new master want from Szeth?**
A: The new master provides Szeth with a list of high-ranking noblemen to assassinate, suggesting a strategic plan to influence political dynamics or settle personal scores.

**Q: How does Szeth's new master gain control over him?**
A: The new master gains control over Szeth by possessing his Oathstone, which legally and magically binds Szeth to follow their orders without resistance.

**Q: What does the severed head of Makkek signify?**
A: The severed head of Makkek signifies a change in power and Szeth's transition to a new master. It underscores the brutal and dangerous nature of the world Szeth inhabits.

**Q: How does Szeth feel about his role as an assassin?**
A: Szeth is conflicted and burdened by his role as an assassin. Despite his skills, he is forced into this life due to his status as Truthless and the control exerted over him through the Oathstone.
