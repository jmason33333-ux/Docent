## Quick Summary

Rysn Ftori is on a trading expedition to Shinovar with her babsk, Vstim. Rysn finds the landscape of Shinovar peculiar, particularly the grass that doesn't retract when stepped on, unlike in other parts of Roshar. Vstim, her mentor, instructs her to set up a fabrial device that alerts them to approaching people. This task is part of her training to become a capable trader.

When the Shin arrive for the trade, Rysn is surprised to see that the supposed guards are dressed plainly, which contrasts sharply with the societal norms she's accustomed to. Vstim explains to her that in Shin society, farmers hold the highest status, while warriors are considered the lowest. This reversal of roles intrigues Rysn and highlights the cultural differences between Shinovar and the rest of the world.

Vstim engages in trade negotiations with Thresh-son-Esan, a Shin man, offering him pieces of Soulcast metal in exchange for exotic goods, including chickens. The metal is unique because it is certified to be Soulcast from organic materials rather than mined, which increases its value. During the negotiations, Vstim inquires about obtaining another servant like the one he had received seven years ago. Thresh reveals that the servant was a Truthless, a term of shame in Shinovar, and hopes there will not be another like him, referring to Szeth.
