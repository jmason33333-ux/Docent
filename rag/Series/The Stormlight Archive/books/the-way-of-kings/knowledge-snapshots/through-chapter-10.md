## Major Characters (Introduced So Far)

### Kaladin
- **First appearance:** Chapter 1
- **Role:** Former soldier turned slave, now a member of Bridge Four in Highprince Sadeas's army.
- **Physical description:** Described as having dark Alethi skin and long black hair.
- **Key relationships:**
  - **Sylphrena (Syl):** A windspren who becomes his companion, showing unusual intelligence and interest in him.
  - **Tvlakv:** The slave trader who captured him, representing exploitation and betrayal.
- **Abilities/Powers:** Skilled fighter with leadership qualities; protective instincts highlighted in battle.
- **Motivations/Goals:** Initially driven by the desire to protect and lead, now struggling with despair and a search for purpose.
- **Character Arc (through Ch 10):**
  - **Starting point:** A capable squadleader in Amaram's army, known for keeping his men alive.
  - **Key moments:** Saving Cenn in battle (Ch 1), becoming a slave (Ch 2), surviving a brutal bridge run (Ch 6).
  - **Current state:** Despondent and emotionally broken after the death of a recruit who reminded him of his brother Tien (Ch 9).
- **Key scenes/chapters:** Chapters 1, 2, 6, 9.
- **Notable quotes:** "Honor is dead. But I'll see what I can do."

### Shallan Davar
- **First appearance:** Chapter 3
- **Role:** Young noblewoman seeking to become Jasnah Kholin's ward to save her family from financial ruin.
- **Physical description:** Red-haired and fair-skinned, typical of her family's Veden heritage.
- **Key relationships:**
  - **Jasnah Kholin:** A potential mentor and target for Shallan's plan to steal her Soulcaster.
  - **Kabsal:** An ardent who befriends her and discusses philosophy.
- **Abilities/Powers:** Talented artist with a keen intellect, adept at sketching and learning.
- **Motivations/Goals:** Aims to secure her family's future by stealing Jasnah's Soulcaster.
- **Character Arc (through Ch 10):**
  - **Starting point:** Desperate and determined, arriving in Kharbranth to seek Jasnah's tutelage.
  - **Key moments:** Being rejected then accepted as Jasnah’s ward (Ch 5, Ch 8).
  - **Current state:** Accepted as Jasnah's ward, secretly plotting to acquire the Soulcaster.
- **Key scenes/chapters:** Chapters 3, 5, 8.
- **Notable quotes:** "I will find a way to save my house. I have to."

### Szeth
- **First appearance:** Prologue
- **Role:** Assassin known as Truthless from Shinovar, tasked with killing King Gavilar.
- **Physical description:** Pale-skinned and bald, typical of Shin nationals.
- **Key relationships:**
  - **Gavilar Kholin:** His assassination target, whose death sets major events in motion.
- **Abilities/Powers:** Wields Surgebinding abilities, allowing him to manipulate gravity and adhere to surfaces.
- **Motivations/Goals:** Bound by the mysterious status of being 'Truthless,' compelled to obey those who hold his Oathstone.
- **Character Arc (through Ch 10):**
  - **Starting point:** A reluctant assassin fulfilling a deadly mission.
  - **Key moments:** Assassinating Gavilar and fleeing with a mysterious black sphere (Prologue).
  - **Current state:** Disillusioned and haunted by his actions, having left a significant impact on the world.
- **Key scenes/chapters:** Prologue.
- **Notable quotes:** "The Truthless of Shinovar have no choice."

### Jasnah Kholin
- **First appearance:** Mentioned in Chapter 3, appears in Chapter 5
- **Role:** Renowned scholar and Soulcaster, sister of the Alethi king.
- **Physical description:** Tall and regal, with an air of authority and intelligence.
- **Key relationships:**
  - **Shallan Davar:** Takes her on as a ward, impressed by her potential.
  - **King Taravangian:** Has a professional relationship regarding access to the Palanaeum.
- **Abilities/Powers:** Possesses a Soulcaster, allowing her to transform materials at will.
- **Motivations/Goals:** Pursues knowledge and truth, skeptical of religious dogma.
- **Character Arc (through Ch 10):**
  - **Starting point:** Established as a powerful and respected scholar.
  - **Key moments:** Demonstrating Soulcasting to save a child's life (Ch 5), agreeing to mentor Shallan (Ch 8).
  - **Current state:** Begins mentoring Shallan, with high expectations for her ward.
- **Key scenes/chapters:** Chapters 5, 8.

## World-Building / Lore

### Surgebinding
- **First explained:** Prologue
- **What we know:** A mystical ability allowing users to manipulate physical forces, such as gravity and adhesion.
- **How it works:** Users can bind themselves to surfaces and alter gravitational pull, enabling feats like walking on walls.
- **Limitations/Rules:** Requires specific knowledge and skill; seems tied to oaths or specific conditions.
- **Key examples:** Szeth uses Surgebinding to assassinate King Gavilar (Prologue).
- **Related concepts:** Tied to the mythical Heralds and Honorblades.
- **Unanswered questions:** The full scope of Surgebinding's abilities and its origins remain largely unexplored.

### Soulcasting
- **First explained:** Chapter 5
- **What we know:** A magical process that transforms one substance into another, used by those with a Soulcaster device.
- **How it works:** Requires concentration and intent, demonstrated by Jasnah transforming stone into smoke.
- **Limitations/Rules:** Seems to require a special fabrial or device; not everyone can use it.
- **Key examples:** Jasnah saves a child by Soulcasting a stone (Ch 5).
- **Related concepts:** Part of the larger system of fabrials and magical technologies.
- **Unanswered questions:** The nature of Soulcasters and their connection to the broader magical system.

## Common Reader Questions (Up to Chapter 10)

**Q: Who is Kaladin and what happened to him?**  
A: Kaladin is a former squadleader known as "Stormblessed" for his skill in battle. After being betrayed and sold into slavery, he ends up in Highprince Sadeas's warcamp as part of Bridge Four, struggling with despair and loss (Ch 1-10).

**Q: What is the significance of the black sphere Gavilar gives to Szeth?**  
A: The black sphere is mysterious and Gavilar's last message suggests it holds importance for Dalinar. Its true significance is yet to be revealed but is tied to larger, potentially magical or political elements (Prologue).

**Q: What are the bridge crews, and why are they important?**  
A: Bridge crews are groups of slaves used to carry massive bridges across the Shattered Plains during military campaigns. They are crucial for moving troops quickly but suffer high casualties, reflecting the harshness of the war (Ch 6).

**Q: How does Surgebinding work, and who can use it?**  
A: Surgebinding allows manipulation of physical forces like gravity. Szeth demonstrates these abilities in the Prologue, but the full extent and requirements for Surgebinding remain unclear.

**Q: What is Shallan's true intention in becoming Jasnah's ward?**  
A: While Shallan seeks to learn from Jasnah, her underlying plan is to steal Jasnah's Soulcaster to save her family from financial ruin (Ch 5, Ch 8).

**Q: How does Jasnah Kholin use her Soulcaster?**  
A: Jasnah uses her Soulcaster to transform substances, such as turning stone into smoke to save a child, showcasing her mastery over this magical device (Ch 5).

**Q: What are the implications of the Heralds abandoning the Oathpact?**  
A: The Prelude reveals that the Heralds left Talenel to bear the burden alone, suggesting a breach of duty with unknown consequences for the world (Prelude).

**Q: Why does Sylphrena follow Kaladin, and how does she help him?**  
A: Syl, a windspren, seems drawn to Kaladin's innate qualities and provides companionship and motivation, even reviving him after a brutal bridge run (Ch 6).
