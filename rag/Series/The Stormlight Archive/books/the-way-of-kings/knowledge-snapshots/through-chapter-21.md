## World-Building / Lore

### Highstorms
- **First explained:** Ongoing references, particularly in Chapters 12, 19.
- **What we know:** Powerful storms that sweep across the land, affecting all life and structures.
- **How it works:** Highstorms are periodic, bringing destructive winds and rain.
- **Limitations/Rules:** Unpredictable in strength; structures must be built to withstand them.
- **Key examples:** Dalinar’s vision occurs during a highstorm (Ch 19).
- **Related concepts:** Influence on local flora and fauna.
- **Unanswered questions:** Origin and potential deeper significance of highstorms.

### Chasmfiends
- **First explained:** Chapters 12-13
- **What we know:** Large, crustacean-like creatures that inhabit the Shattered Plains.
- **How it works:** Hunted for their valuable gemhearts.
- **Limitations/Rules:** Dangerous to hunt; can appear unexpectedly.
- **Key examples:** Chasmfiend hunt in Chapters 12-13.
- **Related concepts:** Part of the ecosystem of the Shattered Plains.
- **Unanswered questions:** Their role in the ecosystem and potential deeper significance.

## Common Reader Questions (Up to Chapter 21)

**Q: What is the significance of Dalinar’s visions?**  
A: Dalinar’s visions occur during highstorms and seem to offer cryptic guidance about uniting Alethkar and the possibility of ancient threats. (Ch 19)

**Q: Why was Elhokar’s saddle suspected of being sabotaged?**  
A: A cut in Elhokar’s saddle strap during a chasmfiend hunt suggests potential assassination attempts. (Ch 13, 15)

**Q: How does Kaladin plan to improve conditions for Bridge Four?**  
A: Kaladin attempts to train the bridgemen and gathers resources for medical care, despite resistance from Gaz. (Ch 14, 21)

**Q: What are chasmfiends, and why are they hunted?**  
A: Chasmfiends are large creatures on the Shattered Plains, hunted for their valuable gemhearts. (Ch 12-13)

**Q: How is Syl changing, and what does it mean for Kaladin?**  
A: Syl is becoming more aware and articulate, paralleling Kaladin’s growth and hinting at deeper connections. (Ch 14, 21)

**Q: What is the role of the Knights Radiant in Dalinar’s vision?**  
A: The Knights Radiant assist Dalinar in fighting Midnight Essence and reference ancient orders, suggesting a historical significance. (Ch 19)

**Q: Why does Elhokar distrust his allies?**  
A: Elhokar’s paranoia is fueled by past assassination attempts and political tensions. (Ch 15)

**Q: What does “Unite them” mean in Dalinar’s visions?**  
A: It suggests a call to unify Alethkar or possibly a larger coalition against looming threats. (Ch 19)

**Q: Why does Kaladin choose to lead from the front during bridge runs?**  
A: Kaladin believes it sets an example for his men and allows him to protect them more effectively. (Ch 17)

**Q: What motivates Dalinar’s proposal to become Highprince of War?**  
A: Dalinar aims to centralize military command to end the war and protect Alethkar effectively. (Ch 18)

**Q: How does Adolin view his father’s visions?**  
A: Adolin is skeptical but supports his father, despite concerns about Dalinar’s mental health. (Ch 15, 18)

**Q: What is the Midnight Essence in Dalinar’s vision?**  
A: Creatures fought by Dalinar and Knights Radiant, emitting smoke when injured, not fully explained yet. (Ch 19)

**Q: How does Syl’s perspective on lying affect Kaladin?**  
A: Syl’s perspective challenges Kaladin to consider the nature of truth and trust among men. (Ch 21)

**Q: What challenges does Bridge Four face under Sadeas’s command?**  
A: They endure dangerous bridge runs with little support, facing high casualties and resource scarcity. (Ch 17, 21)

**Q: Why does Dalinar reflect on Gavilar’s last words?**  
A: Gavilar’s cryptic last words weigh on Dalinar, motivating his protective approach towards Elhokar. (Ch 15)
