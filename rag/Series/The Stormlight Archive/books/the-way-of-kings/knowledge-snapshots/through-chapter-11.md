## World-Building / Lore

### The Honor Chasm
- **First explained:** Chapter 11
- **What we know:** A deep chasm where despondent soldiers and bridgemen go to commit suicide.
- **How it works:** Serves as a place of finality for those who have lost hope.
- **Limitations/Rules:** Symbolic of ultimate despair; once there, a person is unlikely to return.
- **Key examples:** Kaladin approaches the chasm intending to end his life but is stopped by Syl.
- **Related concepts:** Reflects the harsh conditions and mental toll of life at the Shattered Plains.
