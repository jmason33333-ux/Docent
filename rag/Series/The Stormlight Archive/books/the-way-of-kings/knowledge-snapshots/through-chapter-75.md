## World-Building / Lore

### Soulcasting
- **First explained:** Chapter 70
- **What we know:** Soulcasting transforms one substance into another; linked to Shadesmar.
- **How it works:** Requires a bond with a spren and understanding of the object’s essence.
- **Limitations/Rules:** Easier to Soulcast familiar substances; risk involved without proper knowledge.
- **Key examples:** Shallan’s experiments and Jasnah’s explanations (Ch 70, 72).
- **Related concepts:** Ties to Shadesmar and spren bonds.
- **Unanswered questions:** Full extent of Shallan’s capabilities and the nature of Shadesmar.

### Voidbringers
- **First explained:** Chapter 72
- **What we know:** Historically considered mythological, possibly enslaved as Parshmen.
- **How it works:** Jasnah’s research suggests Voidbringers have been misunderstood.
- **Limitations/Rules:** Not fully understood; public beliefs differ from Jasnah’s findings.
- **Key examples:** Jasnah’s discussion with Shallan (Ch 72).
- **Related concepts:** Connected to the history and myths of Roshar.
- **Unanswered questions:** True nature and history of the Voidbringers.

## Common Reader Questions (Up to Chapter 75)

**Q: How does Soulcasting work?**  
A: Soulcasting involves transforming one substance into another, requiring a bond with a spren and an understanding of the substance’s essence. Shallan and Jasnah discuss this process, highlighting its complexities (Ch 70, 72).

**Q: Who is King Taravangian, and what is his role?**  
A: Taravangian is revealed as Szeth’s employer, orchestrating assassinations for a larger, mysterious purpose (Ch 71).

**Q: What are Voidbringers, according to Jasnah?**  
A: Jasnah theorizes that Voidbringers were real entities, possibly enslaved as Parshmen, challenging traditional beliefs (Ch 72).

**Q: What is the significance of Dalinar’s visions?**  
A: Dalinar’s visions during highstorms seem prophetic, urging him to unite people against the Everstorm, hinting at a larger destiny (Ch 75).

**Q: What are the Ghostbloods, and how is Shallan connected?**  
A: The Ghostbloods are a secretive group linked to Shallan’s father, involved in political intrigue. Shallan discovers their symbol and potential connections (Ch 74).

**Q: Why does Dalinar offer leadership to Kaladin?**  
A: Dalinar sees potential in Kaladin and Bridge Four, offering them a chance to lead and protect, aligning with his vision for unity (Ch 73).

**Q: What drives Szeth in his missions?**  
A: Szeth is bound by an oath as a Truthless, following orders despite his personal moral conflicts (Ch 71).

**Q: How does Shallan’s relationship with Jasnah evolve?**  
A: Shallan’s growing understanding and revelations about Soulcasting and Voidbringers deepen her bond with Jasnah, though tensions persist over hidden truths (Ch 70, 72, 74).

**Q: What are Dalinar’s goals after the vision?**  
A: Dalinar aims to unite the Alethi and potentially rebuild the Knights Radiant, following the vision’s guidance (Ch 75).

**Q: What challenges does Kaladin face with Dalinar’s offer?**  
A: Kaladin grapples with trust issues and the responsibility of leadership, weighing Dalinar’s offer against past betrayals (Ch 73).

**Q: What is Shadesmar, and how does it relate to Soulcasting?**  
A: Shadesmar is a parallel realm involved in the Soulcasting process, where Shallan briefly ventures, indicating her bond with a spren (Ch 70).

**Q: How do Shallan’s discoveries impact her goals?**  
A: Shallan’s revelations about Soulcasting and the Ghostbloods push her towards understanding her past and its implications (Ch 70, 74).

**Q: What is the Everstorm, and why is it significant?**  
A: The Everstorm is a looming threat revealed in Dalinar’s vision, suggesting a catastrophic future that must be averted (Ch 75).

**Q: How does Jasnah view the history of Roshar?**  
A: Jasnah challenges traditional myths, particularly about the Voidbringers, seeking historical truths that could reshape understanding (Ch 72).

**Q: What role does Bridge Four play in the current events?**  
A: Bridge Four, under Kaladin’s leadership, is poised to take on a new role as Dalinar’s guards, marking a shift in their status and potential influence (Ch 73).
