## World-Building / Lore

### Spren
- **First explained:** Chapter 21 (Syl's interactions)
- **What we know:** Spren are mysterious, often invisible entities that embody aspects of the natural world or emotions
- **How it works:** Spren can be seen by some individuals, interact with the physical world in limited ways
- **Limitations/Rules:** Not all spren are visible to everyone; their full nature and purpose remain mysterious
- **Key examples:** Syl assisting Kaladin and interacting with Rock (Ch 21, 23)
- **Related concepts:** Ties into Kaladin’s potential abilities and the nature of the world
- **Unanswered questions:** What are the full capabilities and purpose of spren?

### Shardblades
- **First explained:** Prior to Chapter 21, discussed in Chapter 26
- **What we know:** Powerful, mystical weapons summoned by their bearers, capable of cutting through anything
- **How it works:** Summoned through mental command, disappear when not in use
- **Limitations/Rules:** Only accessible to those who possess them, typically lighteyes or those of high status
- **Key examples:** Dalinar’s use of Oathbringer in battle (Ch 26)
- **Related concepts:** Shardplate, the social status associated with these weapons
- **Unanswered questions:** What is the origin of Shardblades?

## Common Reader Questions (Up to Chapter 30)

**Q: Who is Kaladin, and what is his role in the story?**  
A: Kaladin is a former soldier and current leader of Bridge Four in Sadeas’s army. He strives to improve the lives of his bridgemen, using his leadership skills and determination. (Ch 21-30)

**Q: What are Dalinar’s visions, and how do they affect him?**  
A: Dalinar experiences prophetic visions during highstorms, which guide his actions and decisions. They cause political tension and personal doubt. (Ch 22, 24, 26)

**Q: How does Shallan balance her studies with Jasnah and her family’s needs?**  
A: Shallan is secretly plotting to steal Jasnah’s Soulcaster to aid her family while learning from Jasnah and grappling with ethical dilemmas. (Ch 29)

**Q: What is the significance of Bridge Four’s training under Kaladin?**  
A: Kaladin's training unites and strengthens Bridge Four, challenging the harsh conditions and improving morale. It signifies his leadership growth. (Ch 21, 27, 30)

**Q: How do the highprinces' politics impact Dalinar’s goals?**  
A: Dalinar struggles to unify the highprinces due to political rivalries and skepticism about his visions, affecting his ability to lead effectively. (Ch 22, 24, 28)

**Q: What is the Thrill, and how does it affect Dalinar?**  
A: The Thrill is a battle frenzy experienced by soldiers, invigorating but also causing revulsion in Dalinar during combat, reflecting his inner conflict. (Ch 26)

**Q: Who is Sadeas, and what are his motives?**  
A: Sadeas is a Highprince and political rival to Dalinar, recently appointed as Highprince of Information, potentially undermining Dalinar’s efforts. (Ch 22, 24)

**Q: How do Kaladin’s past experiences influence his current actions?**  
A: Kaladin’s past as a soldier and his guilt over past events drive his determination to protect and lead Bridge Four. (Ch 23, 25)

**Q: What role does Syl play in Kaladin’s life?**  
A: Syl is a spren who accompanies Kaladin, providing moral support and insight, visible only to Kaladin and select others like Rock. (Ch 21, 23, 30)

**Q: What are Shardblades, and who wields them?**  
A: Shardblades are mystical weapons wielded by individuals of high status, such as Dalinar, capable of cutting through anything. (Ch 26)

**Q: How does Shallan’s art influence her character and the plot?**  
A: Shallan’s art reveals her inner thoughts and serves as a tool for her studies and interactions, reflecting her dual life. (Ch 29)

**Q: Why is Gaz concerned about Kaladin’s actions?**  
A: Gaz is wary of Kaladin’s growing influence and leadership, which threaten his control and result in threats from superiors like Lamaril. (Ch 30)

**Q: What are the dynamics between Dalinar and Adolin?**  
A: Their relationship is strained due to differing views on leadership and visions, with Adolin skeptical of Dalinar’s reliance on visions. (Ch 24, 28)

**Q: What challenges does Kaladin face as leader of Bridge Four?**  
A: Kaladin faces internal camp politics, Gaz’s antagonism, and the physical dangers of bridge runs, while trying to protect his men. (Ch 21, 27, 30)

**Q: How does the political environment in Alethkar affect the story?**  
A: The fragmented political landscape complicates alliances and strategies, impacting characters like Dalinar and Sadeas. (Ch 22, 24, 28)
