# Part-Level Knowledge Snapshot: The Illuminating Storms

**Book:** The Way of Kings  
**Part:** 2: The Illuminating Storms  
**Coverage:** Chapters 12-28  
**Safe for readers up to:** Chapter 28 (end of this part)

---

## Part Overview

In Part 2: The Illuminating Storms, the focus shifts between the political dynamics of the Alethi leadership on the Shattered Plains and the struggles of Kaladin as he navigates his role within Bridge Four. Dalinar Kholin grapples with visions that challenge his understanding of leadership and duty, while his son Adolin becomes increasingly concerned about his father's mental state. Dalinar's attempts to unite the highprinces highlight the political intrigue and tension within Alethkar, complicated further by Elhokar's paranoia and Sadeas's cunning maneuvers.

Kaladin, meanwhile, works to improve the conditions for his fellow bridgemen, facing setbacks but slowly gaining their trust and respect. His leadership qualities begin to emerge as he takes strategic actions to care for his men and inspire them. Themes of leadership, trust, and the burden of responsibility permeate this part, setting the stage for future developments and conflicts.

## Common Reader Questions (Up to End of This Part)

**Q: What do Dalinar's visions mean?**  
A: They seem to connect him to the past and the Knights Radiant, offering cryptic guidance that influences his leadership decisions (Ch 19).

**Q: Why is Elhokar paranoid about assassination attempts?**  
A: The broken saddle strap incident (Ch 13) and political tensions contribute to his fears.

**Q: How does Kaladin gain the trust of Bridge Four?**  
A: Through his actions in training, leadership during bridge runs, and fostering camaraderie (Ch 27).

**Q: What is the significance of the chasmfiend hunt?**  
A: It highlights the political dynamics among Alethi leaders and tests Dalinar's protective instincts (Ch 12-13).

**Q: Why does Dalinar consider abdication?**  
A: He struggles with his visions and doubts his leadership role (Ch 28).

**Q: What role does Sadeas play in this part?**  
A: He maneuvers politically, gaining influence and challenging Dalinar's position (Ch 22).

**Q: How does Kaladin handle the knobweed negotiation?**  
A: He outsmarts the apothecary with Syl's help, securing resources for his men (Ch 27).

**Q: What are the challenges facing Dalinar's attempts to unite the highprinces?**  
A: Skepticism, political intrigue, and resistance from other highprinces like Roion (Ch 24).
