## World-Building / Lore

### Soulcasting
- **First explained:** Chapter 42
- **What we know:** A magical process that transforms one substance into another; typically requires a Soulcaster device.
- **How it works:** Users envision the transformation, possibly involving Shadesmar; Shallan's experience suggests a deeper connection.
- **Limitations/Rules:** Requires skill and understanding; not fully controlled by Shallan yet.
- **Key examples:** Shallan's accidental Soulcasting in Shadesmar (Ch 45).
- **Related concepts:** Shadesmar, the cognitive realm that interacts with Soulcasting.
- **Unanswered questions:** How does Shadesmar function? What is Shallan's unique connection?

### Highstorms and Visions
- **First explained:** Chapter 46
- **What we know:** Highstorms are powerful weather events on Roshar; Kaladin experiences visions during them.
- **How it works:** Visions appear to show past or future events; Kaladin sees Szeth and hears a voice linking him to Tanavast and Honor.
- **Limitations/Rules:** Only occur during highstorms; visions may not be fully understood.
- **Key examples:** Kaladin's vision in Ch 46.
- **Related concepts:** The broader cosmology of Roshar, Odium's influence.
- **Unanswered questions:** What is the source of these visions? How do they relate to Kaladin's destiny?
