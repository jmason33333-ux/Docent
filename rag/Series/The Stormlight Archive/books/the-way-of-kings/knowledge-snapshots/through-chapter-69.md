## World-Building / Lore

### Stormlight
- **First explained:** Chapter 67
- **What we know:** A mystical energy that can be inhaled to enhance physical abilities and healing
- **How it works:** Absorbed from gemstones, used to perform extraordinary feats
- **Limitations/Rules:** Requires a source (gemstones), not fully understood
- **Key examples:** Kaladin uses it to enhance his fighting abilities
- **Related concepts:** Connected to the spren and the Knights Radiant
- **Unanswered questions:** Full extent of its abilities and limitations

### Honorspren
- **First explained:** Chapter 67
- **What we know:** A type of spren associated with honor, capable of bonding with humans
- **How it works:** Forms a symbiotic relationship, granting powers
- **Limitations/Rules:** Must be bonded willingly, not all spren are honorspren
- **Key examples:** Syl reveals her true nature to Kaladin
- **Related concepts:** Tied to the Knights Radiant and the use of Stormlight
- **Unanswered questions:** Origins and full capabilities of honorspren

## Common Reader Questions (Up to Chapter 69)

**Q: Who is Kaladin and what is his role?**  
A: Kaladin is the leader of Bridge Four, a former soldier and surgeon, who has begun to harness Stormlight to enhance his abilities, becoming a pivotal figure in the battle at the Tower (Ch 62-69).

**Q: What happened at the Tower?**  
A: Dalinar’s forces were betrayed by Sadeas, leaving them surrounded by Parshendi. Kaladin and Bridge Four intervened, aiding Dalinar’s retreat and survival (Ch 65).

**Q: How does Stormlight work?**  
A: Stormlight can be inhaled to enhance physical abilities, heal wounds, and perform feats of strength and speed. It is drawn from gemstones (Ch 67).

**Q: What is the significance of Syl’s revelation?**  
A: Syl revealing herself as an honorspren indicates a deeper connection to Kaladin’s powers and the legacy of the Knights Radiant (Ch 67).

**Q: Why did Sadeas betray Dalinar?**  
A: Sadeas sought to undermine Dalinar’s influence and eliminate a rival, leaving Dalinar’s forces vulnerable during a critical battle (Ch 65).

**Q: What are Dalinar’s visions?**  
A: While not detailed in these chapters, Dalinar’s visions are considered prophetic and influence his actions and decisions (ongoing context).

**Q: How are Dalinar and Navani related?**  
A: Navani is the widow of Dalinar’s brother Gavilar, and she shares a romantic relationship with Dalinar (Ch 64, 69).

**Q: What are the consequences of Dalinar trading his Shardblade?**  
A: This act demonstrates Dalinar’s commitment to honor and changes the political landscape, freeing the bridgemen and challenging Sadeas (Ch 69).

**Q: What are honorspren?**  
A: Honorspren are a type of spren associated with honor and truth, capable of bonding with humans to grant them abilities (Ch 67).

**Q: How has Bridge Four evolved under Kaladin’s leadership?**  
A: Bridge Four has transformed from a group of expendable soldiers to a respected and cohesive unit under Kaladin, gaining armor and tactical skills (Ch 62-69).

**Q: What are the Alethi Highprinces’ goals?**  
A: The Highprinces, including Dalinar and Sadeas, aim to maintain their power and fulfill the vengeance pact against the Parshendi (ongoing context).

**Q: How do Kaladin and Dalinar’s stories intersect?**  
A: Kaladin’s decision to aid Dalinar during the Tower battle highlights a significant intersection of their paths, driven by shared values of honor (Ch 65-69).

**Q: What are the political implications of the Tower battle?**  
A: The betrayal exposes rifts within the Alethi leadership, setting the stage for potential realignment and struggles for power (Ch 65, 69).

**Q: How does Kaladin’s ability with Stormlight affect his role?**  
A: It enhances his leadership and combat skills, making him a key figure in the battle and recognized by both allies and enemies (Ch 67-68).

**Q: What is the significance of the phrase "Neshua Kadal"?**  
A: It is the Parshendi term for Kaladin, indicating their recognition of his abilities and potential threat (Ch 68).
