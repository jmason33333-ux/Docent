## World-Building / Lore

### Highstorms
- **First explained:** Chapter 26
- **What we know:** Highstorms are powerful weather phenomena impacting the Shattered Plains.
- **How it works:** They bring destruction but also provide stormlight, a source of energy.
- **Limitations/Rules:** Dangerous and unpredictable, requiring shelter.
- **Key examples:** Dalinar experiences visions during highstorms.
- **Related concepts:** Tied to Dalinar's visions and the broader mystical elements of the world.
- **Unanswered questions:** The full nature and purpose of Dalinar's visions during highstorms.

### Alethi War Codes
- **First explained:** Chapter 22
- **What we know:** A set of rules guiding honorable conduct in war.
- **How it works:** Enforced to maintain discipline and order among the Alethi nobility.
- **Limitations/Rules:** Often ignored by nobles, much to Dalinar's frustration.
- **Key examples:** Dalinar's annoyance at the nobles' disregard during the feast.
- **Related concepts:** Tied to Dalinar's sense of honor and justice.
- **Unanswered questions:** How adherence to these codes will affect Dalinar's political standing.
