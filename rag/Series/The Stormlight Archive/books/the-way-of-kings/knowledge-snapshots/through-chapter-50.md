## Major Characters (Introduced So Far)

### Kaladin
- **First appearance:** Chapter 41 in this snapshot
- **Role:** Bridgeman, former squadleader
- **Physical description:** Not detailed in these chapters
- **Key relationships:**
  - **Lirin:** His father, a village surgeon, who influences Kaladin's sense of duty and ethics.
  - **Tien:** His younger brother, whose death deeply affects Kaladin.
  - **Syl:** A spren who accompanies him, providing guidance and support.
- **Abilities/Powers:** Begins to exhibit unusual healing abilities and strength, suggesting a connection to ancient powers.
- **Motivations/Goals:** Driven by a desire to protect those under his care, haunted by past failures.
- **Character Arc (through Ch 50):**
  - **Starting point:** Haunted by his brother's death and his perceived failures.
  - **Key moments:** Experiences visions during a highstorm (Ch 46), begins leading Bridge Four with a new philosophy of caring (Ch 49).
  - **Current state:** Leading Bridge Four with a growing sense of purpose and developing powers.
- **Key scenes/chapters:** Chapter 41 (flashback), Chapter 46 (highstorm vision), Chapter 49 (leading Bridge Four).
- **Notable quotes:** Kaladin's reflections on duty and leadership are key to his character.

### Shallan Davar
- **First appearance:** Chapter 42 in this snapshot
- **Role:** Scholar and apprentice to Jasnah Kholin
- **Physical description:** Not detailed in these chapters
- **Key relationships:**
  - **Jasnah Kholin:** Her mentor, whom she initially admires but also deceives by stealing the Soulcaster.
  - **Kabsal:** A man who shows romantic interest in her and is involved in the poisoning incident.
- **Abilities/Powers:** Experiences visions and encounters with mysterious creatures known as "symbolheads."
- **Motivations/Goals:** Initially seeks to help her family by stealing and using a Soulcaster, but her motivations evolve as she questions Jasnah's methods.
- **Character Arc (through Ch 50):**
  - **Starting point:** Ambitious and morally conflicted.
  - **Key moments:** Attempts to use the stolen Soulcaster (Ch 42), experiences a suicide attempt and recovery (Ch 48-50).
  - **Current state:** Recovering from a poisoning and reflecting on her actions and beliefs.
- **Key scenes/chapters:** Chapter 42 (moral debate with Jasnah), Chapter 45 (exploring Shadesmar), Chapter 50 (awakening in the hospital).
- **Notable quotes:** Her internal conflicts and realizations about her situation are significant.

## World-Building / Lore

### Soulcasting
- **First explained:** Chapter 42
- **What we know:** A magical process that transforms materials from one form to another.
- **How it works:** Requires a Soulcaster, a device that seems to channel the power needed to change substances.
- **Limitations/Rules:** Not everyone can use it; its use is restricted and complex.
- **Key examples:** Shallan's attempts to use the Soulcaster (Ch 42).
- **Related concepts:** Connected to the broader magical systems of Roshar, including Shadesmar.
- **Unanswered questions:** The true nature and origin of Soulcasting, its limitations.

### Shadesmar
- **First explained:** Chapter 45
- **What we know:** A parallel realm that Shallan visits briefly during a moment of crisis.
- **How it works:** Seems to be a place connected to magic and the mind.
- **Limitations/Rules:** Access is limited and potentially dangerous.
- **Key examples:** Shallan's experience in Chapter 45.
- **Related concepts:** Tied to Soulcasting and the symbolheads.
- **Unanswered questions:** The full nature of Shadesmar and its inhabitants.
