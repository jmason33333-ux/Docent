## World-Building / Lore

### Soulcasting
- **First explained:** Chapter 36
- **What we know:** A magical process that transforms material substances, used by Jasnah to defend against attackers.
- **How it works:** Involves using a Soulcaster device to tap into Stormlight, can transform objects (e.g., flesh to crystal).
- **Limitations/Rules:** Requires Stormlight; limited by the user's skill and the Soulcaster's functionality.
- **Key examples:** Jasnah transforms attackers into fire and crystal (Ch 36).
- **Related concepts:** Tied to the broader magic system involving Stormlight.
- **Unanswered questions:** Full mechanics of Soulcasting and its potential uses.

### Stormlight
- **First explained:** Chapter 38
- **What we know:** A source of magical energy, used by Kaladin unknowingly to heal.
- **How it works:** Absorbed through spheres, enhances physical abilities and accelerates healing.
- **Limitations/Rules:** Requires spheres containing Stormlight; usage depletes the light.
- **Key examples:** Kaladin draws in Stormlight during a highstorm, healing his wounds (Ch 38).
- **Related concepts:** Integral to Soulcasting and other magical abilities.
- **Unanswered questions:** Full extent of Stormlight's applications, its source.
