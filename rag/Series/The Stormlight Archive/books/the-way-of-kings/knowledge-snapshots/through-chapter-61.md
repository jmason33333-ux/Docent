## World-Building / Lore

### Surgebinding
- **First explained:** Chapter 57
- **What we know:** Surgebinding involves drawing in and using Stormlight to perform extraordinary feats.
- **How it works:** Kaladin inhales Stormlight, allowing him to enhance physical abilities and perform acts like creating a stone ladder.
- **Limitations/Rules:** Requires Stormlight from spheres; still limited understanding of full capabilities.
- **Key examples:** Kaladin's use of Stormlight to survive falls and enhance strength (Ch 59).
- **Related concepts:** Tied to the ancient orders of the Knights Radiant.
- **Unanswered questions:** The full extent and limitations of Surgebinding remain unclear.

### Shardblades and Shardplates
- **First explained:** Chapter 52
- **What we know:** Powerful weapons and armor left by the Radiants, highly coveted in battles.
- **How it works:** Shardblades can cut through anything, Shardplates grant immense durability and strength.
- **Limitations/Rules:** Limited by the skill and strength of the wielder.
- **Key examples:** Dalinar and Adolin's use in battles (Ch 56).
- **Related concepts:** Connected to the history of the Radiants and the Day of Recreance.
- **Unanswered questions:** The reason behind the Radiants abandoning their Shards is still a mystery.

## Common Reader Questions (Up to Chapter 61)

A: Kaladin draws Stormlight to enhance his abilities, allowing him to perform feats like surviving falls and creating a stone ladder (Ch 59).

A: It marks the moment the Knights Radiant abandoned their Shardblades and Shardplates, a pivotal historical event witnessed by Dalinar in his visions (Ch 52).

**Q: How do Dalinar and Sadeas view each other?**  
A: They share a complicated relationship, with past friendship overshadowed by current political tensions, though they attempt to maintain an alliance (Ch 54, Ch 56).

A: Kaladin resents the lighteyes for their treatment of darkeyes, particularly the exploitation he and Bridge Four endure under their command (Ch 53).

**Q: How does Navani feel about Dalinar?**

**Q: What does Adolin think of his father’s leadership?**
