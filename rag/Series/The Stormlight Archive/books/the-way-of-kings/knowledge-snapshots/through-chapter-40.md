## World-Building / Lore

### Soulcasting
- **First explained:** Chapter 36
- **What we know:** A magical process that transforms objects into different substances.
- **How it works:** Requires a Soulcaster, a fabrial device, and Stormlight as a fuel.
- **Limitations/Rules:** Not everyone can use it; effectiveness depends on the user's skill and the Soulcaster's condition.
- **Key examples:** Jasnah Soulcasts an attacker into fire (Ch 36)
- **Related concepts:** Tied to the broader magic system involving Stormlight
- **Unanswered questions:** How widespread is this ability? Are there other methods?

### Stormlight
- **First explained:** Chapter 38
- **What we know:** A mystical energy that can be inhaled and used for various purposes, including healing.
- **How it works:** Captured in spheres, can be drawn upon by certain individuals.
- **Limitations/Rules:** Limited by the availability of Stormlight and the individual's ability to harness it.
- **Key examples:** Kaladin unknowingly uses Stormlight to heal (Ch 38)
- **Related concepts:** Integral to the functioning of Soulcasters and possibly other fabrials
- **Unanswered questions:** What are the full capabilities and limitations of Stormlight?

## Common Reader Questions (Up to Chapter 40)

**Q: Who is Kaladin and what is his role?**  
A: Kaladin is a former surgeon's apprentice turned bridgeman and leader of Bridge Four. He strives to protect his crew and survive the brutal conditions of the Shattered Plains (Ch 31-40).

**Q: What happened during the side-carry maneuver in Chapter 32?**  
A: Kaladin innovated a new way of carrying the bridge to minimize casualties, successfully protecting his men during a bridge run.

**Q: How does Soulcasting work?**  
A: Soulcasting is a magical process that transforms materials using a Soulcaster and requires Stormlight. It is not fully understood but is shown to be powerful and dangerous (Ch 36).

**Q: What are Shallan's motivations for stealing the Soulcaster?**  
A: Shallan aims to save her family from financial ruin by acquiring a functional Soulcaster, despite her moral reservations (Ch 33-36).

**Q: How does Kaladin survive the highstorm in Chapter 34?**  
A: Through a combination of resilience and an unexplained ability to use Stormlight, which heals him during the storm.

**Q: What is the significance of Shallan's sketches in Chapter 39?**  
A: They reveal unconscious visions and events, suggesting a deeper connection to the magic and mysteries of the world.

**Q: How do Kaladin and Syl relate to each other?**  
A: Syl is a windspren who supports and guides Kaladin, playing a crucial role in his survival and growth (Ch 32-38).

**Q: What is the current state of Sadeas's Warcamp?**  
A: The warcamp is experiencing internal instability following the execution of Lamaril, with Gaz maintaining his position amid the chaos (Ch 34).

**Q: How does Stormlight function in the story?**  
A: Stormlight is a mystical energy used for healing and powering fabrials like Soulcasters. It is inhaled and utilized by certain individuals (Ch 38).

**Q: What ethical dilemmas does Shallan face in Chapters 36-39?**  
A: Shallan struggles with the morality of stealing Jasnah's Soulcaster and the implications of using it for her family's benefit.

**Q: What is the role of Bridge Four in the story?**  
A: Bridge Four is a group of bridgemen led by Kaladin, who seeks to protect and uplift them through innovative strategies and leadership (Ch 32-40).

**Q: Who is Jasnah Kholin and what is her significance?**  
A: Jasnah is a renowned scholar and heretic, mentor to Shallan, and a powerful Soulcaster who influences Shallan's ethical journey (Ch 33-36).

**Q: What are the Shattered Plains and why are they important?**  
A: The Shattered Plains is the central battlefield between humans and the Parshendi, where much of the book's action and Kaladin's story unfolds (Ch 32-40).

**Q: What powers does Kaladin potentially possess?**  
A: Kaladin appears to have the ability to draw in and use Stormlight, which grants him enhanced healing and possibly other abilities (Ch 38).

**Q: What unresolved questions remain about Kaladin's powers?**  
A: The full extent and potential of Kaladin's Stormlight abilities are unknown, raising questions about his future role and impact on the story.
