## World-Building / Lore

### Highstorms
- **First explained:** Chapter 19 (via Dalinar’s vision)
- **What we know:** Highstorms are powerful, periodic storms that affect the world, causing devastation but also leaving behind stormlight-infused spheres.
- **How it works:** They seem to have a spiritual or prophetic connection, as evidenced by Dalinar’s visions during them (Ch. 19).
- **Limitations/Rules:** They are dangerous and unpredictable; only certain individuals experience visions during them.
- **Key examples:** Dalinar’s vision during a highstorm (Ch. 19).
- **Related concepts:** Stormlight, a form of power harnessed from the storms.
- **Unanswered questions:** What is the true nature of highstorms and their connection to the visions?

### Shardbearers
- **First explained:** Chapter 12 (during the chasmfiend hunt)
- **What we know:** Shardbearers wield Shardblades and wear Shardplate, granting them enhanced strength and combat abilities.
- **How it works:** Shardblades can cut through almost anything, and Shardplate provides significant protection and strength (Ch. 13).
- **Limitations/Rules:** Shardplate can be damaged; Shardblades require ten heartbeats to summon.
- **Key examples:** Dalinar and Adolin use Shardplate and Shardblades during the chasmfiend battle (Ch. 13).
- **Related concepts:** Knights Radiant, who historically wielded Shardblades.
- **Unanswered questions:** What is the origin of Shardblades and Shardplate?

## Common Reader Questions (Up to Chapter 20)

**Q: Why does Kaladin decide to lead Bridge Four?**  
A: After contemplating suicide, Kaladin is persuaded by Syl’s encouragement to try to improve the dire circumstances of the bridgemen, seeing leadership as a way to protect his men (Ch. 11).

**Q: What are Dalinar’s visions about?**  
A: Dalinar experiences visions during highstorms, offering cryptic guidance related to uniting the highprinces and understanding the past, such as the Knights Radiant (Ch. 19).

**Q: How does Kaladin motivate the bridgemen?**  
A: Kaladin tries to implement a training regime to increase their survival chances, despite resistance from Gaz and initial reluctance from the bridgemen (Ch. 14, 17).

**Q: What happened during the chasmfiend hunt?**  
A: Dalinar, Adolin, and Elhokar hunt a chasmfiend; Dalinar saves Elhokar from a fatal accident, showcasing his leadership and bravery (Ch. 13).

**Q: Why is there tension between Dalinar and Sadeas?**  
A: Political rivalry and differing approaches to warfare create tension; Dalinar’s visions and Sadeas’s taunts exacerbate this (Ch. 15, 18).

**Q: How does Adolin support his father?**  
A: Adolin defends Dalinar’s honor against Sadeas’s insults and investigates potential threats, like the tampered saddle strap (Ch. 15, 18).

**Q: What is the significance of Elhokar’s saddle incident?**  
A: It suggests possible sabotage and underlying threats to the king’s safety, reflecting political tensions (Ch. 15).

**Q: How does Syl support Kaladin?**  
A: Syl offers emotional support, encourages Kaladin to lead, and helps him maintain hope and resolve (Ch. 11, 14).

**Q: What was the outcome of Kaladin’s fight with Jost?**  
A: Kaladin performed well but ultimately lost; this experience highlighted his potential with the quarterstaff and the bond with his brother Tien (Ch. 16).

**Q: What role does Wit play in the court dynamics?**  
A: Wit serves as a court jester, using humor and insight to diffuse tensions and affect court proceedings, as seen in his interaction with Sadeas (Ch. 15).

**Q: How do highstorms affect the characters?**  
A: Highstorms are dangerous but also have mystical properties, such as triggering Dalinar’s visions, which provide guidance and warnings (Ch. 19).

**Q: What are gemhearts, and why are they important?**  
A: Gemhearts are valuable resources obtained from chasmfiends, crucial for wealth and power; they are a focal point of the war on the Shattered Plains (Ch. 13).

**Q: Why is Dalinar concerned about uniting the highprinces?**  
A: Dalinar believes unity is essential for Alethkar’s stability and success against external threats, as indicated by his visions (Ch. 19).

**Q: How does Kaladin cope with his past failures?**  
A: Kaladin is haunted by memories, particularly of his brother Tien, but finds purpose in protecting Bridge Four and seeking redemption (Ch. 11, 14).

**Q: What is the significance of the Knights Radiant in Dalinar’s vision?**  
A: The Knights Radiant represent a lost order of defenders; their appearance in Dalinar’s vision suggests a connection to his mission and the greater cosmic struggle (Ch. 19).
