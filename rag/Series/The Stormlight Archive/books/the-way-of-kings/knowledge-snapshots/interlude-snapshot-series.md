# Interlude Knowledge Snapshot Series: The Way of Kings

**Book:** The Way of Kings  
**Coverage:** All Interludes (I-1 through I-9)  
**Note:** Interludes may contain spoilers for main narrative chapters. Spoiler callouts are included where applicable.

---

## Overview of Interludes

The interludes in "The Way of Kings" provide glimpses into the broader world of Roshar, introducing new locations, cultures, and minor characters who, while not central to the main storyline, enrich the narrative with diverse perspectives. These interludes expand on the world-building, offer thematic depth, and occasionally foreshadow future plot developments.

---

## Interlude Series Structure

### Interlude Set 1 (After Part 1)

#### Interlude I-1: Ishikk
- **POV Character:** Ishikk
- **Location:** Purelake
- **Time Context:** Concurrent with early events of the main narrative
- **Summary:** Ishikk, a fisherman, interacts with three foreigners searching for someone named Hoid.
- **Key Events:**
  - Ishikk's daily routine in the Purelake.
  - Interaction with three mysterious foreigners.
- **Significance:** Introduces the Purelake culture and hints at Hoid's significance.
- **Connections to Main Narrative:** Hoid is a recurring character across the series, and this interlude hints at his elusive nature.

#### Interlude I-2: Nan Balat
- **POV Character:** Nan Balat
- **Location:** Kharbranth
- **Time Context:** During Shallan's stay in Kharbranth
- **Summary:** Nan Balat deals with family troubles and reflects on Shallan's departure.
- **Key Events:**
  - Balat's inner turmoil and destructive tendencies.
  - Concerns about his family's future.
- **Significance:** Provides insight into Shallan's family dynamics.
- **Connections to Main Narrative:** Offers background on Shallan's motivations and her family's struggles.

#### Interlude I-3: The Glory of Ignorance
- **POV Character:** Szeth
- **Location:** Bavland
- **Time Context:** Concurrent with early events
- **Summary:** Szeth, a Truthless Shin assassin, serves a minor lord while internally grappling with his past actions.
- **Key Events:**
  - Szeth's servitude and obedience.
  - Reflection on his past kills.
- **Significance:** Explores Szeth's character, showcasing his inner conflict and adherence to his oath.
- **Connections to Main Narrative:** Szeth's actions have significant implications for the political landscape of Roshar.

### Interlude Set 2 (After Part 2)

#### Interlude I-4: Rysn
- **POV Character:** Rysn
- **Location:** Shinovar
- **Time Context:** After Part 2 events
- **Summary:** Rysn, an apprentice merchant, visits Shinovar and learns about its unique culture.
- **Key Events:**
  - Observations of Shinovar's landscape.
  - Insights from her mentor, Vstim.
- **Significance:** Highlights Shinovar's distinct ecology and cultural values.
- **Connections to Main Narrative:** Provides context for the Shin people's worldview, relevant to Szeth's background.

#### Interlude I-5: Axies the Collector
- **POV Character:** Axies
- **Location:** Kasitor
- **Time Context:** After Part 2 events
- **Summary:** Axies studies spren and ends up imprisoned after a drunken mishap.
- **Key Events:**
  - Attempt to document spren behavior.
  - Arrest and eventual release.
- **Significance:** Offers insight into the nature of spren.
- **Connections to Main Narrative:** Spren play a crucial role in the magic system and character development across the book.

#### Interlude I-6: A Work of Art
- **POV Character:** Taravangian
- **Location:** Kharbranth
- **Time Context:** After Part 2 events
- **Summary:** King Taravangian tends to the sick and reflects on his secretive, morally complex plans.
- **Key Events:**
  - Taravangian's hospital work.
  - Hints at deeper, darker motives.
- **Significance:** Introduces Taravangian's dual nature and intelligence.
- **Connections to Main Narrative:** Foreshadows his impact on broader political dynamics.
