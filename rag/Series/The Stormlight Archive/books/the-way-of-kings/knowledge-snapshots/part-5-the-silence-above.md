# Part-Level Knowledge Snapshot: The Silence Above

**Book:** The Way of Kings  
**Part:** 5: The Silence Above  
**Coverage:** Chapters 70-75  
**Safe for readers up to:** Chapter 75 (end of this part)

---

## Part Overview

In Part 5: The Silence Above, the narrative explores significant revelations and shifts in character dynamics, particularly focusing on Shallan, Szeth, and Dalinar. Shallan confronts the events leading to Kabsal's death, exposing critical truths about herself and her mentor, Jasnah. This confrontation leads to Shallan's deeper understanding of Soulcasting and her acceptance as Jasnah's apprentice. Meanwhile, Szeth's storyline takes a dark turn as he uncovers the true identity of his master, King Taravangian, who manipulates Szeth into believing his assassinations serve a greater good. Dalinar's arc continues with a vision during a highstorm, where he learns about the impending Everstorm and the need to unite Roshar against it, revealing that the Almighty is dead, killed by Odium.

The part delves into themes of trust, truth, and destiny. Characters grapple with inner conflicts and the consequences of their past actions, setting the stage for future confrontations. The narrative arc builds tension through revelations and strategic positioning, highlighting the characters' evolving roles in the looming conflict.

## Part Structure (Part > Chapters)

### Chapter-by-Chapter Breakdown

#### Chapter 70: Sea of Glass
- **POV:** Shallan Davar
- **Key Events:** Shallan confronts Jasnah about her Soulcaster, entering Shadesmar and forming a bond with spren. She reveals a significant truth about her past.
- **Significance to Part Arc:** This chapter deepens Shallan's character, establishing her as a key player in understanding Soulcasting and setting her on a path of knowledge and responsibility.

#### Chapter 71: Recorded In Blood
- **POV:** Szeth
- **Key Events:** Szeth discovers King Taravangian is his master and is shown a secret hospital where the dying's last words are recorded. Dalinar Kholin is added to Szeth's list of targets.
- **Significance to Part Arc:** This revelation about Taravangian's plans adds a layer of intrigue and danger, positioning Szeth as an unwitting tool in a larger scheme.

#### Chapter 72: Veristitalian
- **POV:** Shallan Davar
- **Key Events:** Shallan questions Jasnah about Soulcasting and the nature of the Voidbringers, leading to the theory that Parshmen are enslaved Voidbringers.
- **Significance to Part Arc:** The chapter expands on the lore of Voidbringers, linking them to the present and setting up future exploration of this theory.

#### Chapter 73: Trust
- **POV:** Kaladin
- **Key Events:** Dalinar offers Kaladin leadership of Bridge Four as his honor guard. Kaladin contemplates trust and agrees to the offer, with conditions.
- **Significance to Part Arc:** This chapter focuses on themes of trust and leadership, highlighting Kaladin's growing role and his acceptance of responsibility.

#### Chapter 74: Ghostblood
- **POV:** Shallan Davar
- **Key Events:** Shallan reads Jasnah's notes and concludes that Parshmen are Voidbringers. She learns about the Ghostbloods and suspects her father's involvement.
- **Significance to Part Arc:** The chapter ties Shallan's personal history to larger conspiracies, hinting at deeper connections and threats.

#### Chapter 75: In the Top Room
- **POV:** Dalinar Kholin
- **Key Events:** During a highstorm vision, Dalinar learns about the Everstorm and the need to rebuild the Knights Radiant. The Almighty reveals Odium killed him.
- **Significance to Part Arc:** This vision sets the stakes for the looming conflict, emphasizing Dalinar's role in uniting Roshar and preparing for the True Desolation.

## Common Reader Questions (Up to End of This Part)

**Q: Why wasn't Jasnah poisoned like Shallan and Kabsal?**  
A: Jasnah used her Soulcaster to transform the poison, ensuring her safety (Ch 70).

**Q: What is the significance of the Ghostbloods?**  
A: The Ghostbloods are a secretive group with connections to Shallan's family, hinting at a larger conspiracy (Ch 74).

**Q: Why does Szeth obey Taravangian's orders?**  
A: Szeth is bound by his status as "Truthless" and believes he must follow orders without question (Ch 71).

**Q: What does the vision during the highstorm mean for Dalinar?**  
A: It emphasizes his role in uniting Roshar and preparing for the coming Everstorm (Ch 75).

**Q: How does Shallan's bond with the spren affect her?**  
A: It grants her deeper understanding of Soulcasting and marks her growth as a character (Ch 70).

**Q: What are the Voidbringers, according to Jasnah?**  
A: Jasnah theorizes they are the Parshmen, enslaved and misunderstood throughout history (Ch 72).

**Q: Why does Kaladin trust Dalinar's offer?**  
A: Kaladin sees Dalinar's honor and integrity, aligning with his own values (Ch 73).

**Q: How does Taravangian justify his actions to Szeth?**  
A: He claims the assassinations are necessary for Roshar's stability and future (Ch 71).

**Q: What does the death of the Almighty signify?**  
A: It reveals the immense threat posed by Odium and the cosmic stakes involved (Ch 75).

**Q: How is Shallan's past affecting her current decisions?**  
A: Her past actions, like killing her father, drive her to seek redemption and knowledge (Ch 70).
