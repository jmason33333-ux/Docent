## Major Characters (Introduced So Far)

### Dalinar Kholin
- **First appearance:** Chapter 61
- **Role:** Highprince of Alethkar, leader in the war against the Parshendi
- **Key relationships:**
  - **Adolin Kholin:** Son, who harbors concerns over Dalinar's leadership and visions.
  - **Navani Kholin:** Sister-in-law, confidante, and potential romantic interest.
  - **Sadeas:** Ally turned enemy, whose betrayal impacts Dalinar deeply.
- **Motivations/Goals:** To unite the Alethi highprinces and understand the visions that guide him.
- **Character Arc (through Ch 70):**
  - **Starting point:** Struggling with the validity of his visions and his role in the war.
  - **Key moments:** His trust in Sadeas is shattered when Sadeas abandons him during a battle (Ch 65).
  - **Current state:** Determined to forge a new path following Sadeas's betrayal.
- **Key scenes/chapters:** Significant involvement in Ch 61, 65, 67, and 69.

### Kaladin
- **First appearance:** Chapter 62
- **Role:** Leader of Bridge Four, former soldier, and slave
- **Key relationships:**
  - **Teft:** A member of Bridge Four who supports Kaladin's leadership.
  - **Dalinar Kholin:** Kaladin's actions during the battle indirectly save Dalinar.
- **Abilities/Powers:** Shows potential Surgebinding abilities, enhancing his effectiveness in battle.
- **Motivations/Goals:** To protect his men and find a sense of belonging and purpose.
- **Character Arc (through Ch 70):**
  - **Starting point:** Leading Bridge Four under dire circumstances.
  - **Key moments:** Successfully protects his crew using new tactics and armor (Ch 62, 63).
  - **Current state:** Gains confidence and purpose after pivotal moments in battle (Ch 65, 68).
- **Key scenes/chapters:** Key developments in Ch 62, 63, 65, 67, and 68.

### Navani Kholin
- **First appearance:** Chapter 69
- **Role:** Queen Dowager, mother to the king, and a skilled fabrial scholar
- **Key relationships:**
  - **Dalinar Kholin:** Supports and develops a deeper bond with Dalinar.
- **Motivations/Goals:** To protect her family and find truth in the political chaos.
- **Character Arc (through Ch 70):**
  - **Starting point:** Concerned about Dalinar's visions and the political landscape.
  - **Key moments:** Confronts the aftermath of Sadeas's betrayal (Ch 69).
  - **Current state:** More resolved and supportive of Dalinar's leadership.
- **Key scenes/chapters:** Significant involvement in Ch 69.

## World-Building / Lore

### Shattered Plains
- **First explained:** Chapter 61
- **What we know:** A vast, broken landscape serving as the battlefield against the Parshendi.
- **Key examples:** Scenes in Ch 61, 62, 65, and 68 take place here.
- **Unanswered questions:** The origins of the Shattered Plains and their significance.

### Surgebinding
- **First explained:** Chapter 68
- **What we know:** A magical ability that Kaladin seems to possess, allowing manipulation of gravity and strength.
- **Key examples:** Kaladin using his abilities during battles (Ch 65, 68).
- **Unanswered questions:** Full potential and limitations of Kaladin's abilities.

## Common Reader Questions (Up to Chapter 70)

**Q: What are Dalinar's visions about?**  
A: They provide glimpses of past events and possibly future dangers, urging him to unite the highprinces (Ch 61, 69).

**Q: Why did Sadeas betray Dalinar?**  
A: Sadeas seeks power and sees Dalinar's leadership and visions as a threat (Ch 65).

**Q: How does Kaladin save Dalinar?**  
A: By using his emerging Surgebinding abilities to navigate the battlefield and protect Dalinar (Ch 68).

**Q: What is the significance of the Tower plateau?**  
A: It is a strategically critical location on the Shattered Plains where major battles occur (Ch 65).

**Q: How does Surgebinding work?**  
A: It involves manipulating gravity and strength, as demonstrated by Kaladin in battle (Ch 65, 68).

**Q: What are the stakes for Bridge Four?**  
A: Their survival and freedom depend on successful leadership and tactics (Ch 62, 63).

**Q: How does Navani support Dalinar?**  
A: She provides emotional support and helps navigate political challenges (Ch 69).

**Q: How do Dalinar and Adolin's views differ?**  
A: Adolin is skeptical of Dalinar's visions, fearing for their impact on leadership (Ch 61, 65).

**Q: What is the role of the Alethi Highprinces?**  
A: They are key players in the war, but their internal conflicts hinder unity (Ch 61).

**Q: What is the current status of the Shattered Plains conflict?**  
A: The betrayal by Sadeas shifts alliances, with Dalinar seeking new strategies (Ch 69).
