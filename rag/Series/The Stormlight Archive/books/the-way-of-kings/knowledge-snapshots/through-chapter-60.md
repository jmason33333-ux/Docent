## World-Building / Lore

### Surgebinding
- **First explained:** Chapter 57
- **What we know:** Surgebinding involves drawing Stormlight from spheres and using it for various abilities.
- **How it works:** Kaladin can unconsciously absorb Stormlight, which enhances physical capabilities.
- **Limitations/Rules:** Requires infused spheres; Kaladin's control is still developing.
- **Key examples:** Kaladin's instinctive use during a confrontation with Teft (Ch 57).
- **Related concepts:** Ties to the Knights Radiant and their Ideals.
- **Unanswered questions:** Full scope of abilities and the history of Surgebinding.

### Highstorm Visions
- **First explained:** Chapter 52
- **What we know:** Dalinar experiences visions during highstorms, showing past events and cryptic messages.
- **How it works:** He is transported to another time and place, often embodying another person.
- **Limitations/Rules:** Visions are uncontrollable, with varied clarity and relevance.
- **Key examples:** Vision at Feverstone Keep (Ch 52), interaction with Nohadon (Ch 60).
- **Related concepts:** Possible connection to the Almighty and the Heralds.
- **Unanswered questions:** Purpose of the visions and their ultimate message.
