## Rowan's "If Asked" Notes

**Q: What is Shadesmar?**  
A: Shadesmar is a mysterious realm connected to spren and the Cognitive Realm, not a physical place as traditionally understood.

**Q: Why is Shallan drawing the ocean of glass beads?**  
A: Shallan is sketching her memories of Shadesmar, a realm she has briefly glimpsed that features an ocean made of glass beads.

**Q: What does Jasnah mean by 'living ideas'?**  
A: Jasnah suggests that spren are manifestations of ideas or concepts from the Cognitive Realm that have gained some level of sentience.
