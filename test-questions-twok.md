## Deep Questions (Should trigger FULL prompt with 6-section format)

9. **Chapter 25** - "I really don't understand what Surgebinding is - help me understand it better"
   - **Type:** Worldbuilding/Clarification, Deep
   - **Chapter:** 25 (Part 2)

     - Short Version: What Surgebinding is
     - What You've Seen: References to Kaladin's powers, chapter citations
     - How to Think About It: Mental model/analogy
     - Why It Matters: Connection to story
     - What's Still Unknown: What's not yet revealed
     - Want to Know More?: Specific follow-ups

## Testing Strategy

### For Each Question, Verify:
1. ✅ Correct prompt used (SHORT vs FULL)
2. ✅ Appropriate context loaded (snapshot vs chapters vs book summary)
3. ✅ Structured format followed (3-section for SHORT, 6-section for FULL)
4. ✅ "Want to Know More?" always included
5. ✅ Chapter citations present
6. ✅ No spoilers beyond stated chapter
7. ✅ Quality of answer matches Dawnshard example

### Expected Context Sources:
- **Chapters 1-10:** Book summary (if first interaction) + snapshot through Ch 10
- **Chapters 11-20:** Snapshot through Ch 10 or Ch 20 depending on question
- **Chapters 21-30:** Snapshot through Ch 20 or Ch 30
- **Chapters 31-40:** Snapshot through Ch 30 or Ch 40
- **Chapters 41-50:** Snapshot through Ch 40 or Ch 50
- **Chapters 51-60:** Snapshot through Ch 50 or Ch 60
- **Chapters 61-70:** Snapshot through Ch 60 or Ch 70
- **Chapters 71-75:** Snapshot through Ch 70 or Ch 75
